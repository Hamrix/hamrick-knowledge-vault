# Source-Origin Firewall

Storage location does not upgrade evidence.

Distinguish:
1. current container;
2. assertion origin;
3. underlying evidence.

Useful analytical provenance classes:
- primary_observed
- scholarly_observed
- derivative_with_traceable_citation
- derivative_untraced
- quarantined_lead
- ai_generated_lead
- unknown_origin

Copies of one assertion are one evidence lineage.

Unsourced trees, GEDCOM, AI reports, untraced family narratives, heraldry marketing pages, `lestrangeheritage.com`, and mirrors of quarantined claims cannot alone support `confirmed` or `strong` parentage.
