# Aetheling Evidence Project - Correction Register

This register records factual corrections to legacy Aetheling, Selby Manor, lineage, heraldic, and institutional materials. Legacy wording remains preserved in source files and Git history; corrected statements govern new public work.

## Correction 001 - Historic England identifier

**Legacy statement:** The White House at Cliffe is Historic England List Entry 1316447.

**Corrected statement:** [[The White House at Cliffe is Grade II listed under entry 1316283]].

**Status:** confirmed correction.

**Evidence:** [[Historic England - The White House Cliffe list entry 1316283]].

**Public wording:** “The White House at Main Street, Cliffe, is a Grade II-listed late-eighteenth-century house, National Heritage List entry 1316283.”

**Limit:** The listing does not establish ownership, manorial status, estate acreage, family succession, mineral rights, or market value.

## Correction 002 - First registration of a lordship title

**Legacy statement:** A new lordship title for Whitehouse or Selby Manor can be first-registered alongside the freehold under the Land Registration Act 2002 and Practice Guide 22.

**Corrected statement:** [[First registration of a lordship title has not been possible since 13 October 2003]].

**Status:** confirmed correction; legacy legal route withdrawn.

**Evidence:** [[HM Land Registry - Practice Guide 22 Manors]].

**Public wording:** “HM Land Registry distinguishes lordship titles, physical manorial land, and manorial rights. It has not accepted first-registration applications for lordship titles since 13 October 2003.”

**Limit:** Physical land can still be registered through ordinary procedures, and alleged manorial rights require separate proof. Neither genealogy nor surname establishes ownership.

**Status:** the listed-building identity is confirmed; the manor equivalence remains a hypothesis requiring primary evidence.

## Website enforcement rule

No public page may use the words **verified**, **confirmed lineage**, **unbroken**, **direct heir**, **owner**, **filed**, **pending registration**, **certified transcript**, or a numerical confidence score unless an atomic vault claim directly supports that exact proposition.

Each displayed claim must carry one of these states:

- **Documented**
- **Supported hypothesis**
- **Family tradition / symbolic identity**
- **Disputed or withdrawn**
