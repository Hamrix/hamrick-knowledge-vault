## Decision rules

### Upgrade to documented

A parent-child relationship may be upgraded only if an original or near-original record explicitly names the relationship, or if multiple independent records correlate without unresolved conflicts under the Genealogical Proof Standard.

### Remain supported hypothesis

The relationship remains a supported hypothesis if the 1880 image confirms Frederick as son in Robert’s household but no independent record yet corroborates the parental pair.

### Split father and mother claims

Robert’s fatherhood and Docia’s motherhood must be evaluated separately. A record naming Robert as father does not automatically prove Docia as mother, and a record naming Docia does not automatically prove Robert unless the record explicitly or contextually supports both.

### Quarantine conflicting names

The name Eliza Payne remains quarantined unless an original record establishes why it belongs in Frederick Payne Selby’s parentage. It may be a conflation, a middle-name inference, a previous spouse/relative confusion, or an unresolved legacy import.
