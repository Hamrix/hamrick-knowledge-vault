# Hon. Benjamin Selby Link Update, 2026-08-04

## Result

The modern Selby evidence has advanced one generation beyond Benjamin “Ben” Selby born in 1826.

A near-contemporary 1878 Kentucky biographical profile reports that the younger Ben Selby was a son of **Hon. Benjamin Selby**. Its distinctive description of the younger man—birth in Columbia, legal career, marriages to Lucy S. Hunter and Docia Stone, and later residence in Eminence—correlates strongly with the reviewed 1880 census and Frederick Payne Selby's 1884 parentage record.

## Corrected stopping point

Hon. Benjamin Selby is now the earliest strongly supported direct paternal ancestor in this segment. The prior model that stopped with the younger Benjamin or treated an undefined Robert Benjamin Selby as the required bridge no longer controls the reconstruction.
