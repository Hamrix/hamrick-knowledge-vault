# Lingan Wilson Selby Adair loose probate request packet - 2026-08-20

## Objective

Retrieve every surviving unbound or separately filed record for Lingan W. or Lingan Wilson Selby's 1821 Adair County estate and determine whether any document explicitly identifies Benjamin Selby as a son, heir, child, or other relative.

## Known locators

- Decedent: Lingan W. Selby / Lingan Wilson Selby; administration granted 5 February 1821.
- Administrator: Benjamin Selby; $3,000 bond; William Owens as security.
- Widow: Mary Selby, directly identified 2 July 1821.
- County Order Book D: page 213, administrator and appraisers; page 238, inventory return; page 239, widow's allotment order.
- Will Book B: page 99, inventory and appraisement certified 30 June 1821 and recorded July 1821.
- Appraisers: Elsey Creel, Lewis Lampton, John Anderson, and Benjamin Lampton, or any three.
- Search variants: `Lingan`, `Lingan W.`, `Lingan Wilson`, `Ligan`, `Kingan`, `Selby`, and `Selly`.

## Records requested

1. Original administration bond, oath, letters, and fiduciary appointment.
2. Appraisers' warrant and original inventory or appraisement papers.
3. Estate sale bill, purchaser list, or proceeds account.
4. Mary Selby's widow-allotment or dower return and any supporting survey.
5. Administrator accounts, interim or final settlements, vouchers, claims, receipts, and releases.
6. Distribution, heir schedule, partition, descent affidavit, or estate and heir deeds.
7. Guardian requests, guardian bonds, or guardian settlements for any minor child receiving from this estate.
8. County- or Circuit-Court civil/equity case file involving Lingan's estate, widow, administrator, heirs, or land.
9. A written negative response identifying each searched series if no file survives.

## Acceptance test

The parentage hypothesis advances only if an original or reliably imaged record explicitly calls Benjamin a son, child, heir, next of kin, or otherwise states a relationship to Lingan. Administrator status, shared surname, proximity, an online tree, or a later family assertion is insufficient. If no relationship-bearing document is returned, the link remains unproved and the people remain unmerged.

## Preservation instructions

Preserve repository citations, staff correspondence, receipt or order number, original filenames, rights statements, and SHA-256 hashes. Keep supplied manuscript images internal until rights are reviewed. Record a negative search result with the exact series and date rather than treating a missing response as evidence.
