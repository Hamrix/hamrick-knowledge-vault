# Aetheling Vault-First Research Rules

## Canonical working layer

The local Obsidian vault at `C:\Users\hamri\OneDrive\Documents\Hamrick-Knowledge-Vault` is the canonical compiled research layer for the Aetheling Evidence Project. The private GitHub repository `Hamrix/hamrick-knowledge-vault` is its connected versioned copy.

For every Aetheling or family-history task, search the vault before relying on conversation memory, an online tree, an AI-generated narrative, or a new general web search.

## Authority order

1. Reviewed primary-source images, original records, and exact transcriptions.
2. Structured source and claim notes in the Hamrick Knowledge Vault.
3. Correlated project conclusions and approved project-state notes.
4. Secondary compilations and derivative indexes, with their limitations stated.
5. Family tradition, user research conclusions, and working hypotheses, clearly labeled.
6. Online trees, GEDCOM files, and AI-generated reports as discovery aids only.

Primary records remain the ultimate evidentiary authority even when the vault is the first research stop.

## GEDCOM boundary

Do not use the current GEDCOM to support, reject, reconcile, or extend a claim unless the user explicitly requests GEDCOM analysis. It requires updating and may contain duplicated, merged, or incorrect identities.

## Exact names and correlated identities

Preserve the exact form used by each source. A project-level correlated identity may unite multiple source forms when the total record pattern supports it, but the project note must distinguish:

- the name written in each record;
- the correlated identity conclusion;
- the evidence supporting the correlation;
- remaining documentary gaps.

## Publication boundary

A verified modern household or parent-child relationship does not automatically establish a colonial, transatlantic, medieval, noble, or royal connection. Those bridges require their own identity-specific evidence chain.

## Required response pattern

Vault-backed findings should report:

1. the current conclusion;
2. the vault notes and primary sources used;
3. conflicts or limitations;
4. the next record needed;
5. any proposed or completed vault update.
