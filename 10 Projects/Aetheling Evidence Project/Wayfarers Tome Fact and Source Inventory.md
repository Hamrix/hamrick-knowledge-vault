# Wayfarer's Tome Fact and Source Inventory

This note imports the public tome's fact/source ledger into Obsidian. It was extracted from `tome-companion/app/page.tsx` during the 2026-07-28 Codex session backup.

The public app is [The Wayfarer's Tome](https://wayfarers-tome.hamrix.chatgpt.site).

## Evidence boundary

The ledger presents documented historical context and active research leads. It does not prove a modern Selby, Haskins, Stafford, Peters, or living-family line to the medieval people in the app.
