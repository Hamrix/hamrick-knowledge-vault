# Modern Selby Chain Extracted from GEDCOM

## Scope

This note records the current paternal Selby research chain after correcting the uploaded GEDCOM against firsthand testimony and source audit findings. GEDCOM links are treated as hypotheses unless corroborated from identity-specific evidence.

The two most recent generations are additionally supported by [[Firsthand family relationship statement - 2026-07-29]].

The GEDCOM continues from Benjamin Selby through Burridge Hutchins Selby, Samuel Selby, William Selby, Sgt James Selby, Thomas James Selby, and John Selby.

That sequence is **excluded from the active paternal lineage** following Hamrick Ethelred Selby's direct correction that his family is not connected to the Burridge family line. See [[Firsthand lineage correction - Burridge branch excluded]] and [[The Burridge Selby branch is excluded from the active paternal lineage]].

The Burridge assignment is treated as an erroneous or conflated GEDCOM branch, not as a speculative ancestral route.

## Immediate evidence assessment

### Modern generations

The relationships Hamrick Ethelred Selby → Hamrick Selby and Hamrick Selby → Frederic Ethelred Selby are strongly supported in the private research context by direct testimony and consistent GEDCOM family links.

They have not yet been supported in the vault by inspected civil certificates or equivalent contemporaneous records. Public material should therefore describe them as confirmed by family testimony, not as civil-record-certified.

The GEDCOM contains multiple record citations for events associated with Robert Benjamin Selby, Frederick Payne Selby, James Benjamin Sanford Selby, Frederic Ethelred Selby, and related spouses. Each parent-child bridge must still be separately verified.

### Benjamin Selby of Adair County

The active chain presently reaches a historically evidenced adult Benjamin Selby in Adair County:

- deed-index entries place him in legal and property transactions from 1811 to 1818;
- county-history transcriptions identify him as a deputy sheriff beginning in 1812;
- an 1822 court transcription lists him in Columbia's first fire company;
- an 1823 court abstract names him as a commissioner in a title conveyance;
- an 1820 marriage transcription records him as an attesting witness.

This profile is recorded in [[Benjamin Selby of Adair County identity reconstruction]] and [[Benjamin Selby was active in Adair County Kentucky by 1811]].

The evidence does **not** yet establish that this Benjamin was Robert Benjamin Selby's father. It also does not support the GEDCOM suffix `Jr.`, a 1779 Maryland birth, or a death on 10 June 1856. See [[Benjamin Selby's Jr suffix and vital details remain unverified]].

## Public-use rule

The website may describe the immediate modern relationships as confirmed by family testimony and may describe an adult Benjamin Selby as active in Adair County by 1811.

It must not:

- state that Robert Benjamin was proven to be Benjamin's son;
- attach `Jr.`, 1779 Maryland, or 10 June 1856 as established facts;
- show the Burridge branch or any later attached English sequence as ancestral;
- call the complete chain verified, unbroken, proven, or continuous to England.
