# Selby proof phase 05 report

Steward review date: 8 August 2026  
Scope: original-record recovery for the Maryland Atkinson network, the Major Selby identity collision, and Benjamin Selby's Kentucky family and professional network

## Outcome

This phase added nine atomic claims and seven accession records. The canonical system now contains 71 claims, 27 accessions, and 21 record-request packets.

No new original directly proves William Atkinson Selby → Major Selby or Major Selby → Benjamin Selby. Those links remain conflicted and unpublished as fact. The phase instead produced one high-value relationship cluster, two same-name separations, and several precise search anchors.

## New findings

### John Atkinson's 1780 estate proceeding

An August 1780 Worcester County Orphans' Court order names Angelo Atkinson, John Atkinson, John Selby, William Atkinson Selby, Rachel Flemming, Mary White, and Nancy White as John Atkinson's residuary legatees. A 25 August appeal bond repeats the group, recites John Atkinson's will dated 21 March 1779, and identifies widow/executrix Mary as the later wife of Levin Blake.

The originals prove the legatee group and place John Selby and William Atkinson Selby together in the Atkinson estate. They do not state each person's relationship. RR-020 now makes recovery of the 1779 will and appeal disposition the first closure target.

### At least two Angelo Atkinsons

Joshua Atkinson's 1773 will names an underage son Angelo and directs that Solomon Townsend care for him, with William Townsend as successor before age twenty-one. That young man cannot be the deceased Angelo whose will was proved in 1766. The single-identity proposition is disproven at high confidence. RR-021 routes the younger Angelo through guardianship, land, tax, and adult-deed records.

### William Atkinson Selby in the Parker estate

A Worcester County estate-account continuation records an allowed payment to William Atkinson Selby after the Scarborough Parker inventory was exhibited in 1789. FamilySearch full-text discovery supplied an incorrect Bourbon County collection label; the original's Worcester heading and parties control. This adds a contemporary identity and associate cluster, not a parent-child statement.

### Major Selby in the 1820 census

Both original Nicholas County pages—111 and 129—are now preserved. They are distinct images with different page neighborhoods. The identity question remains open pending a column-level household transcription and correlation with tax districts, deeds, neighbors, and later censuses.

### Benjamin Selby in Kentucky

- The 1840 Frankfort Benjamin household has one male age fifty to fifty-nine and no male age fifteen to nineteen. It is not headed by, and does not place, the Benjamin born about 1824–1827.
- A 9 April 1849 Henry Circuit Court order admits Benjamin Selby and George C. Drane to practice law and records their oaths.
- William McCracken's 1860 will was witnessed by Ben Selby and E. P. Thomas Jr.; the probate entry says Benjamin Selby and Thomas proved it.
- Robert C. Selby's original 1938 death certificate names Benjamin Selby and Docie/Docia Stone. The FamilySearch index's “Dora” reading is incorrect.
- An image-unavailable 1874 Henry County birth index for Roberts Selby independently names Benjamin Selby and Docie Stone. Because the original image was unavailable, that claim remains sourced but unverified.

These records strengthen the Benjamin–Docia household reconstruction and provide professional associates for targeted research. None names Benjamin's parents.

4. William Atkinson Selby → Rhody Selby: verified, high.
5. William Atkinson Selby → Major Selby: conflicted/unproved, low.
6. Major Selby → Polly Selby: verified, high.
7. Major Selby → Benjamin Selby: conflicted/unproved, low.
8. Benjamin and Docie/Docia Stone → Robert C. Selby: verified as a record statement, high.
9. Benjamin and Sophia/Docia Stone → Frederick Payne Selby: verified, high, with the Sophia/Docia name equivalence still inferential.
