# Website information architecture

## Public

1. Research home - project scope, current audit posture, featured questions.
2. Evidence ledger - redacted atomic claims with status, confidence, and citations.
3. People and lineage - deceased/public profiles, relationship evidence, gap markers.
4. Estates and manors - property identity, tenure history, claim status, maps when verified.
5. Document exhibits - rights-cleared excerpts and transcriptions, never sensitive originals.
6. Contradiction log - open conflicts and resolution notes.
7. Research tasks - evidence requests, archive targets, transcription needs.
8. Method and changes - classification policy, source-quality rubric, release history.
9. Contribute - evidence submission guidance, volunteer interests, privacy terms.
10. Support - engagement information; donations disabled until governance is ready.

## Steward-only future area

- Full source viewer, living-person records, private citations, submission quarantine, merge review, redaction queue, legal-review notes, publication approvals, and change history.
