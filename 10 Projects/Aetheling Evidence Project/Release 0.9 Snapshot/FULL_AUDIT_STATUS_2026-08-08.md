## Four-record William packet

1. **1782 marriage:** a 1929 Headen family Bible transcript reports the couple and date; original Bible and independent church evidence remain missing.
2. **1779 treason matter:** the official MSA biography and series locator support the lead; the underlying indictment, recognizance, securities, and disposition remain missing.
3. **1809 Bourbon probate:** no such probate has been identified. An 1811 orphan order was recovered and proves death before the order and William → Rhody.
4. **Angelo's 1766 will:** directly proves William was John's son and Angelo's grandson. It does not name Mary, so Mary Atkinson → William remains sourced but unverified.

## Identity boundary

Records naming Major Selby currently form multiple clusters: an adult Worcester landholder in 1783, an adult Bourbon land purchaser in 1811, two Nicholas census index entries in 1820, Major as Polly's consenting father in 1825, and a later Maryland/Indiana compiled profile. These records cannot be merged by name alone.

Both distinct 1820 Nicholas County originals are now preserved. Their household columns, tax districts, deed neighborhoods, and later identities remain to be correlated.

Benjamin Selby's 1854 marriage identifies him as age 29, resident at Frankfort, and born in Henry County. His 1849 Henry Circuit Court bar admission and 1860 McCracken probate role establish a professional network. The 1880 census reports both parents born in Kentucky. No inspected record names his parents. An older Benjamin heading an 1840 Frankfort household was age fifty to fifty-nine and must remain separate.
