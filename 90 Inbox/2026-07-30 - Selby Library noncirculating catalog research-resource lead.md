# Selby Library noncirculating catalog research-resource lead

Status: repository / catalog lead; not lineage evidence.

## Source as supplied

The user uploaded `NoncircFeb2014.pdf`, a PDF titled **Genealogical Section of Selby Library: List of Noncirculating Books as of February 2014**.

The file is a library catalog/list of noncirculating genealogy and history books. It is not itself a Selby-family genealogy and should not be used as evidence of any Selby relationship.

## Why this appears in searches

This PDF appears in Selby-related searches because the institution/header contains the phrase **Selby Library**. Search engines may therefore match the word `Selby` even though the document is about the library collection, not the Selby family.

## Genealogical value

The value is indirect. The catalog identifies research books that may be useful for the broader project, especially Maryland, Virginia, Kentucky, heraldry, peerage, and genealogical-method sources.

Examples visible in the uploaded PDF include:

- **A closer look at Worcester County wills** / David V. Heise — Maryland, useful for Worcester County probate targets.
- **A collection of Maryland church records** / Henry C. Peden Jr. — Maryland, useful for parish and marriage/baptism checks.
- **Settlers of Maryland, 1679-1700** / Peter Wilson Coldham — Maryland, useful for colonial immigration/patent context.
- **Settlers of Maryland, 1679-1783** / Peter Wilson Coldham — Maryland, useful for later colonial Maryland settler references.
- **Second census of Kentucky, 1800** / G. Glenn Clift — Kentucky, useful for Bourbon/Nicholas/Rush migration context.
- **A calendar of Delaware wills: New Castle County, 1682-1800** — potentially useful for Townsend/White Delaware leads.
- Heraldry and peerage reference works such as Burke, Fox-Davies, and related English sources, useful only for background, not proof.

## Research caution

Do not confuse `Selby Library` with a Selby family repository or evidence of Selby ancestry. The catalog is a finding aid: it tells us what books exist in that library, not what the books say.

Any useful book from the catalog would need to be separately obtained and cited at page level.
