# Sarah Eveline Stafford proposed royal-lineage gateway

## Claim received

A proposed lineage identifies **Sarah Eveline Stafford** as the wife of **James Benjamin Sanford Selby**, described as a son of Frederick Payne Selby and Elizabeth Dolan. The claim further asserts that Sarah's paternal Stafford ancestry descends from the medieval Staffords, including Edmund Stafford, 5th Earl of Stafford, and Anne of Gloucester, granddaughter of King Edward III.

## Proposed modern connection

- Sarah Eveline Stafford
- married James Benjamin Sanford Selby
- James Benjamin Sanford Selby proposed as son of Frederick Payne Selby and Elizabeth Dolan
- this couple proposed as direct ancestors in the user's lineage

Each relationship above requires civil, church, census, obituary, succession, or other contemporary evidence before being promoted from lead to proved claim.

## Proposed medieval descent

The supplied reconstruction is:

1. King Edward III
2. Thomas of Woodstock, 1st Duke of Gloucester
3. Anne of Gloucester
4. Edmund Stafford, 5th Earl of Stafford
5. Humphrey Stafford, 1st Duke of Buckingham
6. later Stafford gentry generations
7. a colonial William Stafford, said to have arrived in Virginia in 1622 aboard the *Furtherance*
8. southern colonial and American Stafford generations
9. Sarah Eveline Stafford

## Important correction in nomenclature

The woman who married Edmund Stafford, 5th Earl of Stafford, was **Anne of Gloucester**, a daughter of Thomas of Woodstock and granddaughter of Edward III. Calling her merely "Lady Anne Plantagenet" obscures her correct historical identity and may encourage conflation with unrelated Anne Plantagenets.

## Evidence-status assessment

### Stronger medieval segment

The following general medieval relationships are historically well known and should be checked against authoritative peerage and charter sources:

- Edward III was father of Thomas of Woodstock.
- Thomas of Woodstock was father of Anne of Gloucester.
- Anne of Gloucester married Edmund Stafford, 5th Earl of Stafford.
- Their son Humphrey Stafford became 1st Duke of Buckingham.

This establishes that **some descendants of this Stafford ducal line possessed royal descent from Edward III**.

### Unproved transition points

The present material does not yet prove:

- that the later Stafford gentry line used in the reconstruction descends from the ducal Stafford line;
- that the immigrant William Stafford of 1622 was a descendant of that specific gentry branch;
- that the Virginia William Stafford is the ancestor of Sarah Eveline Stafford;
- that every American generation between the colonial immigrant and Sarah Eveline Stafford is correctly identified;
- that Sarah Eveline Stafford is the correct spouse of James Benjamin Sanford Selby in the user's direct line.

The phrase "multiple gentry generations" currently conceals the most important part of the pedigree.

## FamilySearch caution

FamilySearch is a collaborative tree and record-discovery platform, not itself a primary historical database proving every attached relationship. A lineage shown there must be decomposed into atomic parent-child claims and supported by the underlying records. User-contributed profiles and merged trees cannot be treated as proof merely because records are attached somewhere in the tree.

## Colonial gateway claim to test

The specific immigrant claim is:

- William Stafford, approximately 1602–1643
- arrived in Virginia in 1622 aboard the *Furtherance*
- younger son of armigerous Stafford gentry of Buckinghamshire
- settled in York County, Virginia

Required evidence:

1. passenger or muster record identifying this William Stafford;
2. age and residence evidence sufficient to distinguish him from other Staffords;
3. English baptism or probate record naming his parents;
4. proof that those parents belong to the claimed armigerous Stafford branch;
5. a continuous American descent from him to Sarah Eveline Stafford.

## Modern bridge acquisition plan

Priority records for Sarah Eveline Stafford and James Benjamin Sanford Selby:

1. marriage license or church marriage record;
2. death certificates and obituaries;
3. census households showing ages, birthplaces, and children;
4. baptismal records for their children;
5. succession or probate files;
6. records naming James Benjamin Sanford Selby's parents;
7. records naming Sarah Eveline Stafford's parents;
8. each Stafford generation reconstructed backward from Sarah using original records.

## Publication-safe wording

> Sarah Eveline Stafford is a promising candidate for an independent Stafford royal-descent route. The medieval Stafford descent from Anne of Gloucester and Edward III is historically established for the ducal family, but the continuous chain from Sarah Eveline Stafford through the colonial American Staffords to that medieval line has not yet been demonstrated in the vault.

## Current verdict

**Potentially important independent gateway; medieval royal segment credible, modern-to-colonial and colonial-to-medieval bridges unproved.**
