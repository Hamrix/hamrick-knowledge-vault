# William Selby Alderminster baptism, immigration, and Prince George's probate identity cluster

Status: research lead only; identity continuity unproved.

## Purpose

Preserve a FamilySearch source cluster that appears to combine an English baptism, a 1659 immigration-index entry, and late-seventeenth-century Prince George's County, Maryland probate records for a man named William Selby. Because William Selby is a recurring name in several unrelated Selby branches, these records must be treated as separate events until original documents prove they concern one individual.

### Evidentiary value

The index is a useful lead for a William Selby, son of James and Mary, baptized at Alderminster in 1630. Without the original parish image, transcript context, residence, occupation, later burial, or probate, it does not prove that this child emigrated to Maryland.

## Prince George's County probate cluster

Attached records displayed:
- `Prince George's. Probate Records 1698–1770, 1770–1808`.
- `Will of William Selby of Prince George's County, 5 November 1698 - 25 February 1698/9; Prince George's Probate Records 1698–1770`.
- `Wills and Testaments of Persons Deceasing in Maryland - William Selby`, displayed under 1699.

### Potential significance

The will is the strongest item in the cluster because an original probate record may name:
- spouse;
- children or other heirs;
- executors;
- witnesses;
- land or personal property;
- former residence;
- associates capable of distinguishing this William from other Selbys.

### Identity problem

The profile appears to suggest a sequence:

William Selby, baptized 1630 at Alderminster, Worcestershire
→ immigration or arrival entry in 1659
→ William Selby of Prince George's County, Maryland
→ will dated 5 November 1698 and proved 25 February 1698/9.

That sequence is chronologically possible, but the screenshot does not prove any of the arrows. The English baptism, immigration index, and Maryland probate may concern three different men.

## Relationship to the principal Maryland Selby project

This William Selby must remain separate from:
- Thomas Selby/Solbie of Accomack and Somerset;
- the proposed Lincolnshire-to-Maryland Thomas Selby line;
- William Selby, son of Thomas Selby III and Margaret Schoolfield;
- William Atkinson Selby, born about 1757/1758;
- the Newcastle William Selby who married Elizabeth Fenwick.

Prince George's County is also geographically distinct from the core Somerset/Worcester County Selby research. A relationship is possible but cannot be assumed from surname and chronology.

## Priority acquisition targets

1. Full image or register transcript of the 16 September 1630 Alderminster baptism.
2. The original parish register reference, including page, parish, and any marginal information.
3. Full details for the 1659 passenger/immigration index: destination, source title, vessel, origin, age, and accompanying persons.
4. Complete image and transcription of the 5 November 1698 will and 25 February 1698/9 probate.
5. Inventory, administration bond, account, and distribution records associated with the probate.
6. Prince George's County deeds, rent rolls, tax lists, court records, and church records for William Selby.
7. Search for named heirs or witnesses in Somerset, Worcester, Accomack, or English records.
8. Any record explicitly naming James and Mary as parents, Alderminster as origin, or an English sibling or associate.

## Current conclusion

A William Selby was indexed as baptized at Alderminster in 1630 to James and Mary, a William Selby appears in a 1659 immigration index, and a William Selby of Prince George's County left a will dated in 1698. These are valuable research targets, especially the probate, but they do not yet form a proved identity chain or a proved connection to William Atkinson Selby's Somerset/Worcester ancestry.
