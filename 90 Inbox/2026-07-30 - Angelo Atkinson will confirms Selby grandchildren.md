## Key will abstract details

The abstract reports the following major bequests and relationships:

- To son **John Atkinson**: right and title to part of the lot and house in Snow Hill Town where Angelo's storehouse stood, and half a parcel in Bald Cypress Swamp taken up jointly with William Allen on the east side of the Pocomoke River opposite William Allen's plantation.
- To son **John Atkinson**: `Johns Choice`, containing 52 acres on the mouth of Corker's Creek, on the east side of the Pocomoke River.
- To son **Joshua Atkinson**: right and title to land bought from Solomon Gray, being on Nassawadox Neck and joining Sheldon's Mill.

- To son **John Atkinson**: Angelo's part if Angelo died without male issue.

- To daughter **Patience**: enslaved people Nancy, Lerreus, and Sam, bed and furniture, double case of drawers.
- To granddaughter **Rachel Fleming**: enslaved girl Tilla.
- To daughter **Betty Tilney**, wife of Hezekiah Tilney: 30 pounds.

- To grandson **John Selby**: five pounds.
- Executors: sons **John Atkinson** and **Joshua Atkinson**.
