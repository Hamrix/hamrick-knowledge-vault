# Francis Atkinson WikiTree early Atkinson-Selby network lead

Status: online collaborative tree / source-lead; not standalone proof.

## Source captured

User supplied screenshot from WikiTree profile for **Francis Atkinson** while moving upward from Angelo Atkinson's tree.

The visible profile biography states:

- **Francis Atkinson** was born about **1635 in England**.
- Parents shown: **Christopher Atkinson** (1593-1670) and **Dorothy Case** (1604-1669).
- Francis married **Catherine Violet Stroude / Stroud** (1647-1688).
- Children listed:
  - **Timothy Atkinson** (1662-1738)
  - **James Atkinson** (1663-1721)
  - **Charles Atkinson** (1665-1703)
  - **John Atkinson** (1676-1677)
- Emigrated from England to Maryland in **1668**.
- Passed away **14 Aug 1690** in Somerset County, Maryland.

## Selby connection shown

The profile states that in **1686**, **Francis Atkinson** and **Thomas Selby** were witnesses to the last will of **Alexander Williams**.

Visible source note says:

- Somerset County, Maryland Wills, volume 4, page 268, will of Alexander Williams.
- Will dated **2 Mar 1686**.
- Probate **11 Aug 1687**.
- Witnesses listed: **Thos. Sellbe**, **Jno. Bishopp**, **Thos. Sellbie**, and **Francis Atkinson**.

The profile comment also states that this **Sellbe/Sellbie** may be father and son, both named Thomas Selby, and that in the 1750s two Atkinson descendants married two Selby descendants.

## Why this matters

This is potentially important because it suggests an earlier social/legal network between the Atkinson and Selby families in Somerset County well before the later John Selby / Mary Atkinson marriage.

Possible interpretation:

- 1686/1687: Francis Atkinson and Thomas Selby/Sellbe appear in the same probate witness network.
- Later Atkinson line: Francis Atkinson -> James Atkinson -> Angelo Atkinson -> Mary Atkinson.
- Later Selby line under review: Thomas Selby -> later Thomas / John Selby -> William Atkinson Selby.
- 1750s: John Selby married Mary Atkinson, and Angelo Atkinson's will later names Selby grandchildren.

This could indicate that the Selby and Atkinson families were already part of the same Somerset legal/neighborhood network decades before the direct marriage connection.

## Evidence cautions

This is not proof of Angelo Atkinson's ancestry by itself.

Specific cautions:

- WikiTree is a collaborative online tree and must be treated as a lead aggregator.
- The claimed parents Christopher Atkinson and Dorothy Case require independent English parish/probate support.
- The chain from Francis Atkinson to James Atkinson to Angelo Atkinson needs original Maryland records or high-authority compiled sources.
- The Alexander Williams will witness list is valuable, but the original will image/transcript should be obtained and checked.
- The presence of Francis Atkinson and Thomas Selby as co-witnesses proves a network contact, not kinship or descent.
- The statement about `father and son, both named Thomas Selby` is a hypothesis unless supported by original probate/land records.
