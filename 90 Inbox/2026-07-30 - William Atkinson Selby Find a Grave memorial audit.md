Status: derivative memorial source; useful for corroboration and record targeting, but not sufficient by itself to prove parentage, military service, death place, burial, or complete child list.

## Correlation with stronger vault evidence

The militia claim broadly aligns with the 1929 *Selby Family Notes* transcription reporting William Atkinson Selby in Captain Quinton's Company, Class 7, in the Wicomico Battalion in 1780.

The spouse and several children also align closely with the Selby-Headen Bible transcription preserved in the 1929 compilation, particularly John, Barkley, William, Joshua, Hasty, Rhoda, and a later male child. This makes the memorial useful as corroborating derivative evidence.

However, differences remain:
- The memorial gives exact modernized dates and later death places that may come from contributor research rather than original records.
- The memorial lists Charles R. Selby and Samuel Selby, while the 1929 family record also includes Sarah and Polly and may use different names or omit later identifications.
- The memorial states parents John Selby and Mary Atkinson, but the 1929 compilation says William's parents were unknown and only speculates that John may have been his father.
- The memorial places William's death in Bourbon County, Kentucky, whereas the 1929 family Bible transcription records only the death date and does not itself establish the place.
- Burial is explicitly unknown, so the page is not evidence from a gravestone or cemetery record.

## Evidence classification

Treat as:
- useful derivative memorial;
- corroborating lead for spouse, militia unit, and descendant identities;
- source locator for attached family-history, marriage, birth, and memorial records;
- not an original record;
- not proof of parentage;
- not proof of Kentucky residence or death place;
- not proof of military service without the underlying militia record;
- not proof of burial.

## Priority acquisition targets

1. Original or image-backed Maryland militia list naming William Atkinson Selby in Captain Quinton's Company, 1780.
2. Original marriage record for William Selby and Sarah White Townsend, 28 February 1782.
3. Original Selby-Headen family Bible pages or the earliest surviving transcription.
4. Worcester County deeds, tax lists, probate, and court records identifying William's family and residence.
5. Bourbon County, Kentucky, probate, tax, land, court, or burial records proving William's presence and death there in 1809.
6. Original evidence identifying John Selby and Mary Atkinson as William's parents.
7. Probate and deed evidence identifying all surviving children and reconciling Sarah, Polly, Charles R., Samuel, Rhoda, and the other listed descendants.
8. Memorial edit history and contributor notes, if available, to determine where the exact birth, death, parentage, and child data originated.

## Current conclusion

The memorial is valuable because it independently repeats several details already present in the 1929 family compilation, especially the 1780 militia service, spouse Sarah White Townsend, and much of the child group. Its parentage claim and Bourbon County death location remain unproved until supported by original records.
