## Evidence cautions

This profile is not proof by itself.

WikiTree is a collaborative online tree. It can be useful if the biography and source sections cite original records, but most cited material here appears derivative or census-level rather than direct parentage proof.

Specific cautions:
- Parent-child links must still be tied to original or high-authority records.
- The Thomas Selby generations are prone to same-name confusion.
- The Sarah White Townsend parentage remains a major proof target.
- The WikiTree relationship labels should be compared against the 1929 Selby family notes, the Maryland State Archives legislative biography, Angelo Atkinson probate evidence, Find a Grave memorials, and original land/probate/church records.
- The screenshot shows an incomplete Townsend side: `Townsend grandfather? please help` and `Townsend grandmother? please help`, meaning the Sarah White Townsend ancestry is unresolved within this WikiTree view.
- Some birthplaces assigned to Shelby County, Illinois before Barkley's likely Illinois migration may be impossible or unlikely and should be corrected only after original records are checked.
- Ralph Selby's status as child of Barkley and Elizabeth Drummond/Drummans is a specific conflict point.
