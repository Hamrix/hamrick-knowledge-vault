# Sarah White Townsend and the proposed Caldicott White royal gateway

## Status

Research lead only. This note preserves a potentially important independent gateway hypothesis through the wife of William Atkinson Selby. None of the transatlantic or medieval links below should be treated as proved until the cited wills, deeds, parish registers, and pedigrees are obtained and audited.

## Proposed colonial chain

- William Atkinson Selby (c. 1757-1809)
- wife: Sarah White Townsend (c. 1762-1809)
- proposed father: Barkley Townsend (c. 1745-1795)
- proposed mother: Jemima, maiden name presently unknown
- proposed paternal grandparents: Jeremiah Townsend III (c. 1704-1784) and Sarah White (c. 1715-1784)
- proposed White line: Sarah White -> Stevens White -> Capt. John White, immigrant to Somerset County, Maryland

## Barkley Townsend lead

Claims supplied during the 30 July 2026 research session:

- Barkley Townsend was a substantial Delmarva landowner.
- He is associated with the early development or laying out of Laurel, Delaware.
- He was allegedly an active British Loyalist during the American Revolution.
- His property may have been threatened with confiscation because of Loyalist activity.
- His daughter Sarah White Townsend married William Atkinson Selby, who is separately reported in family material as a Maryland militiaman.

### Evidence required

1. Obtain and transcribe Barkley Townsend's reported 1795 Worcester County will.
2. Determine whether it explicitly names daughter Sarah and identifies her husband.
3. Locate confiscation, deposition, court, or legislative records proving Barkley's personal Loyalist conduct rather than merely Townsend-family involvement.
4. Separate Barkley Townsend from similarly named Townsend men on the Delmarva Peninsula.
5. Document the Laurel claim from Delaware archival or municipal records.

## Proposed Caldicott Manor gateway

The supplied hypothesis identifies Sarah White Townsend's grandmother Sarah White as a descendant of:

- Stevens White
- Capt. John White, alleged immigrant to Somerset County, Maryland, in 1664
- Thomas White, Esq., allegedly of Caldicott or Caldecote Manor near Newport Pagnell, Buckinghamshire
- Elizabeth Fisher, allegedly daughter of William Fisher, Esq., described as a deputy auditor in Yorkshire

The hypothesis further claims that the White-Fisher ancestry enters Roane, Neville, and Plantagenet-descended gentry lines.

## Critical Atlantic bridge

The decisive question is whether the Somerset County Capt. John White is demonstrably the same person as a son of Thomas White of Caldicott Manor.

Required proof may include:

- an English baptism naming the correct parents;
- an English will naming John in Maryland or Virginia;
- a colonial deposition stating his English parish or parentage;
- a power of attorney, inheritance, merchant record, or land transaction connecting both sides of the Atlantic;
- an authoritative published gateway-ancestor treatment citing original records.

Name, age, rank, approximate migration date, and surname similarity are not sufficient.

## Medieval chain requirements

Even after proving the colonial-to-Caldicott bridge, each claimed generation from the Whites and Fishers into Roane, Neville, and Edward III must be stated by name and supported with appropriate medieval or heraldic evidence.

Avoid summary statements such as "connected to the Nevilles" unless the exact marriage sequence is shown.

## Current evaluation

- Sarah White Townsend as wife of William Atkinson Selby: supported by the 1929 Selby family compilation and family Bible transcription, subject to original-record confirmation.
- Barkley Townsend as her father: promising but requires the 1795 will or equivalent primary proof.
- Barkley Townsend as a prominent Loyalist: plausible regional lead, not yet isolated and proved.
- Jeremiah Townsend III and Sarah White as grandparents: unverified in the present vault.
- Sarah White -> Stevens White -> Capt. John White: unverified chain.
- Capt. John White -> Thomas White of Caldicott Manor: unverified transatlantic bridge.
- Fisher/Roane/Neville/Plantagenet descent: downstream hypothesis dependent on all earlier links.

## Research priority

This branch deserves treatment as an independent gateway candidate alongside Parker and Schoolfield because it does not depend on proving Thomas Selby Sr.'s English parentage.

Priority order:

1. Barkley Townsend's will and estate file.
2. Sarah White Townsend's marriage and identity evidence.
3. Jeremiah Townsend and Sarah White probate and land records.
4. Stevens White probate, deeds, and family relationships.
5. Capt. John White's colonial origin evidence.
6. Caldicott White parish, probate, and manor records.
7. Exact Fisher-Roane-Neville pedigree audit.

## Caution

Online trees have already created chronologically impossible medieval versions of William Atkinson Selby and Sarah White Townsend. All records must therefore be checked for circular sourcing and copied-tree contamination before being admitted as evidence.
