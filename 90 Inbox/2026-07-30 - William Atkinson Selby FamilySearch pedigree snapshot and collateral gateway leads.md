# William Atkinson Selby FamilySearch pedigree snapshot and collateral gateway leads

Status: online-tree evidence lead only; no relationship in this chart is accepted without independent records.

## Purpose

Preserve the ancestry structure displayed in the uploaded FamilySearch pedigree image for William Atkinson Selby. The chart is valuable because it proposes a concrete Maryland parentage line and identifies several collateral maternal families that can be tested through probate, land, church, court, and tax records.

## Central profile assertions

The chart identifies William Atkinson Selby as:
- Son of John Selby and Mary Atkinson Selby.
- Husband of Sarah White Townsend Selby.
- Father of children listed as John Atkinson Selby, Barkley Townsend Selby, Joshua Selby, Hasty Selby, Rhoda T. Selby, Tate Selby, and Charles R. Selby.

The displayed child list differs from both the separate seven-child online reconstruction and the 1929 Selby-Headen Bible transcription already audited in the vault. It must be treated as another competing descendant list.

## Major conflicts and cautions

1. The chart identifies John Selby, born 1732, as William Atkinson Selby's father. Other vault material has proposed a John born about 1722, while the 1929 compilation states William's parents were unknown and only speculates about a John Selby.
2. The chart's Thomas sequence conflicts with the other FamilySearch profile sequence in dates and generation numbering.
3. The image supplies no visible source citations or proof statements for the displayed parent-child links.
4. The displayed descendants of William and Sarah conflict with the Selby-Headen Bible transcription and other online child lists.
5. Interface confirmation icons next to some people do not establish evidentiary quality.
6. The presence of Schoolfield, Atkinson, Powell, Laws, Higaman, Beavens, Bozman, or Awbery families does not prove noble or royal descent.
7. Repeated names and overlapping Maryland families increase the risk of merging cousins and namesakes.

## Highest-priority proof targets

1. Probate, deed, tax, court, or family-record evidence explicitly identifying William Atkinson Selby as son of John Selby and Mary Atkinson.
2. Marriage or estate evidence connecting John Selby to Mary Atkinson.
3. Probate of Thomas Selby, died about 1757, naming John and other children.
4. Original marriage or probate evidence for Thomas Selby and Margaret Schoolfield.
5. Probate of Henry Schoolfield Sr. and records proving Margaret Schoolfield's parentage.
6. Coventry Parish record for Mary Atkinson's claimed 1734 birth or baptism.
7. Probate or guardianship evidence for Angelo Atkinson and Rachel Laws naming Mary.
8. Records establishing the New Jersey-to-Maryland identity of James and Angelo Atkinson.
9. Full probate and land reconstruction of William Laws and Sarah Beavens.
10. Original records resolving the competing child lists of William Atkinson Selby and Sarah White Townsend.

## Current assessment

The image provides a useful, detailed online-tree hypothesis:

> William Atkinson Selby may have been the son of John Selby and Mary Atkinson; John may have been the son of Thomas Selby and Margaret Schoolfield; and Mary Atkinson may descend through Atkinson, Laws, Higaman, Powell, and Beavens colonial families.

The chart is not proof. Its value is that it identifies exact people, approximate dates, locations, and collateral families for targeted record acquisition. The proposed John Selby-Mary Atkinson parentage remains one of the most important unresolved links in the Maryland Selby project.
