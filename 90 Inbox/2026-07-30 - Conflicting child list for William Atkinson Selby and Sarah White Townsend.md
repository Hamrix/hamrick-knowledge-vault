# Conflicting child list for William Atkinson Selby and Sarah White Townsend

## Status
User-supplied research lead. Do not replace the earlier Selby-Headen Bible transcription with this list unless supported by contemporaneous deeds, probate, or family records.

## Newly supplied list
A recent research summary claims that William Atkinson Selby and Sarah White Townsend had seven children:

1. Thomas Selby, born about 1783 in Worcester County, Maryland
2. John Selby, born about 1785 in Worcester County, Maryland
3. William Atkinson Selby Jr., born about 1788 in Worcester County, Maryland
4. Barkley Townsend Selby, born about 1791 in Worcester County, Maryland
5. Mary Selby, born about 1794 in Worcester County, Maryland
6. Jemima Selby, born about 1797 in Worcester County, Maryland
7. Sarah Selby, born about 1801 in Worcester County, Maryland

The summary interprets several names as honorific naming patterns for grandparents and parents.

## Conflict with the 1929 Selby-Headen family record
The 1929 compilation *Selby Family Notes* reproduces a transcription from the Bible of Mrs. Tryphena C. Selby Headen. That transcription gives a materially different child list for William A. Selby and Sarah W. Townsend:

- John Selby, born 10 February 1783
- Barkly T. Selby, born 21 November 1784
- Sarah L. Selby, born 9 December 1786 in the Bible transcription, though another section of the same compilation prints 1789
- William Selby, born 9 June 1791
- Joshua Selby, born 17 July 1795
- Hasty Selby, born 20 or 29 September 1797 depending on the section
- Rodah Selby, born 17 April 1800
- Polly Selby, born 27 November 1802

The 1929 DAR application section also reproduces this same general set of children rather than the newly supplied seven-child list.

## Principal discrepancies

- The new list includes Thomas Selby, William Atkinson Selby Jr., Mary Selby, and Jemima Selby, none of whom appear under those names in the Bible list.
- The Bible list includes Joshua, Hasty, Rodah, and Polly, none of whom appear in the new list.
- Barkley Townsend Selby's birth year is given as about 1791 in the new list, but the Bible record gives 21 November 1784.
- John Selby's birth year is given as about 1785 in the new list, but the Bible record gives 10 February 1783.
- William's 1791 birth may have been transformed into a supposed full-name junior entry without documentary support.
- Mary, Jemima, Sarah, Hasty, Rodah, and Polly may involve nickname, middle-name, transcription, or identity confusion, but no merger should be assumed.

## Working interpretation
The 1929 Bible transcription is presently the stronger family-record source because it claims direct transcription from a surviving family Bible and provides exact dates. It is still derivative and must be checked against the original Bible, probate, deeds, marriage records, and census evidence.

The new seven-child list should remain a competing hypothesis until each claimed child is tied to William and Sarah by direct evidence.

## Verification plan

1. Locate images or archival custody information for the Tryphena C. Selby Headen Bible.
2. Pull William Atkinson Selby's 1809 probate and any guardian or distribution papers.
3. Search Worcester County deeds for conveyances identifying heirs of William A. Selby.
4. Search Bourbon County, Kentucky probate and land records if William died there.
5. Trace each proposed child independently by marriage, probate, migration, and naming evidence.
6. Determine whether Polly was Mary, whether Rodah was Rhoda, whether Hasty was a nickname, and whether William ever used the middle name Atkinson as a junior.
7. Do not infer royal descent through these children until the correct direct child is identified.

## Publication-safe conclusion
There is presently a conflict between a newly supplied seven-child reconstruction and the older Selby-Headen Bible transcription. The Bible-derived list is better supported but not yet fully verified from the original artifact. Both should remain visible until primary records resolve the identities.
