# Audit of Research Lead Sweep - 2026-07-28

## Overall assessment

The sweep contains several high-value archival leads, but it should not be treated as canonical without correction. Its strongest material is the Major Selby tract cluster, the Etheldred Peters land-caveat lead, and the identification of a specific 1911-1992 Frederic/Frederick Ethelred Selby candidate.

## Verified or useful leads

- John Selby is associated in the 1783 Worcester County assessment with Poplar Hill and Exon Enlarged.
- The 1797 chancery packet involving Major Selby and the same tract pair remains a high-priority retrieval target.
- The 1810 Zadock Selby estate litigation involving the same tract cluster is a valid route for reconstructing title and estate relationships.
- The 1787 Etheldred Peters land-caveat abstract is a useful discovery lead to the original patent, survey, caveat, and neighboring landholders.
- FamilySearch profile `LLWQ-6MV` identifies a specific Frederick Ethelred Selby candidate matching the 1911-1992 period.

## Required corrections

### FamilySearch source class

The FamilySearch item is a collaborative Family Tree profile, not an indexed historical person record. It may aggregate attached historical sources, but the profile itself does not prove identity, dates, parentage, or military service.

See [[FamilySearch profile LLWQ-6MV audit]].

### Frederic spelling and dates

The controlled project spelling is `Frederic Ethelred Selby` based on direct family clarification. The following variants occur in derivative material:

- Frederick Ethelred;
- Frederic Ethelered;
- Frederick E.;
- Fred E.;
- Fred.

The working 1911-1992 lifespan is strongly supported as the correct target period, but exact birth and death dates and places remain subject to direct record inspection.

### Parker Selby chancery citations

The two Parker Selby case descriptions in the sweep require rechecking against the official Maryland State Archives index before being repeated. They should remain provisional until the exact official case captions, dates, subjects, and archive identifiers are copied directly from the MSA entries.

### Compiled-tree material

The Major Selby parentage and marriage claims from forums and compiled trees remain search terms only. They must not be promoted into the lineage without original or high-quality derivative evidence distinguishing the older Maryland adult from later men of the same name.
