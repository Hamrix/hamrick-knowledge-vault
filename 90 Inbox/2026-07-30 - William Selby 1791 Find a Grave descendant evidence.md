# William Selby (1791–1844) Find a Grave descendant evidence

Status: derivative memorial source with gravestone-linked burial context; parentage and family reconstruction require original records.

## Memorial identity

Find a Grave profile assertions visible in screenshot:
- Name: William Selby.
- Born 9 June 1791 in Somerset County, Maryland.
- Died 7 April 1844 in Rush County, Indiana, aged 52.
- Buried in Moscow Cemetery, Moscow, Rush County, Indiana.
- Memorial ID: `121038342`.

The memorial links him as a son of:
- William Atkinson Selby, 1757–1809.
- Sarah White Townsend Selby, 1762–1809.

This parentage is consistent with the 1929 Selby–Headen Bible transcription, which lists a son William born 9 June 1791, but the memorial itself is not independent proof of the parent-child relationship.

## Spouses shown

The memorial lists:
- Priscilla Davis Townsend Selby, 1791–1835, marriage shown as 1811.
- Elizabeth Stites Lee, 1808–1860, marriage shown as 1835.

Memorial narrative states that after Priscilla died, William married Elizabeth Machlin on 17 September 1835 in Rush County, Indiana.

Evidence caution:
- The displayed spouse name `Elizabeth Stites Lee` and narrative name `Elizabeth (...) Machlin` may describe the same woman with maiden and prior-marriage surnames, or may reflect a merged profile.
- The 1835 Rush County marriage record should be obtained to establish her exact name and marital status.

## Migration significance

This profile supports a family migration pattern:

Somerset/Worcester County, Maryland
→ Bourbon County, Kentucky or associated Kentucky family network
→ Rush County, Indiana
→ Moscow Cemetery.

The same Indiana destination appears in the memorial for John Atkinson Selby, suggesting that at least two sons of William Atkinson Selby may have moved into the Rush County area.

## Current conclusion

The memorial is a useful descendant-side source because its birth date matches the 1929 Bible-derived entry for William Selby and because it places him in the same Rush County, Indiana migration network as John Atkinson Selby. It does not independently prove parentage, the complete spouse sequence, or the full child list. Treat the memorial as a derivative lead supported by burial context and test it against original Maryland, Kentucky, and Indiana records.
