# William Selby Maryland acquisition continuation backup

## Continuation summary

The William Selby Maryland colonist biography lead has been expanded into a controlled acquisition phase.

## New controlling distinction

William Selby of Calvert / Prince George's County is now treated as a **parallel Maryland Selby research lead**, not as part of the Thomas Selby / Cornelius / Somerset bridge unless original records prove a connection.

This prevents the project from conflating:

- Thomas Selby Sr. / Thomas Selby II / Simpleton / Somerset records;
- William Selby of Calvert and Prince George's County;
- Major Selby or later Worcester County Selbys;
- the modern Selby line.
