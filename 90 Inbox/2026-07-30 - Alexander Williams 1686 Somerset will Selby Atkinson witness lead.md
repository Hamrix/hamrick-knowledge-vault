## Why this matters

This record directly supports the WikiTree/Atkinson-profile claim that a Thomas Selby/Sellbe/Sellbie and Francis Atkinson were in the same Somerset County legal network in the 1680s.

It is valuable because the later audited line proposes:

- **Francis Atkinson** / early Atkinson family

And separately:

- **Thomas Selby** of the Somerset/Worcester Selby line

This 1686/1687 witness list does **not** prove the later parent-child chain by itself, but it is strong neighborhood/network evidence that the Atkinson and Selby families interacted in Somerset County decades before the John Selby / Mary Atkinson marriage.
