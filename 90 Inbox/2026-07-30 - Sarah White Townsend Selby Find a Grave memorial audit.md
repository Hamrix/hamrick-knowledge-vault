## Conflict notes

The child list is close to, but not identical with, the Selby-Headen Bible transcription preserved in the 1929 Selby Family Notes.

Important differences to audit:
- This memorial includes **Charles R. Selby**, **Samuel Selby**, and **Lilly**, who are not in the Bible list as previously transcribed.
- The Bible list preserves **Rodah/Rhoda**, **Polly**, and other dates, but memorial-link dates may differ.
- Memorial page children may combine biological children, linked memorials, contributor assumptions, or collateral descendants.

The Bible transcription remains provisionally stronger for the original William Atkinson Selby/Sarah Townsend household, but the Find a Grave page is highly useful for tracking descendants and migration.
