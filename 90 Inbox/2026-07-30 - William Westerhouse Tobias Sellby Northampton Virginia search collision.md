## Why this appears in Selby searches

The text appears in Selby/Sellby searches because it contains this key clause from the abstracted 1683 will of **William Westerhouse**:

> To Anna Catharina the daughter of Elizabeth Harper my 150-acre plantation where I now dwell, bought of **Tobias Sellby** and **John Winberry, Sr.**, it being the remainder of my land belonging to Capt. Steven Charlton's patent joining upon Mr. Andrews land.

Interpretation:
- The search hit is primarily caused by **Tobias Sellby**, not by a known connection to the William Atkinson Selby line.
- `Sellby` may be a variant spelling of Selby, but the individual must be independently identified.
- Northampton County / Matchepungo / Eastern Shore Virginia is geographically relevant to early Selby/Solbie research, so the hit should not be discarded outright.

## Main Westerhouse content preserved

The article states that:

- **William Westerhouse/Westerhuysen** of Amsterdam brought the ship **St. Beninjo** to New Haven in 1647.
- The cargo included guns, powder, and lead.
- Dutch authorities under **Pieter Stuyvesant** confiscated the ship and remaining cargo.
- Westerhouse became a naturalized or protected resident of New Haven.
- By January 1653 he had moved to Northampton County, Virginia, transporting himself, wife, children, and stepchildren.
- He was a merchant, ship owner, and physician/surgeon.
- His wife was **Margaretha van Hoochstraten**, widow of **Lourens Schyn**.
- The article traces Dutch parents and siblings for William Westerhuysen.

## Eastern Shore / Virginia records in the text

Important Virginia and colonial records mentioned include:

- 2 January 1653 Northampton County certificate for **Mr. Wm. Westerhouse, surgeon**, for 600 acres for transporting himself and family.
- 20 October 1661 patent for **William Westerhouse**, 500 acres near **Matchepungo**, formerly granted 2 October 1654.
- March 1657/58 Virginia denization of William Westerhouse and other Dutchmen.
- 1656 will of **John Cornelius**, witnessed by William Westerhouse and Daniell Jones.
- 1667 Northampton estate administration of **John Andrews**, appraised by William Westerhouse, Sampson Robins, John Haggoman, and William Gaskins.
- 1674 will of **Ann Jones Michael**, witnessed by John Lucas and William Westerhouse.
- 1683 will of William Westerhouse, including the **Tobias Sellby** land-purchase reference.
