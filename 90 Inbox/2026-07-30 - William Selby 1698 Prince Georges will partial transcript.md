# William Selby 1698 Prince George's County will partial transcript

Status: user-supplied partial transcript of original will/probate record; higher-value source lead than compiled summaries, but still requires original image review.

## Source supplied

The user uploaded `story.txt`, containing a partial transcript of the will of **William Selby of Prince George's County, Maryland, planter**.

Visible citation supplied in the text:

- Date range: **5 November 1698 – 20 February 1698/9**
- Register reference: **Liber 6, folio 215**
- The supplied transcript notes that the photocopy did not include the final page.
- A note says the *Calendar of Maryland Wills* states that the executrix was wife **Mary Selby**, and witnesses were **Hugh Tracey**, **Jeremiah Perdew**, **John Wheate**, and **Josh. Cecile**.

## Key identity statement

The will begins:

- Testator: **William Selby**
- Residence: **Prince George's County, Province of Maryland**
- Occupation/status: **Planter**
- Condition: weak in body, of sound and perfect mind and memory

This confirms that the testator is the Prince George's/Patuxent landholder, not the Somerset/Worcester Selby ancestor.

## Named wife / executrix

The transcript repeatedly names **Mary Selby** as William's wife.

The final-page note says the *Calendar of Maryland Wills* identifies Mary Selby as executrix.

The will gives Mary:

- benefit during life of half the orchard on **Twyford**;
- **The Farme**, 100 acres, in fee;
- use during life of the plantation where William dwelled, with houses/edifices, until the estate passed to son William after Mary's death;
- power as executrix to choose cattle for the children and deliver personal bequests at age milestones.

## Children and heirs named in the supplied transcript

The partial transcript names the following children and family members:

1. **Mary Selby**, loving daughter, wife of **Robert Sallers**.
2. **William Selby**, loving son.
3. **Charles Selby**, loving son.
4. **Susannah Selby**, loving daughter.
5. **Samuel/Samuell Selby**, son.
6. **Amee Hacker**, daughter.
7. **Mary Selby**, wife/executrix.

The posthumous daughter **Ann Selby** does not appear in this partial transcript, consistent with the later evidence that she was born after the will or after William's death.

## Land devised in the will

### Good Luck and Park Meadow / Western Branch

To son-in-law **Robert Sallers** and wife **Mary**, William's daughter:

- **200 acres** lying next to **Samuel Magruder** in the **Western Branch**;
- 100 acres called **Good Luck**;
- 100 acres called **Park Meadow**;
- to Robert and Mary and their heirs forever.

This confirms the land description from the compiled summaries and identifies the two Western Branch parcels.

### Leith and Groomes Lodge

To sons **William Selby** and **Charles Selby**:

- the tract where William Sr. then dwelled, called **Leith**, containing **550 acres** in this transcript;
- 150 acres lately bought of **Richard Groomes**, part of **Groomes Lodge**, adjoining Leith.

The transcript then divides the combined land:

- **350 acres** adjoining the plantation, the lowermost part, to son **William Selby**;
- **300 acres**, the uppermost part up the **Patuxent River**, to son **Charles Selby**.

This is a precise internal heirship clue for the Prince George's branch.

### Towgood

To daughter **Susannah Selby**:

- 150 acres, part of a tract called **Towgood**, lying backwards in the woods;
- the tract is stated as containing **350 acres**;
- Susannah's 150 acres to be laid out in the back part of the tract, on the other side of a branch.

To son **Samuel Selby**:

- all the remaining part of that land.

This confirms the Towgood/Twogood distribution: Susannah receives 150 acres and Samuel receives the residue.

### Twyford

To daughter **Amee Hacker**:

- all that tract called **Twyford**, containing **100 acres**;
- except half the orchard, which wife **Mary Selby** was to benefit from during her life;
- to Amee Hacker and heirs of her body forever.

This directly confirms the Amee/Amy Hacker line and explains the orchard exception.

### The Farme

To wife **Mary Selby**:

- **100 acres** called **The Farme**, being part of a tract called The Farme;
- to Mary and her heirs forever.

This is the parcel later central to the posthumous **Ann Selby** evidence.

## Personal property and age conditions

The will gives:

- To **Susannah Selby**: one good feather bed and furniture, one large copper kettle, and ten cattle, to be delivered at age 16 or marriage.
- To **William Selby**: one feather bed and furniture and ten cattle, to be delivered when he arrived at age 18.
- To **Charles Selby**: ten cattle, to be delivered at age 18.
- To **Samuel/Samuell Selby**: one large iron pot and ten cattle, to be delivered at age 18.

These age conditions indicate that William, Charles, Susannah, and Samuel were minors or at least not yet of the specified ages when the will was written.

## Plantation use and son William's future possession

The twelfth clause gives wife Mary use of the plantation where William then dwelled, with sufficient land, houses, and edifices, during her life.

It also provides that if Mary lived until son William turned 18, and if William wished to seat for himself, he could seat backwards on his land with his mother's allowance/use still protected.

After Mary's death, the dwelling plantation and edifices were to pass to son **William Selby** and his heirs forever.

This clause helps explain later references to William Selby owning a portion of **The Farme** or the Leith/Groomes Lodge complex.

## Missing final page

The transcript ends at the beginning of clause 13 and explicitly notes that the photocopy did not include the last page.

The missing final page is important because it likely contains:

- residuary clause;
- executrix appointment;
- witness attestation;
- probate statement;
- possible additional estate instructions.

The note from *Calendar of Maryland Wills* supplies the reported final details:

- executrix: wife **Mary Selby**;
- witnesses: **Hugh Tracey**, **Jeremiah Perdew**, **John Wheate**, and **Josh. Cecile**.
