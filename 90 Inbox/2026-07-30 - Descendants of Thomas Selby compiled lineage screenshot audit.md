# Descendants of Thomas Selby compiled lineage screenshot audit

Status: derivative compiled-lineage source; useful research guide, not primary proof.

## Purpose

Preserve the user-supplied screenshot titled `Descendants of Thomas Selby`, generation 1. The page appears to summarize and adapt work from Donna Valley Russell's *Selby Families of Colonial America* and from the 1929 Selby family reunion notes. It is relevant because it presents a structured descent beginning with an early Thomas Selby of Somerset County, Maryland and then identifies a son Thomas Selby II of Bogerternorton / Bogeternorton.

## Source type

The screenshot itself is a compiled online descendant page. It explicitly says the writer is not directly quoting all of Donna Valley Russell's book or listing Russell's numerous sources, and that the writer is adding some deductions and comments of their own.

Therefore this source must be treated as:

- derivative;
- useful for finding record targets;
- not sufficient by itself for parent-child proof;
- potentially mixing original records, Russell's analysis, Selby Family Notes material, and the webpage author's own deductions.

## Important source warning from the page
