### Required original record

The State Archives of North Carolina should be asked for the complete marriage record, including:

- marriage bond;
- reverse side and endorsements;
- marriage return or ministerial notation;
- consent or guardian document;
- groom and bondsman signatures or marks;

- bond date versus marriage date;

### Evidentiary purpose and limit

1. Elizabeth or Betsey Peters was a daughter of Etheldred Peters;
2. Ethelred R. Stafford was a son of Stephen Stafford and Elizabeth Peters.

Each relationship requires independent evidence such as estate, guardianship, land, census, church, probate, obituary, cemetery, or civil records.
