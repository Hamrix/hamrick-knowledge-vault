# Ben Selby and Sophia E Selby were husband and wife in 1872

## Claim

A Henry County deed dated 24 March 1872 identifies Sophia E. Selby as the titleholder and Ben Selby as her husband. The clerk's 11 November 1872 acknowledgment again identifies them as husband and wife.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Ben and Sophia E Selby 1872 Henry County deed]] directly states the relationship on the recorded deed pages.

## Analysis and limits

This confirms the couple in 1872 and extends the relationship evidence beyond the 1854 marriage-license papers and 1880 census household. The deed does not name Benjamin's parents and does not connect him to Major Selby.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the reviewed original county record states the relationship twice.
