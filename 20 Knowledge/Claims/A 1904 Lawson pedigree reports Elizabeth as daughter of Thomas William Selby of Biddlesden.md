# A 1904 Lawson pedigree reports Elizabeth as daughter of Thomas William Selby of Biddlesden

## Claim

A Lawson pedigree published in 1904 reports that John Lawson, who died on 28 January 1791, married Elizabeth, daughter of Thomas William Selby, Esq., of Biddlesden.

## Subjects

- Thomas William Selby of Biddlesden
- Elizabeth Selby, reported wife of John Lawson
- John Lawson

## Evidence supporting

- [[The Genealogist Volume 20 - 1904]], printed page 254, visually reviewed, contains the explicit father-daughter and marriage statement.

## Evidence against

- The reviewed page does not cite an original record for this specific relationship.
- It gives no birth, marriage, death, probate, or parent information for Thomas William Selby.
- No reviewed record connects this family to William Atkinson Selby or any Maryland Selby.

## Conflicts

The 1929 [[Headen family Bible transcript and Selby family notes - 1929]] includes English Biddleston or Biddlesden material, but that later compilation does not prove that its American Selby subjects descended from this family. Repetition of the place-name across derivative works is not independent corroboration.

## Analysis

This is a specific English Selby research lead and the volume's only substantive Selby-surname passage. It should be tested in original English records before creating person profiles or attaching it to a pedigree.

## Confidence rationale

**Tentative for the relationship:** the 1904 derivative pedigree clearly reports it, but the underlying record has not been identified or reviewed. There is no present evidence connecting this family unit to the user's ancestry.

## Next research action

Identify Biddlesden in the record context and locate Thomas William Selby's will, probate, parish records, land records, and Elizabeth's original marriage record. Determine the pedigree compiler's source, then evaluate the relationship independently before comparing it with the 1929 Biddleston tradition.
