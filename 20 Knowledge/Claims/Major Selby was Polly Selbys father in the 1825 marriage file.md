# Major Selby was Polly Selby's father in the 1825 marriage file

## Claim

Major Selby was the father of Polly Selby, who married William Antrobus in Nicholas County in 1825.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - William Antrobus and Polly Selby 1825 Nicholas marriage file]] contains both the father's consent and the bond joined by Major Selby.

## Analysis and limits

The relationship is direct. The documents do not identify this Major's parents or connect him to William Atkinson Selby.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two parts of the original marriage file directly name Major as Polly's father.
