# Major Selby was Polly Selby's father in the 1825 marriage file

## Claim

Major Selby was the father of Polly Selby, who married William Antrobus in Nicholas County in 1825.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - William Antrobus and Polly Selby 1825 Nicholas marriage file]] contains both the father's consent and the bond joined by Major Selby.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1833 Nicholas County will and 1835 inventory]] independently calls Polly Anterbus, formerly Polly Selby, Major's daughter.
- [[FamilySearch - Margaret Selby 1836 will and estate settlements]] calls Mary Antrobus Margaret's daughter and William Preston Antrobus her grandson, strongly correlating Mary with the same Antrobus family while preserving the different given name.

## Analysis and limits

The relationship is direct and independently repeated in Major's 1833 will after Polly's marriage. Margaret's later Mary Antrobus reference is strongly compatible with Polly because the surname and family context agree and Polly commonly serves as a familiar form of Mary, but the documents use different given names. The records do not identify this Major's parents or connect him to William Atkinson Selby.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two parts of the original marriage file directly name Major as Polly's father, and his later will independently repeats the relationship.
