# The alleged 1666 Whitehouse hearth-tax entry is not authenticated

## Claim

The project has not authenticated the proposition that The National Archives reference `E 179/216/493` contains a 1666 hearth-tax entry reading `Selbye of Whitehouse, Cliffe` or otherwise proves Selby-family occupation of Whitehouse at Cliffe.

## Subjects

- E 179 taxation records
- Whitehouse, Cliffe
- Selby family
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[The National Archives - E 179 database and 1666 hearth-tax reference audit]] establishes that individual taxpayer names are not catalogued in the E 179 database and that the actual record or a reliable published transcription must be inspected.
- [[Legacy Selby Manor evidence bundle file audit]] establishes that the supplied bundle contains no scan or transcription of the alleged hearth-tax entry.
- The project master outline describes the copy as requested, not received and authenticated.
- Current official-catalogue searches did not produce an accessible catalogue description confirming the claimed wording, locality, or identity.

## Evidence against

The legacy dossiers repeatedly assert the wording and cite `E 179/216/493`, but they do not provide the underlying image or a verifiable published transcription.

## Conflicts

The dossier calls the item a scan and uses it as proof of continuous occupation, while the supplied evidence packet omits it and another project file states that a request had merely been submitted.

## Analysis

This is a source-authentication failure, not a finding that no relevant hearth-tax record can exist. Until the record is obtained and inspected, the claimed entry cannot support a named ancestor, a residence, continuity of possession, a manor claim, or a modern lineage.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: the absence of the claimed exhibit from the supplied bundle is directly established, and official guidance confirms that the catalogue cannot substitute for inspection of the taxpayer entry. The conclusion is limited to authentication status.

## Next research action

Obtain an official copy of `E 179/216/493` or a corrected reference; document the full catalogue description, folio, locality heading, exact spelling, household assessment, document date, and archival provenance before using the entry genealogically.
