# Henry III was a son of King John

## Claim

Henry III of England was a son of King John and Isabella of Angoulême.

## Subjects

- Henry III of England
- King John of England
- Isabella of Angoulême
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Royal Family - Henry III]] identifies Henry III as King John’s son.
- [[Westminster Abbey - Henry III]] independently identifies Henry as the eldest son of King John and Isabella.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified.

## Analysis

The two institutional historical profiles independently corroborate the direct relationship John → Henry III. Combined with [[King John was a son of Henry II]], this extends the verified royal context by one generation.

It does not establish Henry III’s descendants, a route to Edward III, or any connection to a Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent institutional historical profiles agree on the direct relationship.
