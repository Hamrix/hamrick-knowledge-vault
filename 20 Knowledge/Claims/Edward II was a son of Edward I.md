# Edward II was a son of Edward I

## Claim

Edward II of England was a son of Edward I and Eleanor of Castile.

## Subjects

- Edward II of England
- Edward I of England
- Eleanor of Castile
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Westminster Abbey - Edward I and Eleanor of Castile]] records that Edward I and Eleanor of Castile had four sons, including Edward II.
- [[DNB 1895 - Edward II]] independently identifies Edward II as the fourth son of Edward I and Eleanor of Castile.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified.

## Analysis

The Westminster Abbey profile and the published *Dictionary of National Biography* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Edward I to Edward II. Combined with [[Edward I was a son of Henry III]], this extends the verified royal context by one generation.

It does not establish Edward II's descendants, a route to Edward III, or any connection to a Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct relationship.
