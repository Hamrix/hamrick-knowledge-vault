# Margaret Selby was wife of Major Selby and sister of Charles Parker

## Claim

Margaret Selby's 1836 will identifies Major Selby as her deceased husband and Charles Parker, executor of Major's estate, as her brother.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Margaret Selby 1836 will and estate settlements]] contains Margaret's original recorded will and the 1835 Major Selby estate settlement.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1833 Nicholas County will and 1835 inventory]] names wife Peggy Selby and executor Charles Parker.

## Analysis and limitations

Margaret's will directly states both relationships. Peggy is a conventional familiar form of Margaret, and the shared executor and estate balance independently join the two records. The records do not identify Margaret and Charles Parker's parents or state that Margaret was the mother of every child named by Major.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: Margaret's own will directly names her husband and brother, and the earlier estate records corroborate both roles.
