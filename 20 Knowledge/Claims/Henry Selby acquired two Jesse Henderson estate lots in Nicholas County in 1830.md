# Henry Selby acquired two Jesse Henderson estate lots in Nicholas County in 1830

## Claim

Henry Selby of Nicholas County acquired two 6.25-acre Brushy Fork and Hinkston parcels in 1830 from heirs of Jesse Henderson: Susan Henderson Williams and William G. Henderson.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby 1830 Nicholas Henderson estate deeds]] contains the original recorded deeds and the Jesse Henderson heirship and partition recitals.
- [[FamilySearch - Jesse Henderson 1821 division and 1823 deed to Henry Selby]] independently assigns Susan lot 1 and William lot 6 in the original division.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1833 Nicholas County will and 1835 inventory]] names a Nicholas County Major Selby's son Henry nearly three years later.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry and Eleanor Selby 1838 Nicholas deed to Parker Selby]] later places a married Henry on a larger Nicholas County Brush Fork home place.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby and Nelly Henderson 1809 Bourbon marriage bond]] names Jesse Henderson as co-obligor for Henry's intended marriage to Nelly Henderson.

## Analysis and limitations

The 1830 deeds directly prove Henry's residence, acquisitions, parcel size, and land neighborhood. They create a local Henry chronology before and after Major's will when correlated with the 1838 deed. The original 1821 division fixes Susan's designation as lot 1 and William's as lot 6; the automated deed transcript's lot-4 reading for William is an OCR error.

No purchase instrument cross-references the purchaser to Major or Major's estate. Margaret Selby's 1836 will now creates a strong independent bridge between the later Nicholas County Henry and Major's immediate family, while the 1809 Henderson bond and 1821-1830 estate sequence create a strong Henderson-network correlation. Jesse's relationship to Nelly and Nelly's identity with Eleanor remain unstated.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the purchases, grantors' heirship, parcel sizes, lot assignments, and Henry's residence are directly recorded. The confidence does not extend to Henry's Selby parentage or his identity with every later record.
