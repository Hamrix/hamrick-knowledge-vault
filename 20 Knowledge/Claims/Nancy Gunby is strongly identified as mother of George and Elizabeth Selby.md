# Nancy Gunby is strongly identified as mother of George and Elizabeth Selby

## Claim

Nancy Gunby is strongly identified as the mother of George Selby and Elizabeth or Betsey Selby, the minor children of deceased Parker Selby.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - John Selby 1790 Worcester County will images]] calls Nancy Gunby the testator's daughter, calls George a son of Nancy, and calls Betsey Selby a daughter of Nancy.
- [[Worcester Digital Archive - Parker Selby children 1790 guardian bond]] places George Selby and Elizabeth Selby, both orphans of Parker Selby, under Nancy Gunby's guardianship seven months earlier.

## Analysis and limits

For George, the combination is unusually specific: the same Nancy Gunby is named as guardian of minor George Selby in June and as mother of a grandson George in John Selby's November will. The will also names Betsey Selby as Nancy's daughter, while the guardianship names a similarly aged Elizabeth Selby in Nancy's care. Betsey is a common form of Elizabeth.

No single reviewed instrument states all three propositions together: Nancy as Parker's wife, George as their joint son, and Elizabeth as the same person as Betsey. The identification is therefore strong rather than confirmed. It does not establish which earlier Parker was the children's paternal grandfather.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: two contemporary Worcester records align on names, guardian or maternal role, and the same 1790 family setting. The remaining weakness is cross-record identity, especially Elizabeth-to-Betsey.

## Next research action

Retrieve Parker's missing will and account, Nancy's marriage evidence, and the children's later guardian accounts or receipts. Seek one instrument expressly identifying Nancy as Parker's widow or naming George and Elizabeth together as her children.
