# 1946 Indiana marriage index reports James A Sparks as son of Martha Selby

## Claim

A FamilySearch derivative index for a 28 November 1946 Greene County, Indiana marriage labels James A. Sparks as the son of Martha Selby and reports his 30 March 1880 birth in Indiana.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Martha Selby named in James A Sparks 1946 Indiana marriage index]] records the displayed mother-son label and James A. Sparks's indexed birth and marriage details.

## Analysis and limit

This is a useful later-life research lead for a woman indexed as Martha Selby.  Because the underlying image remains unreviewed and the index does not state her parents, the claim is deliberately limited to the index's report.  It does not prove that this Martha was in Major Selby's 1850 or 1860 Hamilton County household, nor does it create a Major-to-Martha parent-child link.
