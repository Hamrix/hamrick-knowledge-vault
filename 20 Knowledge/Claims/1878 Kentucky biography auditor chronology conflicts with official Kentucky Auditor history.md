# 1878 Kentucky biography auditor chronology conflicts with official Kentucky Auditor history

## Claim

The auditor-office chronology reported in the 1878 *Biographical Encyclopaedia of Kentucky* profile of Ben Selby conflicts with the current official historical list published by the Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts.

## Subjects

- Hon. Benjamin Selby
- Ben Selby (born 31 January 1826)
- Ben Shelby
- Thomas S. Page
- Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts

## Evidence supporting

- [[Biographical Encyclopaedia of Kentucky - Ben Selby profile research lead]] reports that the elder Hon. Benjamin Selby became Auditor of State in 1834 under Governor Breathitt and remained auditor until his death in 1845.
- [[Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts - office history]] lists **Ben Shelby** as auditor from 1820-1834 and **Thomas S. Page** as auditor from 1834-1846.

## Evidence against

None against the existence of the conflict itself. The unresolved issue is which historical identification or chronology is correct and whether **Ben Shelby** is a spelling variant for the elder Benjamin Selby.

## Conflicts

The two sources cannot both be literally correct about a Benjamin/Ben holding the auditor's office from 1834 through 1845. The 1878 profile may contain a chronology error, may conflate offices or individuals, or the official retrospective list may preserve a spelling/identity distinction that has not yet been reconciled.

## Analysis

Do not use the reported 1834-1845 auditor tenure as an identifying fact for Hon. Benjamin Selby until contemporary state records are inspected. The father-son statement in the 1878 biography is analytically separable from the disputed auditor chronology and remains a strong relationship lead because it is tied to the younger Ben's distinctive birth, spouses, legal career, and later residence.

The name **Ben Shelby** on the official office history should also remain source-exact. It must not be silently converted to Selby.

## Confidence rationale

Confirmed that a documentary conflict exists because the two reviewed sources give directly incompatible office chronologies. This rating does not resolve which chronology or identity is correct.

## Next research action

1. Inspect the original scanned 1878 biographical page rather than the web transcription.
2. Locate Kentucky executive appointments, legislative journals, state almanacs/directories, Auditor reports, payroll records, and official correspondence for 1820-1846.
3. Determine whether contemporary records use **Shelby**, **Selby**, or both for the 1820-1834 auditor.
4. Seek an obituary, legislative memorial, or probate record for Hon. Benjamin Selby, reportedly deceased in 1845.
