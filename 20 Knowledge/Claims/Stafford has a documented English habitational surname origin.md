# Stafford has a documented English habitational surname origin

## Claim

*Stafford* has a documented English habitational surname origin, chiefly associated with Stafford, Staffordshire, while other similarly named English places and an Irish variant origin are also recorded.

## Subjects

- Stafford surname
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Dictionary of American Family Names - Stafford]] identifies an English habitational origin, principally from Stafford in Staffordshire, and records alternative source places and an Irish variant origin.
- [[Staffordshire EUS - Stafford place-name]] independently discusses the historical place-name context of Stafford, Staffordshire.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the narrow statement that Stafford has documented English habitational surname origins was found.

## Conflicts

The surname dictionary records multiple possible English source places and an Irish variant origin. These alternatives prevent any inference that all people named Stafford share one origin or ancestry.

## Analysis

The specialist surname dictionary establishes an English habitational explanation and explicitly records alternatives. The Staffordshire historic-environment assessment independently contextualizes the principal place-name. Together they support linguistic and geographic context only.

This does not identify any particular Stafford ancestor, establish descent from the medieval Stafford family, or connect a Stafford to Selby, Haskins, Plantagenet, Wessex, or a modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: an expert surname dictionary and an independent historic-environment assessment support the narrow contextual conclusion. The claim is not `confirmed` for any individual family because the sources record multiple origins and no identity-specific evidence is available.

## Next research action

Obtain identity-specific civil, parish, probate, land, and other records for any proposed Stafford ancestor before assessing a connection to the medieval Stafford family.
