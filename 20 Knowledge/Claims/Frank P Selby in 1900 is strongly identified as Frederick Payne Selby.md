# Frank P. Selby in 1900 is strongly identified as Frederick Payne Selby

## Claim

Frank P. Selby, enumerated in New Orleans in 1900 with wife Elizabeth D. and son James, is strongly identified as Frederick Payne Selby, who married Elizabeth Dolan in Orleans Parish in 1884.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Payne Selby 1884 Orleans marriage license image]] names Frederick Payne Selby and Elizabeth Dolan as the marriage parties.
- [[FamilySearch - James Benjamin Sanford Selby 1886 Orleans birth index]] names Frederick P. Selby and Elizabeth Dolan as parents of James, born in Orleans Parish in 1886.
- [[FamilySearch - James Selby 1900 US census image]] records Frank P. Selby with wife Elizabeth D. and son James, age 14, in New Orleans.
- [[FamilySearch - James Benjamin Sanford Selby 1910 Orleans marriage license image]] repeats the full-name James and the parents Frederick P. Selby and Elizabeth Dolan.
- [[FamilySearch - James B Selby 1938 Orleans death index]] again repeats the parent pair and New Orleans context.

## Evidence against

No reviewed record states in one sentence that Frank P. Selby and Frederick Payne Selby were the same man.

## Conflicts

The 1900 census uses the first name Frank, while the marriage and child records use Frederick. Both retain the middle initial P. The name difference is material and is not silently normalized.

## Analysis

The spouse, child's name and age, parent pair, chronology, and Orleans locality form a consistent identity cluster. The correlation is substantially stronger than a name match, but the absence of a direct alias or record-to-record cross-reference prevents a confirmed identity rating.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: multiple independent records agree on the family cluster, time, and place. The exact Frank-to-Frederick name bridge remains inferential.
