# Nancy wife of John Gunby was executrix of Parker Selbys will in 1792

## Claim

By 31 December 1792, Nancy, wife of John Gunby, was acting as executrix of the last will and testament of Parker Selby in Worcester County.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - John and Nancy Gunby 1792-1795 Parker Selby estate accounts]] directly identifies "John Gunby and Nancy his wife" and calls Nancy executrix of Parker Selby's will. The fourth and final account repeats the same identity and role in 1795.
- [[FamilySearch - Nancy Selby 1789 Parker Selby executor bond]] names Nancy Selby in the same executrix role for Parker's estate on 8 April 1789.
- [[Maryland State Archives - Parker Selby Worcester estate index]] and [[FamilySearch - Worcester estate docket Parker Selby index page]] place the 1789 opening, 1791 inventory, and 1792-1795 accounting dates in one estate track.

## Analysis and limits

The 1792 account directly proves Nancy's marriage to John Gunby and her office under Parker's will. Continuity of estate, office, and filing trail strongly identifies her with the Nancy Selby who qualified in 1789.

Neither account calls Nancy Parker's widow or wife. They name no children and do not identify Parker's father. This claim therefore does not merge the estate with the separate Parker "of John" or Parker "of Wm." tracks, and it does not by itself prove that the 1790 wards George and Elizabeth belonged to this exact estate file.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the 1792 and 1795 original account headings expressly state Nancy's marital relationship to John Gunby and her executrix role under Parker Selby's will. The confidence does not extend to Nancy's relationship to Parker beyond that office.
