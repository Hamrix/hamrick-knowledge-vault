# John Selby Jr 1782 Worcester estate is a lead, not identification of Major Selby's father

## Claim

The Maryland State Archives' Worcester estate index records an administration for John Selby Jr. beginning in 1782.  Its date makes the file a high-value lead for the deceased John Selby named as father of the minor Major Selby in 1785, but it does not identify the two Johns as the same person.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Maryland State Archives - John Selby Jr 1782 Worcester estate index]] records John Selby Jr., Leah Selby as administrator, and indexed estate activity beginning with an inventory dated 20 October 1782.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1785 Worcester guardianship estate-view image]] directly identifies a minor Major Selby as the orphan of a deceased John Selby on 1 July 1785, but gives neither that John's suffix nor an estate-file reference.

## Analysis and limit

The chronology is compatible: an estate indexed under John Selby Jr. was active before the 1785 guardianship bond.  Compatibility is not identity.  The index does not name Major, and the bond does not call Major's father John Selby Jr.  It also does not establish whether Leah Selby was Major's mother, guardian, or any other relative.

Accordingly, this is a retrieval target, not a family-tree connection.  Do not attach Major to John Selby Jr., Leah Selby, John Selby Esq., Zadock Selby, Parker Selby, or any earlier Selby line on the basis of the shared surname, county, title, or timing alone.

## Next research action

Use the digitized [[Maryland State Archives - John Selby Jr 1782 Worcester estate index|estate-docket locator]] to identify the exact 1782 entry by visual review, then obtain and transcribe the underlying inventory, sale account, distributions, and any guardianship or balance papers.  Look specifically for named minor heirs, widow status, guardians, and releases.  Only an explicit relationship statement or an otherwise unambiguous identity chain can connect it to the 1785 orphan.
