# The Nicholas County Major Selby dead by 1833 was not the Indiana Major alive in 1850

## Claim

The Major Selby whose will was proved in Nicholas County, Kentucky, in August 1833 was not the Major Selby enumerated alive in Hamilton County, Indiana, in 1850 and 1860.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1833 Nicholas County will and 1835 inventory]] records the Kentucky testator as deceased when his will was proved in August 1833.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1850 US census image]] records a living Major Selby, age 67 and born in Maryland, in Hamilton County, Indiana.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1860 US census image]] records a living Major Selby, age 79 and born in Maryland, in the same Indiana county.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1820 Nicholas census pages 111 and 129]] and [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1820 US census image]] independently place adult Major Selby household heads in Kentucky and Maryland in the same census year.

## Analysis

`confirmed`: a man dead by 1833 cannot be the man enumerated alive in 1850 and 1860. The records therefore establish at least two adult Major Selby identity clusters. This separation does not determine which, if either, was the Worcester County ward last directly identified there in 1803.
