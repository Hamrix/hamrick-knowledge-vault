# John Selby was father of Nancy Gunby and grandfather of George and Betsey Selby

## Claim

John Selby, Esquire, was the father of Nancy Gunby and maternal grandfather of her children George and Betsey Selby.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - John Selby 1790 Worcester County will images]] calls Nancy Gunby the testator's daughter, calls George a son of Nancy, and calls Betsey Selby a daughter of Nancy.

## Analysis and limits

The will states both generations within one testamentary family group: John to daughter Nancy, then Nancy to George and Betsey. This establishes the maternal descent without needing to identify Nancy's husband.

The will does not call Betsey "Elizabeth" or name Parker Selby as the children's father. [[Nancy Gunby is strongly identified as mother of George and Elizabeth Selby]] makes that cross-record identification at strong confidence using the separate 1790 guardian bond.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the contemporary will directly states each relationship in the two-generation chain.
