# Frederick E. Selby's 1953 marriage license identifies James B. Selby as his father

## Claim

Frederick E. Selby's 1953 Lenoir County, North Carolina marriage license identifies James B. Selby as his father, then marks James as deceased.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1953 Lenoir County marriage license image]] is a reviewed official license image. Its groom's parent field names James B. Selby as Frederick's father.

## Analysis and limit

This is a direct reported-parentage statement on an official marriage form. It does not establish James B. Selby's own parents or connect Frederick to Major Selby, any colonial Selby person, or a medieval line. The exact identity should be corroborated with additional civil and census records.

## Next research action

Locate James B. Selby's death and probate records, then correlate them with Frederick's 1930, 1940, and 1950 census records.
