# Frederick E. Selby's 1953 marriage license identifies James B. Selby as his father

## Claim

Frederick E. Selby's 1953 Lenoir County, North Carolina marriage license identifies James B. Selby as his father, then marks James as deceased.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1953 Lenoir County marriage license image]] is a reviewed official license image. Its groom's parent field names James B. Selby as Frederick's father.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Ethelred Selby NUMIDENT record]] independently names the exact full-name Frederick and reports his parents as James B. Selby and Sarah E. Stafford.
- [[James B Selby named as Frederick E Selbys father is strongly identified as James Benjamin Sanford Selby]] correlates this reported father with the 1910 marriage, 1938 death record, and resolved Sarah Eveline Stafford identity.

## Analysis and limit

The original marriage license and independent NUMIDENT transcription agree on Frederick's name and parent pair, confirming the documentary father statement. This does not by itself establish James B. Selby's full identity or connect Frederick to Major Selby, any colonial Selby person, or a medieval line; those are addressed only through separate adjacent-link claims.
