# Parker Selbys 1746 will named eldest son John Selby

## Claim

Parker Selby of Worcester County near Snow Hill named John Selby as his eldest son in his will made 8 November 1746.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1746 York County will]] directly calls John the testator's eldest son, devises the 750-acre home plantation to him, and appoints him an executor.
- [[FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1747 Worcester executor bond]] corroborates Mary as the deceased Parker's widow and John and Parker as the acting executors named in the will.

## Analysis and limits

The will directly proves the bounded father-son relationship **Parker Selby -> eldest son John Selby**. It also names John's siblings William, James, Micajah, Parker, Zadock, and Tabitha Baily Selby.

The direct claim ends there. The will does not identify John as the Worcester legislator or the John Selby Esq. whose will was proved in 1790.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the testator expressly calls John his eldest son in an original recorded will.
