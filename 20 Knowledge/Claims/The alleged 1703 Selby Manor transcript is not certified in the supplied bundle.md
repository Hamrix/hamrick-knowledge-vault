# The alleged 1703 Selby Manor transcript is not certified in the supplied bundle

## Claim

The typed Latin and English text presented as the 1703 Selby Manor court-roll transcript is not accompanied by a completed archivist certification or an image of the cited original in the supplied bundle.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Legacy Selby Manor evidence bundle file audit]] records that the certification contains blank archivist-name, signature, and date fields.
- The bundle includes no manuscript image against which the typed Latin can be checked.
- [[DPRI 2 is a Durham probate will-register series, not a manorial court-roll series]] establishes that one alternative reference used elsewhere in the project belongs to a probate series.

## Evidence against

No signed certification, archive covering letter, invoice, reproduction, or official archive email confirming the alleged passage has been supplied.

## Analysis

The typed passage is presently an unauthenticated transcription claim. It must not be described as “verbatim,” “certified,” or archival proof until the original document and provenance are inspected.

This does not establish that no related historical record exists. It establishes that the supplied file does not authenticate the quoted text.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: based on direct inspection of the supplied bundle and the absence of the certification and source image it says should be annexed.

## Next research action

Request from Durham University the exact catalogue reference, repository confirmation, full folio image, reproduction terms, and—if needed—an independent palaeographic transcription.
