# Edwin Selby was enumerated in Eden Township, Marshall County, Iowa, in 1870

## Claim

The 1870 federal census enumerated an Edwin Selby, age 38 and born in Maryland, as head of household 105 in Eden Township, Marshall County, Iowa, with Rebecca, Cora, Ella, and Hester Selby.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Edwin Selby 1870 US census image]] is the reviewed original census schedule and supplies the household, age, birthplace, and location.

## Analysis and limit

This is a narrow census fact only. It does not prove that this Edwin was the 25-year-old Maryland-born Edwin Selby listed in Major Selby's 1860 Hamilton County, Indiana, household. The two entries are a plausible research lead, but the census contains no parentage statement and the estimated ages do not match exactly. It also does not establish Edwin's relationship to Major, Harriet, James F. Selby, or any asserted colonial or royal line.

## Next research action

Locate an original marriage, probate, land, death, obituary, or military-pension record for this Iowa Edwin Selby that names parents or otherwise documents a move from Hamilton County, Indiana. Until an intervening identity record is found, retain the 1860 and 1870 Edwins as separate record subjects.
