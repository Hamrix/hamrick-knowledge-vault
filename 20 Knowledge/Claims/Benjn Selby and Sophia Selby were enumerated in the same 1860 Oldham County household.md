# Benjn Selby and Sophia Selby were enumerated in the same 1860 Oldham County household

## Claim

The 1860 federal census enumerated Benjn Selby and Sophia Selby consecutively in the same Oldham County, Kentucky, household.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Benjn Selby 1860 US census image]] is the reviewed original census schedule and lists Benjn Selby, Sophia Selby, Tammy Selby, Catherine Selby, and Ben Selby as a consecutive household group.

## Analysis and limit

The 1860 census does not report relationships to the household head. It proves co-enumeration only, not a spouse, parent-child, or other family relationship. Although the names, place, and timing make comparison with the 1854 Benjamin Selby and Sophia E. Stone license papers a worthwhile lead, the records do not themselves identify the same two individuals. No such correlation is adopted here.

## Next research action

Locate a post-1860 record that gives both an identity-specific name and a stated relationship, such as a marriage return, probate record, death record, or later census with relationship columns.
