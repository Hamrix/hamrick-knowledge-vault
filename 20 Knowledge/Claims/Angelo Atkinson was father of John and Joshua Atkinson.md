# Angelo Atkinson was father of John and Joshua Atkinson

## Claim

Angelo Atkinson, whose Worcester County will was proved in 1766, was the father of John Atkinson, whose will was proved in 1779, and Joshua Atkinson, whose will was proved in 1773.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Angelo Atkinson 1766 Worcester County will images]] names sons John and Joshua Atkinson.
- [[FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1779 Worcester County will images]] calls Angelo the testator's father and Joshua the testator's brother. It also refers to the plantation where Angelo had lived.
- [[FamilySearch - Joshua Atkinson 1773 Worcester will images]] identifies the separate Joshua household and is chronologically compatible with the two relationship statements.

## Analysis and limits

The 1766 and 1779 wills supply reciprocal relationship language: Angelo names John and Joshua as sons, while John names Angelo as father and Joshua as brother. This closes the specific two-generation Atkinson relationship.

It does not identify John Selby's wife or prove descent from this Atkinson group to Major Selby. Any Selby connection must be established through separately stated relationships.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two reviewed primary wills provide mutually consistent, explicit parent-child and sibling language.
