# The 1874 Roberts Selby birth index names Benjamin Selby and Docie Stone

## Claim

The FamilySearch index for Roberts Selby's 15 April 1874 Henry County birth names Benjamin Selby and Docie Stone as the parents.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Roberts Selby 1874 Henry County birth index]] records the indexed names.

## Analysis and limits

The image was unavailable, so this establishes what the derivative index reports, not a verified transcription of the birth return. The identity connection to Robert C. Selby remains a correlation because the later death certificate reports an 1875 birth.

## Confidence rationale

`moderate`: an official index provides specific names, but the original was unavailable and a date conflict remains.
