# Joshua Atkinson's 1773 will named Sarah Rachel Angelo and John Atkinson

## Claim

Joshua Atkinson's 1773 will named wife Sarah and children Rachel, Angelo, and John and placed underage Angelo in Townsend care.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Joshua Atkinson 1773 Worcester will images]] preserves both will pages.
- [[FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1779 Worcester County will images]] independently calls Joshua the testator's brother and identifies Joshua's sons Angelo and John and daughter Rachel.

## Analysis and limits

The Townsend arrangement establishes an Atkinson–Townsend association. It does not establish Selby parentage.

The two wills independently establish the named Joshua Atkinson household. Neither record establishes Major Selby's parentage.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the reviewed original will directly states the household and care arrangement, and Joshua's brother independently repeats the three children's names in his 1779 will.
