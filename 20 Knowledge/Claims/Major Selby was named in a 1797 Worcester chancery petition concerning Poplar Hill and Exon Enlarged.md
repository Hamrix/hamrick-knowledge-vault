# Major Selby was named in a 1797 Worcester chancery petition concerning Poplar Hill and Exon Enlarged

## Claim

Maryland State Archives indexes a Worcester County chancery case dated 22 December 1797 in which Major Selby was a named party and the stated subject was a petition to sell Poplar Hill and Exon Enlarged.

## Subjects

- Major Selby, party named by the index
- Daniel Patrick
- William Patrick
- Patrick Waters Jr.
- Matilda Waters
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Maryland State Archives - Major Selby 1797 chancery case index]] is the official archival finding aid. Its entry 4177 gives the case caption, Worcester County code, petition description, date, and archival reference.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the existence or indexed description of this case was located in this review.

## Conflicts

The index does not state why Major Selby was a party, whether he held any interest in the tracts, or whether any party was related to him.

## Analysis

This is a verified litigation and land-research lead, not evidence of parentage or inheritance. Shared land names and litigation participation cannot establish a family connection without the petition and supporting papers.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the official Maryland Archives finding aid expressly indexes the case, its parties, date, county, and subject description. This confidence applies to the indexed case information only, not to any unreviewed content of the case packet.
