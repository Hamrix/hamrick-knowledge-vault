# The Adair Lingan W Selby is strongly identified as William Magruder Selbys son

## Claim

The Lingan W. Selby documented in Adair County, Kentucky, from 1803 through 1821 is strongly identified as the Lingan Wilson Selby whom William Magruder Selby named as a son in Prince George's County, Maryland.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - William Magruder Selby 1773 will]] directly names son Lingan Wilson Selby in Prince George's County and assigns him part of the testator's Maryland land.
- [[FamilySearch - Lingan W Selby 1809 Adair power of attorney]] directly identifies Lingan W. Selby of Adair County as owning land in Prince George's County in 1809. The same source note records his 1803 Adair estate-appraisal service and 1809-1810 tax appearances.
- [[FamilySearch - Lingan Wilson Selby 1821 Adair estate inventory]] documents the end of the Adair chronology under the same distinctive name and middle initial.

## Analysis and limits

The distinctive full given name, matching middle initial, direct Prince George's land connection, Selby grantee context, and continuous Adair chronology make the cross-state identity substantially stronger than a same-name guess. No reviewed instrument expressly calls the Adair man William Magruder Selby's son, so the identity remains below confirmed.

This identity finding joins the Maryland William-to-Lingan relationship to the Adair Lingan evidence. It does not bridge Lingan to Hon. Benjamin Selby.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: multiple original records agree on a highly distinctive identity and a specific Maryland-to-Kentucky land connection, but the cross-record identity is inferential rather than expressly stated.
