# Walter de Ver was son of Adam of Goxhill and grandson of Robert son of Erneis

## Claim

Walter de Ver was the son of Adam of Goxhill and grandson of Robert son of Erneis.

## Subjects

- Walter de Ver
- Adam of Goxhill
- Robert son of Erneis

## Evidence supporting

- [[Loyd and Stenton 1950 - Sir Christopher Hattons Book of Seals entry 73]], printed page 52, transcribes Walter's charter as `Walterus de Ver filius Ade de Gousle`. The same charter calls Robert son of Erneis `auus meus` and Adam `pater meus`.
- [[Wilkinson 1999 - Thirteenth-Century Women in Lincolnshire]] independently reconstructs Adam fitzErneis of Goxhill as Walter's father and cites *Registrum Antiquissimum* II, pages 211-212, no. 509n, and *Early Yorkshire Charters* III, page 61.

## Evidence against

No reviewed evidence contradicts these two paternal relationships.

## Conflicts

[[The Genealogist Volume 20 - 1904]] presents competing wider pedigree placements for Walter. The explicit father-and-grandfather clauses resolve Walter's immediate paternal relationships but do not identify his mother or settle the older Ver ancestry.

## Analysis

The relationship wording is direct and first-person within Walter's charter: Adam is called his father and Robert son of Erneis his grandfather. Although the project reviewed a scholarly edition of an early modern copy rather than the medieval original, the exact Latin text and an independent scholarly reconstruction converge on Adam's paternity.

The charter does not identify Gundreda as Walter's mother. It also does not connect this family to a Selby-surnamed line, the earls of Oxford, Maryland, or the user's documented ancestry.

## Confidence rationale

**Strong:** an explicit charter transcription supplies both relationships, and a separate scholarly study with different cited record targets corroborates Adam's paternity. Original-manuscript or parallel charter-edition review is still desirable.
