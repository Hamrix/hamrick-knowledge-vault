# Anne of Gloucester was a daughter of Thomas of Woodstock

## Claim

Anne of Gloucester was a daughter of Thomas of Woodstock, duke of Gloucester.

## Subjects

- Anne of Gloucester
- Thomas of Woodstock, duke of Gloucester
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Rawcliffe 2008 - Humphrey Stafford]] identifies Anne of Gloucester as a daughter of Thomas of Woodstock.
- [[Cokayne - Complete Peerage Stafford-Gloucester relationship]] independently identifies Anne as the child of Thomas, duke of Gloucester.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified. Reference works vary in styling Anne as "of Gloucester," "of Woodstock," or "Plantagenet"; these naming conventions do not alter the documented parent-child relationship.

## Analysis

The ODNB biography and *Complete Peerage* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Thomas of Woodstock to Anne of Gloucester. This records one component of the separately supported claim [[Humphrey Stafford was a great-grandson of Edward III through Anne of Gloucester]].

It does not establish Anne's other descendants, descent from Humphrey Stafford to anyone else, or any connection to a Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: a modern expert biography and an independent peerage reference agree on the direct relationship.
