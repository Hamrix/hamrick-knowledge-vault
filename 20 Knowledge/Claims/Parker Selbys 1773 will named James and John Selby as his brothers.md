# Parker Selby's 1773 will named James and John Selby as his brothers

## Claim

Parker Selby of Worcester County named James Selby and John Selby as his brothers in his will proved 8 June 1773.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1773 Worcester County will images]] is the reviewed recorded will. It expressly refers to James Selby and John Selby as the testator's brothers and also refers to property bought from brother James after their mother's death.

## Analysis and limits

This directly proves the sibling relationships for the people in Parker's will. It does not, by itself, identify brother John as the 1790 testator or name their parents. The Maryland legislative biography reports that identity and names their father as an earlier Parker Selby, but the parent-child link remains a primary-record retrieval target.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original will expressly uses the relationship term "brother."

## Next research action

Retrieve the ca. 1746/47 estate, land division, and Bantry inheritance instruments of the earlier Parker Selby to test the reported Parker -> John/Parker/James sibling generation.
