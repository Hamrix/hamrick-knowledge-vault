# Empress Matilda was a daughter of Henry I

## Claim

Empress Matilda was a daughter of Henry I of England.

## Subjects

- Empress Matilda
- Henry I of England
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Archontology - Henry II]] identifies Empress Matilda as a daughter of Henry I.
- [[1911 Encyclopaedia Britannica - Henry II]] independently identifies Matilda as a daughter of Henry I.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified. Matilda is also called Empress Matilda or Empress Maud; these names and title forms refer to the same person in the sources used here.

## Analysis

The Archontology reference and the *Encyclopaedia Britannica* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Henry I to Empress Matilda. This records one component of the separately supported claim [[Henry II was the son of Empress Matilda, daughter of Henry I]].

It does not establish any particular descent through Matilda, nor a connection to a Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct relationship. The conclusion is restricted to the named parent and child.
