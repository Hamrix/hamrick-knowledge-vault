# Frederick E. Selby's 1953 marriage license identifies Sarah E. Selby as his mother

## Claim

Frederick E. Selby's 1953 Lenoir County, North Carolina marriage license identifies Sarah E. Selby as his mother and marks her as living.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1953 Lenoir County marriage license image]] is a reviewed official license image. Its groom's parent field names Sarah E. Selby as Frederick's mother.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1930 US census image]] independently enumerates Frederick E. Selby as the son of Sarah E. Stafford in New Orleans in 1930.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1940 US census image]] independently enumerates Frederick E. Selby, age 28, as the son of the female Selby household head in New Orleans. The head's name is less clear in the image/index and is used as corroboration rather than as a standalone identity proof.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Ethelred Selby NUMIDENT record]] names the exact full-name Frederick and independently reports his mother as Sarah E. Stafford.
- [[Sarah E Stafford and Evelyn Stafford were Sarah Eveline Stafford]] reconciles Sarah E. with the Eveline or Evelyn Stafford who married James Benjamin Sanford Selby.

## Analysis and limit

The marriage license, NUMIDENT record, and 1930 census independently report the same maternal relationship, while the Sarah Eveline identity proof explains the Sarah, Eveline, Evelyn, Stafford, and Selby name forms. This confirms the documentary parent-child link but does not establish a biological conclusion beyond the records, connect the user to Frederick, or connect this Stafford family to colonial or medieval lines.
