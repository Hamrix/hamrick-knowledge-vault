# Elizabeth McClain as mother of younger Ben Selby remains unproven

## Claim status

No reviewed relationship-bearing record identifies Elizabeth McClain as the mother of the younger Ben Selby born in 1826.

## Evidence considered

- [[FamilySearch - elder Benjamin Selby 1811 marriage and 1830-1840 censuses]] proves that Benjamin Selby married Elizabeth McClain in 1811, before the younger Ben's birth.
- [[Armstrong 1878 - Ben Selby biographical sketch]] names only the younger man's father and does not identify his mother.

## Analysis and limits

The marriage makes Elizabeth a plausible candidate, but it does not prove that the couple remained married through 1826, that this was the same older Benjamin, or that Elizabeth was the child's mother. An original birth, Bible, obituary, probate, guardianship, or sibling record is required.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: the candidate is documented and chronologically possible, but the parent-child relationship is not stated.
