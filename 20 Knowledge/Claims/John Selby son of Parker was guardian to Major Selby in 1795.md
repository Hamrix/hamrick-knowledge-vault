# John Selby son of Parker was guardian to Major Selby in 1795

## Claim

By 10 March 1795, John Selby, son of Parker Selby, was acting as guardian to Major Selby, orphan of John Selby.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - John Selby Esq 1795 Worcester final account]] records a payment to **"John Selby son of Parker guardian to Major Selby Orphan of John Selby"** for Major's legacy from his grandfather.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1785 Worcester guardianship estate-view image]] records John Selby Esq. as guardian in 1785, showing that the named guardian changed or was replaced before the 1795 distribution.

## Analysis and limits

The final account directly identifies the 1795 guardian and his father. The record does not state why the guardianship changed or provide the appointment date. It also does not by itself prove a later identity for Major.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original final account expressly states the guardian's name, patronymic, ward, and role.
