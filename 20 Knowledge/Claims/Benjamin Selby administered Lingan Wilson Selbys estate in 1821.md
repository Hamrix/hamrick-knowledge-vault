# Benjamin Selby administered Lingan Wilson Selbys estate in 1821

## Claim

On 5 February 1821, the Adair County Court granted Benjamin Selby administration of the estate of Lingan Wilson Selby.

## Subjects

- Benjamin Selby
- Lingan Wilson Selby

## Evidence supporting

[[FamilySearch - Lingan Wilson Selby Adair administration and widow orders 1821]] preserves the original Order Book D entry. It states that Benjamin took the required oath and executed a $3,000 bond with William Owens as security.

## Evidence against

None for the administrator appointment itself.

## Conflicts

The FamilySearch tree identifies Benjamin as Lingan's son, but the court order does not state that relationship.

## Analysis

The order proves Benjamin's fiduciary role but does not identify him as Lingan's son, heir, child, or other relative. It must not be converted into parent-child proof.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: a contemporary original court order directly states the administration grant.

## Next research action

Request the administration bond and loose estate packet for the 5 February 1821 grant from the Adair County Clerk or Kentucky archival holdings and test whether either names Benjamin's relationship to Lingan. FamilySearch's Adair catalog exposes no separate administrator-bond series.
