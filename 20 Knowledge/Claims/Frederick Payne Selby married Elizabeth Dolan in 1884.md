# Frederick Payne Selby married Elizabeth Dolan in 1884

## Claim

Frederick Payne Selby and Elizabeth Dolan were recorded in an Orleans Parish marriage-license record dated 18 December 1884.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Payne Selby 1884 Orleans marriage license image]] directly names the parties and carries the record date.

## Analysis and limit

The original license establishes the recorded marriage event. It does not prove the parentage of later “Frank P.” or “James” Selby census entries without independent identity correlation.

## Next research action

Correlate their children's original birth records and the 1900 census with name, date, address, and parental information.
