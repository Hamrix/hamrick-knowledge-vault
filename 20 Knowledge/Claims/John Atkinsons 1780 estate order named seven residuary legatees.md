# John Atkinson's 1780 estate order named seven residuary legatees

## Claim

An August 1780 Worcester County Orphans' Court order named Angelo Atkinson, John Atkinson, John Selby, William Atkinson Selby, Rachel Flemming, Mary White, and Nancy White as residuary legatees of John Atkinson.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1780 estate order and appeal bond]] preserves the order and its appeal bond.

## Analysis and limits

The order places John Selby and William Atkinson Selby together in the Atkinson estate. It does not state the individual relationships or prove that every legatee was a child.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: a reviewed original court order expressly names the legal group.

## Next research action

Recover John Atkinson's complete will dated 21 March 1779, estate accounts, receipts, and the final appeal disposition.
