# Edward the Exile was a son of Edmund Ironside

## Claim

Edward the Exile (Edward Aetheling) was a son of Edmund Ironside.

## Subjects

- Edward the Exile (Edward Aetheling)
- Edmund Ironside
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[People of Medieval Scotland - Margaret queen of Scots]] identifies Edward Aetheling (Edward the Exile) as a son of Edmund Ironside.
- [[DNB 1885-1900 - Margaret queen of Scots]] independently identifies Edward the Exile as a son of Edmund Ironside.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified. Edward is styled both "the Exile" and "Aetheling" in the sources; these are descriptions of the same named historical person.

## Analysis

The scholarly People of Medieval Scotland database and the published *Dictionary of National Biography* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Edmund Ironside to Edward the Exile. This supplies one further historical Wessex-context link before the separately documented claim [[Margaret of Scotland was a daughter of Edward the Exile]].

It does not establish a connection from Edmund Ironside, Edward the Exile, or Margaret to a later Stafford, Selby, Haskins, Plantagenet, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct relationship. The conclusion is confined to the named medieval people.
