# The elder Adair and Frankfort Benjamin Selby was distinct from the younger attorney

## Claim

The older Benjamin Selby who married in Adair County in 1811 and headed a Frankfort household in 1840 was distinct from the younger attorney born in 1826.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - elder Benjamin Selby 1811 marriage and 1830-1840 censuses]] records the 1811 marriage and an 1840 household head aged fifty to fifty-nine.
- [[Armstrong 1878 - Ben Selby biographical sketch]] reports the younger attorney's birth on 31 January 1826 and identifies his father as an older Hon. Benjamin Selby.

## Analysis and limits

The age difference proves two same-name men. Their father-son relationship is strong through the period biography and correlated evidence but is not itself proved by the census or marriage register.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the 1840 head's age is incompatible with the younger man's 1826 birth.
