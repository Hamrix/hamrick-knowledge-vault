# Rhody Selby was the infant orphan of W A Selby deceased in 1811

## Claim

On 7 January 1811, a Bourbon County order identified Rhody Selby as the infant orphan of W. A. Selby, deceased.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Rhody Selby 1811 Bourbon orphan order]] directly states the relationship and gives an age benchmark.

## Analysis and limits

The original proves William → Rhody and William's death before the order. It does not identify William's wife, all children, complete estate, or Major Selby.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: a reviewed original court record directly states the relationship.
