# William Selby and wife Priscilla conveyed Nicholas County Brushy Fork land in 1819

## Claim

William Selby and wife Priscilla, both identified as of Nicholas County, conveyed Armstrong McClintock a small Brushy Fork tract on 5 May 1819; the deed was recorded 1 July 1819.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - William and Priscilla Selby 1819 Nicholas deed]] contains the original recorded conveyance and spousal wording.
- [[FamilySearch - Barkley and Patsey Selby Nicholas Haws survey deeds 1816-1820]] establishes a separate Selby couple in the broader Brushy Fork and Haws-survey land cluster.

## Analysis and limitations

The deed proves the bounded William-Priscilla couple and county residence. It does not identify either spouse's parents or children, name Major or Barkley Selby, or state a relationship among the Selby households. William and Priscilla therefore remain a separate collateral identity cluster.
