# Jesse Henderson conveyed inherited lot 2 to Henry Selby in 1823

## Claim

On 22 August 1823, Jesse Henderson of Franklin County, Indiana, conveyed Henry Selby of Nicholas County, Kentucky, lot 2 from the division of Jesse Henderson deceased's land.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Jesse Henderson 1821 division and 1823 deed to Henry Selby]] contains the original recorded deed, the general-index entry, and the underlying 1821 division.

## Analysis and limitations

The deed directly says the commissioners had allotted lot 2 to the grantor Jesse Henderson and identifies the tract as part of the deceased Jesse Henderson's divided land. The shared name is recorded as written and likely represents a younger heir, but the deed does not state his relationship to the decedent or to Nelly Henderson. The two-dollar consideration does not by itself prove a family gift.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original deed directly records the parties, residences, source of title, and numbered lot.
