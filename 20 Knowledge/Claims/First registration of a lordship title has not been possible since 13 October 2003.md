# First registration of a lordship title has not been possible since 13 October 2003

## Claim

HM Land Registry has not accepted applications for first registration of a lordship title since 2003-10-13.

## Subjects

- HM Land Registry
- manors
- lordship titles
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[HM Land Registry - Practice Guide 22 Manors]] states that, since 2003-10-13, an application cannot be made for first registration of a lordship title.

## Evidence against

No contrary current HM Land Registry guidance was found.

## Conflicts

Legacy Selby Manor materials propose first registration of a lordship title alongside the freehold and describe this as a current legal pathway. That proposition conflicts with the official guidance.

## Analysis

The official guidance separates three concepts: a lordship title, physical manorial land, and manorial rights. Physical land may still be registered through ordinary land-registration procedures, while an already-registered lordship title may remain on the register. That does not restore a route to first registration of a lordship title after 2003-10-13.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the proposition is stated directly in current official HM Land Registry guidance.

## Project effect

- Withdraw the legacy statement that a new Whitehouse or Selby Manor lordship title can be first-registered.
- Do not imply that proving descent from a former manorial family would itself establish title to the house, land, or manorial rights.
- Treat any alleged surviving rights as a separate evidential and legal question requiring original deeds and specialist legal review.
