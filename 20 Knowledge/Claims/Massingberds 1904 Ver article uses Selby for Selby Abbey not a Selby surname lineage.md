# Massingberd's 1904 Ver article uses Selby for Selby Abbey, not a Selby surname lineage

## Claim

In W. O. Massingberd's article on the medieval Ver family of Bottesford, Goxhill, and Sproatley, the word **Selby** identifies Selby Abbey, its convent or sacristan, or its coucher book. The article does not identify a person surnamed Selby as an ancestor, descendant, or spouse of the Ver family.

## Subjects

- The medieval Ver family discussed by Massingberd
- Selby Abbey
- Guy de Ver
- Gilbert, described as a monk or abbot at Selby
- Walter and Simon de Ver

## Evidence supporting

- [[The Genealogist Volume 20 - 1904]], printed pages 73-77, visually reviewed in full.
- Printed page 73 expressly says the relationship evidence comes from the *Coucher Book of Selby Abbey* and describes gifts by Guy de Ver to Selby.
- Printed page 74 refers to the sacristan and convent of Selby and to additional gifts to Selby.
- None of the displayed pedigrees contains a person surnamed Selby.

## Evidence against

No relationship-bearing passage in the article links a Selby-surnamed person to the Ver family.

## Conflicts

The co-occurrence of the terms `Ver`, `de Vere`, and `Selby` can look like a surname connection when extracted from context. The full pages show that this is an institutional and property relationship with Selby Abbey.

The article also leaves Walter de Ver's placement unresolved between two competing pedigrees. It should not be converted into a continuous lineage beyond the author's stated evidence.

## Analysis

Medieval gifts to an abbey can document donors, property, religious patronage, and sometimes relationships named in the charters. They do not make the abbey's place-name into the donor family's surname. Likewise, the printed-page-77 comparison between a Ver seal's boar and the great house of Vere's cognizance is a heraldic observation, not a proved descent.

This article supplies no connection to the Maryland Selby line, William Atkinson Selby, Hon. Benjamin Selby, Frederick Payne Selby, or the user's proven ancestry.

## Confidence rationale

**Confirmed for the article's meaning:** all five article pages and their notes were visually reviewed. This confidence does not extend to every medieval relationship proposed within the article or to any de Vere descent.

## Next research action

If the Ver family itself becomes a legitimate collateral target, retrieve the cited *Coucher Book of Selby* entries and original or authoritative editions of the charters, plea rolls, final concords, and inquisitions. Do not create a Selby-Ver bridge from the abbey name.
