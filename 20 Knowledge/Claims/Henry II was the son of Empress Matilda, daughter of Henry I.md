# Henry II was the son of Empress Matilda, daughter of Henry I

## Claim

Henry II of England was the son of Empress Matilda and Geoffrey Plantagenet; Empress Matilda was a daughter of Henry I of England.

## Subjects

- Henry II of England
- Empress Matilda
- Henry I of England
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Archontology - Henry II]] identifies Henry II as son of Empress Matilda and identifies Matilda as Henry I’s daughter.
- [[1911 Encyclopaedia Britannica - Henry II]] independently gives the same direct parent-child relationships.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named relationships was found.

## Conflicts

Matilda is frequently called Empress Matilda or Empress Maud because of her first marriage. These titles and name forms refer to the same person in the sources used here.

## Analysis

The sources independently corroborate two direct links: Henry I → Empress Matilda → Henry II. Combined with [[Margaret of Scotland linked Edward the Exile’s line to Henry I of England’s family]], this advances the documented royal context by one generation.

It does not establish any link from Henry II to Edward III, the Staffords, or a project-related modern family. Those later links must be separately documented.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the direct parent-child relationships agree across independent reference works and are not contested in the sources reviewed.
