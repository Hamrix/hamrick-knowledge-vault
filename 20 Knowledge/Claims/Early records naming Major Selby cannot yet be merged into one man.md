# Early records naming Major Selby cannot yet be merged into one man

## Claim status

The adult 1783 Worcester taxpayer, the underage 1785 orphan, the adult 1811 Bourbon purchaser, the two 1820 Nicholas entries, the 1825 father of Polly, and later compiled profiles cannot be merged by name alone.

## Evidence considered

- [[Major Selby was assessed for part of Choice in Worcester County in 1783]]
- [[1785 Worcester guardianship record identifies Major Selby as orphan of John Selby]]
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1811 Bourbon deed and index]]
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1820 Nicholas census pages 111 and 129]]
- [[FamilySearch - William Antrobus and Polly Selby 1825 Nicholas marriage file]]

## Analysis

The 1783 adult and 1785 child are necessarily distinct. The Kentucky records still require chronology, migration, household, land, associate, and relationship correlation.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: one collision is chronologically decisive, while the remaining identity assignments are unresolved.
