# Correction - the 1840 Frankfort household head was the elder Benjamin

## Claim

The Benjamin Selby heading a Frankfort household in 1840 was an older man and not the younger attorney born in 1826.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby 1840 Frankfort census image]] reports the head as age fifty to fifty-nine.
- [[Armstrong 1878 - Ben Selby biographical sketch]] reports the younger attorney's birth on 31 January 1826 and identifies his father as an older Hon. Benjamin Selby.

## Analysis and limits

The row excludes the younger Benjamin as household head. The earlier version of this claim also excluded him as a household member because no male aged fifteen to nineteen was present. That reasoning was incorrect: the younger man would have been fourteen on the census date. Because the census names only the head, it cannot prove whether he was present.

The current atomic claim is [[The elder Adair and Frankfort Benjamin Selby was distinct from the younger attorney]].

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original census age for the household head is incompatible with the younger man's 1826 birth. No conclusion is made about unnamed household members.
