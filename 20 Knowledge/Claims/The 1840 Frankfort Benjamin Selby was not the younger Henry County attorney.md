# The 1840 Frankfort Benjamin Selby was not the younger Henry County attorney

## Claim

The Benjamin Selby heading a Frankfort household in 1840 was not the Benjamin born about 1824–1825 who later practiced law.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby 1840 Frankfort census image]] reports the head as age fifty to fifty-nine and no male age fifteen to nineteen.

## Analysis and limits

The row excludes the younger Benjamin both as head and as an age-compatible household member. It does not identify the older Benjamin.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original census age columns are incompatible with the younger man.
