# Frederick Payne Selby's 1884 marriage license identifies Docia Stone as his mother

## Claim

Frederick Payne Selby's 1884 Orleans Parish marriage-license record identifies Docia Stone as his mother.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Payne Selby 1884 Orleans marriage license image]] is the reviewed official license image and names Docia Stone in Frederick's mother field.
- [[FamilySearch - Ben Selby 1880 US census image]] independently enumerates Docia Selby with her son Payne Selby in Kentucky in 1880. The shared Payne name, age progression, and the 1884 license's explicit maternal field corroborate the relationship.

## Analysis and limit

The license gives direct reported parentage, and the earlier original census independently records Docia with son Payne. This verifies the bounded Docia-to-Frederick Payne relationship. It does not identify Docia Stone's parents or establish a connection to an older Selby, Stafford, colonial, medieval, or royal family.
