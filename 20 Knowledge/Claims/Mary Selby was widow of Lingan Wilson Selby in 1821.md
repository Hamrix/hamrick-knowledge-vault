# Mary Selby was widow of Lingan Wilson Selby in 1821

## Claim

On 2 July 1821, the Adair County Court directly identified Mary Selby as the widow and relict of Lingan W. Selby.

## Subjects

- Mary Selby
- Lingan Wilson Selby

## Evidence supporting

[[FamilySearch - Lingan Wilson Selby Adair administration and widow orders 1821]] preserves the original Order Book D entry appointing commissioners to allot and assign Mary's share and report to the court.

## Evidence against

None located.

## Conflicts

None located in the reviewed records.

## Analysis

The record proves the spousal relationship. It names no child or heir and does not establish Benjamin Selby's relationship to either spouse.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: a contemporary original court order directly states the relationship.
