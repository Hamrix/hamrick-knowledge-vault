# Æthelred II was a son of Edgar

## Claim

Æthelred II of England was a son of Edgar and Ælfthryth.

## Subjects

- Æthelred II of England
- Edgar of England
- Ælfthryth
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Royal Family - Ethelred II]] identifies Ethelred as the younger son of Edgar.
- [[DNB 1885-1900 - Æthelred II]] independently identifies Æthelred II as a son of Eadgar and Ælfthryth.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the paternal relationship was identified. The sources use variant spellings Ethelred/Æthelred and Edgar/Eadgar for the same named people.

## Analysis

The Royal Household profile and the published DNB biography independently corroborate the direct relationship Edgar to Æthelred II. This supplies one further historical Wessex-context link before [[Edmund Ironside was a son of Æthelred II]].

It does not establish a connection from Edgar, Æthelred II, or their descendants to a later Stafford, Selby, Haskins, Plantagenet, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct relationship. The conclusion is confined to the named medieval people.
