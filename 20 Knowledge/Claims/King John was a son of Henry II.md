# King John was a son of Henry II

## Claim

King John of England (John Lackland) was a son of Henry II of England and Eleanor of Aquitaine.

## Subjects

- King John of England
- Henry II of England
- Eleanor of Aquitaine
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[DNB 1892 - John king of England]] identifies John as the youngest son of Henry II and Eleanor.
- [[UEA 2021 - John dominus Hibernie]] independently identifies John as the youngest son of Henry II.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

The sources vary in the precision they assign to John’s birth date. That does not affect the parentage claim.

## Analysis

The two sources independently corroborate that John was a son of Henry II. This supplies one additional direct link after [[Henry II was the son of Empress Matilda, daughter of Henry I]].

It does not establish John’s descendants, any route to Edward III, or a connection to the Staffords or a modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: an explicit historical biography and independent modern university research agree on the direct relationship.
