# The 1850 Trimble lawyer Ben Selby is strongly identified as the later Henry County attorney

## Claim

The 29-year-old lawyer Ben Selby enumerated in Trimble County in 1850 is strongly identified as the Benjamin Selby who later practiced in Henry County and lived at Eminence.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby Trimble court orders 1847-1849]] documents the Trimble lawyer immediately before the census.
- [[FamilySearch - Ben Selby 1850 Trimble census image]] records the lawyer, his age, and a wife with initials `L. S.`.
- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby and Lucy S Hunter 1844 Indiana marriage]] independently supplies the spouse name Lucy S. and a chronology compatible with the 1850 household.
- [[Armstrong 1878 - Ben Selby biographical sketch]] gives the later Eminence lawyer the same birth chronology, legal career, and 1844 Lucy S. Hunter marriage.
- [[Courier-Journal - Col Ben Selby 1883 interview]] independently identifies the later lawyer and Eminence resident.

## Analysis and limits

The combined spouse, occupation, age, marriage, and Kentucky chronology strongly identify one man across the records. No single record explicitly states that the Trimble resident moved to Henry County, so the cross-county identity remains below confirmed.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: multiple independent original records and two period accounts agree on a distinctive life pattern with no observed conflict.
