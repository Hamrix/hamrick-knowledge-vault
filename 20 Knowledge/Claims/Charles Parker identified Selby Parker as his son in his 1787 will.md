# Charles Parker identified Selby Parker as his son in his 1787 will

## Claim

Charles Parker of Worcester County, Maryland, expressly identified Selby Parker as his son in a will dated 7 June 1787 and proved 16 February 1788.

## Subjects

- Charles Parker, Worcester County testator
- Selby Parker, named son and later administrator
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1787 Worcester County will]] contains the direct phrase naming Selby as Charles's son.
- [[FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1788 Worcester administration bond]] names Selby Parker administrator of the same Charles Parker estate, with the will annexed, on the probate date.
- [[FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1788 Worcester inventory]] independently records Selby Parker acting as administrator and names John Parker and Ann Parker as nearest kin.

## Evidence against

No contradictory relationship statement was located in the reviewed Charles Parker estate records.

## Conflicts and limits

This proof concerns **Selby Parker**, a Parker-surnamed son of Charles Parker. It does not identify Selby Parker with any person named Major Selby, establish Schoolfield Parker's relationship, identify John Selby (of Ezekiel) as kin, or connect this Maryland Charles Parker with the Kentucky Charles whom Margaret Selby called her brother.

The will names a larger family group, but it does not identify Charles's parents, Ann's maiden name or parents, or every child's later identity.

## Analysis

The will supplies the exact relationship missing from the bond and inventory. The correlation between the named son and administrator is strong because the same uncommon full name appears in the same estate on the same probate date, and the bond expressly says the will was annexed.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the contemporary recorded will directly calls Selby Parker the testator's son. Confidence is limited to that relationship and the same-estate administrator correlation.
