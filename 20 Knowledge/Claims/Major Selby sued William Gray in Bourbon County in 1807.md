# Major Selby sued William Gray in Bourbon County in 1807

## Claim

Major Selby was an adult plaintiff in a Bourbon Circuit Court debt case against William Gray in August 1807.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1807 Bourbon Circuit Court case]] contains the petition, note, summons, and packet endorsements. The summons was issued at Paris, Kentucky, on 5 August 1807.

## Analysis and limits

The record is a direct Kentucky identity anchor four years before the 1811 Bourbon County purchase. The uncommon full name, same county, adult legal capacity, and short interval strongly support correlation with the 1811 purchaser, but the 1807 papers do not state residence or kinship.

The case narrows but does not close the migration gap from the Worcester man who received his share of grandmother Ann Selby's estate in September 1803. That directly documented interval is now less than four years. The case also does not identify the plaintiff with the distinct adult Major Selby taxed in Worcester County in 1783.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original petition and summons directly name Major Selby as plaintiff in Bourbon Circuit Court.
