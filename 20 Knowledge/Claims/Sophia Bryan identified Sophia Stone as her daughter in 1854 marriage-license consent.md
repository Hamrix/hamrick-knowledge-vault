# Sophia Bryan identified Sophia Stone as her daughter in 1854 marriage-license consent

## Claim

In a 4 January 1854 Shelby County, Kentucky, marriage-license consent, Sophia Bryan identified Sophia Stone as her daughter.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby and Sophia E Stone 1854 Shelby County marriage-license images]] preserves the reviewed original consent. Sophia Bryan wrote that she authorized a license for Col Ben Selby “to marry my daughter Sophia Stone.”

## Analysis and limit

This is a direct, contemporaneous maternal statement. It establishes the bounded Bryan-to-Stone relationship in this license transaction. It does not identify Sophia Stone's father, establish that Sophia Stone was the woman enumerated as Sophia Selby in Oldham County in 1860, or connect Sophia, Benjamin, or their possible descendants to Frederick Payne Selby, Major Selby, colonial Maryland Selbys, or any medieval family.
