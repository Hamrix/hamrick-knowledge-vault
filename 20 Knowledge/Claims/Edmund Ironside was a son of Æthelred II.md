# Edmund Ironside was a son of Æthelred II

## Claim

Edmund Ironside was a son of Aethelred II of England and his first wife, Aelfgifu.

## Subjects

- Edmund Ironside
- Aethelred II of England
- Aelfgifu
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[DNB 1885-1900 - Edmund Ironside]] identifies Edmund Ironside as a son of Aethelred the Unready by his first wife, Aelfgifu.
- [[1911 Encyclopaedia Britannica - Edmund Ironside]] independently identifies Edmund Ironside as a son of Aethelred II by Aelfgifu.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting Edmund's paternal relationship was identified. The source notes preserve caution about secondary details of Aelfgifu's own background, which this claim does not rely on.

## Analysis

The DNB biography and the *Encyclopaedia Britannica* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Aethelred II to Edmund Ironside. This supplies one further historical Wessex-context link before the separately documented claim [[Edward the Exile was a son of Edmund Ironside]].

It does not establish a connection from Aethelred II, Edmund Ironside, Edward the Exile, or Margaret to a later Stafford, Selby, Haskins, Plantagenet, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct relationship. The conclusion is confined to the named medieval people.
