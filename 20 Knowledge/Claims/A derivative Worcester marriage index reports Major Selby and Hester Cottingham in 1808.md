# A derivative Worcester marriage index reports Major Selby and Hester Cottingham in 1808

## Claim

A photographed DAR-compiled Worcester County marriage index reports **Hester (Hetty) Cottingham and Major Selby, 8 December 1808**, in reciprocal COTTINGHAM and SELBY entries.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - DAR Maryland marriage index Major Selby and Hester Cottingham 1808]] records the two visually reviewed cross-index entries and source metadata.
- [[Maryland State Archives - Worcester marriage licenses C2027 1795-1851]] identifies the paper county-court license series covering the reported date and supplies the exact retrieval target for the original.

## Analysis and limitations

`strong` applies to the statement that the derivative index reports this entry. The reciprocal entries improve confidence in the compiled reading but are not independent evidence. The underlying original has not been examined, so the marriage event and the parties' identities are not yet confirmed. Because the reported Worcester event follows the August 1807 Bourbon court appearance, it complicates a simple one-way Worcester-to-Kentucky migration model: it may reflect return travel, another same-named adult, or an error in the compilation.

Request the 8 December 1808 license from Worcester County Court (Marriage Licenses), 1795-1851, MSA C2027, then trace Hester Cottingham in later deeds, probate, census, and marriage records before assigning the entry to any Major Selby identity cluster.
