# Matilda of Scotland married Henry I of England

## Claim

Matilda of Scotland married Henry I of England in 1100.

## Subjects

- Matilda of Scotland
- Henry I of England
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[People of Medieval Scotland - Margaret queen of Scots]] identifies Matilda as the wife of Henry I of England.
- [[McDonald 2004 - Matilda of Scotland review]] independently states that Matilda married Henry I in 1100.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named marriage was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the marriage claim was identified. Matilda is also known as Edith; the sources treat these as alternative names for the same person.

## Analysis

The scholarly People of Medieval Scotland database and an independent academic review independently corroborate Matilda's marriage to Henry I. This records one component of the separately supported bridge claim [[Margaret of Scotland linked Edward the Exile’s line to Henry I of England’s family]].

The marriage fact does not by itself establish any particular descent through their children, nor does it establish a connection to a Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the named marriage and its date.

## Next research action

Treat parent-child relationships involving Henry I and Matilda as separate claims, each supported by its own evidence.
