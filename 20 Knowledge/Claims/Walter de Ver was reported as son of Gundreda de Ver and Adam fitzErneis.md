# Walter de Ver was reported as son of Gundreda de Ver and Adam fitzErneis

## Claim

Walter de Ver was reported as the son of Gundreda de Ver and her first husband, Adam fitzErneis of Goxhill.

## Subjects

- Walter de Ver
- Gundreda de Ver
- Adam fitzErneis of Goxhill

## Evidence supporting

- [[Wilkinson 1999 - Thirteenth-Century Women in Lincolnshire]], printed page 105 and pedigree at page 247, gives this parentage and cites *Registrum Antiquissimum* II, pages 211-212, no. 509n, and *Early Yorkshire Charters* III, page 61.

## Evidence against

No directly reviewed record disproves the relationship, but no underlying relationship-bearing record has yet been inspected in this project.

## Conflicts

[[The Genealogist Volume 20 - 1904]] presents competing placements for Walter: either a son of the second Guy de Ver or a grandson through Guy's daughter and Adam de Gousel. That article does not resolve the alternatives.

## Analysis

Wilkinson's reconstruction is specific and source-cited, but the underlying record editions have not yet been reviewed and the older pedigree literature is conflicted. The identity of Adam fitzErneis with the Adam de Gousel described by Massingberd also requires evidence rather than name or place correlation.

This is an upstream medieval hypothesis only. It does not establish a connection to the Selby surname, the earls of Oxford, or the user's documented ancestry.

**Tentative:** a scholarly reconstruction provides exact citations, but direct source review and conflict resolution are still required.
