# A Benjamin Selby was active in Adair probate matters in 1822 and 1823

## Claim

A man named Benjamin Selby bought property at an Adair County estate sale recorded in 1822 and served as a court-appointed estate-account examiner in 1823.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby Adair probate appearances 1822-1823]] preserves the two reviewed original Will Book B entries.
- [[FamilySearch - elder Benjamin Selby 1811 marriage and 1830-1840 censuses]] independently places an older same-name man in Adair County before and after these probate appearances.

## Analysis and limits

The 1822 and 1823 records cannot refer to the younger attorney born in 1826. Their name, place, and chronology are consistent with the older Adair Benjamin, but the entries do not identify his parents or state any relationship to Lingan Wilson Selby. They add local chronology and probate-network context only.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the bounded activity statements come directly from two original court records. No parent-child inference is included.
