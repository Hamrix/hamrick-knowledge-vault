# The claimed 1809 William Atkinson Selby probate has not been located

## Claim

No reviewed record currently supports the project's description of a complete 1809 Bourbon County probate for William Atkinson Selby.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Maryland State Archives - Worcester estate index 1809 William Atkinson Selby negative search]] records a scoped negative search.
- [[FamilySearch - Rhody Selby 1811 Bourbon orphan order]] is the relationship-bearing Bourbon record actually recovered.
- [[Headen family Bible transcript and Selby family notes - 1929]] labels the exact Worcester death date as tradition.

## Analysis and limits

Failure to find an index entry is not proof that no estate records survive. The correction is that the claimed probate has not been identified and must not be described as recovered.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: the correction is supported by a documented negative search and by distinguishing the 1811 orphan order from probate.
