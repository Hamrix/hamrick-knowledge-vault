# Frederic Ethelred Selby was probably a son of James Benjamin Sanford Selby

## Claim

Frederic Ethelred Selby, born about 1911–1912 in Mississippi and later resident in New Orleans, was probably a son of James Benjamin Sanford Selby and Sarah Eveline Stafford.

## Evidence supporting

- Hamrick Ethelred Selby directly identifies Frederic Ethelred Selby as his paternal grandfather and Hamrick Selby as Frederic's son.
- The uploaded GEDCOM places Frederic in family `F230` as the only recorded child of James Benjamin Sanford Selby and Sarah Eveline Stafford.
- The GEDCOM records a marriage between James and Sarah on 20 July 1910 in New Orleans, before Frederic's working birth date of 21 December 1911.
- The GEDCOM's 1930 census transcription places `Frederick E. Selby` on page 19A, ED 0209, New Orleans, as a single son. Sarah Eveline Stafford Selby is separately transcribed on the same page as a divorced household head.
- A 1940 census transcript preserved in the GEDCOM identifies Sarah as the household head, age 50, and Frederick E. Selby as her son, age 28, born in Mississippi, in New Orleans ED 36-325, sheet 3B.
- FamilySearch Family Tree profile `LLWQ-6MV` identifies a specific `Frederick Ethelred Selby (1911-1992)` candidate and may provide a source-discovery path. The collaborative profile itself is not proof.

## Evidence limits

- The 1930 and 1940 census transcriptions directly support Sarah as Frederic's mother or maternal household head, but neither inspected transcription names James as Frederic's father.
- No birth certificate, delayed birth record, baptism, obituary naming both parents, Social Security application, probate record, or other original record explicitly naming James as Frederic's father has yet been inspected.
- The actual 1930 and 1940 census images remain uninspected in the vault.

## Conflicts

- Legacy files give conflicting birth years for Frederic, including 1911, 1919, and 1930.
- The GEDCOM includes the misspelling `Ethelered`, variant forms such as Frederick/Fred, and a secondary birth entry of 1912.
- Birthplace and death-place labels vary across derivative sources.
- James and Sarah are both described as divorced in the 1930 derivative data, so household structure must be read from the actual census image rather than inferred from the family-tree assignment.

## Analysis

Sarah Eveline Stafford Selby's maternity is well supported at the derivative-transcription level by two census years. James Benjamin Sanford Selby's paternity remains probable because the GEDCOM assigns Frederic to James and Sarah's family, their recorded marriage predates Frederic's birth, and no competing father has been identified. That combination is meaningful but not equivalent to a direct parentage record.

`Frederic Ethelred Selby` is the controlled project spelling based on the user's direct clarification. Historical records must still be transcribed exactly as written.

## Confidence rationale

`moderate`: two specific census transcriptions support Sarah as Frederic's mother and the family chronology supports James as the probable father, but no inspected record yet explicitly names James as Frederic's father.
