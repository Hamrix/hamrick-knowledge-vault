# Ethelred Hamrick Stafford was son of John E. Stafford and Eveline Hamrick

## Claim

Ethelred Hamrick Stafford, husband of Fannie B. Coe and father of Sarah Eveline Stafford, was a son of John E. Stafford and Eveline M. Hamrick.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Ethelred Stafford 1880 US census image]] records the approximately 1862-born Ethelred as a son in the New Orleans household of John E. and Eveline Stafford.
- [[FamilySearch - Ethelred Stafford and Fannie Coe 1889 Orleans marriage license image]] directly names John E. Stafford and Eveline M. Hamrick as Ethelred H. Stafford's parents.
- [[FamilySearch - Ethelred Hamrick Stafford 1945 Orleans death index]] independently repeats John Stafford and Eveline Hamrick as his parents and supplies the combined name Ethelred Hamrick Stafford.
- [[FamilySearch - Ethelred Stafford 1945 Times Picayune obituary index]] names brothers whose names align with John and Leon L. in the 1880 household.

## Evidence against

No conflicting parent pair was found in the reviewed records.

## Conflicts

The 1880 index renders the son's given name as `Ethelreid`, while later records use Ethelred. The rare name, age, New Orleans locality, parent pair, sibling pattern, marriage, and death record reconcile the variation.

## Analysis

The original 1880 census and original 1889 marriage license each state the immediate relationship. The 1945 official death index independently repeats the same parent pair and supplies Hamrick as Ethelred's middle name, matching his mother's maiden surname. The obituary's sibling pattern supplies further identity-level corroboration.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two original records and an independent official death index agree on the parent pair, chronology, and locality.

## Next research action

Research John E. Stafford and Eveline M. Hamrick separately through their marriage, Louisiana and Mississippi censuses, death records, and probate. Do not infer a connection to the separately researched medieval Stafford family from the surname.
