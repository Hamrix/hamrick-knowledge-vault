# James Selby was enumerated as Frank P. and Elizabeth D. Selby's son in 1900

## Claim

In the 1900 United States census, James Selby, age 14, was enumerated as the son of Frank P. Selby and Elizabeth D. Selby in New Orleans, Louisiana.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - James Selby 1900 US census image]] is a reviewed original federal census schedule that lists the three people in one household and records James's relationship to the head as “Son.”
- [[FamilySearch - James Benjamin Sanford Selby 1886 Orleans birth index]] names a James of the correct age and locality as the son of Frederick P. Selby and Elizabeth Dolan.
- [[FamilySearch - James Benjamin Sanford Selby 1910 Orleans marriage license image]] supplies James's full name and repeats the Frederick P. Selby and Elizabeth Dolan parent pair.
- [[FamilySearch - James B Selby 1938 Orleans death index]] independently repeats the same parents and New Orleans context.

## Analysis and limit

This remains a census-record relationship. The additional vital records now strongly identify the enumerated James as James Benjamin Sanford Selby and support [[Frank P Selby in 1900 is strongly identified as Frederick Payne Selby|a strong, but not confirmed, identification of Frank P. as Frederick Payne Selby]]. The exact Frank-to-Frederick name variation remains unresolved by a direct alias statement.

It does not prove an older Selby, colonial, medieval, or royal connection.

## Next research action

Obtain the original 1886 birth record and a higher-resolution transcription of the 1910 marriage parent fields. Seek a Frederick death record, obituary, or directory sequence that uses both Frank and Frederick.
