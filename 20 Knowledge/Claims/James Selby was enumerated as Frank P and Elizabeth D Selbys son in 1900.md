# James Selby was enumerated as Frank P. and Elizabeth D. Selby's son in 1900

## Claim

In the 1900 United States census, James Selby, age 14, was enumerated as the son of Frank P. Selby and Elizabeth D. Selby in New Orleans, Louisiana.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - James Selby 1900 US census image]] is a reviewed original federal census schedule that lists the three people in one household and records James's relationship to the head as “Son.”

## Analysis and limit

This is a census-record relationship, not a conclusion that Frank P. is necessarily Frederick Payne Selby or that James is necessarily the later James Benjamin Sanford Selby. Those are plausible research correlations requiring records that resolve the first-name and middle-name differences.

It does not prove an older Selby, colonial, medieval, or royal connection.
