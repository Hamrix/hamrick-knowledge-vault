# William Selby and Mary were parents of William Selby named in the 1698 will

## Claim

The William Selby who made his will on 5 November 1698 in Prince George's County, Maryland, and his wife Mary were the parents of a son William Selby named in that will.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Selby Families of Colonial America pages 64-66 image audit]] reports that the 1698 will named wife Mary and son William.
- The same source identifies the son William as a minor in 1698.
- The source reports that William and his brother Samuel later signed as next of kin in the 1717 Joshua Cecil estate context, supporting survival into adulthood.

## Evidence limits

- The inspected source is derivative, although heavily footnoted to original Maryland records.
- The original 1698 will image has not yet been reviewed in the vault.
- This claim does not by itself prove that the son William was the later testator whose 1732 will named William Magruder Selby.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: the source gives a detailed will-based family reconstruction and cites specific original record groups. Confirmation from the original will remains the next evidentiary step.

## Next research action

Acquire and inspect the original Prince George's County will and probate copy for William Selby, together with the 1717 Cecil estate records naming William and Samuel as next of kin.
