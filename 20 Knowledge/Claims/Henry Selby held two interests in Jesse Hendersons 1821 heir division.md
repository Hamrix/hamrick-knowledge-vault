# Henry Selby held two interests in Jesse Henderson's 1821 heir division

## Claim

The December 1821 division of fifty acres among the heirs of Jesse Henderson assigned two equal 6.25-acre interests, lots 3 and 8, to Henry Selby.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Jesse Henderson 1821 division and 1823 deed to Henry Selby]] contains the visually reviewed court record and plat.
- [[FamilySearch - Henderson heir 1828 deed to Henry Selby]] and [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby 1830 Nicholas Henderson estate deeds]] trace three other numbered shares into Henry's ownership.

## Analysis and limitations

The original division directly supplies the estate context, equal acreage, names, and lot numbers. It does not say why Henry received two interests or call him Jesse's son, son-in-law, or heir in a stated degree. The words "between the heirs" place both interests within the heir division but do not identify whether Henry held either through Nelly, an earlier transfer, or another right.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the recorded division assigns both numbered interests to Henry. The legal or family basis for those interests remains unresolved.
