# Parker Selby signed Juliann Selbys 1845 Nicholas County marriage bond

## Claim

Parker Selby signed as co-obligor with John Stitt on the 13 May 1845 Nicholas County bond for Stitt's intended marriage to Juliann Selby, whom the bond identifies as a daughter of Henry Selby.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby daughter marriage records 1832-1849]] contains the original bond naming John Stitt, Juliann Selby, Henry Selby, and Parker Selby.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry and Eleanor Selby 1838 Nicholas deed to Parker Selby]] records Henry and Eleanor's earlier natural-affection conveyance to Parker.

## Analysis and limitations

The bond directly proves Parker's participation in Juliann's marriage transaction and places him in Henry's family network. It does not call Parker Henry's son, Juliann's brother, John Stitt's relative, or any other relation. The bond therefore strengthens the case for a close association but cannot supply the missing Henry-to-Parker relationship by itself.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: Parker's name and role are explicit in the original bond. The confidence does not extend to any kinship interpretation.
