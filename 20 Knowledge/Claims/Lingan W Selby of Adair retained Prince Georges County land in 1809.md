# Lingan W Selby of Adair retained Prince Georges County land in 1809

## Claim

On 16 April 1809, Lingan W. Selby of Adair County, Kentucky, authorized a Prince George's County, Maryland, attorney to convey all of Lingan's land in that Maryland county.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Lingan W Selby 1809 Adair power of attorney]] includes the original recorded instrument and the general index entry locating it in deed book A, page 363.

## Analysis and limits

The record directly links Lingan's Kentucky residence to a continuing Prince George's County land interest. It names no parent, spouse, child, or heir, and it does not prove that Hon. Benjamin Selby was Lingan's son.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original recorded instrument directly states Lingan's residence, the Maryland county, the land interest, and the authority to convey it.
