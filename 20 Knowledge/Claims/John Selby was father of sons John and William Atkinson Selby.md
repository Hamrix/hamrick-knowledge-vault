# John Selby was father of sons John and William Atkinson Selby

## Claim

The John Selby named in John Atkinson's 1779 Worcester County will was the father of two sons named John Selby and William Atkinson Selby.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1779 Worcester County will images]] expressly describes residuary legatees John and William Atkinson Selby as John Selby's two sons.
- [[FamilySearch - Angelo Atkinson 1766 Worcester County will images]] independently identifies William Atkinson Selby as a son of John Selby and as Angelo Atkinson's grandson.

## Analysis and limits

The two wills independently prove John Selby's fatherhood of William Atkinson Selby. The 1779 will additionally proves that John Selby had a son named John in the record's family group.

The records do not prove that this son John was the John Selby Jr. whose administration began in 1782, the father of the minor Major Selby named in 1785, or the John Selby whose will was proved in 1790. Those remain separate identity questions.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the 1779 will uses explicit father-son wording for both sons, and the 1766 will independently confirms the father-William relationship.
