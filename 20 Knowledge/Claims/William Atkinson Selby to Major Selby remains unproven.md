# William Atkinson Selby to Major Selby remains unproven

## Claim status

No inspected relationship-bearing record names Major Selby as a son of William Atkinson Selby.

## Evidence considered

- [[FamilySearch - Angelo Atkinson 1766 Worcester County will images]] proves William's earlier relationship to John Selby and Angelo Atkinson but does not name Major.
- [[FamilySearch - Rhody Selby 1811 Bourbon orphan order]] proves Rhody as William's child but does not name Major.
- [[FamilySearch - William Antrobus and Polly Selby 1825 Nicholas marriage file]] proves Major as Polly's father and does not name William.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1811 Bourbon deed and index]] proves an adult Major in Bourbon County but no kinship.

## Analysis

The inherited link remains a hypothesis. It must not be published as a fact or used to attach downstream pedigrees.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: multiple relevant originals have been reviewed without a relationship statement, and one previously used attachment proves a different relationship. This is a research-status conclusion, not proof that William could not have been Major's father.
