# James Benjamin Sanford Selby was probably a son of Frederick Payne Selby

## Claim

James Benjamin Sanford Selby, born in 1886 in Kentucky and later resident in New Orleans, was probably a son of Frederick Payne Selby and Mary C. O'Brien.

## Evidence supporting

- The uploaded GEDCOM places James in family `F271` as a child of Frederick Payne Selby and Mary C. O'Brien.
- The GEDCOM cites the 1900 United States Federal Census for James at New Orleans Ward 13, page 3, enumeration district 0125, with his relationship recorded as `Son`.
- Frederick Payne Selby is associated in the GEDCOM with the same 1900 household as head.

## Evidence against

- No contrary parentage record has been identified in the materials inspected.

## Conflicts

- The actual 1900 census image has not yet been inspected in the vault.

## Analysis
