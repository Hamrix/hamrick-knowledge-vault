# Sophia E. Stone is strongly identified as Docia Stone, wife of Benjamin Selby

## Claim

Sophia E. Stone, named in Benjamin Selby's 1854 marriage-license papers and as his wife in 1872, is strongly identified as Docia Stone, named as Benjamin's wife and the mother of Payne or Frederick Payne and Robert C. Selby in later records.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby and Sophia E Stone 1854 Shelby County marriage-license images]] records a license for Benjamin Selby and Sophia E. Stone and identifies Sophia Stone as Sophia Bryan's daughter.
- [[FamilySearch - Benjn Selby 1860 US census image]] places Benjamin, age 35, and Sophia, age 23, in one Oldham County household.
- [[FamilySearch - Ben and Sophia E Selby 1872 Henry County deed]] directly identifies Ben Selby and wife Sophia E. Selby in Henry County.
- [[FamilySearch - Roberts Selby 1874 Henry County birth index]] names Benjamin Selby and Docie Stone as parents of Roberts Selby.
- [[FamilySearch - Ben Selby 1880 US census image]] records Ben, age 53, with wife Docia, age 42, and sons Robert and Payne in Henry County.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Payne Selby 1884 Orleans marriage license image]] names Benjamin Selby and Docia Stone as Frederick Payne Selby's parents.
- [[FamilySearch - Robert C Selby 1938 Kentucky death certificate image]] again names Benjamin Selby and Docie or Docia Stone as Robert's parents.

## Evidence against

No reviewed record uses Sophia and Docia together or expressly calls one an alias for the other.

## Conflicts

The given names Sophia and Docia differ. The 1854 papers also establish only a license and consent, not a reviewed minister's return. The 1860 census has no relationship column, and the 1874 birth evidence is an index whose original image was unavailable.

## Analysis

The paired spouse, compatible age progression, move from Oldham to Henry County, 1872 marital deed, sons Robert and Payne, and repeated Stone maiden name create a continuous family cluster. The evidence is strong enough to treat Sophia and Docia as the leading same-person conclusion while keeping the name discrepancy visible.

If this identity is correct, [[Sophia Bryan identified Sophia Stone as her daughter in 1854 marriage-license consent]] provides a direct maternal statement one generation above Docia or Sophia. That upstream extension remains strong rather than confirmed because it depends on the unresolved Sophia-to-Docia identity.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: multiple original records and one vital-record index create a coherent 1854-1938 correlation. No direct alias statement has been found.

## Next research action

Seek Sophia or Docia's death, obituary, probate, church, deed, or later marriage record, especially a record naming Sophia Bryan or using both given names. Locate a minister's return for the 1854 license.
