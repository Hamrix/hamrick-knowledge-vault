# Selby has a documented English and Scottish habitational surname origin

## Claim

*Selby* has a documented English and Scottish habitational surname origin from Selby, Yorkshire. The place-name includes the Scandinavian place-name element *bý* (“farmstead, village”).

## Subjects

- Selby surname
- Selby, Yorkshire
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Hanks - Dictionary of American Family Names Selby]] identifies an English (Nottinghamshire) and Scottish habitational origin from Selby, Yorkshire, and explains the place-name elements.
- [[Neal 2023 - Anglo-Scandinavian by-names]] independently situates Selby, Yorkshire, among West Riding *bý(r)*-names first attested in the eleventh century.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the documented habitational origin was found.

## Conflicts

The dictionary also notes that, in an American context, *Selby* can be an Americanized form of a similar-sounding Jewish surname. Accordingly, it is not valid to assume that every Selby line derives from the Yorkshire place-name. Variations in the earliest first element’s analysis do not change the evidence for the Scandinavian *-by* element.

## Analysis

The sources support a population-level conclusion about one established origin of the surname and the medieval place-name context. They do not provide evidence for the birthplace, parentage, social status, or descent of any named Selby in this project.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: the conclusion combines a specialist surname dictionary with independent university research on the relevant place-name type. The claim is intentionally limited to documented surname origin, not universal ancestry.
