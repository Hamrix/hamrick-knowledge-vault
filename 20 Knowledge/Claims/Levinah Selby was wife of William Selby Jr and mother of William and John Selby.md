# Levinah Selby was wife of William Selby Jr and mother of William and John Selby

## Claim

Levinah Selby was the wife of William Selby, the younger, and the mother of William Selby and John Selby.

## Subjects

- Levinah Selby
- William Selby, the younger
- William Selby, son of Levinah
- John Selby, son of Levinah
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Maryland State Archives - John Atkinson 1750 will abstract]] reports that John Atkinson's will calls Levinah the wife of William Selby, the younger, and later calls William and John Selby Levinah's sons.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the stated relationships was located in this review.

## Conflicts

The abstract does not give ages, residence, or enough distinguishing information to attach either son automatically to any later man with the same name.

## Analysis

The official abstract directly reports the wife and mother-child terms. It establishes a bounded Selby–Atkinson family group without identifying any member as Major Selby or proving a link to the 1701, 1725, or 1758 Thomas Selby records.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: an official archive abstract expressly reports the relevant relationships. This should be upgraded to `confirmed` only after checking the underlying manuscript will at Liber 27, folio 527.

## Next research action

Obtain and review the image of Liber 27, folio 527. Then seek probate, land, and estate records that distinguish these William and John Selbys from the later Worcester County men of the same names before testing any connection to Major Selby.
