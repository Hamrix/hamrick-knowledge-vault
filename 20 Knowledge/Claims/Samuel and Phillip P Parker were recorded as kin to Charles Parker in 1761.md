# Samuel and Phillip P. Parker were recorded as kin to Charles Parker in 1761

## Claim

Charles Parker's Worcester County inventory, dated 12 March 1761, names Samuel Parker and Phillip P. Parker under the heading "Kin."

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1761 Worcester inventory]] records the visually reviewed original inventory and its legally separated participant headings.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the literal kin entries was located.

## Conflicts
