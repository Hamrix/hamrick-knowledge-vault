# A marriage license was issued for Benjamin Selby and Sophia E Stone in 1854

## Claim

Shelby County, Kentucky, issued a marriage license on 5 January 1854 authorizing a minister to join Benjamin Selby and Sophia E. Stone in marriage.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby and Sophia E Stone 1854 Shelby County marriage-license images]] preserves the reviewed original license, dated 5 January 1854, naming Benjamin Selby and Sophia E. Stone.
- The preceding original consent, dated 4 January 1854, names “Col Ben Selby” and Sophia Stone and was signed by Sophia's mother, Sophia Bryan.

## Analysis and limit

The original papers prove the consent and issuance of a license. The reviewed images do not show a minister's return, so they do not by themselves prove that a marriage ceremony occurred. They also do not prove that this Benjamin Selby is the Benjamin who was enumerated with Docia and Payne Selby in 1880 or was reported as Frederick Payne Selby's father in 1884. The same-name, Kentucky-place, and chronological similarities are research leads only.

## Next research action

Find a minister's return, later civil record, probate file, or census sequence that explicitly correlates this Benjamin Selby with the later Ben/Benjamin Selby records before merging the identities.
