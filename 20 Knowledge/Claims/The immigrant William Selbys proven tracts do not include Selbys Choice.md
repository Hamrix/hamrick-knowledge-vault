# The immigrant William Selbys proven tracts do not include Selbys Choice

## Claim

The currently inspected derivative reconstruction of immigrant William Selby's Maryland landholdings identifies *Good Luck*, *Packs Meadow*, *Leith*, *Groom's Lot*, *Twogood*, *Twyford*, and *The Farme*, but does not identify a tract called *Selby's Choice* among his proven holdings or testamentary devises.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Selby Families of Colonial America pages 64-66 image audit]] transcribes the tract list and will distributions reported on pages 64-66.
- No reference to *Selby's Choice* appears in the inspected pages.

## Evidence limits

This is a bounded negative claim about the source inspected. It does not prove that no later Selby acquired, patented, inherited, or used a tract called *Selby's Choice*.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: the inspected source provides a detailed land inventory and testamentary distribution list for this William. The claim is limited to what that reconstruction supports.

## Next research action

Search Maryland patent, rent-roll, survey, and deed records for the first documented appearance of *Selby's Choice* and identify the exact Selby owner and acquisition path.
