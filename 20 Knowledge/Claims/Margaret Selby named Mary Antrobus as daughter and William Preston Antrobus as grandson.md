# Margaret Selby named Mary Antrobus as daughter and William Preston Antrobus as grandson

## Claim

Margaret Selby's 1836 will directly identifies Mary Antrobus as her daughter and William Preston Antrobus as her grandson.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Margaret Selby 1836 will and estate settlements]] contains the original recorded will.
- [[FamilySearch - William Antrobus and Polly Selby 1825 Nicholas marriage file]] and [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1833 Nicholas County will and 1835 inventory]] establish the related William Antrobus-Polly Selby family context.

## Analysis and limitations

The relationships stated in Margaret's will are direct. Correlating Mary Antrobus with Major's daughter Polly Anterbus is strong because the surname, marriage, county, and family context agree and Polly commonly serves as a familiar form of Mary. The will does not explicitly state that Mary was formerly Polly Selby, so that name correlation is not included in the confirmed claim.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: Margaret directly calls Mary her daughter and William Preston her grandson.
