# Lingan Wilson Selby and Drucilla Fowler parentage of Hon Benjamin Selby remains unproven

## Claim

Lingan Wilson Selby and Drucilla Fowler are the leading proposed parents of Hon. Benjamin Selby of Adair County, Kentucky, but the vault does not yet contain an inspected record explicitly or inferentially proving that parent-child relationship.

## Evidence supporting the hypothesis

- Hon. Benjamin Selby was reported as Maryland-born and later active in the same Adair County, Kentucky, Selby migration network associated with Lingan Wilson Selby and Drucilla Fowler.
- Early Adair County records place Benjamin Selby in a close civic, legal, land, and Beard-family network with other Selby-associated people.
- Later compiled family reconstructions associate Lingan and Drucilla with children bearing Fowler-derived names, including Zachariah Fowler Selby and Zephaniah Fowler Selby.
- Lingan's estate and probate sequence in Adair County is a plausible record group in which Benjamin may appear as heir, administrator, purchaser, creditor, or family representative.

## Evidence limitations

- No reviewed will, distribution, heir deed, family Bible, obituary, legislative memorial, church register, or probate settlement currently calls Hon. Benjamin a son of Lingan and Drucilla.
- Shared geography, recurring surnames, naming patterns, and transactions with the Beard family establish a network but do not distinguish son, nephew, cousin, or unrelated associate.
- Reported dates for Lingan's death and Hon. Benjamin's birth vary in online reconstructions and must not be normalized without original records.

## Required proof

Prioritize:

1. Lingan W. Selby's complete Adair County probate sequence, including inventory, sale, administrator bond, settlements, distributions, guardianships, and court orders;
2. deeds disposing of Lingan's land by named heirs;
3. Hon. Benjamin Selby's 1845 estate, obituary, legislative memorial, and state-auditor records;
4. Prince George's County, Maryland, church, probate, deed, and family-Bible material naming Lingan and Drucilla's children.

## Publication-safe conclusion

Hon. Benjamin can be placed strongly within the Maryland-to-Adair Selby network. His assignment as a son of Lingan Wilson Selby and Drucilla Fowler remains a priority hypothesis, not a proved generation.
