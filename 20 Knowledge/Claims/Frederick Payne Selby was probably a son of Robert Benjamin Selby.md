# Frederick Payne Selby was probably a son of Robert Benjamin Selby

## Claim

Frederick Payne Selby, born in 1861 in Kentucky, was probably a son of Robert Benjamin Selby and Eliza Payne.

## Evidence supporting

- The uploaded GEDCOM places Frederick Payne Selby in family `F358` as a child of Robert Benjamin Selby and Eliza Payne.
- The GEDCOM cites the 1880 United States Federal Census for Frederick Payne at Eminence, Henry County, Kentucky, page 28C, enumeration district 092, with his relationship recorded as `Son`.
- Robert Benjamin Selby is associated in the GEDCOM with the same household as self/head.
- The GEDCOM also cites the 1870 census for both men in Port Royal, Henry County, Kentucky, providing earlier co-residence context.

## Evidence against

- No contrary parentage record has been identified in the materials inspected.

## Conflicts

- The 1870 census did not record relationship to household head, so it cannot independently prove parentage.
- The actual 1880 and 1870 census images have not yet been inspected in the vault.

## Analysis

The 1880 relationship field is potentially direct evidence of the claimed parentage, while the 1870 co-residence citation adds chronological support. Because both citations are currently available only through the GEDCOM transcription, the relationship remains provisional.

## Confidence rationale

`moderate`: a specific 1880 census relationship field and consistent earlier co-residence support the relationship, but the original images and an independent civil, church, probate, or obituary source have not yet been inspected.
