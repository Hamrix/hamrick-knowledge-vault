# Frederick Payne Selby was probably a son of Robert Benjamin Selby

## Claim

Frederick Payne “Frank” Selby, born in Kentucky, was probably a son of **Robert Benjamin Selby**, with the father recorded in Frederick’s 1884 marriage license as **Benjamin Selby**.

Frederick’s 1884 Orleans Parish marriage-license image names his parents as **Benjamin Selby** and **Docia Stone**. This materially strengthens the direct-line parentage claim and withdraws the earlier unsupported maternal wording “Eliza Payne.”

The remaining unresolved point is whether the marriage record’s **Benjamin Selby** is the same person already tracked in the vault as **Robert Benjamin Selby**. The user has identified them as the same person, and the sibling naming pattern including Robert Carr Selby supports the identity-alias hypothesis, but the alias/correlation still needs the 1870/1880 household images, Robert’s death/succession record, or another corroborating source.

## Analysis

The 1884 Orleans Parish marriage-license image is now the strongest source in the vault for Frederick Payne Selby’s parentage because it is a civil record associated with Frederick’s own marriage and names his parents directly. It supports **Benjamin Selby** as father and **Docia Stone** as mother.

For the maternal side, the active conclusion can now move beyond family-confirmed anchor: **Docia Stone is documented as the mother named in Frederick Payne Selby’s 1884 marriage-license record**. Further records are still needed to prove Docia’s own identity, parents, birth details, and later life.

For the paternal side, the source directly names **Benjamin Selby**. The vault’s existing **Robert Benjamin Selby** label is plausible and supported by user testimony and family naming context, but it remains an identity-correlation question until the 1870/1880 census images or Robert’s 1902 death/succession records connect the same household/person.
