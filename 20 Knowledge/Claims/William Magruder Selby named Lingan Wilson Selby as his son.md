# William Magruder Selby named Lingan Wilson Selby as his son

## Claim

William Magruder Selby's will names Lingan Wilson Selby as his son.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - William Magruder Selby 1773 will]] is the original Prince George's County will. A county will abstract corroborates the difficult handwritten name reading.
- [[The Adair Lingan W Selby is strongly identified as William Magruder Selbys son]] separately evaluates the cross-state identity of William's named son with the later Adair County man.

## Analysis and limits

The will directly proves this single father-son relationship. The 1809 Adair power of attorney now strongly identifies William's named son with the Lingan W. Selby later documented in Adair County, but no reviewed record connects that Lingan to Hon. Benjamin Selby, the younger attorney, Frederick Payne Selby, or the user's private modern line.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the testator directly identifies Lingan as a son in his original will.
