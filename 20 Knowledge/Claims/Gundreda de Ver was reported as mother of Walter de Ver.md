# Gundreda de Ver was reported as mother of Walter de Ver

## Claim

Gundreda de Ver was reported as the mother of Walter de Ver.

## Subjects

- Walter de Ver
- Gundreda de Ver

## Evidence supporting

- [[Wilkinson 1999 - Thirteenth-Century Women in Lincolnshire]], printed page 105 and pedigree at page 247, gives this parentage and cites *Registrum Antiquissimum* II, pages 211-212, no. 509n, and *Early Yorkshire Charters* III, page 61.
- [[Loyd and Stenton 1950 - Sir Christopher Hattons Book of Seals entry 73]] independently proves that Adam of Goxhill was Walter's father, but it does not name Walter's mother.

## Evidence against

No directly reviewed record disproves the relationship, but no underlying relationship-bearing record has yet been inspected in this project.

## Conflicts

[[The Genealogist Volume 20 - 1904]] presents competing placements for Walter: either a son of the second Guy de Ver or a grandson through Guy's daughter and Adam de Gousel. The charter transcription now resolves Adam's paternity but does not name the daughter or prove that she was Gundreda.

## Analysis

Wilkinson's maternal reconstruction is specific and source-cited, but the underlying record editions have not yet been reviewed and the older pedigree literature is conflicted. The separately proved father is Adam of Goxhill; that does not by itself prove the identity of Walter's mother.

This is an upstream medieval hypothesis only. It does not establish a connection to the Selby surname, the earls of Oxford, or the user's documented ancestry.

## Confidence rationale

**Tentative:** a scholarly reconstruction provides exact citations, but direct source review and conflict resolution are still required.
