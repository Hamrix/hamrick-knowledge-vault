# William Atkinson Selby wartime loyalty and Townsend marriage

## Supported findings

- The Maryland State Archives biographical dictionary states that William Atkinson Selby, born about 1757 and a son of John Selby and Mary Atkinson, was indicted by the Worcester County Court in June 1779 for "going to the enemy."
- The reviewed 1766 will of Angelo Atkinson directly identifies William Atkinson Selby as Angelo's grandson and as a son of John Selby. It does not name William's mother or establish the biographical dictionary's identification of her as Mary Atkinson.
- November 1775 depositions describe Levin Townsend's contact with a British man-of-war at Norfolk, ammunition and secrecy oaths, and approximately 60 to 70 people gathered at William Barkley Townsend's house. Several depositions were taken before John Selby.
- A compiled Selby family paper reports that William Atkinson Selby married Sarah White Townsend on 28 February 1782 and identifies Barkley Townsend Selby, born 21 November 1784, as their son.
- [[FamilySearch - Rhody Selby 1811 Bourbon orphan order]] directly identifies Rhody as William's infant orphan and proves that William was deceased before 7 January 1811.
- [[FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1780 estate order and appeal bond]] places John Selby and William Atkinson Selby together among seven residuary legatees of John Atkinson, but does not state each relationship.
- [[FamilySearch - Scarborough Parker estate account images]] records an allowed Worcester County estate payment to William Atkinson Selby after the Parker inventory was exhibited in 1789.

## Evidence boundary

The Townsend marriage and Barkley child currently rely on a compiled genealogical paper rather than a reviewed original marriage return, Bible, probate file, or civil record. Rhody's relationship is direct from an original. The sources reviewed do not establish that the marriage was intended as political reconciliation, that William received a pardon, or that his brother denounced him. They also do not name Major Selby as William's child. See [[William Atkinson Selby to Major Selby remains unproven]].
