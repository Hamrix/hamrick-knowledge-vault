# Major Selby was enumerated in Somerset County, Maryland, in 1820

## Claim

The 1820 federal census includes an entry for Major Selby in Election District 1, Somerset County, Maryland.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1820 US census image]] is the reviewed original schedule and includes the handwritten Major Selby entry on page 347.

## Analysis and limit

The 1820 census records age-and-sex columns, not the names or relationships of household members. This entry does not identify Major's spouse, children, or parents. It also cannot by itself identify the Somerset entry with the differently located Major Selby records of 1840-1860, attach Benjamin Selby to the household, or establish a link to the 1783 Worcester County Major Selby assessment.

## Next research action

Seek a deed, probate order, marriage return, or other record that names this Major Selby's associates and explicitly connects him to a later dated record.
