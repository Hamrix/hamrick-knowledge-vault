# The 1825 Polly Selby file does not prove William Atkinson Selby was her father

## Claim tested

The William Antrobus–Polly Selby marriage file proves that Polly was a daughter of William Atkinson Selby.

## Evidence against

- [[FamilySearch - William Antrobus and Polly Selby 1825 Nicholas marriage file]] expressly identifies Major Selby—not William—as Polly's father.
- [[FamilySearch - Rhody Selby 1811 Bourbon orphan order]] records W. A. Selby as deceased by 7 January 1811.

## Analysis

The online attachment of this file to William's compiled profile creates a false generational bridge. The record supports Major → Polly only.

## Confidence rationale

`contradicted`: the original record says something materially different from the tested proposition.
