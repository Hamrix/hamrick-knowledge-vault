# Major Selby appeared in the 1812 and 1813 Bourbon County tax lists

## Claim

Original Bourbon County tax lists contain Major Selby entries in both 1812 and 1813, extending his consecutive reviewed tax presence from 1811 through 1813.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1812 and 1813 Bourbon tax lists]] preserves the direct image citations and separately reviewed annual section covers.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1811 Bourbon tax list]] establishes the preceding year's appearance.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1812 Bourbon ejectment and 1815 survey]] independently places Major among Bourbon land occupants served in November 1812.

## Limitations

The lists establish name, county, and years but no relationship, former residence, or later residence. They strengthen continuity within the adult Bourbon cluster; they do not prove identity with the Worcester ward or the Nicholas County household.

`confirmed`: the name entries and corresponding year covers are visible on the reviewed original images.
