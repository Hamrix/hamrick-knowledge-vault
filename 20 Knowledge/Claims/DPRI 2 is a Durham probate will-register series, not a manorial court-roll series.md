# DPRI 2 is a Durham probate will-register series, not a manorial court-roll series

## Claim

Durham University identifies series DPRI/2 as registers of wills proved in the diocese of Durham. It is not catalogued as a manorial court-roll series.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Durham University - DPRI probate record series]] is the official collection description and states that DPRI/2 contains registered copies of wills.

## Evidence against

The legacy materials repeatedly cite `DPRI/2/23` for an alleged 1703 manorial court proceeding styled Selby v Ogle, but no archive image, complete catalogue result, or archive correspondence supporting that description has been supplied.

## Analysis

The reference is structurally inconsistent with the claimed document type. Before any historical conclusion can be drawn, the exact archival reference must be corrected and the original image or an official archive response must be obtained.

This finding does not prove that no Selby or Ogle probate entry appears in a DPRI/2 volume. It establishes that the cited series is probate, not a manorial court-roll series, and therefore cannot be represented as an authenticated manorial reference without further evidence.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: Durham University's official catalogue explicitly describes the series.

## Public wording

Use: “The cited Durham reference belongs to a probate will-register series and has not been authenticated as a manorial court record.”

Do not use: “Durham's certified 1703 manorial roll proves hereditary possession.”
