# Edward I was a son of Henry III

## Claim

Edward I of England was a son of Henry III and Eleanor of Provence.

## Subjects

- Edward I of England
- Henry III of England
- Eleanor of Provence
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Westminster Abbey - Edward I and Eleanor of Castile]] identifies Edward as the eldest child of Henry III and Eleanor of Provence.
- [[DNB 1895 - Edward I]] independently identifies Edward as the eldest son of Henry III and Eleanor of Provence.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified.

## Analysis

The Westminster Abbey profile and the published *Dictionary of National Biography* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Henry III to Edward I. Combined with [[Henry III was a son of King John]], this extends the verified royal context by one generation.

It does not establish Edward I's descendants, a route to Edward III, or any connection to a Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct relationship.
