# The underage Angelo Atkinson living in 1773 was not the 1766 testator

## Claim tested

The Angelo Atkinson under age twenty-one and living in 1773 was the same man whose will was proved in 1766.

## Evidence against

- [[FamilySearch - Joshua Atkinson 1773 Worcester will images]] identifies a living, underage Angelo in 1773.
- [[FamilySearch - Angelo Atkinson 1766 Worcester County will images]] documents the older testator as deceased by probate of his will in 1766.

## Analysis

The chronologies cannot belong to one person. At least two separate Angelo Atkinson identity records are required.

## Confidence rationale

`contradicted`: the life statuses and dates are mutually exclusive.
