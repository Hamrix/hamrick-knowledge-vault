# Congress removed political disabilities from a Benjamin Selby of Henry County in 1871

## Claim

Private act chapter 171, approved 3 March 1871, removed Fourteenth Amendment legal and political disabilities from a person named Benjamin Selby of Henry County, Kentucky.

## Evidence supporting

- [[United States Statutes at Large - Benjamin Selby 1871 political disabilities]] names Benjamin Selby and Henry County in the enacted list.

## Analysis and limits

The exact statutory event is confirmed. The same name, county, legal occupation, and public chronology strongly favor identification with the target Benjamin Selby, but that identity inference is not part of this narrower claim. The act does not prove military service or parentage.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the official enacted statute directly states the name, county, and relief granted.
