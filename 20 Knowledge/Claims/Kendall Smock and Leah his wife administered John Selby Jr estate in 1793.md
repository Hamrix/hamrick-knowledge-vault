# Kendal Smock and Leah were married by 1783 while Leah administered the John Selby Jr estate

## Claim

On 18 October 1783, Kendal Smock and Leah his wife appeared to return the inventory of John Selby; the oath identifies Leah as administratrix. By 13 March 1793, the couple were jointly described as accountants administering the estate of John Selby Junior.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - John Selby of Captain John 1782 Worcester inventory]] names Kendal Smock and Leah his wife in the 18 October 1783 return and identifies Leah as administratrix.
- [[FamilySearch - John Selby Jr 1793 Worcester estate account images]] expressly identifies Kendal Smock and Leah his wife as administrators in the heading of the estate's third account.
- [[Maryland State Archives - John Selby Jr 1782 Worcester estate index]] identifies Leah Selby as administrator when the estate bond was filed on 20 October 1782.

## Analysis and limits

The records establish that Leah was Kendal's wife by October 1783, ten years earlier than the previously reviewed third account, and establish a continuous administrative identity from Leah Selby in 1782 to Leah, wife of Kendal Smock. Neither record expressly calls Leah John Selby's widow, identifies her as Major's mother, or names a child. `Kendal` is the manuscript spelling; `Kendall` is retained only in this note's filename for link stability.

This improves the search route through Kendal and Leah's land and court records. The separate 1790 will and 1795 grandfather's final account now prove Major's father as John; the exact correlation to the 1782 junior estate rests on the combined estate chronology and identifying designation, not on this marriage evidence alone.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original estate account expressly states the spouses' names and their administrative role.
