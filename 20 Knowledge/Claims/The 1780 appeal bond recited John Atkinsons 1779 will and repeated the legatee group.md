# The 1780 appeal bond recited John Atkinson's 1779 will and repeated the legatee group

## Claim

The appeal bond dated 25 August 1780 recited that John Atkinson made a will on 21 March 1779 and bound Levin Blake and George Layfield to the same residuary legatees named in the estate dispute.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1780 estate order and appeal bond]] preserves the original bond.

## Analysis and limits

The bond also identifies Mary, John Atkinson's widow and executrix, as the later wife of Levin Blake. It does not itself state the legatees' relationships.

`confirmed`: the reviewed original bond directly supplies the date, parties, and legal context.
