# Major Selbey was enumerated in Wayne Township Hamilton County in 1840

## Claim

The 1840 federal census includes Major Selbey as a household head in Wayne Township, Hamilton County, Indiana.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selbey 1840 US census image]] preserves the reviewed original schedule and its linked indexed entry for Major Selbey, page 160.

## Analysis and limit

This record establishes a named 1840 census entry in the same township as later Hamilton County Selby records. Because the schedule names only a household head and contains no birthplace or relationship column, it does not identify this Major Selbey with any later Major Selby record or prove any family connection.

## Next research action

Seek a deed, probate, tax, or court record from Hamilton County that gives this man's residence, associates, spouse, heirs, or children. Use such a record to test identity across the 1840, 1850, 1860, and 1918 evidence without assuming continuity from the name alone.
