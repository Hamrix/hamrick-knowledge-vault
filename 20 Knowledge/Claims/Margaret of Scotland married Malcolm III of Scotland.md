# Margaret of Scotland married Malcolm III of Scotland

## Claim

Margaret, queen of Scots, married Malcolm III, king of Scots.

## Subjects

- Margaret, queen of Scots
- Malcolm III, king of Scots
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[People of Medieval Scotland - Margaret queen of Scots]] records Margaret's marriage to Malcolm III.
- [[DNB 1885-1900 - Margaret queen of Scots]] independently notes that Margaret married Malcolm III of Scotland.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named marriage was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the marriage claim was identified. This note does not depend on a precise marriage date, which varies in historical discussion.

## Analysis

The scholarly People of Medieval Scotland database and the published *Dictionary of National Biography* entry independently corroborate Margaret's marriage to Malcolm III. This records one component of the separately supported bridge claim [[Margaret of Scotland linked Edward the Exile’s line to Henry I of England’s family]].

The marriage fact does not by itself establish any descent through their children, nor a connection to a Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the named marriage. The conclusion deliberately excludes a disputed or imprecise marriage date and later descent.
