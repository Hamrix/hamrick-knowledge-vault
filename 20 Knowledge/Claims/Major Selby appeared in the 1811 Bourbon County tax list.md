# Major Selby appeared in the 1811 Bourbon County tax list

## Claim

An original Bourbon County tax list for 1811 contains a handwritten entry for Major Selby.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1811 Bourbon tax list]] preserves the direct image citation and the separately reviewed 1811 volume cover.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1812 and 1813 Bourbon tax lists]] continues the same uncommon name in the next two annual lists.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1811 Bourbon deed and index]] independently describes Major Selby as of Bourbon County on 27 June 1811.

## Limitations

The tax entry establishes name, county, and year but no relationship or prior residence. It strongly correlates with the same-year purchaser, while identity with the Worcester ward, the Nicholas County household, or the later Indiana Major remains a separate proof question.
