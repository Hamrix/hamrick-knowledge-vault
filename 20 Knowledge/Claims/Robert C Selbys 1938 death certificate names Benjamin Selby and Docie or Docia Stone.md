# Robert C Selby's 1938 death certificate names Benjamin Selby and Docie or Docia Stone

## Claim

Robert C. Selby's original Kentucky death certificate reports Benjamin Selby as his father and Docie/Docia Stone as his mother.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Robert C Selby 1938 Kentucky death certificate image]] was directly re-reviewed at original resolution and hashed.

## Analysis and limits

The original verifies what the record states. The parent information was supplied by Robert's wife, Sallie Selby, decades after his birth, so biological accuracy remains informant-supplied evidence. The record does not explicitly equate Docie/Docia with Sophia E. Stone.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the reviewed original directly contains the parental fields; confidence applies to the record statement, not independent biological proof.
