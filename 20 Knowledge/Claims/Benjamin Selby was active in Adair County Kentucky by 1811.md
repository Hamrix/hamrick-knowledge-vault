# Benjamin Selby was active in Adair County Kentucky by 1811

## Claim

An adult Benjamin Selby was established in Adair County, Kentucky, by 1811 and remained active in county legal and civic affairs through at least 1823.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Benjamin Selby of Adair County identity reconstruction]] records multiple deed-index entries involving Benjamin Selby in 1811-1818.
- Published county-history transcriptions identify Benjamin Selby as a deputy sheriff beginning in 1812.
- An 1822 county-court transcription lists Benjamin Selby among Columbia's first fire company.
- An 1823 court abstract names Benjamin Selby as one of three commissioners appointed to convey title following Matthew McDowell's death.
- An 1820 marriage transcription records Benjamin Selby as an attesting witness.

## Evidence against

No contrary evidence affecting the existence of this adult Adair County resident has been identified.

## Analysis
