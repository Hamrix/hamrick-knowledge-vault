# Henry Selby was named as father of four daughters in Nicholas County marriage records

## Claim

Original Nicholas County marriage records identify Henry Selby as father of Maria Selby in 1832, Catharine Selby in 1842, Juliann Selby in 1845, and Dulcina Selby in 1849.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby daughter marriage records 1832-1849]] contains four original bonds or certificates that explicitly call each woman Henry's daughter.
- [[FamilySearch - Margaret Selby 1836 will and estate settlements]] independently calls Kitty and Duland Selby granddaughters of Margaret and daughters of Henry Selby.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry and Eleanor Selby 1850 Nicholas census]] places Henry and Eleanor in Nicholas County in 1850.

## Analysis and limitations

The originals directly state all four father-daughter relationships. Margaret's will adds an independent family statement for Kitty and Duland. Kitty is a strong familiar-form match for Catharine, and Duland is strongly compatible with Dulcina, but the spellings are not identical. No reviewed record names the daughters' mother. Parker Selby's signature on Juliann's bond is a proved association, not a stated relationship.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: four original records explicitly state that Henry was the women's father.
