# An adult Major Selby purchased Bourbon County land in 1811

## Claim

An adult Major Selby of Bourbon County purchased about 100 acres there on 27 June 1811 and witnessed a related Dudley-heirs deed proved 15 July 1811.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1811 Bourbon deed and index]] preserves the purchase, grantor index, and related deed bearing Major's marked witness attestation.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1807 Bourbon Circuit Court case]] places an adult of the same uncommon name in a Bourbon County court proceeding four years earlier.
- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1811 Bourbon tax list]] independently places Major in the county during the same year.

## Analysis and limits

This anchors an adult Bourbon County identity. The uncommon full name, same county, adult legal capacity, and four-year interval strongly correlate the 1807 plaintiff and 1811 purchaser as one person, while the tax list independently corroborates his 1811 presence. The witness act places him inside the same Dudley-estate land network but supplies no kinship. None identifies his parents or establishes that he was the 1785 Worcester ward, the single verified 1820 Nicholas household head, or the 1825 father of Polly. The 1783 Worcester taxpayer remains a necessarily separate person from the 1785 child.
