# Lingan Wilson Selby as father of Hon Benjamin Selby remains unproven

## Claim

The proposition that **Lingan Wilson Selby** and **Drucilla/Drusilla Fowler** were the parents of the elder **Hon. Benjamin Selby** associated with Adair County, Kentucky, is presently an unproven research hypothesis and must not be incorporated into the canonical lineage as fact.

## Subjects

- Lingan Wilson Selby
- Drucilla / Drusilla Fowler
- Hon. Benjamin Selby
- Ben Selby (born 31 January 1826)
- Selby family of Maryland and Kentucky

## Evidence supporting

The hypothesis has circumstantial features worth investigating:

- Lingan Wilson Selby belonged to the Prince George's County, Maryland Selby family that migrated into Kentucky.
- The 1878 biographical profile describes the elder Hon. Benjamin Selby as Maryland-born and later resident in Adair County.
- Both names occur within the broader Maryland-to-Kentucky Selby migration context.

These points establish a plausible research neighborhood, not a parent-child relationship.

## Evidence against

No reviewed primary or near-primary record currently names Hon. Benjamin Selby as a son of Lingan Wilson Selby.

[[Prince Georges County MDGenWeb - William Magruder Selby will abstract]] names Lingan Wilson Selby as a son of William Magruder Selby, but that 1773 record is one generation too early to establish Lingan's children.

The currently reviewed 1878 biography identifies Hon. Benjamin Selby as the father of the younger Ben born in 1826, but does not identify Hon. Benjamin's own parents.

## Conflicts

Online compiled genealogies and unsourced family-tree material vary in how they assign children to Lingan Wilson Selby. None reviewed in this research pass is sufficient to establish the proposed Benjamin relationship.

The separate conflict over the elder Benjamin's alleged service as Kentucky Auditor further warns against identifying him by office title alone. See [[1878 Kentucky biography auditor chronology conflicts with official Kentucky Auditor history]].

## Analysis

Do not merge Hon. Benjamin Selby into Lingan Wilson Selby's household merely because the families share surname, Maryland origin, and Adair County associations. A direct parent-child bridge should require an heir list, probate distribution, deed naming heirs, obituary/biography naming parents, family Bible, church record, or equivalent correlated evidence.

The strongest current lineage statement is only:

**Ben Selby (born 1826) <- reported son of Hon. Benjamin Selby**

The next generation upward from Hon. Benjamin remains open.

## Confidence rationale

Speculative. The proposed relationship is geographically and chronologically plausible, but no relationship-level evidence has yet been reviewed.
