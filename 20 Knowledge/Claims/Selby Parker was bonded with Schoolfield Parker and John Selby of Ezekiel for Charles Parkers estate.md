# Selby Parker was bonded with Schoolfield Parker and John Selby of Ezekiel for Charles Parker's estate

## Claim

The opening text of Charles Parker's 16 February 1788 Worcester County administration bond names Selby Parker, Schoolfield Parker, and John Selby, identified parenthetically as "of Ezekiel," as the bound parties.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1788 Worcester administration bond]] records the visually reviewed original bond and continuation.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the opening bond text was located.

## Conflicts

The third signature is overwritten or difficult and may read James or John Selby. The opening text clearly names John Selby (of Ezekiel), so the textual claim is retained while the signature conflict remains open.

## Analysis

The record places a Selby-named participant in the Parker estate cluster and supplies the upstream identifier Ezekiel. It does not state that John was Ezekiel's son, that any named bond party was related to Charles Parker, or that this Charles was Margaret Selby's brother.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the bond's opening text expressly names the three parties. The confidence is limited to that wording and association.

## Next research action

Identify John Selby and Ezekiel Selby in contemporary probate, deed, tax, and church records, and compare signatures only after obtaining independent exemplars.
