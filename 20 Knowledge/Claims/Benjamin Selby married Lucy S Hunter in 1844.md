# Benjamin Selby married Lucy S Hunter in 1844

## Claim

Benjamin Selby and Lucy S. Hunter married in Clark County, Indiana, on 27 February 1844.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby and Lucy S Hunter 1844 Indiana marriage]] is the original county marriage register.

## Analysis and limits

The register directly proves the marriage but names no parent. Identifying this groom with the later Kentucky attorney is strong through the 1878 biography and 1850 household correlation, not through a unique identifier in the register itself.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the marriage and date are directly recorded in a contemporary civil register.
