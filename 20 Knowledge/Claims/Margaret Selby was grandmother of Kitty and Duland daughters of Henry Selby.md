# Margaret Selby was grandmother of Kitty and Duland, daughters of Henry Selby

## Claim

Margaret Selby's 1836 will identifies Kitty Selby and Duland Selby as her granddaughters and as daughters of Henry Selby.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Margaret Selby 1836 will and estate settlements]] contains the original recorded will.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby daughter marriage records 1832-1849]] independently names Catharine and Dulcina as daughters of a Nicholas County Henry Selby.

## Analysis and limitations

The will directly states the grandmother-granddaughter and father-daughter relationships. Kitty is a strong familiar-form match for Catharine, and Duland is strongly compatible with Dulcina, but those cross-record identities remain correlations because the spellings differ. The will does not name the girls' mother or call Henry Margaret's son, so it does not alone establish whether Margaret's grandmaternity ran through Henry or through the girls' unnamed mother.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the relationships in the claim are stated directly in Margaret's will. The Catharine-Kitty and Dulcina-Duland cross-record identities are excluded from the confirmed core.
