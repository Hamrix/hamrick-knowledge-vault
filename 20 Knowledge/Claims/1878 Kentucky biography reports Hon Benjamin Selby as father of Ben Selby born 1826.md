# 1878 Kentucky biography reports Hon Benjamin Selby as father of Ben Selby born 1826

## Claim

A near-contemporary 1878 Kentucky biographical profile reports that Ben Selby, born 31 January 1826 in Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky, was a son of Hon. Benjamin Selby.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Biographical Encyclopaedia of Kentucky - Ben Selby profile research lead]] records the profile's explicit father-son statement and its distinctive details: Columbia birth, legal career, marriages to Lucy S. Hunter and Docia Stone, and later residence in Eminence.
- [[FamilySearch - Ben Selby 1880 US census image]] independently places Ben Selby and wife Docia in Eminence with sons Payne and Robert.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Payne Selby 1884 Orleans marriage license image]] identifies Benjamin Selby and Docia Stone as Frederick Payne Selby's parents.

## Analysis and limit

The combination of explicit relationship language and multiple distinctive biographical details strongly identifies the profile's younger Ben with the Ben Selby in the reviewed modern household records. This advances the direct paternal line one generation from Ben Selby born in 1826 to Hon. Benjamin Selby.

The vault has not yet reviewed an image of the original 1878 printed page. The claim is therefore rated strong rather than confirmed. It does not identify Hon. Benjamin's parents or establish a connection to Lingan Wilson Selby, Drucilla Fowler, William Atkinson Selby, or another colonial branch.

Acquire the original printed profile image and then seek Hon. Benjamin Selby's obituary, legislative memorial, probate, and family records for independent corroboration and parental identification.
