# 1878 Kentucky biography reports Hon Benjamin Selby as father of Ben Selby born 1826

## Claim

A near-contemporary 1878 Kentucky biographical profile reports that Ben Selby, born 31 January 1826 in Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky, was a son of Hon. Benjamin Selby.

## Subjects

- Ben Selby (born 31 January 1826)
- Hon. Benjamin Selby
- Sophia / Docia Stone Selby

## Evidence supporting

- [[Biographical Encyclopaedia of Kentucky - Ben Selby profile research lead]] records the profile's explicit father-son statement and its distinctive details: Columbia birth, legal career, marriages to Lucy S. Hunter and Docia Stone, and later residence in Eminence.
- [[FamilySearch - Ben Selby 1880 US census image]] independently places Ben Selby and wife Docia in Eminence with sons Payne and Robert.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Payne Selby 1884 Orleans marriage license image]] identifies Benjamin Selby and Docia Stone as Frederick Payne Selby's parents.

## Evidence against

No reviewed source currently contradicts the biography's explicit father-son statement.

## Conflicts

The 1878 profile also reportedly gives an auditor-office chronology for the elder Hon. Benjamin Selby that conflicts with the official history of the Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts. See [[1878 Kentucky biography auditor chronology conflicts with official Kentucky Auditor history]].

That conflict concerns the elder man's public-office chronology and possible identity with official auditor **Ben Shelby**. It does not directly contradict the profile's statement that the younger Ben was his son.

## Analysis

The combination of explicit relationship language and multiple distinctive biographical details strongly identifies the profile's younger Ben with the Ben Selby in the reviewed later household and parentage records. This advances the direct paternal line one generation from Ben Selby born in 1826 to Hon. Benjamin Selby.

The vault has not yet reviewed an image of the original 1878 printed page. The claim therefore remains **strong rather than confirmed**.

This claim does **not** identify Hon. Benjamin's parents or establish a connection to Lingan Wilson Selby, Drucilla Fowler, William Atkinson Selby, or another colonial branch. See [[Lingan Wilson Selby as father of Hon Benjamin Selby remains unproven]].

## Confidence rationale

Strong because the near-contemporary biography provides an explicit father-son statement tied to a highly distinctive younger-Ben identity that is independently corroborated by later records. Not confirmed because the original printed page and an independent relationship-level record for the elder father have not yet been inspected.

Acquire the original printed profile image and then seek Hon. Benjamin Selby's obituary, legislative memorial, probate, and family records for independent corroboration and parental identification. Do not use the disputed auditor dates as an identity shortcut.
