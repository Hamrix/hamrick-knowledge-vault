# Major Selby was assessed for part of Choice in Worcester County in 1783

## Claim

The Maryland State Archives' index to the 1783 assessment records lists Major Selby in Worcester County's Acquango Hundred with part of the tract *Choice*, 140 acres.

## Subjects

- Major Selby, assessed person as indexed
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Maryland State Archives - 1783 Worcester assessment Major Selby]] records the indexed name, tract description, acreage, hundred, and archival reference.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the indexed entry was located in this review.

## Conflicts

The assessment entry contains no age or family relationship. It is therefore insufficient to identify this man with a later Major Selby of the same name, including one reported by compiled trees as born in 1783. [[Bunce 1948 - Some of the Ancestors Selby genealogy]] reports a different Major as a child of Thomas and Elizabeth Selby and cites their *Choice* deeds, but that derivative report supplies neither an identity statement nor a chain of title to this 140-acre entry.

## Analysis

The record establishes only that the archive index associates a person named Major Selby with a 140-acre assessed interest in part of *Choice* in Acquango Hundred in 1783. It does not establish how he acquired the interest, whether he owned it, his parents, or descent from any Selby or Atkinson family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the official Maryland Archives index expressly gives the name, tract, acreage, and jurisdiction. This confidence is limited to the indexed assessment entry.

## Next research action

Locate and review the original MSA S1161-11-5 assessment page, then trace title to the 140-acre part of *Choice* through deeds, surveys, probate, and releases. Include Worcester Deeds B:203, F:542, and G:303 and the 1802 Thomas Jr. conveyance before evaluating the reported Thomas-Elizabeth-Major family connection.
