# Margaret of Scotland was a daughter of Edward the Exile

## Claim

Margaret, queen of Scots, was a daughter of Edward the Exile (Edward Aetheling).

## Subjects

- Margaret, queen of Scots
- Edward the Exile (Edward Aetheling)
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[People of Medieval Scotland - Margaret queen of Scots]] identifies Margaret as a child of Edward Aetheling (Edward the Exile).
- [[DNB 1885-1900 - Margaret queen of Scots]] independently identifies Margaret as a daughter of Edward the Exile.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the paternal relationship was identified. This claim deliberately does not identify Margaret's mother, whose ancestry has been disputed in historical scholarship.

## Analysis

The scholarly People of Medieval Scotland database and the published *Dictionary of National Biography* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Edward the Exile to Margaret. This records one component of the separately supported bridge claim [[Margaret of Scotland linked Edward the Exile’s line to Henry I of England’s family]].

It does not establish a descent from Margaret to any later royal, Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct paternal relationship. The conclusion excludes unresolved maternal ancestry and later descent.
