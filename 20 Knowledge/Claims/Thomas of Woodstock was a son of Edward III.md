# Thomas of Woodstock was a son of Edward III

## Claim

Thomas of Woodstock, duke of Gloucester, was a son of Edward III of England.

## Subjects

- Thomas of Woodstock, duke of Gloucester
- Edward III of England
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Rawcliffe 2008 - Humphrey Stafford]] identifies Anne of Gloucester as a daughter of Thomas of Woodstock, whom it identifies as the youngest son of Edward III.
- [[Cokayne - Complete Peerage Stafford-Gloucester relationship]] independently identifies Thomas, duke of Gloucester, as a son of Edward III.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified.

## Analysis

The ODNB biography and *Complete Peerage* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Edward III to Thomas of Woodstock. This records one component of the separately supported claim [[Humphrey Stafford was a great-grandson of Edward III through Anne of Gloucester]].

It does not establish Thomas's other descendants, descent from Anne of Gloucester to anyone other than the already documented Humphrey Stafford, or any connection to a Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: a modern expert biography and an independent peerage reference agree on the direct relationship.

## Next research action

Keep each remaining proposed parent-child relationship in a separate evidence note; do not infer a post-medieval descent from this medieval fact.
