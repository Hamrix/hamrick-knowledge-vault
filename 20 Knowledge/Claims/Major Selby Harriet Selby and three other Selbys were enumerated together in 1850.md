# Major Selby Harriet Selby and three other Selbys were enumerated together in 1850

## Claim

The 1850 federal census enumerates Major Selby, Harriet Selby, Ellen Selby, Edwin Selby, and Martha Selby consecutively in one Wayne Township, Hamilton County, Indiana household.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1850 US census image]] is the reviewed original schedule. It gives Major's age as 67 and birthplace as Maryland, Harriet's age as 52 and birthplace as Maryland, and records the three younger Selbys immediately after them.

## Analysis and limit

This is a documented shared household, not a stated family relationship. The 1850 schedule lacks a relationship column. The age and birthplace pattern can be compared with the 1860 Hamilton County household and the 1918 James F. Selby death certificate, but no record in this source names James F. Selby or states that any household member was Major's spouse or child.

The source therefore does not prove that this Major was James F. Selby's father, that Harriet was his wife, that the listed younger Selbys were their children, or that this household connects to any earlier Major Selby in Maryland.

## Next research action

Seek Hamilton County probate, deed, tax, or court records dated 1850-1861 that name the household members by relationship. Compare those records with the original 1918 James F. Selby death certificate before treating them as the same family unit.
