# The public Selby Y-DNA Project does not yet document the Major Selby line

## Claim

As reviewed on 27 July 2026, the public FamilyTreeDNA Selby Project Y-DNA results page does not display a subgroup labeled "Thomas Selby (1623–1702) / Eastern Shore Maryland & Virginia," nor does it provide a published baseline STR profile or terminal SNP for William Atkinson Selby or Major Selby Sr.

## Evidence

- The public project results page is accessible and explains that it displays members' Y-STR results, haplogroups, and subgroups.
- The currently displayed chart contains ten kits under a single heading, **Ungrouped**.
- The displayed paternal-ancestor descriptions do not identify Thomas Selby of the Eastern Shore, William Atkinson Selby, or Major Selby Sr.

## Interpretation limits

- A project subgroup, STR signature, or shared terminal SNP can support a shared direct paternal-line ancestor among tested men. It does not prove a documentary parent-child relationship.
- Y-DNA follows the direct father-to-son line only. It cannot prove descent through Mary Atkinson, Levinah Atkinson, or another maternal connection.
- Even a future match to a documented descendant of Thomas Selby Sr. (c. 1635–1702) would establish a common paternal ancestor, not by itself identify Major Selby Sr.'s parents.
- Haplogroups displayed in green on FamilyTreeDNA are SNP-confirmed; red haplogroups are predictions. A reported terminal SNP should therefore be recorded together with its test status and kit/subgroup context.

## Correct procedure if a relevant subgroup later appears

1. Open the public [Y-DNA Results Overview](https://www.familytreedna.com/public/selby?iframe=ydna-results-overview).
2. Search the **Paternal Ancestor Name** and subgroup headings for a documented direct male-line descendant of the target Selby ancestor.
3. Record the kit identifier, ancestral description, tested marker panel, the actual STR values, and the subgroup mode/minimum/maximum values separately.
4. Open the public [Y-SNP report](https://www.familytreedna.com/public/selby?iframe=ysnp) and record the terminal SNP, including whether it is confirmed or predicted.
5. Compare the genetic result with an independently documented paper trail. Do not use the DNA result alone to assign Major Selby Sr.'s parents.

## Next research action

Identify and recruit a living, documented, unbroken male-line descendant of Major Selby Sr. (or of each proposed paternal line) for Y-111 or Big Y-700 testing. Compare the result with independently documented male-line descendants of the proposed Selby branches.
