# James B. Selby named as Frederick E. Selby's father is identified as James Benjamin Sanford Selby

## Claim

James B. Selby, named as father of Frederick Ethelred Selby, was James Benjamin Sanford Selby, who married Sarah Eveline Stafford under the name Evelyn Stafford in 1910 and died in 1938.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1953 Lenoir County marriage license image]] directly names deceased James B. Selby and living Sarah E. Selby as Frederick's parents.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1930 US census image]] enumerates Frederick E. Selby, age 18, as the son of Sarah E. Stafford in New Orleans.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick Ethelred Selby NUMIDENT record]] names the exact full-name Frederick Ethelred Selby and explicitly reports James B. Selby and Sarah E. Stafford as his parents.
- [[FamilySearch - James Benjamin Sanford Selby 1910 Orleans marriage license image]] records James Benjamin Sanford Selby's marriage to Evelyn Stafford in Orleans Parish.
- [[FamilySearch - James B Selby 1938 Orleans death index]] names Evelyn Stafford as James B. Selby's spouse and reports his 1938 death, consistent with the 1953 license marking Frederick's father deceased.
- [[Sarah E Stafford and Evelyn Stafford were Sarah Eveline Stafford]] resolves the former Sarah/Evelyn conflict through the full-name birth entry and original 1900, 1910, 1930, and 1953 records.

## Evidence against

Frederick's original Mississippi birth record has not been reviewed, and the NUMIDENT source is an official transcription without an available image. No reviewed record conflicts with the correlated identity.

## Conflicts

The records vary the mother's name among Sarah Eveline, Eveline, Evelyn, and Sarah E., and between Stafford and Selby. The 1890 full-name birth index and repeated parent, spouse, child, age, and locality evidence reconcile those forms in one historical person.

## Analysis

The exact full-name Frederick NUMIDENT entry independently repeats the same James B. and Sarah E. Stafford parent pair found across the original 1930 census and 1953 marriage license. Sarah's full-name birth entry and 1910 marriage then connect that parent pair directly to James Benjamin Sanford Selby and Sarah Eveline Stafford. James's 1938 death record independently repeats spouse Evelyn Stafford and places his death before the 1953 license. Together these records confirm the historical identity correlation. They do not establish the user's private modern descent from Frederick Ethelred Selby.

`confirmed`: two independent official sources explicitly name Frederick's James B. and Sarah E. parent pair, and the 1890-1938 record sequence resolves Sarah's full identity, marriage to full-name James Benjamin Sanford Selby, and James's death before 1953.
