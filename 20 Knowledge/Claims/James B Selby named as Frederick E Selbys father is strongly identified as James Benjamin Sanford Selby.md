# James B. Selby named as Frederick E. Selby's father is strongly identified as James Benjamin Sanford Selby

## Claim

James B. Selby, named as deceased father of Frederick E. Selby in Frederick's 1953 marriage license, is strongly identified as James Benjamin Sanford Selby, who married Evelyn Stafford in 1910 and died in 1938.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1953 Lenoir County marriage license image]] directly names deceased James B. Selby and living Sarah E. Selby as Frederick's parents.
- [[FamilySearch - Frederick E Selby 1930 US census image]] enumerates Frederick E. Selby, age 18, as the son of Sarah E. Stafford in New Orleans.
- [[FamilySearch - James Benjamin Sanford Selby 1910 Orleans marriage license image]] records James Benjamin Sanford Selby's marriage to Evelyn Stafford in Orleans Parish.
- [[FamilySearch - James B Selby 1938 Orleans death index]] names Evelyn Stafford as James B. Selby's spouse and reports his 1938 death, consistent with the 1953 license marking Frederick's father deceased.

## Evidence against

No reviewed birth record for Frederick E. Selby names both parents, and no reviewed record explicitly equates Evelyn Stafford with Sarah E. Stafford or Sarah E. Selby.

## Conflicts

The available records vary the mother's name between Evelyn Stafford, Sarah E. Stafford, and Sarah E. Selby. The name forms and family context are compatible but not yet directly reconciled.

## Analysis

The repeated James B. name, the Evelyn or Sarah E. Stafford cluster, Frederick's New Orleans location, and James's death before 1953 support a strong identification. This closes the historical chain only at a strong level; it does not establish the user's private modern descent from Frederick Ethelred Selby.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: the official 1953 parentage statement is correlated with the 1930 household, the 1910 marriage, and the 1938 death index. A child birth record, 1920 household, obituary, or probate record naming Frederick would be needed for confirmation.
