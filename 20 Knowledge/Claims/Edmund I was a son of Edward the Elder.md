# Edmund I was a son of Edward the Elder

## Claim

Edmund I, king of the English, was a son of Edward the Elder and Eadgifu.

## Subjects

- Edmund I of England
- Edward the Elder
- Eadgifu
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[DNB 1885-1900 - Edmund I]] identifies Edmund I as a son of Edward the Elder and Eadgifu.
- [[1911 Encyclopaedia Britannica - Edmund I]] independently identifies Edmund I as the son of Eadgifu, third wife of Edward the Elder.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting Edmund I's paternal relationship was identified. The sources use variant spellings Eadmund/Edmund and Eadgifu.

## Analysis

The DNB biography and the *Encyclopaedia Britannica* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Edward the Elder to Edmund I. This supplies one further historical Wessex-context link before [[Edgar was a son of Edmund I]].

It does not establish a connection from Edward the Elder, Edmund I, Edgar, or their descendants to a later Stafford, Selby, Haskins, Plantagenet, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct relationship. The conclusion is confined to the named medieval people.
