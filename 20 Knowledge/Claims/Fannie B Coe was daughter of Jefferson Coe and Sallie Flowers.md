# Fannie B. Coe was daughter of Jefferson Coe and Sallie Flowers

## Claim

Fannie B. Coe, wife of Ethelred H. Stafford and mother of Sarah Eveline Stafford, was a daughter of Jefferson Coe and Sallie Flowers.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Ethelred Stafford and Fannie Coe 1889 Orleans marriage license image]] directly identifies Jefferson Coe and Sallie Flowers as the parents of 23-year-old Fannie B. Coe.

## Evidence against

- [[FamilySearch - B F Coe 1870 Franklin census rejected identity]] is attached to Fannie in the FamilySearch tree, but its subject was a 17-year-old household head with young children in 1870. That is incompatible with the unmarried 23-year-old Fannie described by the 1889 license.

## Conflicts

FamilySearch's automated transcript reads the mother's surname as `Thomas`, while the structured index reads `Flowers`. Magnified visual review of the original license supports `Flowers`. This is treated as an OCR conflict, not a second maternal identity.

## Analysis

The contemporary official marriage license expressly states both parents and is internally consistent with Fannie's age and Franklin Parish origin. The incompatible 1870 tree attachment has been rejected rather than used to construct an earlier household.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the parent pair is directly stated in a visually reviewed original marriage license, and the disputed maternal surname was resolved against the image and structured index.

## Next research action

Locate Fannie with Jefferson Coe and Sallie Flowers in an original pre-1889 household, and retrieve Jefferson and Sallie's 1847 marriage and probate records before extending either parent another generation.
