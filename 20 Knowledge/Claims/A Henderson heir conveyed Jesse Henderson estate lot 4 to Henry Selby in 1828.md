# Levin Henderson conveyed Jesse Henderson estate lot 4 to Henry Selby in 1828

## Claim

On 6 December 1828, Levin Henderson of Franklin County, Indiana, conveyed Henry Selby of Nicholas County one equal share, identified as lot 4, of Jesse Henderson's divided land.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Henderson heir 1828 deed to Henry Selby]] contains the original recorded deed and its estate and partition recitals.
- [[FamilySearch - Jesse Henderson 1821 division and 1823 deed to Henry Selby]] independently names Levin and assigns him lot 4 in the original division.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby 1830 Nicholas Henderson estate deeds]] records two later acquisitions by Henry from Susan Henderson Williams and William G. Henderson.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby and Nelly Henderson 1809 Bourbon marriage bond]] names Jesse Henderson as co-obligor for Henry's intended marriage to Nelly Henderson.

## Analysis and limitations

The deed proves the acquisition, the parties' residences, and Levin's ownership of an equal share of Jesse's land. The original division independently resolves the given name and lot assignment.

The transaction strengthens the repeated Henderson association around Henry but does not state that Jesse was Nelly's father, that the grantor was Nelly's sibling, or that the 1828 purchaser was the 1809 groom.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the original deed and original division agree on Levin's name, the equal estate share, lot 4, and Henry as grantee.
