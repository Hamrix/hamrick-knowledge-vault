# Humphrey Stafford was a son of Anne of Gloucester

## Claim

Humphrey Stafford, first duke of Buckingham, was a son of Anne of Gloucester and Edmund Stafford, fifth earl of Stafford.

## Subjects

- [[Humphrey Stafford, first duke of Buckingham]]
- Anne of Gloucester
- Edmund Stafford, fifth earl of Stafford
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Rawcliffe 2008 - Humphrey Stafford]] identifies Humphrey as the son of Edmund Stafford and Anne of Gloucester.
- [[Cokayne - Complete Peerage Stafford-Gloucester relationship]] independently identifies Humphrey Stafford as a son of Edmund Stafford and Anne.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified. The cited references use title and name variants for the parents, but agree on the relationship.

## Analysis

The ODNB biography and *Complete Peerage* entry independently corroborate Humphrey Stafford's parentage. This records one component of the separately supported claim [[Humphrey Stafford was a great-grandson of Edward III through Anne of Gloucester]].

It does not establish a descent from Humphrey Stafford to any later Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: a modern expert biography and an independent peerage reference agree on the direct relationship.
