# Thomas Selby of Worcester County was the father of John Selby

## Claim

Thomas Selby Sr. of Worcester County, Maryland, whose will was dated 15 December 1757 and proved in 1758, was the father of John Selby.

## Subjects

- Thomas Selby Sr. of Worcester County, died 1758
- John Selby of Worcester County
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Maryland State Archives - Thomas Selby 1757 will abstract]] reports that Thomas Selby expressly left a plantation "to son John Selby."
- [[Maryland State Archives - William Atkinson Selby biography]] independently identifies John Selby as a son of Thomas Selby who died in 1758.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the stated father-son relationship was located in this review.

## Conflicts

This Thomas Selby died in 1758. He must not be conflated with [[Thomas Selby Sr c1635-1702|Thomas Selby Sr. (c. 1635-1702)]] or assumed to be that man's descendant without a separately documented link.

## Analysis

The official will abstract directly names John as Thomas's son, and the Maryland State Archives biographical reference independently agrees on that relationship. This establishes one atomic relationship inside the Worcester County Selby research field.

It does not prove a relationship from the 1758 Thomas to Thomas Selby Sr. (c. 1635-1702), William Selby in the 1725 Simpleton deed, Major Selby, Etheldred Peters, the Staffords, the Plantagenets, Wessex, or any modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`strong`: a primary-record derivative supplies explicit parent-child wording and an independent historical reference agrees. The original will image has not yet been reviewed, so this is not marked `confirmed`.
