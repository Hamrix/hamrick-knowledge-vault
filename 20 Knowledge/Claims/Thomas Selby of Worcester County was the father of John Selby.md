# Thomas Selby of Worcester County was the father of John Selby

## Claim

Thomas Selby Sr. of Worcester County, Maryland, whose will was dated 15 December 1757 and proved in 1758, was the father of John Selby.

## Subjects

- Thomas Selby Sr. of Worcester County, died 1758
- John Selby of Worcester County
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Thomas Selby Sr 1757 Worcester County will images]] is a digitized manuscript record.  Its first Selby page calls Thomas Selby "Senr." and gives the 120-acre plantation "unto my Son John Selby."  The following page continues Thomas's will and records the witnesses' probate oath on 8 March 1758.
- [[Maryland State Archives - Thomas Selby 1757 will abstract]] reports that Thomas Selby expressly left a plantation "to son John Selby."
- [[Maryland State Archives - William Atkinson Selby biography]] independently identifies John Selby as a son of Thomas Selby who died in 1758.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the stated father-son relationship was located in this review.

## Conflicts

This Thomas Selby died in 1758. He must not be conflated with [[Thomas Selby Sr c1635-1702|Thomas Selby Sr. (c. 1635-1702)]] or assumed to be that man's descendant without a separately documented link.

## Analysis

The reviewed digitized manuscript record directly calls John "my Son" in Thomas Selby's testament. The Maryland State Archives abstract independently reports the same dispositive clause, and the archive's biographical reference agrees. This establishes one atomic relationship inside the Worcester County Selby research field.

It does not prove a relationship from the 1758 Thomas to Thomas Selby Sr. (c. 1635-1702), William Selby in the 1725 Simpleton deed, Major Selby, Etheldred Peters, the Staffords, the Plantagenets, Wessex, or any modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: a reviewed digitized manuscript probate record expressly identifies John as Thomas's son, and two Maryland State Archives sources independently agree. The manuscript record should not be conflated with the separately cited statewide Prerogative Court Liber 30, folio 458 until that volume's image is also obtained.

## Next research action

Obtain and compare the statewide Prerogative Court copy at Liber 30, folio 458 with the reviewed Worcester County manuscript record. Then seek probate and land records that identify Thomas's own parents before testing any proposed link to the earlier Thomas Selby Sr. or to Major Selby.
