# The White House at Cliffe is Grade II listed under entry 1316283

## Claim

The building named The White House at Main Street, Cliffe, North Yorkshire, is Grade II listed under National Heritage List for England entry 1316283.

## Subjects

- The White House, Cliffe
- Historic England
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Historic England - The White House Cliffe list entry 1316283]] gives the official name, location, grade, list-entry number, and listing date.

## Evidence against

No contrary official listing record was found.

## Conflicts

Legacy project files repeatedly cite list-entry number 1316447 for this building. The official Historic England record identifies the correct number as 1316283.

## Analysis

The official list entry establishes the building's protected heritage status only. It does not establish ownership, estate boundaries, manorial status, family succession, mineral rights, acreage, or valuation.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: the facts are taken directly from the statutory-list record.

## Project effect

- Replace 1316447 with 1316283 wherever the project refers to The White House at Cliffe.
- Do not use the listing record as evidence of ownership, lordship, manorial continuity, or descent.
