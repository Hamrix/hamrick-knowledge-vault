# Robert C Selby's 1938 death certificate reports Benjamin Selby and Dora Stone as parents

## Claim

Robert C. Selby's 1938 Kentucky death certificate reports Benjamin Selby as his father and Dora Stone as his mother.

## Evidence supporting

- [[FamilySearch - Robert C Selby 1938 Kentucky death certificate image]] is the reviewed original certificate. Its parental fields name Benjamin Selby and Dora Stone; the informant was Mrs. Sallie Selby.

## Analysis and limit

This proves what the 1938 certificate reported, not an independently verified conclusion about the mother's identity. An image-unavailable 1874 birth index for Roberts Selby names Benjamin Selby and Docie Stone, while an 1880 census names Ben Selby, Docia Selby, and Robert Selby. The name, place, and near-date pattern warrants further investigation of a Docie/Docia/Dora variant, but it does not prove that the records concern one person. None of these sources gives Sophia as the mother's name; they do not prove that Docia or Dora was Sophia E. Stone.

## Next research action

Locate an original birth register, a marriage return, probate record, or a record that explicitly pairs Sophia with Docia or Dora for the same woman. Do not merge Sophia E. Stone with Docia/Docie/Dora Stone until such a bridge is found.
