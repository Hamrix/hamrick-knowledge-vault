# The Dora Stone reading on Robert C Selby's 1938 certificate is not supported by the original

## Claim tested

Robert C. Selby's 1938 Kentucky death certificate reports Benjamin Selby as his father and Dora Stone as his mother.

## Evidence against

- [[FamilySearch - Robert C Selby 1938 Kentucky death certificate image]] was re-reviewed at original resolution. The handwritten mother field reads Docie/Docia Stone, while Dora is the FamilySearch index rendering.

## Analysis and limit

The earlier vault transcription silently promoted the index's Dora reading into the manuscript. That is corrected. The original supports the bounded record statement in [[Robert C Selbys 1938 death certificate names Benjamin Selby and Docie or Docia Stone]]. It still does not prove biological accuracy or explicitly equate Docie/Docia with Sophia E. Stone.

## Confidence rationale

`contradicted`: the original handwriting does not support the tested Dora reading, and an independent 1874 index also uses Docie.

## Next research action

Locate the original 1874 birth return and a record explicitly pairing Sophia with Docie/Docia for the same woman.
