# Edward III was a son of Edward II

## Claim

Edward III of England was a son of Edward II and Isabella of France.

## Subjects

- Edward III of England
- Edward II of England
- Isabella of France
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Westminster Abbey - Edward III and Philippa of Hainault]] identifies Edward III as the son of Edward II and Isabella of France.
- [[DNB 1895 - Edward III]] independently identifies Edward III as the eldest son of Edward II and Isabella of France.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified.

## Analysis

The Westminster Abbey profile and the published *Dictionary of National Biography* entry independently corroborate the direct relationship Edward II to Edward III. Combined with [[Edward II was a son of Edward I]], this completes a separately documented royal context from Henry III to Edward III.

The project already has a separate, independently supported claim concerning [[Humphrey Stafford was a great-grandson of Edward III through Anne of Gloucester]]. This note does not establish any unrecorded intervening relationship, descent from Humphrey Stafford, or any connection to a Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct relationship.
