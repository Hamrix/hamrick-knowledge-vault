# Major Selby's 1833 will named wife Peggy and children Henry and Polly

## Claim

Major Selby of Nicholas County named his wife Peggy Selby, son Henry Selby, and daughter Polly Anterbus, formerly Polly Selby, in his will dated 4 January 1833.

## Evidence

- [[FamilySearch - Major Selby 1833 Nicholas County will and 1835 inventory]] is the reviewed original recorded will and later inventory.
- [[FamilySearch - Margaret Selby 1836 will and estate settlements]] directly identifies Margaret's deceased husband as Major, her daughter as Mary Antrobus, and her granddaughters Kitty and Duland as daughters of Henry Selby.
- [[FamilySearch - William Antrobus and Polly Selby 1825 Nicholas marriage file]] independently identifies Major as Polly's father eight years earlier.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry and Eleanor Selby 1838 Nicholas deed to Parker Selby]] places a married Henry Selby on a Nicholas County Brush Fork home place five years later. This is strong identity correlation, not another parent-child statement.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry Selby 1830 Nicholas Henderson estate deeds]] places a Henry Selby of Nicholas County acquiring Brushy Fork and Hinkston parcels nearly three years before the will, without naming his father.
- [[FamilySearch - Henry and Eleanor Selby 1850 Nicholas census]] records Henry, age 66 and born in Maryland, with Eleanor in Nicholas County. It strongly continues the 1838 couple but does not cross-reference Major's estate.

## Confidence and limits

`confirmed`: the will directly states each relationship, and the 1825 file independently corroborates Major-to-Polly. Margaret's will and the later daughter records now strongly identify the local 1821-1850 Henry as Major's son, although no post-1833 record repeats the father-son wording. The 1850 Henry's reported birth about 1784 rules out the Worcester Major who was about seven in 1785 as his father if this identification is correct. The will does not identify Major's parents or Peggy's maiden name, state that Henry and Polly were his only children, or connect this family to Benjamin Selby or the Hamilton County, Indiana, Major.
