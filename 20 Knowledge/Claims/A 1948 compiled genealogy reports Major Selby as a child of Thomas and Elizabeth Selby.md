# A 1948 compiled genealogy reports Major Selby as a child of Thomas and Elizabeth Selby

## Claim

Julia Locke Frame Bunce's 1948 compiled genealogy reports a child named Major among the children of Thomas Selby and Elizabeth, whose surname the compiler tentatively gives as Houston.

## Subjects

- Major Selby, person reported in the compiled genealogy
- Thomas Selby and Elizabeth, reported parents
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[Bunce 1948 - Some of the Ancestors Selby genealogy]] records the visually reviewed page-48 child list and its attribution to a statement of Capt. John Selby.

## Evidence against

No evidence contradicts the narrow source-report claim that the book contains this statement.

## Conflicts

The parent-child relationship itself is not confirmed. The book is a derivative genealogy published in 1948, the child list is attributed to a family statement transmitted through later generations, and no contemporaneous relationship-bearing record is cited for Major.

The book does not identify its reported Major with the adult Major assessed in Worcester County in 1783, the minor Major documented in guardianship records from 1785, the Kentucky testator who died by 1833, or the Indiana man alive in 1850. Those identity clusters remain separate.

## Analysis

This is a confirmed claim about what a source reports, not a confirmed genealogical link. The report creates a specific upstream hypothesis and supplies a tract-centered route for testing it. Original Worcester deeds B:203, F:542, and G:303, later conveyances, the original 1783 assessment, and Thomas-family probate records should be correlated before the hypothesis is accepted or rejected.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: visual review verifies the exact published report and its provenance note. This confidence does not transfer to the reported parentage.

Retrieve and transcribe the cited Worcester deeds and reconstruct title to *Choice* and *Dull Folly*. Search Thomas and Elizabeth Selby's probate, estate, church, and land records for a complete child class or an instrument naming Major.
