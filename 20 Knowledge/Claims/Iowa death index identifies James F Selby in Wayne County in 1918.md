# Iowa death index identifies James F Selby in Wayne County in 1918

## Claim

The State Historical Society of Iowa's official death-certificate index identifies James F. Selby as dying in Wayne County, Iowa, on 29 August 1918.

## Evidence supporting

- [[Iowa State Historical Society - James F Selby 1918 death index]] provides the indexed date and place of death, birth details, certificate number 93-1265, and box D2553.

## Analysis and limit

The index proves its own entry and supplies the retrieval reference. It names no father and gives no mother's maiden name for James F. Selby. It therefore cannot prove his parentage or identify him with a particular Major Selby.

## Next research action

Obtain and inspect certificate 93-1265, box D2553. Treat any parental information on the certificate as an informant statement to be corroborated with earlier records.
