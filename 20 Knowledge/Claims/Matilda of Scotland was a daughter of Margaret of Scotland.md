# Matilda of Scotland was a daughter of Margaret of Scotland

## Claim

Matilda of Scotland was a daughter of Margaret, queen of Scots, and Malcolm III, king of Scots.

## Subjects

- Matilda of Scotland
- Margaret, queen of Scots
- Malcolm III, king of Scots
- [[10 Projects/Aetheling Evidence Project/Project Index|Aetheling Evidence Project]]

## Evidence supporting

- [[People of Medieval Scotland - Margaret queen of Scots]] identifies Matilda as a daughter of Margaret and Malcolm III.
- [[McDonald 2004 - Matilda of Scotland review]] independently identifies Matilda as the daughter of Malcolm III and Margaret.

## Evidence against

No evidence against the named parent-child relationship was found.

## Conflicts

No conflict affecting the parentage claim was identified. Matilda is also known as Edith; the sources treat these as alternative names for the same person.

## Analysis

The scholarly People of Medieval Scotland database and an independent academic review independently corroborate the direct relationship Margaret and Malcolm III to Matilda. This records one component of the separately supported bridge claim [[Margaret of Scotland linked Edward the Exile’s line to Henry I of England’s family]].

It does not establish a descent from Matilda to any later royal, Stafford, Selby, Haskins, or modern family.

## Confidence rationale

`confirmed`: two independent historical reference works agree on the direct parent-child relationship. The conclusion is restricted to the named parents and child.
