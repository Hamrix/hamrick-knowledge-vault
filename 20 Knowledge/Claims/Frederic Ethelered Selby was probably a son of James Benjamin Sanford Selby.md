# Frederic Ethelered Selby was probably a son of James Benjamin Sanford Selby

## Claim

Frederic Ethelered Selby, born about 1911–1912 in Mississippi and later resident in New Orleans, was probably a son of James Benjamin Sanford Selby and Sarah Eveline Stafford.

## Evidence supporting

- The uploaded GEDCOM places Frederic in family `F230` as a child of James Benjamin Sanford Selby and Sarah Eveline Stafford.
- The GEDCOM cites the 1930 United States Federal Census for both Frederic and the household, at New Orleans, page 19A, enumeration district 0209, with Frederic's relationship recorded as `Son`.
- The same GEDCOM associates James with the household-head role on that census citation.

## Evidence against

- No contrary parentage record has been identified in the materials inspected.

## Conflicts

- Legacy files give conflicting birth years for Frederic, including 1911, 1919, and 1930.
- The GEDCOM includes variant given names and a secondary birth entry of 1912.
- The actual 1930 census image has not yet been inspected in the vault.

## Analysis

The cited 1930 census relationship field would be strong evidence if the image confirms the transcription and household identity. At present, the vault holds only the derivative GEDCOM citation, not the census image. The relationship is therefore stronger than a bare online-tree assertion but not yet confirmed.

## Confidence rationale

`moderate`: a specific census citation and relationship field support the parentage, but the underlying image and an independent vital or obituary source have not been inspected.

## Next research action

Obtain and inspect the 1930 census image, Frederic's birth record or delayed birth certificate, obituary, Social Security application, and James's probate or obituary.
