# Gundreda de Ver was wife of Nicholas de Chavencurt

## Claim

Gundreda de Ver was the wife of Nicholas de Chavencurt.

## Subjects

- Gundreda de Ver
- Nicholas de Chavencurt

## Evidence supporting

- [[Fowler 1893 - Coucher Book of Selby Volume II page 296]] transcribes Nicholas's charter referring to `Gundredae de Ver, uxoris meae` and again to `praedictae Gundredae uxoris meae`, explicitly calling Gundreda de Ver his wife.
- [[Farrer 1916 - Early Yorkshire Charters Volume III Goxhill pedigree]], printed page 61, reports the marriage and cites the Selby charter edition.

## Evidence against

No reviewed evidence contradicts the marriage.

## Conflicts

The charter does not identify Gundreda as Adam of Goxhill's widow or Walter de Ver's mother. Those are separate claims.

## Analysis

The first-person marital wording is explicit in a scholarly charter edition. Nicholas also calls William de Ver `filii mei`, but that phrase is not used here to decide whether William was a biological son or stepson.

This is an upstream medieval relationship only. It does not establish a connection to the Selby surname or the user's documented ancestry.

## Confidence rationale

**Strong:** Nicholas explicitly names Gundreda de Ver as his wife twice in the charter text.
