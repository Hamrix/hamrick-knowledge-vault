# McDonald 2004 - Matilda of Scotland review

## Citation

R. Andrew McDonald, review of Lois L. Huneycutt, *Matilda of Scotland: A Study in Medieval Queenship*, *The Medieval Review* 04.06.06 (2004), https://scholarworks.iu.edu/journals/index.php/tmr/article/view/15743.

## Description

Scholarly review of a specialist monograph on Matilda of Scotland and medieval queenship.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Matilda as the daughter of Malcolm III of Scotland and Margaret.
- States that her marriage to Henry I of England took place in 1100.

## Reliability and limitations

This review independently corroborates Matilda’s parentage and marriage as described in a specialist historical study. It is secondary evidence and does not prove a relationship between Matilda’s descendants and any particular later family without an independently documented generational chain.
