# Maryland State Archives - John and Parker Selby legislative biographies

## Citation

Edward C. Papenfuse et al., *A Biographical Dictionary of the Maryland Legislature, 1635-1789*, Archives of Maryland, volume 426, entries for John Selby and Parker Selby, [page 719](https://msa.maryland.gov/megafile/msa/speccol/sc2900/sc2908/000001/000426/html/am426--719.html) and [page 720](https://msa.maryland.gov/megafile/msa/speccol/sc2900/sc2908/000001/000426/html/am426--720.html), Maryland State Archives, accessed 8 August 2026.

## Information reported

The compiled biography reports that John Selby, who died in 1790, was the eldest son of Parker Selby, who died about 1746/47, and Mary Watts. It reports John's brothers as Parker, William, Zadock, James, and Micajah/William II, and reports his children as James, Daniel, William, John, Nancy, Polly, and Betty.

The adjacent Parker biography reports that the ca. 1746/47 Parker was a son of an older Parker Selby (1675-1705/06), who was in turn the eldest son of Daniel Selby the Elder and Mary Parker. It says the younger Parker inherited land from his father and the elder John inherited 750 acres from that younger Parker.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a scholarly institutional compilation, not a substitute for the underlying wills, deeds, estate divisions, and land records. Parker's 1746 primary will now directly proves that he had an eldest son John. The biography remains part of the correlation identifying that eldest son as the John Selby who died in 1790; that full identification is strong rather than confirmed until a later primary record cross-references the men.

The biography says John's son John died in 1780. That date conflicts with the primary inventory appraised 8 November 1782 if the records describe the same son. The primary estate record controls the death chronology unless an additional John or a different interpretation is demonstrated.
