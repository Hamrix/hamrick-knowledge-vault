# Maryland State Archives - John Selby Jr 1782 Worcester estate index

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, Worcester County Register of Wills, *General Index to Estates, from 1742 to 1908*, entry for **John Selby Jr.**, digitized index scan, [PDF](https://msa.maryland.gov/megafile/msa/coagser/c2500/c2517/000000/000001/pdf/mdsa_c2517_1.pdf), accessed 2026-08-08.

## Record examined

Visual review of printed page 4 of the official index confirms an entry for **John Selby Jr.** with **Leah Selby** in the administrator column. The row records:

- bond filed: 20 October 1782;
- inventory filed: 18 October 1783;
- accounts passed: 31 October 1783 and 13 March 1793.

The corresponding original estate-docket spread has now also been reviewed in FamilySearch Image Group 105017200, [image 68](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSB8-TSZ1-X?cat=olib%3A2859504&view=explore&lang=en). It confirms Leah Selby as administrator and adds the bond sureties **Jacob Selby** and **Nehemiah Dorman**. The Dorman reading is consistent with the surname in the official [1783 Worcester County assessment](https://www.msa.maryland.gov/msa/stagser/s1400/s1437/html/1437wo.html). The downloaded review image has SHA-256 `EBE97A6FA19F92A5408E158E1AED2B15F0154D8BFBFAE526097A608C7FE4F472` and remains outside the Git-backed vault pending rights review.

The row does not display a sale or distribution date. This corrects earlier column-label readings that treated 20 October 1782 as the inventory date, 18 October 1783 as a sale date, and 13 March 1793 as a distribution. The recorded 1793 instrument is the estate's third account, not a distribution to named heirs.

## Reliability and limitations

The printed source is an official archival index, and the corresponding original docket row has also been reviewed. These entries directly support only the recorded estate-administration information. The 1783 inventory and 1793 third account are now separately reviewed; the bond and first and second accounts remain outstanding.

Neither the printed index nor the original docket row names John Selby Jr.'s children, identifies Leah's relationship beyond her administrator role, or mentions Major Selby. The separate 1782 inventory, 1790 grandfather's will, and 1795 final account now create a strong identity correlation to Major's father, documented in [[John Selby Jr 1782 Worcester estate is a lead, not identification of Major Selby's father]]. The index itself should not be cited as direct parent-child evidence.

## Follow-up research location

The Maryland State Archives identifies the relevant original as Worcester County Register of Wills, **Estate Docket**, book 1, 1742-1821, MSA CE391-1 / CR 8972-1. The archive's electronic-series page says that image access is available only on Archives search-room computers. FamilySearch separately catalogs the digitized docket as Image Group 105017200. See the [MSA series description](https://guide.msa.maryland.gov/pages/series.aspx?id=CE391), [FamilySearch catalog entry](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/olib:2859504), and [FamilySearch full-text search](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/full-text/results?q.groupName=105017200&openEdit).

An exact full-text search for `"John Selby Jr"` in that image group returned no result, but manual image review located the row. This demonstrates that the zero-result OCR search was not evidence of absence.

The inventory and later third account have been located and reviewed at [[FamilySearch - John Selby of Captain John 1782 Worcester inventory]] and [[FamilySearch - John Selby Jr 1793 Worcester estate account images]]. The next retrieval targets are the original 1782 bond and first and second accounts.
