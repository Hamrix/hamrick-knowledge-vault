## Audit finding

Several attached sources are not evidence for Robert Benjamin Selby's reported 1902 New Orleans death or his parents:

- The Kentucky death-record collection is attached to Robert's identity and birth fields but contains no page or record identifier establishing that it concerns Robert. It is more plausibly relevant to later Kentucky relatives.
- The Indiana marriage collections support the reported 27 February 1844 Clark County marriage to Lucy S. Hunter, not Robert's parentage or New Orleans death.
- The school-catalog source may support residence or education but is not identified to a specific catalog page in the GEDCOM.
- The 1870 and 1880 census citations support a Robert Selby household in Henry County, Kentucky, but the original images remain uninspected.
- Ancestry Family Trees are derivative and cannot prove the 1902 death or the parent-child assignment to Benjamin Selby and Elizab. McClane.

## Family assignments in the GEDCOM

- Family `@F274@`: Robert Benjamin Selby with Docia Stone; children Frederick Payne Selby and Robert Carr Selby.
- Family `@F275@`: Robert Benjamin Selby with Lucy S. Hunter; marriage reported 27 February 1844 in Clark County, Indiana.
- Family `@F662@`: Robert assigned as child of Benjamin Selby and Elizab. McClane; marriage of the proposed parents reported only as 1811 in Adair County, Kentucky.

The parent-child assignment in `@F662@` is unsupported by an inspected original record.

## Evidentiary conclusions

- **Supported lead:** Robert Benjamin Selby resided in Henry County, Kentucky, in 1870 and 1880, pending image inspection.
- **Supported lead:** Robert married Lucy S. Hunter in Clark County, Indiana, in 1844, pending original record inspection.
- **Supported hypothesis:** Robert was the father of Frederick Payne Selby and Robert Carr Selby, based on household and family-tree structure, pending original census and vital records.
- **Unresolved:** Robert's 16 July 1902 death in New Orleans.
- **Unresolved:** Robert's parents.
- **Unresolved:** Robert as son of Benjamin Selby and Elizab. McClane.
- **Withdrawn as proof:** treating the attached Kentucky death, Indiana marriage, school-catalog, or derivative-tree sources as evidence for Robert's parentage or 1902 death.
