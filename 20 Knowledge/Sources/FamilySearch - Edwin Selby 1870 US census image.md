# FamilySearch - Edwin Selby 1870 US census image

## Citation

United States, 1870 census, Eden Township, Marshall County, Iowa, population schedule, page 14, household 105, Edwin Selby household; National Archives microfilm publication M593; digitized image, FamilySearch, [image viewer](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:S3HT-DT59-J75?view=index&personArk=%2Fark%3A%2F61903%2F1%3A1%3AMDVK-FK5&action=view&cc=1438024&lang=en), accessed 2026-08-04; indexed record [Edwin Selby](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MDVK-FK5).

## Record examined

The reviewed schedule enumerates Edwin Selby, age 38, Maryland-born, as head of household 105 in Eden Township. The same household includes Rebecca Selby, 38, Indiana-born, and Cora (12), Ella (10), and Hester (8) Selby.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed digitized image of an original federal census schedule. It directly supports the enumerated names, ages, birthplaces, residence, and household grouping shown on the page.

It does not name Edwin's parents or establish that he is the Maryland-born Edwin Selby enumerated in Major Selby's 1860 Hamilton County household. Although the names, Maryland birthplace, and approximate age make that a research lead, the reported ages differ and no intervening record has been found that identifies the two entries as one man. It cannot prove a relationship to Major Selby, Harriet Selby, James F. Selby, or any earlier Maryland Selby line.
