## Record examined

The reviewed license names Frederick E. Selby, age 41, resident of New Orleans, Louisiana, and Frances Marion Haskins, age 29, resident of Kinston, North Carolina. The groom's parental line on the form states that he was the son of James B. Selby, marked deceased, and Sarah E. Selby, marked living. The certificate records a marriage on 6 March 1953 at Kinston.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed digitized image of the official county marriage-license and certificate form. It directly establishes the names, reported parentage, and marriage event recorded on that form.

The form does not establish the parents' own ancestry, identify any colonial Selby ancestor, connect Frederick or Frances to Major Selby, or establish a line to medieval Staffords or any royal family. Parentage on a marriage license is an official reported statement and should be correlated with census, birth, death, or probate records for a complete modern proof argument.
