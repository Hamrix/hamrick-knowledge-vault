## Record examined

The deed describes Sophia E. Selby as the person in whom title lay and Ben Selby as her husband. The parties conveyed land for Henry County's public use. The clerk's acknowledgment on the following page again identifies Ben Selby and wife Sophia E. Selby and records their acknowledgment on 11 November 1872.

The historical instrument uses period terminology for the county institution. That wording is not needed to establish the relationship and is not repeated here beyond the citation description.

## Reliability and limitations

The reviewed digitized county deed pages directly establish the named couple's marital relationship in 1872 and Sophia's ownership interest. The original handwriting and the automated transcript agree on the relationship, although the transcript mistranscribes several surnames and words.

The deed does not name either spouse's parents. It does not connect Benjamin to Major Selby or identify Sophia with the 1854 bride by itself. The same-name, spouse, county, and chronology evidence makes that identity correlation strong, but it remains an analysis assembled from multiple records.
