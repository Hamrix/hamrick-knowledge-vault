# FamilySearch - DAR Maryland marriage index Major Selby and Hester Cottingham 1808

## Citation

Daughters of the American Revolution (Maryland), compiled Maryland marriage-record material, volume 9, Worcester County section, FamilySearch image group 008960145, [COTTINGHAM entry, image 145 of 798](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QHV-135R-DHRX?view=fullText&keywords=Major+Selby&lang=en) and [SELBY entry, image 354 of 798](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QHK-935R-D4NN?view=fullText&keywords=Hetty+Cottingham&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

Two photographed typescript pages were visually reviewed. Under **COTTINGHAM**, image 145 reads **Hester (Hetty) & Major Selby, Dec. 8, 1808**. Under **SELBY**, image 354 reciprocally reads **Major & Hetty Cottingham, Dec. 8, 1808**. Both readings come from the images rather than reconstruction from the automated transcript.

## Reliability and limitations

These are reciprocal entries in one derivative compilation, not two independent sources and not the original Worcester County license, bond, return, or register entry. Their agreement strengthens the reading and shows deliberate cross-indexing, but it does not by itself prove that a ceremony occurred, identify either spouse's parents, or identify this Major with the Worcester ward, the Bourbon/Nicholas man, the Somerset man, or the later Indiana man.

The exact original-record target is now known: [[Maryland State Archives - Worcester marriage licenses C2027 1795-1851]] identifies the paper license series covering the reported date. The original has not yet been retrieved.

Later Worcester probate records naming a Hetty Selby as administratrix of Levin Selby of Levin were also reviewed. They do not identify that woman as Hester Cottingham and must not be merged with this entry by given name alone.
