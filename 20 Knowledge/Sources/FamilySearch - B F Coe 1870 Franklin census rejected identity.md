# FamilySearch - B F Coe 1870 Franklin census rejected identity

## Citation

1870 U.S. census, Franklin Parish, Louisiana, Ward 3, household 72, B. F. Coe; NARA microfilm publication M593; digitized image and index, FamilySearch, [record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:M87M-RJ8) and [image](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:S3HT-DCN9-VX9), accessed 21 August 2026.

## Record examined

The record identifies B. F. Coe as a 17-year-old female household head born about 1853 in Mississippi. The household includes Z. Coe, age 4, and M. Coe, age 1.

## Reliability and limitations

The FamilySearch tree attaches this census to the later Fannie B. Coe who married Ethelred H. Stafford. That identification is rejected. The 1889 original marriage license describes Ethelred's wife as age 23, born about 1866, unmarried, and a daughter of Jefferson Coe and Sallie Flowers. The 1870 B. F. Coe was already age 17 and heading a household containing young children. The thirteen-year age conflict and incompatible family status are too large to merge.

This rejection does not identify B. F. Coe or determine whether she was related to the later Fannie.
