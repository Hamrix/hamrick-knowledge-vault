## Immediate analytic effect

This screenshot supports a working distinction between at least two early Thomas Selby identities:

1. **Thomas Selby Sr.**, active in Virginia and Maryland in the mid-to-late seventeenth century, with a will reportedly dated 28 October 1701.
2. **Thomas Selby II**, born before 1656 and associated with Bogertennorton / Boquetenorton, Somerset County, Maryland, before 1725.

It does **not** identify either man as the later **Major Selby** of the late-eighteenth-century Maryland/Worcester record cluster.

## Relation to the Geni “M Thomas Selby” lead

A prior user-supplied Geni screenshot showed **M Thomas Selby**, born 1665, as a son of Thomas Selby Sr. and Mary Selby.

This compiled-note screenshot conflicts with that Geni model in important ways:

- the likely Thomas II is shown as born before 1656, not 1665;
- Thomas II appears as child of Thomas Selby and an uncertain wife, not clearly Mary;
- Thomas and Mary's listed children in the screenshot are Elizabeth, Grace and Mary, not necessarily an additional 1665 Thomas;
- `M Thomas` may be a Geni profile artifact, possible duplicate, or misattached child.

Therefore, the Geni profile should not be treated as evidence for Major Selby or for a clean father-son line without the original probate and land records.
