# FamilySearch - Frederick Payne Selby 1905 Panama service card

## Citation

United States, Panama Canal Zone, employment records, Service Record Cards, 1904-1920, box 33, F. P. Selby (Frederick Payne), entered duty 20 September 1905; digitized image, FamilySearch, [service card](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L9FQ-4XHW), accessed 21 August 2026; associated [indexed record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:QVSH-WNYR).

## Record examined

The original service card was opened and visually reviewed. Its name line reads `Selby, F. P. (Frederick Payne)`. It gives legal residence as Orleans, Louisiana; age 44; entry on duty on 20 September 1905; assignment to the Engineering Department, Machinery and Equipment office; and salary of $100 per month. The position appears to be timekeeper.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a digitized image of an original federal personnel card. It supplies the full expansion of F. P. and places Frederick Payne in the same Orleans locality shortly after the 1900 Frank P. Selby household.

The card does not use the name Frank, name a spouse or child, or expressly cross-reference the 1900 census. It strengthens but does not complete the Frank-to-Frederick identity bridge.
