# Wilkinson 1999 - Thirteenth-Century Women in Lincolnshire

## Citation

Louise Jane Wilkinson, *Thirteenth-Century Women in Lincolnshire* (PhD thesis, King's College London, September 1999), especially printed pages 105, 108-109, 113-114, 119-120, and pedigree at 247.

## Description

Scholarly study of women, family, property, wardship, and litigation in thirteenth-century Lincolnshire, with extensive citations to edited medieval records.

## Relevant findings

- Wilkinson identifies Walter de Ver as Gundreda de Ver's son by her first husband, Adam fitzErneis of Goxhill. She cites *Registrum Antiquissimum* II, pages 211-212, no. 509n, and *Early Yorkshire Charters* III, page 61.
- Wilkinson identifies Simon as Walter's son and heir, under age in 1213 and in Fulk D'Oyry's custody. She cites *Curia Regis Rolls* VII, pages 18-19; a continuation involving Simon is cited at page 160.
- She describes Sybil, Walter's widow, pursuing dower from numerous tenants and later pursuing it with Nicholas de Chavincurt, her next husband, at the 1218-1219 Lincolnshire eyre.
- Her appendix pedigree presents Walter and Sybil as parents of Simon de Ver, but the chart is derivative and must be read with the cited records.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a scholarly doctoral thesis with precise citations to edited medieval record series. It is useful for locating relationship-bearing originals and editions, but it is not itself a contemporary record. The cited *Curia Regis Rolls*, *Registrum Antiquissimum*, and *Early Yorkshire Charters* passages have not yet been directly reviewed in this project.

Wilkinson calls the woman Sybil or Sybil de Ver in the reviewed passages. She does not independently establish the surname `de Kyme`. Her analysis supplies no Selby-surname or modern-family bridge.

## Claims supported

- [[Simon de Ver was reported as son and heir of Walter de Ver]]
- [[Walter de Ver was reported as son of Gundreda de Ver and Adam fitzErneis]]
- [[Sibilla wife of Nicholas de Schauencurt was formerly wife of Walter de Ver]] - scholarly corroboration only; the final concord is the direct evidence.

## Claims contradicted

None directly. The thesis does not supply independent support for calling Sybil `de Kyme`.
