# Hunter 1844 - Pipe Roll 1 Richard I page 61

## Citation

Joseph Hunter, editor, *The Great Roll of the Pipe for the First Year of the Reign of King Richard the First, A.D. 1189-1190* (London: Record Commission, 1844), printed page 61.

## Description

Published transcription of the royal Pipe Roll. Printed page 61 was visually reviewed through Google Books.

## Relevant extracts

The Yorkshire account states that `Wido de Ver` owed money `pro concordia ... facta inter eum et Adam generum suum in Curia Regis`, for an agreement made between him and Adam, his son-in-law, in the king's court. The following entry begins `Adam gener Wido`, again calling Adam Guy's son-in-law.

## Reliability and limitations

The relationship term is explicit and repeated in a published transcription of a contemporary royal account. The project did not review the original Pipe Roll membrane.

The entry does not name Adam's wife or describe her as Gundreda. It therefore proves Adam's relationship to Guy by marriage but not the identity of Guy's daughter.
