# FamilySearch - Lingan Wilson Selby 1821 Adair estate inventory

## Citation

Adair County, Kentucky, Will Book B, page 99, inventory and appraisement of the estate of Lingan Wilson Selby, 30 June 1821 and recorded July 1821, FamilySearch image 375 of 700, Image Group Number `004818716`, ARK `3:1:33S7-9P3X-XQ7`; Adair County court order, 2 July 1821, page 238, ARK `3:1:3QHV-N3Z2-5YSC`.

## Record examined

The inventory and court-order images were visually reviewed. The filmed book opens with a `B` title page at image 325, and image 327 begins page 1 with April 1818 records; the Lingan inventory at image 375 is therefore in Will Book B, page 99. The appraisers certified the inventory on 30 June 1821, and the county court ordered it recorded at its July 1821 term. The inventory includes three enslaved boys named John, James, and Jefferson and household and farm property. It names no estate representative or relative.

FamilySearch group-level full-text searches for `Lingan` and `Selby` each returned zero results even though the image-level transcript for image 375 contains Lingan's full name. With the image transcript panel enabled, images 376-525 were then reviewed sequentially for `Lingan` or `Selby`. No later Lingan estate, sale, settlement, distribution, or heir entry was found in that bounded sweep. The only Selby hits were separate 1822 and 1823 appearances of a Benjamin Selby in other estates, preserved in [[FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby Adair probate appearances 1822-1823]].

## Reliability and limitations

The original records confirm an Adair County estate for Lingan Wilson Selby in 1821. The reviewed entries name no administrator, spouse, child, heir, or parent. The sequential sweep is limited by imperfect automated transcription and pages for which no transcript was available; it is not a substitute for a page-by-page manuscript review or a separate estate packet. The evidence therefore does not support the online-tree assertion that Hon. Benjamin Selby was Lingan's son.
