# FamilySearch - Lingan Wilson Selby 1821 Adair estate inventory

## Citation

Adair County, Kentucky, will records, volume C, estate inventory and appraisement of Lingan W. Selby, recorded July 1821, FamilySearch image 66 of 388, ARK `3:1:33S7-9P3X-XQ7`; Adair County court order, 2 July 1821, page 238, ARK `3:1:3QHV-N3Z2-5YSC`.

## Record examined

The inventory and court-order images were visually reviewed. The court recorded the returned inventory and appraisement of "Ligan W Selly," deceased, on 2 July 1821. Adjacent May-July court pages and neighboring inventory images were also inspected.

## Reliability and limitations

The original records confirm an Adair County estate for Lingan Wilson Selby in 1821. The reviewed entries name no administrator, spouse, child, heir, or parent. They therefore do not support the online-tree assertion that Hon. Benjamin Selby was Lingan's son.
