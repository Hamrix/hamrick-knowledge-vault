# FamilySearch - Joshua Atkinson 1773 Worcester will images

## Citation

Joshua Atkinson will dated 22 January 1773 and proved 23 April 1773, Worcester County will-book pages 143–144; FamilySearch images `33SQ-GTBJ-S5K6` and `33SQ-GTBJ-S5GZ`.

## Record examined

Joshua names wife Sarah and children Rachel, Angelo, and John. He places underage son Angelo in Solomon Townsend's care, with William Townsend as successor if Solomon died before Angelo reached twenty-one.

## Reliability and limitations

The original directly establishes the named household and an Atkinson–Townsend association. It does not establish Selby parentage. Its underage Angelo cannot be the older Angelo whose will was proved in 1766.
