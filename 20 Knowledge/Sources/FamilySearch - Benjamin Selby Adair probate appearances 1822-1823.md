# FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby Adair probate appearances 1822-1823

## Citations

- Adair County, Kentucky, Will Book B, pages 130-131, sale of the estate of Matthew McDowell, returned at the May 1822 county-court term, FamilySearch image 398 of 700, Image Group Number `004818716`, ARK `3:1:33SQ-GP3X-X5T`.
- Adair County, Kentucky, Will Book B, page 166, report of Benjamin Selby and Clayton Miller on William H. Price's administration of Gideon Price's estate, returned at the December 1823 county-court term, FamilySearch image 413 of 700, Image Group Number `004818716`, ARK `3:1:33SQ-GP3X-FH3`.

## Records examined

The original manuscript images and their automated transcripts were reviewed. The Matthew McDowell estate sale lists Benjamin Selby among the purchasers and was ordered recorded at the May 1822 term. The later report states that, under an order made at the October 1823 term, Benjamin Selby and Clayton Miller examined administrator William H. Price's sale list and account for Gideon Price's estate. The court approved and recorded their report at the December 1823 term.

## Reliability and limitations

These are contemporary original records and directly place a Benjamin Selby in Adair County probate activity in 1822 and 1823. The younger attorney was not born until 1826, so these entries cannot refer to him. They are consistent with the older Adair County Benjamin documented in 1811 and later censuses, but neither record states his age, office, parents, or relationship to Lingan Wilson Selby. Participation in Lingan's county and probate network is not proof of kinship.
