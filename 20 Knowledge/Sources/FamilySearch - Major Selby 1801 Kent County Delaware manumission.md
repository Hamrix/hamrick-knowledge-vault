# FamilySearch - Major Selby 1801 Kent County Delaware manumission

## Citation

Kent County, Delaware, recorded manumission by Major Selby, 16 May 1801, deed books G2-H2; FamilySearch image group 008217887, image 62 of 596, [`3Q9M-CSGD-LSPR-8`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSGD-LSPR-8?view=fullText&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

Major Selby declared that an enslaved man named Jacob would be emancipated after a further term of ten years. Major signed the instrument on 16 May 1801 and personally acknowledged it that day before Nathaniel Smithers, a justice of the peace for Kent County.

## Genealogical value

The record places an adult Major Selby before a Kent County official in May 1801, shortly after the last located Worcester guardian account for the younger Major. It is therefore relevant to the same-name identity problem and to possible movement through the Delmarva region.

## Reliability and limitations

The recorded instrument directly establishes the signer, date, act, and acknowledgment. It does not state Major's age, birthplace, parents, spouse, children, prior residence, or landholding. It cannot presently be assigned either to the former Worcester ward or to the older adult Major assessed there in 1783.

The document records the delayed emancipation of an enslaved person. It should be described plainly and not treated merely as a geographic clue.
