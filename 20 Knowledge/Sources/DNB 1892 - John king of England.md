# DNB 1892 - John king of England

## Citation

Kate Norgate, “John (1167?–1216),” in *Dictionary of National Biography*, vol. 29 (London: Smith, Elder & Co., 1892), digitized at Wikisource, https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Dictionary_of_National_Biography,_1885-1900/John_(1167%3F-1216).

## Description

Historical scholarly biography of King John.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies John as the youngest son of Henry II and Eleanor.
- Discusses the origin of the byname “Lackland” in relation to his father’s division of lands among his elder sons.

## Reliability and limitations

This is an older secondary biography. It provides an explicit statement of John’s immediate parentage and is used here with independent modern scholarly corroboration. It should not be used by itself for contested medieval genealogical questions.
