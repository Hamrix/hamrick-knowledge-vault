# British Library - Stowe Ch 37

## Citation

British Library, Stowe Ch 37, “Will of Æthelstan Ætheling (d. 1014),” dated 1014, probably 25 June, Archives and Manuscripts Catalogue, record 040-001953985, https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-001953985.

## Description

Catalogue record and digital surrogate for the surviving upper portion of Æthelstan Ætheling’s will, written on parchment in Old English.

## Relevant extracts

- The British Library dates the document to 1014, probably 25 June.
- A contemporary endorsement identifies it as the will of Æthelstan Ætheling.
- The catalogue identifies Æthelstan as a son of King Æthelred and describes the item as the upper portion of a chirograph.

## Reliability and limitations

The manuscript is contemporary primary evidence that a form of the title was applied to Æthelstan. This note relies on the British Library’s catalogue description rather than a new transcription or palaeographic examination of the digitized manuscript. It establishes historical usage, not a connection to any modern person or surname.
