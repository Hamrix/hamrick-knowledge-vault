# Adair County Kentucky land records index 1811-1815 - Benjamin Selby

## Citation

Kentucky Genealogy, "Adair County Land Records Index: 1811-1815," entries for Benjamin Selby, accessed 15 August 2026. The page describes the material as an index to Adair County land records.

## Description

Derivative online index pointing to early Adair County deed/land entries. The underlying deed images have not yet been inspected in this research pass.

## Relevant extracts

The index includes four entries under the Selby surname:

- Selby, Benjamin — indenture 186
- Selby, Benjamin — indenture 188
- Selby, Benjamin — indenture 599
- Selby, Benjamin & Betsy — indenture 596

## Reliability and limitations

The index is useful for establishing that a Benjamin Selby appears repeatedly in Adair County land records during the 1811-1815 period. It is not enough by itself to identify this Benjamin with the elder Hon. Benjamin Selby of the 1878 biography, nor does the joint entry "Benjamin & Betsy" by itself prove Betsy's precise relationship to him without the deed text.

The underlying county deeds should be obtained before extracting transaction details or relationship language.
