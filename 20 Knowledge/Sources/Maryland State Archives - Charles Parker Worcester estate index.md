# Maryland State Archives - Charles Parker Worcester estate index

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, Worcester County Register of Wills, *General Index to Estates, from 1742 to 1908*, printed page 28, entry for Charles Parker with Selby Parker as administrator, digitized index scan, https://msa.maryland.gov/megafile/msa/coagser/c2500/c2517/000000/000001/pdf/mdsa_c2517_1.pdf, accessed 2026-08-13.

## Description

Official printed estate index created for the Worcester County probate record series.

## Record examined

Visual review of printed page 28 of the official index confirms a Charles Parker estate entry with **Selby Parker** in the administrator column. The entry displays:

- dates of 16 February 1786 and 16 February 1788 in the will/bond column;
- an inventory filed 29 April 1788;
- a second inventory filed 12 March 1793; and
- an account dated 28 May 1790.

The two dates in the will/bond column require the underlying docket or instruments to determine which event each represents. They are preserved as displayed rather than silently resolving the index layout.

## Reliability and limitations

This official archival index confirms the estate entry, the administrator's name, and the indexed filing dates. It does not state Selby Parker's relationship to Charles Parker, identify Charles as Margaret Selby's father or brother, name heirs, or connect this Maryland estate to the later Kentucky Charles Parker whom Margaret Selby called her brother.

The underlying inventory is especially important because Maryland inventories may name next of kin. The original 1788 and 1793 inventories, bond or administration papers, accounts, and Orphans Court proceedings must be reviewed before promoting any Parker parentage or sibling conclusion.
