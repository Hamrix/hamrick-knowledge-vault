# FamilySearch - Roberts Selby 1874 Henry County birth index

## Citation

FamilySearch index entry for Roberts Selby, born 15 April 1874 in Henry County, Kentucky; child ARK `FWKK-B5Q`, father entry `FWKK-B57`, mother entry `FWKK-B5W`.

## Record examined
