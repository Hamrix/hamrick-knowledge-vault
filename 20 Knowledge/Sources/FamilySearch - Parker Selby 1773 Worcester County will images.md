# FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1773 Worcester County will images

## Citation

Parker Selby will, Worcester County, Maryland, proved 8 June 1773, *Wills 1769-1783*, volume 4, recorded page 218 and continuation; FamilySearch image [`33SQ-GTBJ-S5FK`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBJ-S5FK?view=fullText&keywords=Atkinson&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

Parker Selby names wife Mary and children Samuel, David, John, Sarah Atkinson, Mary Selby, Leah Selby, Ann Selby, and Parker Selby. He names James Selby and John Selby as his brothers. The will also refers to property bought from brother James after their mother's death.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary recorded will and is useful for separating the several Parker and John Selby households in Worcester County. The daughter called Sarah Atkinson establishes a Selby-Atkinson marriage in this household, but the will does not name Sarah's husband.

The record does not prove that Parker's son John was John Selby Jr., Major Selby's father, or the John Selby named in the Atkinson wills. It must remain a collateral family group until a relationship-bearing bridge is found.
