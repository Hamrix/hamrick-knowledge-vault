## Record location established

The FamilySearch catalog places Worcester Liber V and W on film 14543, DGS 8141714. The Maryland State Archives guide independently identifies volume V as MSA CE30-21, dated 1801-1803. These two finding aids establish the exact repository and image-group target for the reported 8 October 1802 George Selby deed at Liber V, folios 236-238.

## Access result

The signed-in FamilySearch catalog marks DGS 8141714 as viewable only at a FamilySearch Center or affiliate library. Its full-text-search link is likewise disabled outside those locations. The original deed image was therefore not reviewed in this batch.

This is an access and retrieval finding, not evidence that the printed transcription is accurate and not a new relationship proof. [[Scarborough 1951 - George Selby 1802 Pleasant Grove deed transcription]] remains the reviewed derivative text until the original folios are inspected.

## Retrieval instructions

At a FamilySearch Center or affiliate library, open film 14543 / DGS 8141714 and navigate to Liber V, manuscript pages 236-238. Capture the page images or stable ARKs if permitted, then transcribe the father-son, heir-at-law, prior-contract, tract, acreage, grantor, grantee, witness, acknowledgment, and enrollment language exactly.
