# Worcester Digital Archive - Parker Selby children 1790 guardian bond

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, guardian bond for George Selby and Elizabeth Selby, 11 June 1790, *Orphans Court Proceedings, 1789-1791*, JW 21, manuscript page 48, digital image 690 of the 1789 volume; Worcester County Library Digital Archive, [viewer image](https://worcester.advantage-preservation.com/viewer/?t=31483&i=t&d=01011789-12311797&fn=worcester_county_register_of_wills_orphans_court_proceedings_usa_maryland_snow_hill_17890101_english_690&df=1&dt=10), accessed 13 August 2026.

## Record examined

The bond names Nancy Gunby as principal, with Littleton Robins and John Barnell Marshall as bound parties. It identifies George Selby and Elizabeth Selby as orphans of Parker Selby, deceased. The court narrative says George was about eleven years old and chose Nancy Gunby as guardian; Elizabeth was about eleven years and six months old, and the court appointed Nancy as her guardian. The instrument is dated 11 June 1790.

## Genealogical value

The repeated phrase "orphans of Parker Selby deceased" directly establishes Parker as father of George and Elizabeth. It also documents the two children as minors in Nancy Gunby's guardianship in June 1790.

This record complements the later [[FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1791 Worcester de bonis non bond]], but it does not by itself prove that both instruments concern the same deceased Parker. That later bond expressly calls its Parker a son of Captain John Selby; JW 21 does not repeat Parker's parentage.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a contemporary recorded guardian bond and court entry, visually reviewed in the original manuscript image. It directly proves only the named parent-child relationships and guardianship. It does not identify the children's mother, define Nancy Gunby's relationship to them, or merge Parker with the testator of [[FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1773 Worcester County will images]].

The first surety's surname is read as Robins but the hand is not perfectly clear. That reading is not used for any relationship conclusion.
