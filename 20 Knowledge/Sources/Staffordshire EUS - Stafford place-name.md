# Staffordshire EUS - Stafford place-name

## Citation

Staffordshire County Council, *Staffordshire Extensive Urban Survey: Stafford Historic Character Assessment* (revised August 2011), accessed 2026-07-27, https://www.staffordshire.gov.uk/environment/Environment-and-countryside/HistoricEnvironment/Documents/Stafford-EUS-Report-Final.pdf.

## Description

Local-authority historic-environment assessment discussing Stafford's landscape, archaeology, and place-name interpretation.

## Relevant extracts

- Discusses interpretations of the Stafford place-name in connection with a landing-place and a ford or causeway.
- Notes that the apparent *-ford* element may have a historically broader sense than a simple river crossing.

## Reliability and limitations

This public historic-environment assessment independently supplies place-name context for Stafford, Staffordshire. It does not analyze all Stafford surname origins, identify a particular surname bearer, or support a genealogical connection.
