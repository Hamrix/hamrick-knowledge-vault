# William Atkinson Selby proof path and Townsend conflict lead

## Purpose

This note preserves a user-supplied proof-path lead for William Atkinson Selby and the Townsend/Selby Revolutionary-era conflict cluster. It is not a completed proof statement. Each generation below must be tested against original or near-original marriage, will, probate, land, militia, court, indictment, tax, census, and family-record evidence before being used in a public lineage conclusion.

## User-supplied working chain

1. **William Atkinson Selby**
   - Reported birth: 1757, Somerset County, Maryland.
   - Reported death: 1809, Bourbon County, Kentucky.
   - Reported service: Private, Maryland Militia, Revolutionary War.
   - Reported marriage: Sarah White Townsend, 28 February 1782, Snow Hill, Worcester County, Maryland.
   - Reported conflict lead: 1779 indictment or court proceeding for going over to the enemy / aiding British forces.

2. **Parents: John Selby and Mary Atkinson**
   - Reported father: John Selby of Nassawadux / Pocomoke Hundred, Worcester County, Maryland.
   - Reported mother: Mary Atkinson.
   - Working interpretation: William's middle name Atkinson may reflect his mother's surname, but this is an onomastic clue only until supported by record evidence.

3. **Grandparents: John Selby and Margaret**
   - Reported landholding family in Pocomoke Hundred.
   - Required proof: will, deed, estate, guardianship, tax, or court record connecting John Selby and Margaret to the next generation.

4. **Earlier reported ancestor: Thomas Selby Jr.**
   - Reported death: about 1758 in Maryland.
   - Reported role: landholding ancestor passing property to son John.
   - Required proof: original/register-copy will, estate, land, and probate file.

5. **Earlier reported ancestor: Thomas Selby Sr.**
   - Reported death: 1702.
   - Reported migration: Accomack/Northampton County, Virginia, to Somerset County, Maryland by 1674.
   - Required proof: 1701 will, 1703 inventory, and the earlier Cornelius/Westerhouse/Selby land-chain records already governed by the colonial bridge docket.

## Townsend/Selby Revolutionary conflict lead

The user supplied a historically important conflict cluster:

- **William Barkley Townsend** is reported as a wealthy Somerset/Worcester Maryland and Sussex County, Delaware landowner, and as a Tory / British loyalist during the Revolution.
- **Sarah White Townsend**, reported daughter of William Barkley Townsend, is reported as the wife of William Atkinson Selby.
- **Barkley Townsend Selby** is reported as a son of William Atkinson Selby and Sarah White Townsend, born 1784 and died 1874, apparently carrying the maternal Townsend name.
- Local revolutionary conflict lead: in November 1775 Maryland Council of Safety depositions reportedly concerned 60 to 70 armed loyalist men gathered at William Barkley Townsend's house.
- Local magistrate / Whig suppression lead: John Selby and Joshua Selby reportedly took or participated in depositions against Townsend's Tory faction.
- 1779 conflict lead: William Atkinson Selby reportedly faced a formal court indictment for going over to the enemy or aiding British forces.

## Tree-error warning

The user specifically flagged a severe public-tree error: some online trees allegedly merge William Atkinson Selby of Maryland, born 1757, with medieval or early Northumberland Selby material around Twizell Castle and a false date around 1457. This is treated as an error/hoax/algorithmic conflation lead.

Do not merge William Atkinson Selby of Maryland with any 1400s Northumberland/Twizell Selby profile unless original records establish each intervening generation. A 1757 Maryland birth cannot belong to a 1457 English medieval profile.
