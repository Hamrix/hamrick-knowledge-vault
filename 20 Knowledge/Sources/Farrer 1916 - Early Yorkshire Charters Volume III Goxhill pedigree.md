# Farrer 1916 - Early Yorkshire Charters Volume III Goxhill pedigree

## Citation

William Farrer, editor, *Early Yorkshire Charters*, volume III (Edinburgh: Ballantyne, Hanson & Co., 1916), printed pages 60-62, nos. 1341-1344 and editorial account.

## Description

Scholarly charter edition and editorial reconstruction of the Goxhill family. Printed pages 61-62 and the surrounding charter material were visually reviewed from the Internet Archive scan.

## Relevant extracts

Printed page 61 reports that Robert son of Erneis was succeeded by his son Adam, also styled of Goxhill or Sproatley; Adam married Gundreda, daughter and heir of Guy de Vere; Walter, Adam's son and heir, adopted his mother's surname; and Walter died by 1213 leaving by Sibyl a son Simon, then under age.

Farrer cites Pipe Roll 1 Richard I, page 61, for Guy de Vere's agreement with Adam, his son-in-law; *Coucher Book of Selby* II, page 296, for Gundreda's later marriage to Nicholas de Chavencurt; and Curia Regis Roll 58, membrane 11, for Walter, Sibyl, and Simon.

Charter no. 1344 begins `Walterus de Ver filius Ade de Gousla`, independently supporting Walter's paternity. Charter no. 1341 concerns Robert son of Erneis and Goxhill church but does not name Gundreda.

## Reliability and limitations

The charter texts are a scholarly edition of medieval material; the kinship narrative on printed page 61 is modern editorial synthesis. The directly reviewed underlying Pipe Roll proves that Adam was Guy's son-in-law but does not name Adam's wife. The reviewed Selby charter proves Gundreda was Nicholas's wife but does not identify her as Adam's widow or Walter's mother. Accordingly, Farrer's reconstruction does not by itself close the Gundreda identity gap.

The account does not connect this Ver family to a Selby-surnamed family or the user's documented ancestry.
