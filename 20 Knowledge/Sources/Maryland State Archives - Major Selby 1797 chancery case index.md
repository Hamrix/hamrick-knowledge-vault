## Reviewed content

- Entry 4177 names Daniel Patrick, William Patrick, Patrick Waters Jr., and Matilda Waters as parties against Major Selby.
- The entry is coded for Worcester County and describes a petition to sell the tracts Poplar Hill and Exon Enlarged.
- It gives the accession number 17,898-4177, series reference MSA S512-6-4300, archive location 1/37/1/, and date 22 December 1797.

## Reliability and limitations

This is an official archival finding aid, not the underlying petition. It reliably identifies the indexed case and its archival call information, but it does not supply the allegations, parties' relationships, chain of title, or the court's disposition. The original packet must be obtained and reviewed before drawing genealogical conclusions.
