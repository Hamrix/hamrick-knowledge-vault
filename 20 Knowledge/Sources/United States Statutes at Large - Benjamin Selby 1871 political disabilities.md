# United States Statutes at Large - Benjamin Selby 1871 political disabilities

## Citation

United States, *Statutes at Large*, volume 16, pages 694-695, private act chapter 171, "An Act to remove political Disabilities from certain Persons therein named," approved 3 March 1871; Government Publishing Office, [page 694](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-16/pdf/STATUTE-16-Pg694.pdf) and [page 695](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-16/pdf/STATUTE-16-Pg695-2.pdf), accessed 2026-08-08.

## Record examined

The act removed legal and political disabilities imposed by the Fourteenth Amendment from a list of named Kentucky residents. The list includes "Benjamin Selby, Henry county."

## Reliability and limitations

The enrolled federal statute directly proves the congressional action for a person of that name and county. It does not state his age, occupation, parents, precise wartime conduct, military unit, or residence within Henry County.

The target lawyer was repeatedly documented in Henry County before and after 1871, so the identification is a strong correlation. The statute alone does not conclusively distinguish him from every possible same-name Henry County resident and does not prove that he served in the Confederate military. Political disability could arise from covered prewar officeholding followed by participation in the rebellion in forms other than enlisted service.

## Archival follow-up

The U.S. House series *Petitions and Other Records Relating to the Removal of Political Disabilities, July 1867-March 1871* is National Archives Identifier 563356, Record Group 233. The series is unrestricted but has no item-level digital catalogue entry for Benjamin Selby. A targeted Center for Legislative Archives request should ask for the Kentucky application or supporting papers underlying private act chapter 171, approved 3 March 1871.
