# The National Archives - E 179 database and 1666 hearth-tax reference audit

## Citation

The National Archives, *E 179 Database* and *Taxation before 1689* research guide, accessed 2026-07-29.

## Description

Official catalogue and research guidance for Exchequer taxation records in series E 179, including hearth-tax assessments and returns.

## Relevant findings

- The E 179 database provides descriptions of the surviving taxation documents, their dates, physical form, administrative purpose, tax grant, and place-name headings.
- The database explicitly does not catalogue the names of individual taxpayers, although it indicates whether a document contains names.
- A statement that a specific individual appears in an E 179 record therefore requires inspection of the actual record image or an independently published transcription.
- The project files cite `E 179/216/493` for a supposed 1666 entry reading `Selbye of Whitehouse, Cliffe`.
- The supplied project materials contain neither a scan nor a transcript of that alleged hearth-tax entry.
- The project master outline describes the copy as only requested, under the asserted request reference `TNA1749900491S75`, rather than received and authenticated.
- Current official-catalogue searches performed during this audit did not locate an accessible catalogue description confirming the claimed wording or the association of `E 179/216/493` with Whitehouse at Cliffe.
- A genuine nearby North Riding hearth-tax record, `E 179/216/458`, is officially catalogued as a 1664 assessment. It is a different reference and does not validate the project claim.

## Evidential assessment

The existence of E 179 hearth-tax materials for Yorkshire is established. The specific proposition that `E 179/216/493` names a Selby householder at Whitehouse, Cliffe is not established from the files or catalogue evidence inspected.

This finding does not prove that the cited record is nonexistent. It means the reference, locality, wording, and identity must be verified from an actual reproduction or a reliable published transcription.

## Reliability and limitations

The National Archives catalogue is authoritative for catalogue descriptions. It is not a name index to individual taxpayers. Absence of a taxpayer name from catalogue search results is therefore not evidence that the person does not appear in the underlying record.
