# FamilySearch - Charles Parker account search negative result, 2026-08-13

## Question

Can FamilySearch Full-Text Search expose Charles Parker's indexed May 1762 account, 28 May 1790 account, 12 March 1793 second inventory, or the will copy referenced by the 1788 administration?

## Searches performed

- Exact-name search for `"Charles Parker"`, year range 1759-1763, filtered to Worcester County, Maryland.
- Exact-name search for `"Collins Parker"`, year range 1760-1763, filtered to Worcester County.
- Combined search for `Collins Parker Charles Parker`, year range 1761-1763, filtered to Worcester County.
- Exact-name search for `"Selby Parker"`, year range 1788-1791, filtered to Worcester County.
- Exact and unquoted searches for `Charles Parker deceased`.
- DGS-restricted exact-name searches within FamilySearch DGS 007737829, *Testamentary Proceedings*, volumes 35-39, for `"Charles Parker"` and `"Collins Parker"`.
- Manual review of the alphabetical Parker sequence in Millard Fillmore Hudson's compiled *Balance books at Annapolis: distribution of estates for Worcester County, Maryland, 1750-1776*, FamilySearch DGS 007735651.

## Result

The searches located or repeated the known 1761 administration bond, 1761 inventory, estate indexes and dockets, the 1788 inventory, and collateral occurrences of the name Charles Parker. They did not expose an identifiable image of the May 1762 account, 28 May 1790 account, 12 March 1793 second inventory, or will copy through the search-results route.

## Subsequent resolution

Manual browsing of the Worcester `Wills 1783-1790 vol 13` waypoint located the will on FamilySearch DGS 005080348, image 383. [[FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1787 Worcester County will]] records the result.

A later DGS-restricted search and direct folio review located [[FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1761-1762 testamentary proceedings]]. Liber 38 folio 93 abstracts the 1761 administration bond and folio 163 records the inventory. Folios 133 and 328 are collateral surety appearances by Charles Parker in other estates. None is the May 1762 account or contains distribution language.

Archive-series research subsequently resolved the 1762 target. The Maryland Prerogative Court accounts index identifies Charles Parker at Liber 48, folio 128, Worcester County. Direct review of the original account at folios 128-129 is recorded in [[Maryland State Archives - Charles Parker 1762 Worcester administration account]]. It confirms Collins Parker as administrator and the account's passage on 21 May 1762, but names no heirs and states no relationship between Collins and Charles. The 1790 account or distribution and 1793 second inventory remain unresolved. This negative search is retained because it documents real OCR and record-type limits, not because the records were absent.

The Hudson balance-book film was then reviewed manually at the surname transition. Image 125 contains Benjamin Parramore; image 126 begins the Parker entries and contains Samuel Parker and George Parker. No Charles Parker entry appears in that transition or Parker page. The reviewed images are https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C913-V36G-L?cat=680763&view=explore&lang=en and https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C913-V362-X?cat=680763&view=explore&lang=en. Because the film is a later compiled distribution abstract rather than the original account book, the omission cannot establish that the account was never filed or had no distribution.

## Limitation

This is a negative search result, not evidence that the records do not survive. FamilySearch returned zero results for some phrases even though those phrases appear in already reviewed records, demonstrating incomplete OCR or search coverage. The missing papers require catalog-level volume identification, manual image browsing, or an archive request.
