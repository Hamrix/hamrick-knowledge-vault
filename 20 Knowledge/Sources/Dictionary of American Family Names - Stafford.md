## Description

Specialist surname-dictionary entry for Stafford, displayed separately from Ancestry member-tree material.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies an English habitational origin, principally from Stafford in Staffordshire but also from several similarly named places.
- Explains that the named places share Old English *ford* but have different initial elements.
- Records an Irish (Wexford) variant origin from Stocker.

## Reliability and limitations

The cited dictionary is an expert-authored reference work published by Oxford University Press. It identifies population-level surname origins, not an individual ancestor. Its multiple English source places and separate Irish origin mean it cannot identify a particular Stafford family, demonstrate noble descent, or establish any connection to the project families.
