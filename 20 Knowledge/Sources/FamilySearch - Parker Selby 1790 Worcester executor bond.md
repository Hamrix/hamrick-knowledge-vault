# FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1790 Worcester executor bond

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, executor bond for the estate of Parker Selby, 29 January 1790, probate bonds 1786-1793, manuscript page 194; FamilySearch image group 005080351, image [`33SQ-GT1P-ZCV`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GT1P-ZCV?view=fullText&keywords=Parker%20Selby&searchForm=advanced&lang=en), accessed 13 August 2026.

## Record examined

The bond names James Selby as executor of the last will and testament of Parker Selby, deceased. William Selby and Dennis Hudson are the other bound parties. The instrument is dated 29 January 1790 and requires an inventory by 29 April.

The bond does not state Parker's father, identify James or William's relationship to him, or name Parker's widow or children.

## Genealogical value

This instrument establishes the opening administrative history of the Parker estate associated with the 1790 index row: James Selby acted as executor under Parker's will. That history differs from [[FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1791 Worcester de bonis non bond]], which appoints James Selby and John Gunby administrators de bonis non and says John Selby, Esquire, had been the former administrator.

The differing histories support keeping the two estate files separate. They do not by themselves prove that the decedents were different men; the missing will and complete account history remain necessary for a final identity conclusion.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a contemporary recorded probate bond visually reviewed in the manuscript image. FamilySearch's automated transcript reads some surnames poorly, so names and the manuscript page were checked against the image.
