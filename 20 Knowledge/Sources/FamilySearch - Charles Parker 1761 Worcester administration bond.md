# FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1761 Worcester administration bond

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, probate records, administration bond for the estate of Charles Parker, manuscript pages 350-351, bond dated 11 February 1761 and letters of administration dated 12 February 1761; FamilySearch image 182 of 474, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GT1V-22T?view=fullText&keywords=Charles+Parker&searchForm=advanced&lang=en, accessed 2026-08-13.

## Record examined

The original recorded bond names **Collins Parker, Charles Parker, and John Parker** as jointly and severally bound. Its condition identifies Collins Parker as administrator of the goods, chattels, rights, and credits of **Charles Parker, late of Worcester County, deceased**. The execution block carries all three Parker names. The following entry grants letters of administration to Collins Parker on 12 February 1761.

## Date correction

Visual review reads the bond date as 11 February 1761 and the letters date as 12 February 1761. FamilySearch's automated transcript renders the final digit inconsistently as 1760 and 1766. The general estate index uses 12 February 1761, corresponding to the grant of letters. The original manuscript and estate sequence control over the OCR.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a contemporary recorded administration bond and directly proves the administrative and bond-party roles. It does not state how Collins Parker, the bound Charles Parker, John Parker, and the deceased Charles Parker were related. In particular, the repeated Charles name must not be treated as proof that the co-obligor and decedent were the same person.
