# John Cornelius and Thomas Selby colonial bridge acquisition docket

## Purpose

This docket governs a new colonial bridge acquisition phase. The records below must be obtained as images, archive-grade copies, or certified/archival transcriptions before the line is extended to any later Thomas Selby, the 1758 Thomas Selby, Major Selby, or any Maryland/Worcester Selby network.

## Controlling research rule

Do **not** extend the line to the 1758 Thomas Selby or Major Selby until these earlier records are acquired, transcribed, cited, and correlated.

The immediate objective is to establish the next generation from original or near-original records, not from derivative abstracts, profile pages, compiled pedigrees, or later family-tree structures.

## Virginia / Northampton County target records

### 1. John Cornelius 1656 will

- Name: John Cornelius, Gent.
- Will date reported: 14 April 1656
- Probate/proof date reported: 21 April 1656
- Wife: Mary, reportedly executrix
- Reported estate language: estate in Virginia, Holland, or elsewhere
- Reported witnesses: William Westerhouse and Daniell Jones
- Required record: image or certified/archival transcription of the will and probate entry.

### 2. 1660 conveyance

- Target: any conveyance in the Cornelius/Westerhouse/Selby land chain.
- Parties to search: John Cornelius, Mary Cornelius, William Westerhouse, Thomas Selby, Daniell Jones, and variant spellings.
- Required record: deed/conveyance image or certified/archival transcription, including all acknowledgments, witnesses, marginal notes, and recording date.

### 3. 1664 conveyance

- Target: any conveyance in the same Cornelius/Westerhouse/Selby chain.
- Required record: deed/conveyance image or certified/archival transcription.

### 4. 1675 patent or land entry

- Target: any patent or land entry connected to the same land or parties.
- Possible repositories: county record books and/or Virginia Land Office patent books.
- Required record: patent image, full abstract, patent-book citation, or archive-certified transcription.

### 5. 1677 sale

- Target: any sale or conveyance connected to the same land/persons.
- Required record: deed/sale image or certified/archival transcription.

## Virginia record access route

The official first route is the Library of Virginia, especially Northampton County court-record microfilm.

Known official route:

- Library of Virginia Northampton County microfilm listing identifies `Deeds, Wills, No. 7, 8, 1655 [1654]-1668`, which contains `Deeds, Wills, Orders, 1654-1657` and `Deeds & Wills, 1666-1668`.
- FamilySearch catalog also identifies Northampton County `Deeds, wills, court orders, etc., 1640-1867 ; index to deeds, 1632-1917`, microfilmed from original records at the Northampton County Courthouse and photostats at the Virginia State Library.

The LVA request should ask staff to identify the exact volume, folio/page, and microfilm citation before paid copying.

## Maryland target records

### 6. Thomas Selby Sr. original 1701 will

- Name: Thomas Selby Sr.
- Record: original will, register-copy will, or archive-certified transcription.
- Date lead: will dated 28 October 1701; probate lead 24 October 1702.
- Required record: complete will image/transcription, including witnesses, executor, heirs, land/property language, and proof/probate notation.

### 7. Thomas Selby Sr. 1703 inventory

- Name: Thomas Selby Sr.
- Record: estate inventory.
- Date lead: around March 1703.
- Required record: complete inventory image/transcription, including appraisers, valuation, return/probate date, any account or administration bond, and full archive citation.

### 8. Thomas Selby 1725 Simpleton / Singleton transaction

- Name: Thomas Selby of Somerset County.
- Associated wife: Margaret.
- Associated party: William Selby of Somerset County.
- Date lead: 12 October 1725.
- Land/tract: Simpleton or Singleton, associated with Boquetenorton / Bogertennorton and Selby's Bay.
- Reported content: deed language identifying an earlier Thomas Selby as the grantor's eldest grandfather and naming the grantor as heir-at-law of his father and grandfather.
- Required record: complete deed image/transcription, including opening clause, consideration, tract history, heir-at-law language, signatures or marks, witnesses, acknowledgments, and full book/page citation.

## Maryland record access route

The official first route is Maryland State Archives.

Known official route:

- Maryland State Archives maintains colonial probate series and indexes, including wills, inventories, accounts, and colonial probate indexes.
- MSA copy-order guidance allows estate/probate copy requests and gives `msa.helpdesk@maryland.gov` for record-transfer or order questions.

The MSA request should ask whether the record belongs to Somerset County, Worcester County, or a provincial Prerogative Court series, because Worcester County was not a separate county until after some earlier Eastern Shore records and many colonial probate records were recorded at the provincial level.

The 1725 deed should be searched in Somerset County land records and related provincial land-record series before any later Worcester County conclusion is made.
