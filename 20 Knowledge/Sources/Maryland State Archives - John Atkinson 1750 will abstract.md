# Maryland State Archives - John Atkinson 1750 will abstract

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, "Abstracts of Maryland Wills - Liber 27 1748-1750," entry for John Atkinson, Worcester County, will dated 15 February 1750 and proved 6 March 1750, Liber 27, folio 527, accessed 2026-08-02, https://msa.maryland.gov/msa/stagser/s500/s538/html/s538-27.html.

## Description

Official published abstract of the statewide Prerogative Court will record for John Atkinson of Worcester County.

## Reviewed content

- It calls Levinah Selby the "wife of Wm. Selby, the younger."
- It identifies William and John Selby as "said Levinah sons."
- The abstract gives the will date as 15 February 1750 and the probate oath as 6 March 1750.

## Reliability and limitations

The Maryland State Archives abstract is a derivative of the probate manuscript, not a substitute for a reviewed image of the will. It directly reports the relationships in its transcription but the underlying Liber 27 page has not yet been compared. It does not identify Major Selby, prove that these William and John Selbys are the same as every later person of those names, or prove any parentage beyond the relationships stated.
