# FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1787 Worcester County will

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, Register of Wills, *Wills, 1783-1790*, volume 13, pages 190-191, will of Charles Parker, dated 7 June 1787 and proved 16 February 1788; FamilySearch DGS 005080348, image 383 of 421, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9TBJ-S5SH?view=fullText&keywords=Charles%20Parker&lang=en, accessed 2026-08-13.

## Record examined

The recorded will identifies **Charles Parker of Worcester County** as testator and expressly names:

- wife **Ann**, whom Charles appointed an executor with son John and to whom he left the residue of his estate;
- sons **John**, **Charles**, **Selby**, and **Thomas**;
- daughters **Winnefred**, **Lize**, **Peggy**, **Sophia**, **Sarah**, and **Ann**;
- the heirs of daughter **Polly**.

The will gives Selby one shilling sterling and no more of the estate. It names **Schoolfield Parker**, **William White**, and **Curtis Henderson** as witnesses. The probate text records John Parker and Ann Parker making oath on 16 February 1788 and records the three witnesses proving the will on the same date.

The manuscript also records enslaved people as estate property and distributes them among family members. Those clauses were reviewed for the family relationships but are not reproduced here beyond what is necessary to document the record accurately.

## Reliability and limitations

This contemporary recorded will is direct evidence that the testator Charles Parker called Selby Parker his son. The same uncommon name, county, estate, and probate date strongly identify the son as the Selby Parker who received administration with the will annexed on 16 February 1788. The will also resolves the 1788 inventory's nearest-kin entries: John was Charles's son and Ann was Charles's wife.

The will does not state Schoolfield Parker's relationship to Charles, identify John Selby (of Ezekiel), connect this Charles Parker to Margaret Selby's Kentucky brother Charles, or connect Selby Parker to any person named Major Selby. It also does not identify the parents of Charles or Ann.

## Transcription cautions

The name **Selby** is legible in the manuscript despite inconsistent OCR. The OCR also misreads the probate year as 1780 in places; the recorded will date, volume chronology, administration bond, and manuscript year establish probate on **16 February 1788**. Some daughter-name spellings are retained as written or as the clearest manuscript reading.
