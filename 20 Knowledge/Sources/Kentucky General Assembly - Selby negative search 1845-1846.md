# Kentucky General Assembly - corrected Selby search 1845-1846

## Search performed

The complete searchable PDFs of the Kentucky House and Senate journals for the session from 31 December 1845 through 24 February 1846 were initially searched for exact `Selby`, `Ben Selby`, and proximity combinations of `Benjamin` with `Selby` or `Shelby`.

The House PDF contains 506 pages and the Senate PDF 378 pages. The initial exact search returned no relevant occurrence. A later broader auditor-succession search found the missed Senate entry on page 37 because its OCR text misread `Ben. Selby` as `Ben. Sebly`. Visual review of the original image confirms the surname is **Selby**.

## Result

No legislative memorial, family petition, estate measure, or relationship statement was found. The corrected search did find a decisive personnel entry: on 5 January 1846, Governor William Owsley nominated Harry I. Bodley as Auditor of Public Accounts "in place of Ben. Selby, deceased," and the Senate consented. This proves the prior negative result was incomplete and independently confirms the deceased auditor's identity.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a bounded text-layer search corrected by visual review of the newly located page. The `Sebly` OCR miss demonstrates why exact negative searches cannot prove that no memorial or family statement existed. A memorial may still have appeared in a newspaper, unpublished petition, committee file, executive record, or another legislative session.

## Newspaper follow-up

Kentucky Historical Society newspaper holdings identify four high-value Frankfort targets covering 1845:

- *The Commonwealth*, 1843-1846, reel `78-0207`;
- *Frankfort Commonwealth*, 1843-1847, reel `78-0196`;
- *Daily Commonwealth*, 1844-1846, reel `78-0208`;
- *The Yeoman*, 1843-1851, reel `78-0266`.

[[Boons Lick Times - Benjamin Selby death notice reprint 1845|A Missouri newspaper reprint]] now reports that the Frankfort *Commonwealth* announced Benjamin Selby's death on 25 May 1845. The original Frankfort notice remains the next newspaper target because it may contain fuller wording or relatives.
