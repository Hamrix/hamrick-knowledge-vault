# Kentucky General Assembly - Selby negative search 1845-1846

## Search performed

The complete searchable PDFs of the Kentucky House and Senate journals for the session from 31 December 1845 through 24 February 1846 were searched for exact `Selby`, `Ben Selby`, and proximity combinations of `Benjamin` with `Selby` or `Shelby`.

The House PDF contains 506 pages and the Senate PDF 378 pages. No exact `Selby` occurrence and no Benjamin-near-Selby-or-Shelby occurrence was returned in either journal. Numerous `Shelby` results concerned Shelby County, Shelbyville, Shelby College, or unrelated names and were not evidence about Benjamin Selby.

## Result

No legislative memorial, death notice, estate measure, family petition, or personnel entry for the reported Hon. Benjamin Selby was found in the first General Assembly session after the reported 1845 death.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a bounded text-layer search of complete official-session PDFs. It is not proof that no memorial existed: OCR or text extraction can miss a damaged or unusual spelling, and a memorial may have appeared in a newspaper, unpublished petition, committee file, executive record, or another legislative session.

## Newspaper follow-up

Kentucky Historical Society newspaper holdings identify four high-value Frankfort targets covering 1845:

- *The Commonwealth*, 1843-1846, reel `78-0207`;
- *Frankfort Commonwealth*, 1843-1847, reel `78-0196`;
- *Daily Commonwealth*, 1844-1846, reel `78-0208`;
- *The Yeoman*, 1843-1851, reel `78-0266`.

The relevant runs are not presently available as a complete open full-text corpus. A microfilm or archive request should search the period after the 21 May 1845 tax certification through the end of 1845, then widen to the full year if needed.
