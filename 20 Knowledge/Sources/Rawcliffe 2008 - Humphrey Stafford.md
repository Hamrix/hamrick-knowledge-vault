# Rawcliffe 2008 - Humphrey Stafford

## Citation

Carole Rawcliffe, “Stafford, Humphrey, first duke of Buckingham (1402–1460), soldier and magnate,” *Oxford Dictionary of National Biography* (Oxford University Press, 2004; online edition, January 2008), https://doi.org/10.1093/ref:odnb/26207.

## Description

Modern scholarly biography of Humphrey Stafford, first duke of Buckingham.

## Relevant extracts

- Rawcliffe identifies Humphrey as the son of Edmund Stafford, fifth earl of Stafford, and Anne of Gloucester.
- Anne is identified as a daughter of Thomas of Woodstock, the youngest son of Edward III.

## Reliability and limitations

The *Oxford Dictionary of National Biography* is an expert-edited scholarly reference work. This article is strong secondary evidence for Humphrey’s immediate parentage and royal ancestry. It does not document any descent from Humphrey or another Stafford to a modern family in this project.
