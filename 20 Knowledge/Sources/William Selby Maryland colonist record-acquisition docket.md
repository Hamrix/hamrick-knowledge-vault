# William Selby Maryland colonist record-acquisition docket

## Purpose

This docket turns the William Selby Maryland colonist biography lead into a controlled source-acquisition plan.

William Selby of Calvert / Prince George's County must be treated as a **parallel Maryland Selby research lead**, not as a proven connection to Thomas Selby Sr., Thomas Selby II, Major Selby, or the modern Selby line.

## Controlling rule

Do **not** merge William Selby into the Thomas Selby / Cornelius / Somerset bridge unless original Maryland records establish a relationship.

Do **not** extend the modern lineage through William Selby unless an independent chain connects him forward to the documented modern Selby line.

## Source basis

The immediate source basis is a user-supplied biography text, preserved in [[William Selby Maryland colonist biography lead]]. That biography includes numerous derivative citations and record leads, but the original images or archive-certified transcriptions remain mostly uninspected in the vault.

## Target records

### 1. Maryland Patents Liber R, folio 84

- Date lead: 29 December 1659.
- Reported event: Lieutenant Thomas Trueman claimed headrights.
- Reportedly named: Hugh Lawson, John Kelly, Mary Steward, William Jenkins, John Ormans, William Selby, Christopher Hands.
- Purpose: test William Selby's transportation / headright appearance in Maryland.
- Required record: patent-book image or certified/archival transcription with exact citation.

### 2. William Selby will, 1698/1699

- Testator: William Selby of Prince George's County.
- Date lead: 5 November 1698.
- Probate lead: 25 February 1698/99.
- Derivative citation: Prince George's County wills / Maryland Register of Wills records; also Prerogative Court Wills Liber 6, folio 215.
- Required record: complete will image or register-copy transcription.
- Required extraction: heirs, wife, executor/executrix, witnesses, probate notation, land clauses, and child names.

### 3. William Selby estate administration / inventory / accounts

- Date lead: 30 July 1701.
- Reported administrator: Mary Cecill, wife of Joshua Cecil.
- Derivative citation: Prerogative Court Inventories & Accounts, Liber 20, folio 237.
- Required record: inventory, accounts, administrator/executrix language, debts, appraisers, valuation, and any related estate papers.

### 4. Prince George's Deeds Liber A, folio 68A

- Date lead: 28 April 1697.
- Grantor: Walter Bigger of Calvert County.
- Grantee: William Selby of Prince George's County.
- Land: 350 acres called To Good / Towgood.
- Location: west side of the Patuxent River near Croome/Groome, Timberly, and Generals Gift.
- Required record: deed image or archive-certified transcription including consideration, boundaries, witnesses, acknowledgment, and recording note.

### 5. Leith conveyance

- Date lead: 17 June 1686.
- Grantor lead: James Moore of Calvert County, carpenter, and Elizabeth his wife.
- Grantee: William Selby of Calvert County, planter.
- Land: tract called Leith on the west side of the Patuxent River, surveyed for about 500 acres.
- Citation: unknown in the supplied biography; must be located in Calvert / Prince George's land records or patent records.
- Required record: deed/patent image or certified transcription and exact citation.

### 6. Maryland Patents Liber CC 4, folio 57

- Derivative citation appears in connection with William Selby, Leith, or patent/resurvey context.
- Required record: patent-book image or certified transcription.
- Purpose: determine whether the citation concerns William Selby, his sons Charles and William, or the Leith tract.

### 7. Prince George's Land Records Liber C, folio 61

- Date lead: 23 June 1703.
- Reported transaction: Joshua Cecil paid for 309 acres of The Farme.
- Location: formerly in Calvert County and later Prince George's County; west side of Patuxent adjoining Mansfield.
- Reported context: part of The Farme owned by Thomas Truman and part by William Selby.
- Required record: deed image or certified transcription, full party list, chain of title, and acknowledgment.

### 8. Robert Hucker will, 1711

- Testator: Robert Hucker / Hucker variants.
- Date lead: 20 April 1711.
- Probate lead: 3 May 1711.
- Location: Prince George's County.
- Relevance: reportedly names land connected to Amy Selby Hucker and William Selby deceased, and may corroborate William Selby's children and land descent.
- Required record: complete will image/citation.

3. diplomatic or near-diplomatic transcription;
4. normalized abstract;

6. land descriptions and chains of title;
7. witnesses, appraisers, executors, administrators, heirs, and neighbors;
8. whether the record supports William's identity, wife, children, landholding, or later connections;
