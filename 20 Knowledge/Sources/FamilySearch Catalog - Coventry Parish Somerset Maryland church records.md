# FamilySearch Catalog - Coventry Parish Somerset Maryland church records

## Citation

Coventry Parish (Rehobeth, Maryland : Protestant Episcopal), *Coventry Parish, Somerset Co., Maryland, 1724-1891*, copied 1899-1900 by L. H. Harrison; microfilm of manuscript at the Maryland Historical Society, Baltimore; FamilySearch catalog [140472](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/140472), film 14417 items 3-4, digital image group 7575976; digitized images, FamilySearch [film 007575976](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-L9XW-F97F-N?i=83&groupId=2385204&lang=en), accessed 2026-08-03.

## Reviewed content

The catalog describes births, deaths, marriages, lists of communicants, and vestry minutes. The associated image group is accessible in the reviewed signed-in session.

The inspected typed index page for the Selby surname (viewer image 84) lists several Selby entries but does not list a Major Selby. This is only a limited check of one index page, not a complete negative search of the register or proof that no such entry exists.

## Reliability and limitations

The images are a digitized microfilm of a historical manuscript held by the Maryland Historical Society. The catalog and the inspected images establish that the parish-register collection exists and can be searched as a potential original-record source.

They do not, from the pages reviewed so far, establish a marriage between Major Selby and Harriet Mezick/Messick, identify either person's parents, or connect the later Indiana Major Selby to any Worcester County Major Selby. The FamilySearch marriage index remains a derivative index until the relevant original entry is located and read.

## Research use

Search the register systematically for the claimed 1817 marriage entry, including alternate spellings of Mezick/Messick and the possibility that Major was entered as a given name. Transcribe and cite the original page before adding any marriage or parentage claim.
