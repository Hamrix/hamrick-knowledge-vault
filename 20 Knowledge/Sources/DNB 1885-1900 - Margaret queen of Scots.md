## Relevant extracts

- Notes that she married Malcolm III of Scotland.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a historical biographical reference rather than a contemporary record. It independently corroborates Margaret's paternal relationship, but it should not be used as sole proof for disputed questions about her mother, birth, or more distant genealogy.
