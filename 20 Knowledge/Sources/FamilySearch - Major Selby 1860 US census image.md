## Record examined

The reviewed schedule lists Major Selby, age 79, born in Maryland, with Harriet Selby, age 64, born in Maryland, and Martha Selby, age 22, born in Indiana, consecutively in a Wayne Township household.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed digitized image of an original federal census schedule. It directly supports the names, ages, birthplaces, location, and shared enumeration shown on the page.

The 1860 schedule does not state relationships to the household head. It does not therefore prove that Harriet was Major's wife or that Martha was their daughter, despite the household pattern. It also does not identify this Maryland-born Major Selby with the separate adult Major Selby assessed in Worcester County, Maryland, in 1783, or prove parentage for either man.
