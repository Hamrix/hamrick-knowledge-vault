## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed image of an original federal census schedule. The 1840 census names only the household head and records age-and-sex tallies for the household. It does not state the names or relationships of other household members, a birthplace, a spouse, children, parents, or an exact age.

The spelling "Selbey" is the record's indexed name. This record does not by itself prove that the 1840 household head was the Major Selby enumerated in Hamilton County in 1850 and 1860, the father reported on James F. Selby's 1918 death certificate, the Major Selby named in Somerset County records, or the separate adult Major Selby assessed in Worcester County in 1783.
