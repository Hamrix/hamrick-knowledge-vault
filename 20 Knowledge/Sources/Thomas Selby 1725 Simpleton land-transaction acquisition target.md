# Thomas Selby 1725 Simpleton land-transaction acquisition target

## Purpose

This target isolates the reported 12 October 1725 Simpleton / Singleton land transaction as a potentially decisive generation-bridging record in the early colonial Selby line.

## Reported transaction

A derivative PDF/search snippet reports a deed or indenture dated **12 October 1725** involving:

- **Thomas Selby** of Somerset County, Maryland;
- **Margaret his wife**;
- **William Selby** of the same county;
- a tract called **Simpleton** or **Singleton**;
- location language around **Boquetenorton / Bogertennorton** on the seaside;
- language reportedly stating that an earlier **Thomas Selby**, eldest grandfather of the grantor, had a patent for 1,250 acres;
- language reportedly identifying the grantor as heir-at-law of his father and grandfather;
- transfer of remaining Simpleton land to William Selby.

## Why this record matters

If the original deed contains the reported language, it may directly or indirectly establish a generational structure:

1. earlier Thomas Selby, patent holder of Simpleton / Singleton;
2. an intermediate father of the 1725 grantor;
3. Thomas Selby grantor in 1725;
4. William Selby as purchaser or close associate.

This is potentially stronger than a compiled genealogy because land-title deeds sometimes recite heirship, descent, prior patents, and property succession.

## Required record

Obtain an image, archive-grade copy, or certified/archival transcription of the complete deed, including:

- full opening clause;
- parties and residences;
- wife Margaret's release/acknowledgment/dower language;
- land name and acreage;
- metes and bounds;
- prior patent history;
- all heir-at-law or grandfather/father language;
- consideration;
- signatures or marks;
- witnesses;
- acknowledgment and recording date;
- book/liber, folio/page, and archive citation.

## Likely repository

The official first route is Maryland State Archives or Somerset County land records for colonial-era Maryland deeds.

The request should include these variant tract and place names:

- Simpleton;
- Singleton;
- Boguetmorton;
- Boquetenorton;
- Bogertennorton;
- Selby's Bay.

## Evidence classification before acquisition

- 1725 Simpleton transaction: **specific derivative lead; original uninspected**.
- Thomas Selby grantor as grandson/heir-at-law of earlier Thomas: **reported lead only**.
- Link from this 1725 Thomas to the 1758 Thomas or Major Selby: **not established**.
