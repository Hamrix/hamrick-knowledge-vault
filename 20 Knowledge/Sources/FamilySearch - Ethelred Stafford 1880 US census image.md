# FamilySearch - Ethelred Stafford 1880 US census image

## Citation

1880 U.S. census, Orleans Parish, Louisiana, New Orleans enumeration district 5, sheet 125A, John E. Stafford household, Ethelred Stafford; NARA microfilm publication T9; digitized image, FamilySearch, [image](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GYBV-9KJ5), accessed 21 August 2026; associated [indexed record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MDXM-VP6).

## Record examined

The original census image was opened and visually reviewed with its attached index. The schedule enumerates Ethelred Stafford, age 18, born in Louisiana, as a son in the household of John E. Stafford, age 58, and Eveline Stafford, age 43. It also lists siblings including Forrest L., Leon L., John, Eugene, Susie, and Gertrude Stafford.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a digitized image of an original federal census schedule. The household and explicit `Son` relationship directly support the immediate parent-child link. The index renders the son's name as `Ethelreid`, but the age, household, locality, later marriage record, 1945 death index, and sibling names identify the same Ethelred Stafford.

The census supplies Eveline's married surname only. Her Hamrick maiden surname comes from the 1889 marriage license and 1945 death index.
