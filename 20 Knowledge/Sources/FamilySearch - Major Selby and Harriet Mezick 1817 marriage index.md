## Indexed information reviewed

The reviewed index entry names Major Selby and Harriet Mezick and gives 23 June 1817 as the marriage date. Its displayed event-place field says Calvert, Maryland, while its separate "Event Place (Original)" field says Worcester, Maryland.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a derivative index entry, not a reviewed marriage-license image, minister's return, or clerk's original list. The inconsistent displayed place fields show that the index cannot safely establish the location. The entry also does not distinguish a license issuance from a completed ceremony, identify the groom or bride beyond the indexed names, or state a parent-child relationship.

It does not establish that the named Major Selby was the same man later in Hamilton County, Indiana; that Harriet Mezick was the Harriet Selby in the 1850 or 1860 households; or that either person connects to the 1783 Worcester County taxpayer, Benjamin Selby, colonial Maryland Selbys, or a royal line.
