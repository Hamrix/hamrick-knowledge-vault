# FamilySearch - Major Selby 1812 and 1813 Bourbon tax lists

## Citation

Bourbon County, Kentucky, tax lists, 1812 and 1813; FamilySearch image group 007834402; 1812 section cover at image 407 and [Major Selby entry at image 459 of 1,174](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS3J-F9QF-6?view=fullText&keywords=Major+Selby&lang=en); 1813 section cover at image 471 and [Major Selby entry at image 529 of 1,174](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS3J-F9QX-S?view=fullText&keywords=Selby+Major%2CSelby&lang=en); accessed 8 August 2026.

## Records examined

The original section covers and tax pages were visually reviewed. Image 407 is labeled **Bourbon County 1812**, and image 459 contains a handwritten Major Selby row. Image 471 is labeled **Bourbon County 1813**, and image 529 contains a handwritten **Selby Major** row.

## Reliability and limitations

Together with the separately reviewed 1811 list, these originals establish three consecutive annual tax appearances for a person named Major Selby in Bourbon County. The rows do not name his wife, children, parents, former residence, or later residence. The sequence strongly supports continuity within the 1807-1815 Bourbon cluster but does not itself identify him with the Worcester ward or Nicholas County household.

The 1814 Bourbon section was located at image 546, but the relevant pages are dark and did not yield a responsibly readable Major Selby entry. No 1814 tax claim is made.
