# FamilySearch - Major Selby 1811 Bourbon tax list

## Citation

Bourbon County, Kentucky, tax list, 1811; FamilySearch image group 007834402, [image 390 of 1,174](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CS3J-F97P-1?view=fullText&keywords=Selby+Major%2CSelby&lang=en); volume cover identifying Bourbon County and 1811 at image 343; accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

The original tax page was visually reviewed. Its alphabetical list includes a handwritten entry for **Major Selby**. The section's original cover, image 343, is plainly labeled **Bourbon County 1811**.

## Reliability and limitations

The original tax list directly places a person named Major Selby in Bourbon County in 1811 and independently corroborates the same-year deed describing Major as of Bourbon County. The tax row does not name his wife, children, parents, former residence, or later residence. Name, county, and year make correlation with the 27 June 1811 purchaser strong, but the tax entry alone does not prove identity with the Worcester ward or any later Major Selby.
