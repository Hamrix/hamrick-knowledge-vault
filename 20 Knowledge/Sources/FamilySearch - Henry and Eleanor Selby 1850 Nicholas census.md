## Genealogical value

The same Henry-Eleanor name pair and county make this household a strong continuation of the couple who conveyed Henry's Brush Fork home place in 1838. Henry's reported age implies birth about 1784. That chronology is compatible with the 1809 groom but incompatible with Henry being a son of the Worcester Major who was about seven in 1785.

This age conflict is conditional on identifying the 1850 Henry with the 1838 deed grantor and the Henry named as Major's son in 1833. The first identification is very strong from the paired names, documented 1838 marriage, and county; the second remains unproved because no reviewed post-will record cross-references Major or his estate.

## Reliability and limitations

The original population schedule directly reports names, ages, birthplaces, and household placement. Ages and birthplaces are informant-supplied and may be approximate. The census does not name parents, define relationships, or prove that Henry and Eleanor were the 1809 Henry and Nelly.
