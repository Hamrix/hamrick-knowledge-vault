# FamilySearch - Henry Selby and Nelly Henderson 1809 Bourbon marriage bond

## Citation

Bourbon County, Kentucky, marriage bond, Henry Selby and Nelly Henderson, 5 June 1809, *Bourbon. Marriage Bonds 1807-1810*, [FamilySearch original image 637 of 949](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:9398-SL9Y-VR?view=explore&lang=en); [indexed record V5Z4-D7D](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:V5Z4-D7D?lang=en); accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

The bond says Henry Selby and Jesse Henderson were bound to Kentucky governor Charles Scott for 50 pounds on 5 June 1809. Its condition states that a marriage was intended shortly between Henry Selby and **Nelly Henderson of Bourbon County**.

Henry signed by mark. Jesse Henderson also signed by mark as the second obligor. The instrument does not state Jesse's relationship to Nelly.

## Genealogical value

The original supplies the bride's given name, which the FamilySearch index omits. It directly proves the intended Henry Selby-Nelly Henderson marriage and places both parties in the Bourbon County record system in 1809.

The name Nelly is compatible with Eleanor, and later Nicholas County records name Henry Selby's wife as Eleanor. The bond itself does not equate the two names. Jesse Henderson's participation, followed by Henry's 1830 purchases from Jesse Henderson's heirs, creates a strong Henderson-family-network correlation but does not by itself prove that Jesse was Nelly's father or brother.

## Reliability and limitations

The original bond is direct evidence for the parties, date, place, intended marriage, and surety. It is not a marriage return and does not prove that the ceremony occurred. It does not name either party's parents, ages, prior residences, or later children.
