# Kentucky House Journal - Ben Selby elected state librarian 1851

## Citation

Kentucky General Assembly, House of Representatives, *Journal of the House of Representatives of the Commonwealth of Kentucky*, 1850-1851 session, printed pages 277-280; Thomas B. Monroe Jr., ed., *A New Kentucky State Register* (Louisville, 1852), 32.

## Record examined

On 15 January 1851, House Journal page 277 records that J. F. Todd nominated **Mr. Ben. Selby** for state librarian. After multiple ballots, page 280 reports the joint vote as Harlan 47, Selby 69, and Harris 18 and declares Mr. Selby duly elected librarian.

The following year's state register lists **Benjamin Selby, of Washington County**, as state librarian, elected in 1851 for a term expiring in 1853 at a salary of $250.

## Identity analysis

These original publications prove the name, election, office, term, and reported county of the 1851 officeholder. They do not give his age, spouse, parents, or prior offices.

Two later biographies assign this office to different men. The 1878 Ben Selby sketch assigns it to the lawyer born in 1826 who later lived at Eminence. The Kentucky Auditor's modern biography assigns it to its Benjamin Shelby born in 1796, son of David Shelby Jr., who died in 1876. The original election record does not resolve that conflict. The state register's Washington County label also does not match the younger man's 1850 Trimble County enumeration and has not yet been independently connected to either candidate.

The librarian office must therefore remain an identity clue, not a unique identifier or parentage proof. The primary record corrects the spelling to Selby in this office sequence but does not prove which later biography is accurate.

## Reliability and limitations

The journal is the contemporary official legislative record and is direct evidence of the election. The 1852 register is a near-contemporary compiled state directory. Neither is a genealogical relationship record. Exact 1850-1851 FamilySearch residence searches for Benjamin Selby and Benjamin Shelby in Washington County returned no indexed result, but that bounded index search cannot disprove a short residence or a register error.
