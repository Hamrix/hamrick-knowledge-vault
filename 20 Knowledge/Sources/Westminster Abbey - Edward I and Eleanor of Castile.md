# Westminster Abbey - Edward I and Eleanor of Castile

## Citation

Westminster Abbey, "Edward I and Eleanor of Castile," accessed 2026-07-27, https://www.westminster-abbey.org/abbey-commemorations/royals/edward-i-and-eleanor-of-castile.

## Description

Institutional historical profile for Edward I and Eleanor of Castile, maintained by Westminster Abbey.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Edward as the eldest child of Henry III and Eleanor of Provence.
- Dates Edward's birth to 17 June 1239 at the Palace of Westminster.

## Reliability and limitations

Westminster Abbey records Edward I's royal commemoration and historical context. It provides direct support for the named parent-child relationship, but it does not prove a more distant pedigree or any modern descent.
