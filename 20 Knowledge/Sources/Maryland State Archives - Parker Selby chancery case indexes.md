# Maryland State Archives - Parker Selby chancery case indexes

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, *Chancery Court (Chancery Papers), 1795-1796*, MSA S512 index, accessed 2026-08-03, https://msa.maryland.gov/msa/stagser/s500/s512/html/s512c.html; and Maryland State Archives, *Chancery Court (Chancery Papers), 1807-1808*, MSA S512 index, accessed 2026-08-03, https://msa.maryland.gov/msa/stagser/s500/s512/html/s512i.html.

## Reviewed index entries

- 12 November 1795: *Joseph Delestatius and Sarah Delestatius v. John Selby*, Worcester County; estate of Parker Selby; tracts Venture, Cedar Grove, Proventun, and Robertsons Inheritence; Chancery Record 38, p. 341; accession 17,898-4668; MSA S512-6-4837; location 1/37/1.
- 22 July 1808: *John Selby v. Anthony Bacon and George S. Gunby*, Worcester County; estate of Parker Selby; tracts Prevention and Addition to Willetts Outlet; Chancery Record 105, p. 770; accession 17,898-4936; MSA S512-6-5087; location 1/37/1.

## Reliability and limitations

These are official archival index entries, useful for identifying and retrieving the underlying chancery papers and recorded chancery volumes. They do not provide the pleadings or establish the parties' kinship. Neither reviewed entry names Major Selby or the tracts Poplar Hill and Exon Enlarged.
