# FamilySearch - John Selby 1790 Worcester County will images

## Citation

"Worcester, Maryland, United States records," images, FamilySearch, image group 005080347, Worcester County Wills, 1783-1790, volume 13, images 138-139 of 142, will of John Selby, dated 20 November 1790 and proved 10 December 1790, Hall of Records (Annapolis, Maryland), accessed 2026-08-02, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9TBJ-SRVN?i=137&cc=1803986.

## Description

Digitized manuscript will record. The will occupies the opening and continuation pages recorded in images 138-139.

## Reviewed content

- The testator signs as John Selby; the probate entry identifies him as John Selby, Esquire, late of Worcester County.
- The will calls James Selby, Daniel Selby, and William Selby the testator's sons.
- The will is dated 20 November 1790. The probate entry records Ann Selby's oath on 10 December 1790.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary manuscript record reviewed in image form. It directly proves only the relationships and events expressed in this will. Although secondary reference material may suggest an identity for this testator, the reviewed will does not name his father. It therefore does not establish that he was the John Selby named in Thomas Selby's 1758 will, a son of Parker Selby, or a connection to Major Selby.
