## Retrieval targets

The 1 November 1793 distribution indexed for the Nancy-administered Parker Selby estate falls within **Distributions LH 3, 1792-1797, MSA C2007-2**. The external MSA guide supplies no public image link for that volume.

Any guardian accounts for George Selby and Elizabeth Selby during their minority fall within **Guardian Accounts JW 17, 1787-1796, MSA C2013-1**. The MSA guide states that guardianship records are restricted under Maryland Rule 16-914 and cannot be viewed except with a court order.

## Digital-archive search note

The Worcester County Library Digital Archive exposes a combined Register of Wills image run for 1789-1797, but an exact-phrase search for Parker Selby and an any-words search for John Gunby within 1793 returned no results. Because John Gunby is visibly present in the independently recovered 1793 account images, these are OCR false negatives and are not evidence that the distribution or guardian accounts are absent.

## Genealogical value and limits

These finding aids identify the exact volumes most likely to name distributees, heirs, or later guardian-account relationships. They do not themselves identify any heir, prove that Nancy was Parker's widow, equate Elizabeth with Betsey, or identify Parker's father. No lineage confidence changes until an underlying record is reviewed.
