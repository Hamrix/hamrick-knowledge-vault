# FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1780 estate order and appeal bond

## Citation

Worcester County Orphans' Court, distribution order and appeal bond concerning the estate of John Atkinson, August 1780, pages 132–133; FamilySearch images `3Q9M-CSYX-BSJ1-8` and `3Q9M-CSYX-BSVS-Q`.

## Record examined

The distribution order names Angelo Atkinson, John Atkinson, John Selby, William Atkinson Selby, Rachel Flemming, Mary White, and Nancy White as residuary legatees of John Atkinson. The 25 August bond repeats the group, recites John Atkinson's will dated 21 March 1779, and identifies widow and executrix Mary as the later wife of Levin Blake.

## Reliability and limitations

The originals directly prove the named legal group. They do not state each legatee's relationship to John Atkinson and cannot be converted into a complete child list.
