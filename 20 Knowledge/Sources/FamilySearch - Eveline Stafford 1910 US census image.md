# FamilySearch - Eveline Stafford 1910 US census image

## Citation

1910 U.S. census, Orleans Parish, Louisiana, New Orleans Ward 11, enumeration district 176, sheet 10A, household 203, Stafford household; NARA microfilm publication T624; digitized image, FamilySearch, [image 724 of 1242](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GRKS-9S8P), accessed 21 August 2026; associated [indexed household record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MPYV-P6X).

## Record examined

The original federal schedule was opened and visually reviewed with the attached record panel. It enumerates Ethelred H. Stafford, wife Fanny Stafford, and daughters Eveline Stafford and Margarite Stafford. Eveline is age 20, born about 1890 in Louisiana, single, and recorded as a daughter of the household head.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a digitized image of an original census schedule. The family group, relationship, age, birthplace, and locality agree with the 1890 birth index and 1900 household. The FamilySearch index renders the head's name as `Ethelared H Stafford`; the source note uses Ethelred H. only as the correlated normalized form supported by the birth and marriage records.

The census does not spell Eveline's first name as Sarah and predates her marriage, so the full identity conclusion depends on correlation with the birth index, July 1910 marriage, later records naming Sarah E., and Frederick's NUMIDENT record.
