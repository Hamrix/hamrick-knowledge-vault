# FamilySearch - Burns 1936 Nicholas deed abstract 1796-1818

## Citation

Annie Walker Burns, *Abstract of deeds, Nicholas County, Kentucky: 1796-1818* (1936), printed page 56, digital viewer page 58, [FamilySearch Digital Library](https://www.familysearch.org/library/books/records/item/550010-abstract-of-deeds-nicholas-county-kentucky), film 928557 item 8, DGS 7647178; [FamilySearch catalog record](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:77355); accessed 8 August 2026.

## Entries examined

The alphabetized Selby section abstracts a deed from Barkley Selby of Nicholas County to Elijah C. Dazey of Nicholas County, dated 26 June 1817 and recorded in Deed Book E, page 103. The original has now been reviewed in [[FamilySearch - Barkley and Patsey Selby Nicholas Haws survey deeds 1816-1820]]. No relationship to Major Selby is stated.

The same printed page abstracts a deed from Laban Shipp and wife Elizabeth of Bourbon County to Thomas Rule of Bourbon County, dated 14 October 1817 and recorded in Deed Book E, page 133. That entry led to the original deed on FamilySearch images 358-359.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a derivative typed abstract, not the deed book. It was useful as a locator but omitted Barkley's wife Patsey, acreage, Haws-patent language, neighbors, and warranty limits. It should not support a negative conclusion by itself. Barkley is not assigned to Major Selby's family.

## Result of follow-up

The original page 103-104 deed was retrieved. It identifies Patsey as Barkley's wife and the 18.5 acres as part of a Samuel Haws patent. Additional originals establish Barkley's acquisition of the parent 116-acre tract in 1816 and continued possession of the 97.5-acre residue in October 1820.
