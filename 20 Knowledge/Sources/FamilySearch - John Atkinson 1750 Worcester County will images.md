# FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1750 Worcester County will images

## Citation

"Worcester, Maryland, United States records," images, FamilySearch, image group 005080347, Worcester County Wills, 1742-1759, volume 2, image 40 of 108, will of John Atkinson, dated 15 February 1750 and proved 6 March 1750, Hall of Records (Annapolis, Maryland), accessed 2026-08-02, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBJ-9HB1?i=39&cc=1803986.

## Description

Digitized manuscript will record. John Atkinson's will begins on the lower part of the left-hand page of image 40 and continues on the right-hand page.

## Reviewed content

- The testator identifies himself as John Atkinson of Worcester County, innholder.
- He calls Levinah Selby the wife of William Selby, the younger.
- He directs that, after his wife's death, the real estate be divided between William and John Selby, described as sons of Levinah.
- The will is dated 15 February 1750; the witnesses' probate oath is dated 6 March 1750.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary manuscript record reviewed in image form. It directly proves only the relationships expressly stated in the will. It does not identify Major Selby, establish the identity of every later William or John Selby, or bridge this household to the 1701, 1725, or 1758 Thomas Selby records.
