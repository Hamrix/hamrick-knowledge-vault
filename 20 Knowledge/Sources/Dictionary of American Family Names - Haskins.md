# Dictionary of American Family Names - Haskins

## Citation

Patrick Hanks et al., *Dictionary of American Family Names*, 2nd ed. (Oxford University Press, 2022), s.v. "Haskins," hosted by Ancestry.com, accessed 2026-07-27, https://www.ancestry.com/last-name-meaning/haskins.

## Description

Specialist surname-dictionary entry for Haskins, displayed separately from Ancestry member-tree material.

## Relevant extracts

- Gives an English explanation: a variant of Haskin with a post-medieval excrescent final *-s*.
- Notes that the surname is sometimes adopted for Irish O hUiscin.
- Also gives an alternative, more probable explanation in one context as an altered diminutive of Fuarghus.

## Reliability and limitations

The cited dictionary is an expert-authored reference work published by Oxford University Press. Its entry records multiple possible origins; it does not select an origin for a particular Haskins family, establish that all bearers share one origin, or prove any relationship to the project families. It is therefore retained as an uncertainty source, not used to support a lineage or a single surname-origin claim.
