# William Atkinson Selby colonial Maryland proof path lead

## Purpose

This note preserves the working proof path for William Atkinson Selby while explicitly separating proof from compiled-tree narrative. The objective is to prove each relationship with original or near-original records before using William Atkinson Selby as a bridge into the earlier Maryland/Somerset/Worcester Selby network.

## User-supplied proof-path lead

The user supplied the following proposed backward structure on 2026-08-02:

1. William Atkinson Selby, reportedly born 1757 in Somerset County, Maryland, and died 1809 in Bourbon County, Kentucky.
2. William reportedly served as a Private in the Maryland Militia during the Revolutionary War.
3. William reportedly married Sarah White Townsend on 28 February 1782 in Snow Hill, Worcester County, Maryland.
4. William's parents are proposed as John Selby of Nassawadux / Pocomoke Hundred, Worcester County, Maryland, and Mary Atkinson.
5. The Atkinson middle name is proposed as an onomastic clue supporting Mary Atkinson as mother.
6. The next prior generation is proposed as John Selby and Margaret.
7. The next prior generation is proposed as Thomas Selby Jr., died about 1758 in Maryland.
8. The next prior generation is proposed as Thomas Selby Sr., died 1702, formerly of Accomack County, Virginia, then Somerset County, Maryland.

## Critical tree-error warning

It must not be used as ancestry, evidence, or narrative support for William Atkinson Selby born about 1757.
