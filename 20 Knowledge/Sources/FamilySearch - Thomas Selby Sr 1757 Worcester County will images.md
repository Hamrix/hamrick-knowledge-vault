# FamilySearch - Thomas Selby Sr 1757 Worcester County will images

## Citation

"Worcester, Maryland, United States records," images, FamilySearch, image group 005080347, images 318-319 of 426, Hall of Records (Annapolis, Maryland), Maryland County Court (Worcester County), accessed 2026-07-31, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBJ-9HFX?view=fullText&keywords=Thomas+Selby&lang=en.

## Description

Digitized manuscript pages in Worcester County probate records. The pages contain Thomas Selby Sr.'s will and its probate notation.

## Reviewed content

- The first page identifies the testator as "Thomas Selby Senr" of Worcester County, planter.
- In the first dispositive clause, Thomas gives the plantation where John lived, containing 120 acres, "unto my Son John Selby."
- The continuation names Daniel, Ezekiel, Henry, Thomas, William, Ann, Peggy, and wife Marget; it appoints Ezekiel and Marget as executors/executrix.
- The continuation records the subscribing witnesses' oath on 8 March 1758 and the widow's election on 24 March 1758.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary manuscript record, reviewed in image form with FamilySearch's machine transcription used only as navigation and reading assistance. The digitized volume is Worcester County material; it is not identified in its own metadata as the statewide Maryland Prerogative Court will book, Liber 30, folio 458. It directly proves only the stated Thomas-to-John relationship and the other relationships expressly written in the will. It does not prove Thomas's parentage, ancestry, or a connection to any other Selby line.
