# FamilySearch - Major Selby 1820 Nicholas census pages 111 and 129

## Citation

1820 United States census, Nicholas County, Kentucky, pages 111 and 129; FamilySearch images `33S7-9YYJ-TY9` and `33SQ-GYYJ-BZS`; NARA microfilm publication M33.

## Record examined

The preserved page 111 image contains a handwritten **Major Selby** household. Its free-white age columns record:

- one male age 26 through 44;
- one female age 10 through 15; and
- one female age 26 through 44.

No other free-white age column contains a person in that household.

The age-column interpretation was checked against the National Archives' [1820 census question list](https://www.archives.gov/research/census/1820), which defines the relevant categories as male 26 and under 45, female 10 and under 16, and female 26 and under 45.

The separately preserved page 129 image was reviewed from top to bottom. It does **not** contain a handwritten Major Selby entry. The second indexed image is therefore a nonmatching or mislinked image, not a second confirmed census occurrence.

## Reliability and limitations

Page 111 is a reviewed image of an original federal census schedule and directly supports the name and age-column profile. The page 129 review resolves the former two-entry uncertainty: only one Major Selby household is visible in the two preserved images.

The schedule does not name household members other than the head or state relationships, exact ages, birthplaces, or previous residence. The age profile is compatible with, but does not identify this man as, the minor described as about seven in the 1785 Worcester guardianship record. A likely later Henry Selby's estimated 1784 birth creates a separate conflict against automatically treating this 26-44-year-old householder as the 1833 testator who called Henry his son. The census also does not by itself identify him with the 1811 Bourbon purchaser, the 1825 father of Polly, or the later Indiana man.
