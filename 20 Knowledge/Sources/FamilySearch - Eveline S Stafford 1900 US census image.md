# FamilySearch - Eveline S Stafford 1900 US census image

## Citation

1900 U.S. census, Orleans Parish, Louisiana, New Orleans, enumeration district 119, sheet 11A, lines 3-6, Stafford household; NARA microfilm publication T623; digitized image, FamilySearch, [image 27 of 680](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:S3HT-6SG3-9LY), accessed 21 August 2026; associated [indexed child record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MS55-9NW).

## Record examined

The original federal schedule was opened, magnified, and visually reviewed. It enumerates a Stafford household headed by the man indexed as Auther Stafford, with wife Fannie and daughters Eveline S. Stafford and Margaret J. Stafford. Eveline is age 10 and was born in Louisiana in March 1890. Fannie's entry reports that she had borne two children and that both were living.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a digitized image of an original census schedule. Eveline's name, daughter relationship, age, birth month and year, and Louisiana birthplace are legible and consistent with the 1890 birth index. The household head's given name is difficult to read and the FamilySearch index renders it as `Auther`; this source is not used by itself to prove that spelling or to equate it with Ethelred H. Stafford.

As with all census evidence, the informant is not identified and the record reports household information rather than a vital event.
