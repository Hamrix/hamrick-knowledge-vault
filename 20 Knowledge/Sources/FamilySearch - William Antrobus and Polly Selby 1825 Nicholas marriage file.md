# FamilySearch - William Antrobus and Polly Selby 1825 Nicholas marriage file

## Citation

Nicholas County, Kentucky, marriage authorization and bond for William Antrobus and Polly Selby, 16 August 1825; FamilySearch images `33SQ-G1GN-YWN` and `33S7-L1GN-P78`.

## Record examined

The authorization records Major Selby's personal consent for his daughter Polly's marriage. The bond was executed by William Antrobus and Major Selby, with Major signing by mark.

## Reliability and limitations

The two original documents independently establish Major Selby as Polly's father. Margaret Selby's 1836 will later calls Mary Antrobus her daughter and William Preston Antrobus her grandson. That record strongly correlates Mary with this Antrobus family but does not literally identify Mary as Polly. The records do not name William Atkinson Selby and do not prove a relationship between Major and William.
