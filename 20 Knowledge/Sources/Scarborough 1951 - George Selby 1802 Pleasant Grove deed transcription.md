# Scarborough 1951 - George Selby 1802 Pleasant Grove deed transcription

## Citation

Jewel Davis Scarborough, *Southern Kith and Kin: A Record of My Children's Ancestors*, volume 1 (1951), 176-179, transcription of George Selby to Marshall Smith, 8 October 1802, Worcester County Deed Liber V, folios 236-238; [digital scan](https://www.seekingmyroots.com/members/files/G003924.pdf), accessed 13 August 2026.

## Reported deed content

The printed transcription identifies grantor George Selby as the eldest son and heir at law of deceased Parker Selby. It says Parker had contracted to sell 150 acres called *Pleasant Grove* to Philip Parker; George inherited Parker's interest and completed the conveyance to Marshall Smith on 8 October 1802. The transcription closes with an enrollment statement identifying Worcester Deed Liber V, folios 236-238.

## Genealogical value

If accurate to the original, the deed independently confirms Parker-to-George and establishes George as Parker's eldest son and heir at law. It also supplies a land title that may distinguish Parker from same-named Worcester men.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a page-image review of a printed deed transcription, not the original deed image. It provides a precise archival target and detailed legal wording, but transcription error remains possible. The father-son statement is therefore used as corroboration and a retrieval lead until Liber V, folios 236-238 are inspected directly.

[[FamilySearch Catalog - Worcester Land Records Liber V-W]] now identifies that original as FamilySearch film 14543 / DGS 8141714 and Maryland State Archives CE30-21. The signed-in FamilySearch catalog restricts the film to a FamilySearch Center or affiliate library, so the original was not reviewed on 13 August 2026 and this source's evidentiary status does not change.

The book's surrounding family conclusions are not treated as independent evidence for this Selby line.
