# DNB 1895 - Edward I

## Citation

T. F. Tout, "Edward I (1239-1307)," in *Dictionary of National Biography*, 1885-1900, vol. 17 (1895), accessed 2026-07-27, https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Dictionary_of_National_Biography,_1885-1900/Edward_I.

## Description

Published biographical entry on Edward I by historian T. F. Tout, digitized by Wikisource.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Edward I as the eldest son of Henry III and Eleanor of Provence.
- Dates his birth to 17-18 June 1239 at Westminster.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a historical scholarly biography rather than a contemporary record. It independently corroborates the direct parent-child relationship, but the 1895 entry should not be treated as sole proof for disputed or more distant genealogy.
