# Whiting 2021 - Lincolnshire eyre dower litigation

## Citation

Andrew Whiting, *The origins and development of professional legal attorneys by reference to thirteenth-century eyre proceedings* (DPhil thesis, University of Oxford, Trinity 2021), printed page 62, DOI 10.5287/ora-jnvap1b9r.

## Description

Doctoral study of legal attorneys and eyre procedure that uses Nicholas and Sybil's repeated dower litigation as a case example.

## Relevant finding

Whiting identifies nine entries in the 1218-1219 Lincolnshire eyre in which Nicholas de Chavincurt and his wife Sybil sought restitution of dower from Sybil's previous marriage. He cites *Rolls of the Justices in Eyre*, nos. 524, 531, 581 twice, 618, 648, 701, 705, and 912.

## Reliability and limitations

This independently confirms a repeated Nicholas-and-Sybil dower litigation pattern and identifies the exact eyre entries to inspect. The reviewed passage does not name Sybil's previous husband or call her `de Kyme`; it therefore cannot prove either fact by itself. The underlying eyre entries have not yet been directly reviewed.

## Claims supported

- [[Sibilla wife of Nicholas de Schauencurt was formerly wife of Walter de Ver]] - context and repeated-litigation corroboration only.

## Claims contradicted

None directly. The reviewed passage does not identify Walter or use `de Kyme`.
