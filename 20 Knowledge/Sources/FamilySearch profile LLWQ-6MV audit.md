## Correct source classification

This is a **FamilySearch Family Tree person profile**, not an indexed historical record. Family Tree profiles are collaborative compiled identities that may aggregate user-entered conclusions and attached source records.

The profile is therefore a useful identity lead and source-discovery hub, but the profile itself does not independently prove:

- the preferred spelling of the full name;
- birth date or birthplace;
- death date or death place;
- parentage;
- military service;
- residence history.

Each conclusion must be traced to and evaluated against its attached census, obituary, Social Security, military, civil, cemetery, probate, or other record.
