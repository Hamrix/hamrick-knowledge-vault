## 5. BIRLS death-file entry

### Current lead

The GEDCOM attaches a Department of Veterans Affairs BIRLS entry and converts two dates into generic military-service events:

- 18 July 1941;
- 15 February 1943.

BIRLS is a benefits-related VA database containing beneficiary records, including veterans whose survivors sought death benefits. In the derivative database, the paired military dates normally represent an entry/enlistment date and a release date, but the exact Frederic record must be inspected before assigning those meanings.

### Required extraction

Record the exact name, birth and death dates, SSN or VA file number if releasable, branch or conflict field if present, and every entry/release period.

### Conflict requiring resolution

A purported release date in February 1943 does not explain a Navy muster-roll event dated 10 April 1946. Possible explanations include multiple service periods, a reserve or station record, a database interpretation problem, or attachment of records belonging to different men.

### Evidentiary rule

BIRLS can support service dates and identify a VA claims file, but it is not a complete military personnel record and cannot establish ship, unit, rating, duties, POW status, intelligence work, or overseas service by itself.

## 8. Official Military Personnel File

### Why decisive

The OMPF can establish date and type of enlistment, service number, branch, duty stations, assignments, training, qualifications, awards, administrative remarks, and separation or discharge. It usually does not provide a detailed narrative of combat participation.

### Archival status

If Frederic separated in or before 1946, the file is more than 62 years old and is archival. Archival OMPFs are open to anyone for a copying fee; a next-of-kin signature is not required.

### Request procedure and current fees

Submit an archival OMPF request through the National Archives military-record request system or with Standard Form 180 to the National Personnel Records Center, St. Louis.

Current complete-file fees:

- routine OMPF of five pages or fewer: $25;
- routine OMPF of six pages or more: $70; most OMPFs fall into this category.
