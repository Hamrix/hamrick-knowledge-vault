# Maryland State Archives - Thomas Selby 1757 will abstract

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, "Abstracts of Maryland Wills - Liber 29 1753-1755," entry for Thomas Selby Sr., Worcester County, will dated 15 December 1757 and proved 8 March 1758, Liber 30, folio 458, accessed 2026-07-31, https://msa.maryland.gov/msa/stagser/s500/s538/html/s538-30.html.

## Description

Official published abstract of the probate record for Thomas Selby Sr. of Worcester County, Maryland.

## Relevant extracts

- The abstract expressly gives the plantation where John lived "to son John Selby."
- It separately names sons Daniel, Ezekiel, Henry, Thomas, and William, and daughters Ann and Peggy.

## Reliability and limitations

The Maryland State Archives abstract is a reliable derivative of a primary probate record and directly reports the parent-child wording. It is not a substitute for reviewing the original Liber 30 folio 458 image or a certified transcription, which remains necessary for a final proof-standard conclusion and for all details beyond the explicit relationship.
