# FamilySearch - Henderson heir 1828 deed to Henry Selby

## Citation

Nicholas County, Kentucky, Deed Book H, pages 285-286, Levin Henderson to Henry Selby, 6 December 1828, [FamilySearch image 180 of 612, DGS 8402419](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSBY-Q921-8?view=fullText&keywords=Henry%2CSelby&lang=en); [FamilySearch catalog record](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:432725); accessed 9 August 2026.

## Record examined

The recorded deed identifies the grantor as **Levin Henderson** of Franklin County, Indiana, and Henry Selby as a resident of Nicholas County, Kentucky. The original Jesse Henderson division independently writes the same heir's name as Levin. FamilySearch's automated transcript alternates among "Seven," "Leven," and "Levin" and is corrected from the two original records.

For $65, the grantor conveyed Henry one equal part of the lands of Jesse Henderson deceased. The deed says commissioners had laid off the share and identifies it as lot 4. The acreage appears to be 6.25 acres, consistent with the eight-part division; the automated transcript renders it as 64 acres and is not relied upon for that figure. The grantor signed the deed, and the clerk recorded an acknowledgment dated 6 December 1828.

## Genealogical value

The deed directly places Henry Selby in Nicholas County and acquiring a Jesse Henderson estate share by December 1828, earlier than the two 1830 Henderson-heir purchases. It also places the grantor in Franklin County, Indiana, the same county later given for Susan Henderson Williams and William G. Henderson.

This strengthens the Henderson family-network correlation with Henry's 1809 marriage bond to Nelly Henderson, on which Jesse Henderson was co-obligor. It does not state Jesse's relationship to Nelly, Henry's relationship to the grantor, or Henry's Selby parentage.

## Reliability and limitations

The original recorded deed directly supplies the parties, residences, consideration, Jesse Henderson estate context, equal-share language, and lot number. Levin's name and the 6.25-acre share are independently resolved by the original 1821 division.

The 1828 deed assigns lot 4 to Levin. The original 1821 division assigns William Henderson lot 6, resolving FamilySearch's automated lot-4 rendering in William's 1830 deed as an OCR error.
