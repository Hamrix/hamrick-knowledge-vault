# Kentucky Auditor - historical auditor list

## Citation

Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts, "History of the Auditor's Office," historical officeholder list, accessed 14 August 2026.

## Record examined

The official list gives "Ben Shelby" as Kentucky auditor from 1820 through 1834 and Thomas S. Page as auditor from 1834 through 1846. The linked one-page biography identifies the auditor as Benjamin Shelby, born 8 April 1796, husband of Nancy Enocx from 1824, son of David Shelby Jr., and deceased 4 May 1876. It also assigns this Benjamin the state-librarian office in 1851.

[[Kentucky Senate journals - Benjamin Selby auditor appointment and death succession 1834-1846|Visually reviewed original Senate journals]] contradict that chronology and identification. They show Porter Clay still serving as auditor through 1833, Governor John Breathitt nominating **Benjamin Selby** in Clay's place on 4 January 1834, Senate consent on 14 January, a 10 October 1834 auditor statement signed by Benjamin Selby, and Harry I. Bodley's January 1846 appointment in place of **Ben. Selby, deceased**. A contemporaneous newspaper reprint independently reports that Benjamin Selby, Auditor of State, died 25 May 1845.

[[Kentucky House Journal - Ben Selby elected state librarian 1851|Contemporary records]] now verify that the 1851 officeholder was elected as **Ben Selby**, and the 1852 state register calls him **Benjamin Selby, of Washington County**. Those original records do not give age, spouse, parents, or prior office. They therefore do not prove that the librarian was the Auditor biography's 1796-1876 Benjamin Shelby. The 1878 biography independently assigns the same librarian office to the younger lawyer born in 1826, creating an unresolved identity conflict between the two later accounts.

## Reliability and limitations

These are current official historical summaries, not a contemporaneous commission or cited genealogical proof. Their auditor chronology is now directly contradicted by original legislative records, and the linked biography's 1796-1876 identity cannot describe the Benjamin Selby whom the Senate appointed in 1834 and replaced after death in 1846. The linked biography's librarian assignment is also uncorroborated by the original election record, which names the officeholder but does not identify him with the 1796-1876 man.

The official webpage and linked PDF appear to contain a materially incorrect chronology or same-name conflation. They should not be used to identify Hon. Benjamin's parents. The original journals now independently corroborate the 1878-1883 office chronology, but they name no relatives and do not prove the younger-to-elder relationship.
