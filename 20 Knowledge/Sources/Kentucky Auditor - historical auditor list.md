# Kentucky Auditor - historical auditor list

## Citation

Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts, "History of the Auditor's Office," historical officeholder list, accessed 14 August 2026.

## Record examined

The official list gives "Ben Shelby" as Kentucky auditor from 1820 through 1834.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a current official historical summary, not a contemporaneous commission or legislative journal. The surname spelling and 1820-1834 dates conflict with the 1878 biography's statement that the elder Benjamin Selby served from 1834 through 1845. The officeholder identity and dates require original legislative or executive records before either chronology is treated as settled.
