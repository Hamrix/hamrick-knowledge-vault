# Kentucky Auditor - historical auditor list

## Citation

Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts, "History of the Auditor's Office," historical officeholder list, accessed 14 August 2026.

## Record examined

The official list gives "Ben Shelby" as Kentucky auditor from 1820 through 1834 and Thomas S. Page as auditor from 1834 through 1846. The linked one-page biography identifies the auditor as Benjamin Shelby, born 8 April 1796, husband of Nancy Enocx from 1824, son of David Shelby Jr., and deceased 4 May 1876. It also assigns this Benjamin the state-librarian office in 1851.

[[Kentucky House Journal - Ben Selby elected state librarian 1851|Contemporary records]] now verify that the 1851 officeholder was elected as **Ben Selby**, and the 1852 state register calls him **Benjamin Selby, of Washington County**. Those original records do not give age, spouse, parents, or prior office. They therefore do not prove that the librarian was the Auditor biography's 1796-1876 Benjamin Shelby. The 1878 biography independently assigns the same librarian office to the younger lawyer born in 1826, creating an unresolved identity conflict between the two later accounts.

## Reliability and limitations

These are current official historical summaries, not a contemporaneous commission or cited genealogical proof. The linked biography's librarian assignment is now specifically uncorroborated by the original election record, which names the officeholder but does not identify him with the 1796-1876 man. It may contain a same-name conflation, but the present evidence does not establish which later biography is wrong.

The biography's dates and family description plainly do not describe the elder Benjamin whom the 1878 biography says died in 1845. Thomas S. Page's listed 1834-1846 term also makes the 1878 claim that the elder Benjamin served from 1834 until 1845 impossible as written. Original auditor appointment, resignation, payroll, and executive records remain necessary.
