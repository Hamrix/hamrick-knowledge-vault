# Kentucky Auditor - historical auditor list

## Citation

Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts, "History of the Auditor's Office," historical officeholder list, accessed 14 August 2026.

## Record examined

The official list gives "Ben Shelby" as Kentucky auditor from 1820 through 1834 and Thomas S. Page as auditor from 1834 through 1846. The linked one-page biography identifies the auditor as Benjamin Shelby, born 8 April 1796, husband of Nancy Enocx from 1824, son of David Shelby Jr., and deceased 4 May 1876. It also reports that this Benjamin served as state librarian in 1851.

## Reliability and limitations

These are current official historical summaries, not a contemporaneous commission, legislative journal, or cited genealogical proof. The linked biography may itself contain a same-name or state-librarian conflation and therefore cannot establish ancestry. Even so, its dates and family description plainly do not describe the elder Benjamin whom the 1878 biography says died in 1845. Thomas S. Page's listed 1834-1846 term also makes the 1878 claim that the elder Benjamin served from 1834 until 1845 impossible as written. Original appointment, resignation, payroll, and legislative records remain necessary.
