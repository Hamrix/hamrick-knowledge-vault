# FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1791 Worcester de bonis non bond

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, administration de bonis non of Parker Selby, 15 June 1791, probate bonds 1786-1793, manuscript page 200; FamilySearch image group 005080351, image [`33S7-9T1P-CX6`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9T1P-CX6?view=fullText&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

A renunciation and the following bond identify the estate as that of **Parker Selby, son of Captain John Selby, deceased**. The bond appoints James Selby and John Gunby as administrators de bonis non and states that the estate had been left unadministered by **John Selby, Esquire**, the former administrator, who was also deceased. The administrators and their sureties executed the bond on 15 June 1791.

## Genealogical value

The instrument directly establishes a father-child statement for the particular deceased Parker in this estate: Captain John Selby was his father. It also links the unfinished Parker estate administratively to the recently deceased John Selby, Esquire.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary probate bond, but it does not state that John Selby, Esquire, was Parker's son, brother, or other relative. Multiple men named Parker and John Selby were active in Worcester County. The record therefore cannot be used alone to identify this Parker as the ca. 1746/47 father reported in the legislative biography, the Parker whose will was proved in 1773, or any other same-named person.
