# FamilySearch and KDLA - Lingan Selby Adair probate sequence audit 1821

## Primary index citation

Adair County, Kentucky, *Index to Order Books*, Selby entries, FamilySearch image 471 of 604, Image Group Number `008151273`, ARK `3:1:3Q9M-CSKX-JSXD-R`, accessed 20 August 2026.

## Recorded estate sequence

The original county-court order index lists exactly four entries under `Selby, Lingan W. (Est.)`:

| Year | Nature of order | Volume:page |
|---|---|---|
| 1821 | Appointment of administrator | D:213 |
| 1821 | Appointment of appraisers | D:213 |
| 1821 | Inventory and appraisement | D:238 |
| 1821 | Allotment to widow | D:239 |

These references match the already reviewed original orders. Book D, page 213 grants Benjamin Selby administration and appoints appraisers; pages 238-239 record the inventory return and identify Mary Selby as Lingan's widow. The index assigns no additional Lingan estate entry for a sale, settlement, account, distribution, heir, partition, or guardianship.

The same index page includes other Selby civic and probate entries. It does not identify Benjamin as Lingan's son, heir, child, or relative. It also does not connect Drucilla Fowler to this estate or equate that name with the directly recorded widow Mary Selby.

## Catalog coverage audit

- The Adair place catalog exposes eight probate titles. Its original-record holdings are will indexes and will books; the other titles are compiled abstracts or transcriptions. Catalog `128755` identifies DGS `004818715` as the will index and DGS `004818716` as wills A-C, 1804-1836.
- County-court catalog `128524` identifies DGS `008151273` as the order index and DGS `008685666` as Order Books D-E, 1817-1833. No separate Adair administrator-bond, executor-bond, settlement-book, distribution-book, sale-bill, or loose-estate-packet film is listed.
- The only Adair guardianship catalog title is Carolyn Gupton and Lila Ford's 2007, 130-page transcription *Guardian bonds and guardian requests*. The catalog offers a physical FamilySearch Library copy, not digital images of the underlying records.
- Circuit-court catalog `128406` identifies DGS `008690249` as the general index and DGS `008685675` as Order Books E-F, 1817-1826. Exact `Lingan` full-text searches returned no result in either group. Every one of the thirteen Selby-bearing general-index transcript results was swept for `Lingan`, `Ligan`, and `Kingan`; none contained a variant. This remains OCR- and transcript-dependent and is not proof that no unindexed civil file exists.

## Archival gap

Kentucky's current county-clerk retention schedule describes pre-1870 unbound estate-settlement files as potentially containing maps, deeds, family information, correspondence, and other material not recorded in the bound books. KDLA's county-record inventory confirms Adair holdings beginning in 1802 but expressly states that the public inventory is not comprehensive and that probate records can be filed under several titles.

The online recorded sequence is therefore substantially audited, but the complete surviving estate cannot be claimed until Adair or KDLA staff search the unbound files and related civil-case holdings. [[Lingan Wilson Selby Adair loose probate request packet - 2026-08-20]] preserves the exact request scope.

## Relationship conclusion

No relationship-level evidence was found. Benjamin's administrator role remains direct association evidence only. Hon. Benjamin Selby must not be merged as Lingan's son or heir on the basis of this sequence.
