# FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1746 York County will

## Citation

Parker Selby will, made 8 November 1746 and proved 16 March 1746/47, York County, Virginia, *Wills and Inventories 20, 1745-1759*, manuscript pages 64-65, FamilySearch image 50 of 295, image group 004027493, [`3QS7-892Z-ZRN9`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QS7-892Z-ZRN9?view=fullText&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

Parker described himself as living in Worcester County near Snow Hill, Maryland. He named:

- "my Eldest Son John Selly," to whom he devised the 750-acre home plantation;
- sons William, James, Micajah, Parker, and Zadock;
- daughter Tabitha Baily Selby; and
- wife Mary.

He appointed Mary and sons John and Parker joint executors. John presented the will in York County and qualified when it was proved.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary recorded will and directly proves Parker's stated relationship to eldest son John and the other named children. The original image was visually reviewed. The handwritten date is 1746, and the probate date is 16 March 1746/47; automated handwriting text misread those dates.

The will does not call this eldest son the later "John Selby Esq." or state a 1790 death date. Identifying the son with the Worcester legislator and 1790 testator requires correlation with later primary records and the Maryland State Archives biography.
