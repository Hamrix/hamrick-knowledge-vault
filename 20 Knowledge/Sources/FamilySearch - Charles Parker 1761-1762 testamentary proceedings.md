# FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1761-1762 testamentary proceedings

## Citation

Maryland Prerogative Court, *Testamentary Proceedings*, Liber 38, folios 93, 133, 163, and 328, 1761-1762; FamilySearch DGS 007737829, images 656, 676, 691, and 775 of 1,120; indexed in Maryland State Archives, Probate Records, Colonial, Index, volume 16, page 108, https://msa.maryland.gov/megafile/msa/stagser/s500/s530/000000/000016/pdf/mdsa_s530_16.pdf; FamilySearch images cited below; accessed 2026-08-13.

## Record examined

The MSA person index assigns the same four folios, **93-133-163-328**, to both Charles Parker and Collins Parker of Worcester County for 1761-1762. Direct review of each manuscript folio shows that the list combines estate records with later appearances by Charles Parker as a surety:

- **Folio 93:** abstracts the administration bond for deceased Charles Parker. Collins Parker is administrator, with living Charles Parker and John Parker as sureties in fifty pounds sterling. The entry is dated 12 February 1761. FamilySearch image 656: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C914-K4ZF-8?view=fullText&keywords=Collins%2CParker&searchForm=advanced&lang=en.
- **Folio 133:** records Charles Parker and Samuel Dreaden as sureties for Mary Marsden, administratrix of Angus Marsden. The surname is independently legible in the Maryland colonial probate index as `Dreaden`. This entry does not concern administration of Charles Parker's estate. FamilySearch image 676: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C914-K4ZV-1?view=fullText&keywords=Collins%2CParker&searchForm=advanced&lang=en.
- **Folio 163:** records that Charles Parker's inventory was among Worcester County proceedings exhibited by deputy commissary Benton Harris and ordered to be recorded. It supplies no heir or relationship statement. FamilySearch image 691: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C914-K4FC-3?view=fullText&keywords=Collins%2CParker&searchForm=advanced&lang=en.
- **Folio 328:** records Charles Parker and Adam Stevenson as sureties for Daniel Burgess, administrator of Micajah Burgess, on a bond dated 11 March 1762. It does not concern administration of Charles Parker's estate. FamilySearch image 775: https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-C914-K4XQ-T?view=fullText&keywords=Collins%2CParker&searchForm=advanced&lang=en.

## Reliability and limitations

These are contemporary Prerogative Court entries summarizing proceedings returned from Worcester County. Folios 93 and 163 independently corroborate the administration and inventory sequence already established from the county record books. Folios 133 and 328 establish only that a Charles Parker acted as surety in two other estates; they do not identify him beyond name and county.

The shared index folio string must not be treated as four relationship-bearing records for both Charles and Collins Parker. Only folio 93 names Collins Parker, and none of the four folios states a kinship between Collins, either living surety, and the deceased Charles Parker. The reviewed folios do not contain the indexed May 1762 estate account or any distribution language.
