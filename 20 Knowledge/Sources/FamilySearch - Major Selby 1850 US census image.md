## Record examined

The reviewed schedule has a household headed by handwriting read as **Major Selby**. The index renders his surname as "Gilby." The household lists:

- Major Selby, 67, male, farmer, born Maryland;
- Harriet Selby, 52, female, born Maryland;
- Ellen Selby, 30, female, born Maryland;
- Edwin Selby, 16, male, born Maryland; and
- Martha Selby, 12, female, born Indiana.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed image of an original federal census schedule. It directly supports the names as written/read from the image, reported ages, birthplaces, occupations, property column entries, location, and shared enumeration.

The 1850 census has no relationship-to-head column. It does not prove that Harriet was Major's wife, that Ellen, Edwin, or Martha were their children, or that this Major Selby was the father reported on James F. Selby's 1918 death certificate. The surname reading is also an image interpretation because the index calls the household "Gilby."

It does not identify this man with the separate adult Major Selby assessed in Worcester County, Maryland, in 1783; prove the 1817 license entry; establish parentage; or connect any person to Benjamin Selby, colonial Maryland Selbys, or a royal line.
