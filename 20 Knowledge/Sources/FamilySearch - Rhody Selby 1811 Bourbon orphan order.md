# FamilySearch - Rhody Selby 1811 Bourbon orphan order

## Citation

Bourbon County, Kentucky, court order dated 7 January 1811, binding out Rhody Selby, infant orphan of W. A. Selby, deceased; FamilySearch image `3Q9M-CSLX-V5KP`.

## Record examined

The original manuscript directly calls Rhody Selby the infant orphan of W. A. Selby, deceased. It records that Rhody would be eleven on the following 17 April, implying a birth near 17 April 1800.

## Reliability and limitations

This is direct evidence for the record's relationship statement and for William's death before 7 January 1811. It is not an 1809 probate, complete heir list, will, inventory, estate settlement, or proof that Major Selby was William's child.
