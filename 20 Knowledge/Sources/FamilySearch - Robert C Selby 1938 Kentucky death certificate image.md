## Record examined

The reviewed certificate records Robert C. Selby's death on 13 March 1938 and gives his birth as 18 April 1875 in Kentucky. Its parental fields name Benjamin Selby and a mother whose handwritten given name reads Docie/Docia Stone. The informant was Mrs. Sallie Selby.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed digitized image of an original Kentucky death certificate. It directly establishes the information reported on the certificate, including the named informant and parental entries.

The certificate was completed decades after the reported birth, so the parental information is informant-supplied rather than a contemporaneous birth record. FamilySearch's index renders the mother's name as Dora, but direct inspection of the manuscript does not support that reading. The certificate does not explicitly equate Docie/Docia with Sophia Stone or prove that Robert C. Selby is the same individual as the 1874 Roberts Selby index entry.
