## Record examined

The reviewed certificate records Robert C. Selby’s death on 15 March 1938 and gives his birth as 18 April 1875 in Kentucky. Its parental fields name Benjamin Selby, born in Kentucky, and Dora Stone, born in Kentucky. The informant was “Mrs. Sallie Selby,” who signed the certificate.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed digitized image of an original Kentucky death certificate. It directly establishes the information reported on the certificate, including the named informant and parental entries.

The certificate was completed decades after the reported birth, so the parental information is informant-supplied rather than a contemporaneous birth record. It does not name Sophia Stone, use the name Docia/Docie for the mother, or identify Robert C. Selby as the same individual as the 1874 indexed birth entry for Roberts Selby. The similar father, place, and near birth date make that a research correlation, not a proven merger.
