# William Selby Maryland colonist biography lead

## Source status

This note preserves and organizes a user-supplied biography for **William Selby**, described as a Maryland colonist associated with Calvert County, Prince George's County, the Patuxent River, Mt. Calvert / Charles Town, Western Branch, Charles Branch, and several Maryland land tracts.

The supplied biography appears to draw heavily from compiled genealogy sources, FamilySearch/WikiTree-style profile material, Maryland patent and probate citations, Prince George's County land records, and secondary works such as *Early Families of Southern Maryland*. It is therefore a **derivative biographical lead** until each cited record is inspected from original images, archive copies, or reliable register copies.

## Caution

Do not use this note to extend the active Selby lineage by itself. Use it as a structured research guide for original-record acquisition and correlation.

This note does not prove a bridge from William Selby to Thomas Selby Sr., Thomas Selby II, the 1758 Thomas Selby, Major Selby, or the modern Selby line.

## Working identity

- Name: **William Selby**
- Possible birth/christening lead: born or christened in 1630; christening reported as 16 September 1630 at Alderminster, Worcester, Worcestershire, England.
- Reported parents in supplied bio: John and Mary, with Mary's surname uncertain.
- Alternative unsourced family-record claim: born in Badley, Suffolk, England; mother Mary Armes.
- Colonial setting: Calvert County / Prince George's County, Province of Maryland.
- Death/probate lead: will signed 5 November 1698; proved 25 February 1698/99; estate administration noted 30 July 1701.

## Migration / headright lead

The biography reports that William Selby was transported to Maryland in 1659. On 29 December 1659, Lieutenant Thomas Trueman reportedly claimed headrights for William Selby and six others in Maryland Patents Liber R, folio 84.

Names in the reported headright group:

- Hugh Lawson
- John Kelly
- Mary Steward
- William Jenkins
- John Ormans
- William Selby
- Christopher Hands

This should be checked against Maryland patent images/register copies and Gust Skordas, *The Early Settlers of Maryland*.

## Property summary from supplied biography

The supplied biography says William Selby held or devised over 1,000 acres in seven tracts. The reported tracts and devisees include:

- **Good Luck** and **Meadow**: 200 acres to son-in-law Robert Sollers and Mary, William's daughter.
- **Leith** and **Groome's Lodge**: divided between sons William Selby and Charles Selby.
- **To Good / Towgood**: portions to daughter Susannah Selby and son Samuel Selby.
- **Twyford / Thyford**: to daughter Amy / Amee Hucker, with an orchard-use exception for wife Mary.
- **The Farme**: 100 acres to wife Mary Selby.

The biography reports that a Prince George's County tract map for 1696-1702 shows several of these tracts on the Western Branch and Charles Branch.

## Specific land-record leads

### Leith

Reported conveyance:

- Date: 17 June 1686
- Parties: James Moore of Calvert County, carpenter, and wife Elizabeth Moore to William Selby of Calvert County, planter
- Consideration: 16,000 pounds of tobacco
- Tract: **Leith**, west side of the Patuxent River
- Acreage: reported 500 acres, with a later resurvey by sons Charles and William around October 1698.

### To Good

Reported conveyance:

- Date: 28 April 1697
- Grantor: Walter Bigger of Calvert County, gentleman
- Grantee: William Selby of Prince George's County
- Tract: **To Good**, 350 acres on west side of Patuxent River, near Croome/Groome, Timberly, and Generals Gift
- Consideration: £100
- Citation lead: Prince George's Deeds Liber A, folio 68A.

### The Farme

Reported posthumous/related transaction:

- Date: 23 June 1703
- Party: Joshua Cecil reportedly paid £120 for 309 acres of **The Farme**, formerly in Calvert County and later Prince George's County, adjoining Mansfield.
- The biography says part of The Farme had been owned by William Selby.
- Citation lead: Land records, Liber C, folio 61.

## Government / community activity lead

The biography states that William Selby was active in the Calvert/Prince George's County community and was connected to a 1684-1685 petition concerning the building of a town and port of trade on the Patuxent River, likely Charles Towne.

Reported citation lead:

## Will abstract from supplied biography

The supplied biography says William Selby of Prince George's County signed his will with mark **W** on 5 November 1698. The will was reportedly proved 25 February 1698/99.

Key bequests reported in the supplied bio:

- To son-in-law Robert Sollers and Mary his wife, William's loving daughter: 200 acres, including Good Luck and Meadow.
- To sons William and Charles Selby: Leith and Groome's Lodge, divided between them.
- To daughter Susannah Selby: 150 acres of To Good and household goods at age 16 or marriage.
- To son Samuel Selby: remaining part of To Good, household goods, and cattle at age 18.
- To daughter Amy Hucker: Twyford / Thyford, 100 acres, except orchard use reserved to wife Mary during her life.
- To wife Mary Selby: 100 acres called The Farme and use of the dwelling plantation until son William reached age 18.
- Residue to wife Mary Selby; Mary appointed executrix.

Reported FamilySearch image lead:

- Maryland, Register of Wills Records, 1629-1999
- Prince George's > Wills 1698 > image 8 of 335
- FamilySearch ARK lead included in supplied biography.
- Hall of Records, Annapolis.

This will should be obtained and transcribed directly from the image before any child list or land succession is treated as documented.
