# FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1761 Worcester inventory

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, Register of Wills, *Inventories, 1742-1763*, volume 8, manuscript page 479, inventory of Charles Parker, 12 March 1761, sworn by administrator Collins Parker on 13 June 1761; FamilySearch DGS 005080352, image 437 of 723, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GT1J-XZ8?view=fullText&keywords=Parker&lang=en, accessed 2026-08-13.

## Record examined

The original recorded inventory is dated 12 March 1761. Under the heading **"Kin"**, it names:

- Samuel Parker
- Phillip P. Parker

The record separately names Grace & Parker as a creditor, Joshua Sturgis and Henry Turner as appraisers, and Collins Parker as administrator. Collins Parker swore to the inventory on 13 June 1761.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a contemporary recorded probate instrument and is direct evidence that Samuel Parker and Phillip P. Parker were entered in the legally relevant kin section. It does not state whether either man was a son, brother, nephew, or another relation. The creditor, appraiser, and administrator roles are preserved separately and are not converted into kinship claims.

The FamilySearch automated transcript renders the oath year as 1767. Visual review of the original page and correlation with the 1761 estate index support 1761; the OCR reading is not adopted.

## Independent record-book corroboration

[[FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1761-1762 testamentary proceedings]] records the Prerogative Court entry at Liber 38, folio 163, stating that Charles Parker's inventory was among the Worcester County proceedings exhibited and ordered recorded. That abstract confirms the inventory's transmission and recording but adds no kinship or distribution language.
