# People of Medieval Scotland - Margaret queen of Scots

## Citation

People of Medieval Scotland, “Margaret, queen of Scots (d. 1093),” person record 246, accessed 2026-07-27, https://poms.ac.uk/record/person/246/.

## Description

Scholarly prosopographical database entry that collates medieval source material and modern reference work for Margaret, queen of Scots.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Margaret as the child of Edward Ætheling (Edward the Exile), son of Edmund Ironside.
- Records her marriage to Malcolm III, king of Scots.
- Identifies their daughter Matilda as the wife of Henry I of England.
- Links relationship statements to cited medieval charters and documentary editions.

## Reliability and limitations

People of Medieval Scotland is a scholarly research database that provides its source references. The record supports the named relationships, but it does not itself document any descent from this royal family to the Aetheling project’s modern families.
