# FamilySearch - Ben Selby 1850 Trimble census image

## Citation

1850 United States census, Trimble County, Kentucky, household of Ben Selby; NARA census population schedules; FamilySearch record `M65G-F6J`, original image ARK `3:1:S3HY-6XHZ-M29`.

## Record examined

The household contains Ben Selby, age 29, lawyer, born Kentucky; L. S. Selby, age 23; L. F. and H. K. Selby, each age 5; and Mattie Selby, age 3. All are reported born in Kentucky.

## Reliability and limitations

The census is a contemporary household enumeration but does not state relationships in 1850. The wife's initials and children's ages correlate closely with the 1844 Lucy S. Hunter marriage and the 1878 biography, but the census alone cannot identify the household's parents or establish every household relationship.
