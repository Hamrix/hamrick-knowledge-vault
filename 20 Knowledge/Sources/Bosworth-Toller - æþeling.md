# Bosworth-Toller - æþeling

## Citation

Joseph Bosworth, “æþeling,” in *An Anglo-Saxon Dictionary Online*, edited by Thomas Northcote Toller, Christ Sean, and Ondřej Tichý (Prague: Faculty of Arts, Charles University, 2014), https://bosworthtoller.com/38056.

## Description

Historical-dictionary entry for the Old English word *æþeling*. The entry defines its principal royal sense as a prince of an English royal house and supplies examples from Old English texts.

## Relevant extracts

- The principal definition is “a prince of an English royal house.”
- Examples include Cædwalla, Æðelwald, Eadwine, Eadmund, Eadward, and Ælfred described with forms of the term.
- The entry also records broader literary uses for non-English nobles and for Christ; the word therefore requires contextual interpretation.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a scholarly online edition of a foundational historical dictionary hosted by Charles University. It is strong lexical evidence, but dictionary examples alone do not establish a modern person’s ancestry or validate a family-tree connection.
