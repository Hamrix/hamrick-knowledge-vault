# FamilySearch - William and Priscilla Selby 1819 Nicholas deed

## Citation

Nicholas County, Kentucky, Deed Book E, page 352, William Selby and wife Priscilla to Armstrong McClintock, 5 May 1819, recorded 1 July 1819, [FamilySearch image 475 of 621](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSK4-6TFW?view=fullText&keywords=Selby&lang=en), DGS 8141837; [FamilySearch catalog record](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:432725); accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

The deed identifies William Selby and Priscilla his wife as residents of Nicholas County. For $196 they conveyed Armstrong McClintock a small tract described as containing 45 poles on the waters of Brushy Fork. Its bounds refer to Johnston's division line and the Limestone road. William and Priscilla signed, and the deed was acknowledged by William and recorded on 1 July 1819.

## Genealogical value

The original directly establishes the William-Priscilla marriage and their Nicholas County residence in 1819. It also places another Selby household in the Brushy Fork land cluster between Major Selby's 1815 Bourbon survey and the 1820 Nicholas census.

## Reliability and limitations

The parties, spousal wording, date, consideration, county, and Brushy Fork location appear in the original recorded deed. The exact tract-area wording should be rechecked if acreage becomes material because it is a very small parcel and the automated transcript may normalize the survey notation incorrectly.

The deed does not name Major Selby, Barkley Selby, either couple's parents or children, or any relationship between these Selby households. Shared surname and neighborhood are collateral evidence only.

FamilySearch full-text search of this deed-film group returned no OCR hit for "Major." That is not proof that no Major Selby entry exists because handwriting recognition and indexing are incomplete.
