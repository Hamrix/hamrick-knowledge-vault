## Description

Published biographical entry on Edmund Ironside, digitized by Wikisource.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Edmund Ironside as a son of Aethelred the Unready.
- Identifies his mother as Aelfgifu, Aethelred's first wife, while noting uncertainty over some maternal detail.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a historical biographical reference rather than a contemporary record. It independently corroborates the direct paternal relationship, but it should not be used as sole proof for contested biographical details or more distant genealogy.
