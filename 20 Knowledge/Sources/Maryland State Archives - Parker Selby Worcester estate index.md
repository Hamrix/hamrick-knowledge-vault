# Maryland State Archives - Parker Selby Worcester estate index

## Citation

Worcester County Register of Wills, *General Index to Estates, 1742-1908*, Selby page 5, entries for Parker Selby; Maryland State Archives, [digital index PDF](https://msa.maryland.gov/megafile/msa/coagsere/ce300/ce393/000000/000001/pdf/mdsa_ce393_1.pdf), accessed 13 August 2026.

## Entries examined

The visually reviewed Selby page distinguishes several Parker estates. Three late-eighteenth-century tracks are directly relevant:

- **Parker, Nancy Selby estate track**: filings dated 3 and 18 April 1789; Nancy Selby named as executor or administrator; inventory filed 25 August 1791; accounts dated 31 December 1792 and 28 April 1795; distribution dated 1 November 1793.
- **Parker (of Wm.)**: bond filed 29 January 1790; James Selby named as executor or administrator; inventory filed 24 June 1790; accounting dated 15 May 1792.
- **Parker (of John)**: a 26 April 1776 filing names John Selby; a later administration de bonis non filing dated 15 June 1791 names James Selby and John Gunby; inventory filed 12 August 1791; accounting dated 19 February 1793.

The page also records a Parker of Buckingham estate beginning 23 September 1791 under Glippery Selby. The parenthetical labels and distinct rows are indexing distinctions, not relationship statements found in the original bonds. The original instruments must control any lineage conclusion.

## Reliability and limitations

This is an official twentieth-century estate index derived from the probate files. It is authoritative for locating and distinguishing estate tracks but remains derivative from the original records. Column alignment is dense, and search-engine extraction merges adjacent rows; the entries above were checked against the page image.
