# Loyd and Stenton 1950 - Sir Christopher Hattons Book of Seals entry 73

## Citation

Lewis C. Loyd and Doris Mary Stenton, editors, *Sir Christopher Hatton's Book of Seals* (Oxford: Clarendon Press for the Northamptonshire Record Society, volume 15, 1950), printed pages 52-53, no. 73.

## Description

Scholarly edition of the seventeenth-century Book of Seals. Entry 73 transcribes a charter of Walter de Ver, dated editorially to 1205-1213, confirming his family's gift of Goxhill church to Bridlington Priory. The title page, entry, Latin relationship clauses, seal description, and editorial note were visually reviewed.

## Relevant extracts

The charter begins `Walterus de Ver filius Ade de Gousle`, directly identifying Walter de Ver as son of Adam of Goxhill. It describes `Robertus filius Ernisii auus meus` and later `Adam pater meus`, explicitly identifying Robert son of Erneis as Walter's grandfather and Adam as his father.

Nicholas de Chavencurt appears among the charter witnesses. Richard, abbot of Selby, also witnesses; here `Selby` is the abbey title, not a surname relationship.

The editors' note on printed page 53 states that Walter was dead by Michaelmas 1213 when Sybil his widow sued for dower; that she married Nicholas de Chavencurt as her second husband; and that Walter's heir Simon was a minor in the bishop of Lincoln's custody during the 1218-1219 litigation. The note cites *Curia Regis Rolls* III, page 64, and VII, page 5; *Book of Fees*, page 159; and *Rolls of the Justices in Eyre for Lincolnshire, 1218-19*, case 705.

## Reliability and limitations

The Latin relationship clauses are explicit and internally first-person, but this project reviewed a modern scholarly edition of an early modern manuscript copy or facsimile, not the medieval original. The edition says `BS FACSIMILE, first 11 words` and gives a complete Latin text. The editors also point to related material in *Early Yorkshire Charters* III, no. 1341, and *Registrum Antiquissimum* II, page 212.

The charter proves Walter's father Adam and grandfather Robert son of Erneis. It does not name Walter's mother, call Sybil `de Kyme`, state that Simon was Walter's son, or create a Selby-surname or modern-family connection. The Sybil, Nicholas, and Simon statements are modern editorial synthesis pending direct review of the cited court records.

## Claims supported

- [[Walter de Ver was son of Adam of Goxhill and grandson of Robert son of Erneis]]
- [[Sibilla wife of Nicholas de Schauencurt was formerly wife of Walter de Ver]] - independent scholarly corroboration of widowhood and second marriage.
- [[Simon de Ver was reported as son and heir of Walter de Ver]] - supports Simon as heir, but not the unreviewed son wording.
- [[Gundreda de Ver was reported as mother of Walter de Ver]] - supports Adam's paternity only; Gundreda is not named.

## Claims contradicted

- The charter does not support treating the Selby Abbey witness title as a Selby-surname family relationship.
- It does not support upgrading Gundreda's maternity or the `de Kyme` identification.
