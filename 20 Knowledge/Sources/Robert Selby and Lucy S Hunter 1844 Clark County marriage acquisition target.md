## Official repositories and access

The Clark County Clerk maintains the recorded marriages of the county. Indiana's judicial guidance directs marriage-certificate requests to the clerk of the county where the license was issued. The Clark County records-request page currently lists certified marriage copies at $4 per copy, but staff must confirm whether the online process covers an 1844 record and whether the complete underlying historical image can be supplied.

The Indiana State Library's Clark County research guide lists marriage records for 1825–1901. A FamilySearch catalog entry for Ruth M. Slevin's compiled Clark County marriage records identifies volume 1 as covering Books A–F, 1825–1855. This is a finding aid, not a substitute for the original county record.
