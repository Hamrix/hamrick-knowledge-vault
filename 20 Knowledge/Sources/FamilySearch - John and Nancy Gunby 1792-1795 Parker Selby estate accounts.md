# FamilySearch - John and Nancy Gunby 1792-1795 Parker Selby estate accounts

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, four accounts of John Gunby and Nancy his wife, executrix of Parker Selby, passed 31 December 1792 through 28 April 1795, *Accounts 1792-1796*; FamilySearch image group 005080358, images 29-30, 39-40, 79, and 119; images [`33S7-9TBV-9YMX`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9TBV-9YMX?view=fullText&lang=en), [`33S7-9TBV-9TY4`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9TBV-9TY4?view=fullText&lang=en), [`33SQ-GTBV-9TCX`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBV-9TCX?view=fullText&lang=en), [`33S7-9TBV-9TLK`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9TBV-9TLK?view=fullText&lang=en), [`33SQ-GTBV-9TD3`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBV-9TD3?view=fullText&lang=en), and [`33SQ-GTBV-9R1F`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBV-9R1F?view=fullText&lang=en), accessed 13 August 2026.

## Records examined

All four headings identify the accountants as **John Gunby and Nancy his wife**, with Nancy described as executrix of the last will and testament of **Parker Selby**, late of Worcester County.

| Account | Date passed or exhibited | Images | Continuity evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | 31 December 1792 | 29-30 | Charges the accountants with Parker's inventory exhibited 25 August 1791. |
| Second | 19 February 1793 | 39-40 | Charges the balance of the former account exhibited 31 December 1792. |
| Third | 30 November 1793 | 79 | Charges the balance of the second account exhibited 19 February 1793; the fourth account supplies the exact 30 November date. |
| Fourth and final | 28 April 1795 | 119 | Charges the balance of the third account exhibited 30 November 1793. |

John Gunby swore to each reviewed account. The second account records payments to several Selby-named creditors or estates, including Tabitha Selby as executrix of David Selby and the estate of Captain John Selby. It does not state any relationship between those parties and Parker. The third account records additional estate disbursements but likewise names no heir or relationship class.

## Genealogical value

The 1792 account directly establishes that Nancy, wife of John Gunby, was Parker Selby's executrix. Read with [[FamilySearch - Nancy Selby 1789 Parker Selby executor bond]], it strongly identifies the Nancy Selby who qualified in 1789 with the married Nancy Gunby who continued the same administration in 1792 and 1795.

The complete account sequence confirms that the 25 August 1791 inventory and all four account dates belong to this Nancy-administered estate track. It does not name Parker's heirs, call Nancy his widow, identify George or Elizabeth, or state Parker's father. The accounts therefore strengthen the Nancy-Parker estate identity without assigning Parker to a paternal line.

## Reliability and limitations

These are contemporary recorded estate accounts accepted under oath. The headings and relationship wording were visually reviewed against the manuscript images. Automated transcription remains imperfect, especially for personal names and monetary figures, so no unverified amount is used genealogically. The images remain outside the Git-backed vault.
