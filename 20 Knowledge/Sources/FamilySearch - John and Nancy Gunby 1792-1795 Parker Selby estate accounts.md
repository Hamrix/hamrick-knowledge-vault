# FamilySearch - John and Nancy Gunby 1792-1795 Parker Selby estate accounts

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, account of John Gunby and Nancy his wife, executrix of Parker Selby, passed 31 December 1792, *Accounts 1792-1796*, manuscript pages 45-46; FamilySearch image group 005080358, images 29-30, images [`33S7-9TBV-9YMX`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9TBV-9YMX?view=fullText&lang=en) and [`33S7-9TBV-9TY4`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9TBV-9TY4?view=fullText&lang=en); fourth and final account, passed 28 April 1795, manuscript page 223, image 119, image [`33SQ-GTBV-9R1F`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBV-9R1F?view=fullText&lang=en), accessed 13 August 2026.

## Records examined

The 1792 heading identifies the accountants as **John Gunby and Nancy his wife**, with Nancy described as executrix of the last will and testament of **Parker Selby**, late of Worcester County. It charges them with Parker's inventory, exhibited 25 August 1791, and records disbursements from the estate. John Gunby swore to the account on 31 December 1792.

The fourth and final account repeats the same heading and role. It charges the accountants with the balance of a third account exhibited 30 November 1793, records payments arising from Parker's land purchase and later litigation, and was sworn by John Gunby on 28 April 1795.

## Genealogical value

The 1792 account directly establishes that Nancy, wife of John Gunby, was Parker Selby's executrix. Read with [[FamilySearch - Nancy Selby 1789 Parker Selby executor bond]], it strongly identifies the Nancy Selby who qualified in 1789 with the married Nancy Gunby who continued the same administration in 1792 and 1795.

The accounts also confirm that the 25 August 1791 inventory and the 1792, 1793, and 1795 filings belong to this Nancy-administered estate track. They do not name Parker's heirs, call Nancy his widow, identify George or Elizabeth, or state Parker's father. The accounts therefore strengthen the Nancy-Parker estate identity without assigning Parker to a paternal line.

## Reliability and limitations

These are contemporary recorded estate accounts accepted under oath. The headings and relationship wording were visually reviewed against the manuscript images. Automated transcription remains imperfect, especially for personal names and monetary figures, so no unverified amount is used genealogically. The images remain outside the Git-backed vault.
