# Westminster Abbey - Henry III

## Citation

Westminster Abbey, “Henry III,” accessed 2026-07-27, https://www.westminster-abbey.org/abbey-commemorations/royals/henry-iii.

## Description

Institutional historical profile accompanying Henry III’s commemoration and burial at Westminster Abbey.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Henry as the eldest son of King John and Isabella.
- Dates his birth to 1 October 1207 at Winchester Castle.

## Reliability and limitations

Westminster Abbey is the site of Henry III’s burial and maintains a historical profile of the monarch. The profile independently corroborates the direct parent-child relationship, but is not itself evidence for more distant ancestry or a modern descent claim.
