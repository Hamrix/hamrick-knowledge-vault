# Cokayne - Complete Peerage Stafford-Gloucester relationship

## Citation

George Edward Cokayne, *The Complete Peerage of England, Scotland, Ireland, Great Britain and the United Kingdom*, first edition, vol. 2 (London: George Bell & Sons, 1887), 57, digitized at Wikisource, https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:The_Complete_Peerage_Ed_1_Vol_2.djvu/64.

## Description

Peerage reference entry for the Buckingham title. The entry gives the descent relevant to Humphrey Stafford’s maternal royal ancestry.

## Relevant extracts

- The entry identifies Humphrey Stafford as a son of Edmund Stafford, earl of Stafford, and Anne.
- It identifies Anne as the sister and heir of Humphrey, duke of Gloucester, placing her in the family of Thomas of Woodstock, a son of Edward III.
- A related Hereford entry explicitly identifies Anne as the child of Thomas, duke of Gloucester, and Thomas as a son of Edward III: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:The_Complete_Peerage_Ed_1_Vol_4.djvu/213.

## Reliability and limitations

*The Complete Peerage* is a standard compiled peerage reference and cites historical records, but it is a secondary compilation and this note uses its older first edition. Its account independently corroborates the relationship stated by the modern ODNB biography. The cited pages do not establish any post-medieval connection to a modern family.
