# FamilySearch - Major Selby 1803 Ann Selby estate distribution

## Citation

Polly Selby, administratrix of James Selby of John, administration account, Worcester County, Maryland, sworn 30 September 1803, *Inventories C1990-5 & C2017-9 & C1991-6*, volume JBR 5, manuscript pages 299-301, FamilySearch image 310 of 381, image group 104822424, [`3Q9M-CSYH-Y331-6`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSYH-Y331-6?view=fullText&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

The account heading identifies Polly Selby as administratrix of James Selby "of John," late of Worcester County. The final page credits payments from Ann Selby's estate, which James had administered:

- John Selby Jr. received his distributive share of his grandmother Ann Selby's estate.
- John Gunby received shares due to his son John and daughter Nancy from their grandmother Ann Selby.
- Major Selby, expressly called a grandson of Ann, received his part of her estate by receipt and order.

Polly Selby swore to the account on 30 September 1803.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary recorded estate account. The original image was visually reviewed; handwriting-recognition text was used only to navigate and cross-check names. The direct payment to Major in his own name, supported by a receipt and order rather than a guardian payment, establishes an adult estate transaction in the same family group by September 1803.

The account does not state Major's residence after the payment and does not identify him as the later Bourbon County plaintiff. It also should not be merged by name alone with the adult Major Selby taxed in 1783 or the man named in the 1801 Kent County, Delaware, manumission.
