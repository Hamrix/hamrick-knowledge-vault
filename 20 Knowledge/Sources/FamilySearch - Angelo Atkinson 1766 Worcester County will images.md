# FamilySearch - Angelo Atkinson 1766 Worcester County will images

## Citation

"Worcester, Maryland, United States records," images, FamilySearch, image group 005080347, Worcester County Wills, 1759-1769, volume 3, images 56-57 of 104, folios 109-112, will of Angelo Atkinson, dated 3 February 1766 and proved 3 May 1766, Hall of Records (Annapolis, Maryland), accessed 2026-08-03, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9TBJ-9H19?i=56&cc=1803986.

## Description

Digitized manuscript will record. Angelo Atkinson's will begins on the lower part of folio 109 (image 56), continues through folio 110, and completes on folio 111 (image 57); folio 112 contains the probate entry.

## Reviewed content

- The will identifies the testator as Angelo Atkinson of Worcester County.
- It names sons John, Joshua, and Angelo Atkinson.
- In its tenth bequest, it calls Sarah Selby a granddaughter and William Atkinson Selby a grandson, then describes them as the daughter and son of John Selby.
- The will separately names a grandson John Selby, but does not in that clause state his parent.
- The will is dated 3 February 1766 and the probate entry is dated 3 May 1766.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary manuscript record reviewed in image form. It directly proves the stated grandparent-grandchild and parent-child terms. It does not identify John Selby's wife, establish the identity of every John Selby in Worcester County, name Major Selby, or prove any connection from this household to Major Selby or to a different Selby line.
