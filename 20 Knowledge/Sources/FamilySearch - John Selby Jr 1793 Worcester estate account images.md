## Citation

## Record examined

The account heading identifies the accountants as **Kendal Smock and Leah his wife** and describes them as administrators of the goods, chattels, and credits of John Selby Junior, late of Worcester County, deceased. This note preserves the manuscript's `Kendal` spelling; later indexes or compiled sources may normalize it as `Kendall`.

The account carries forward the balance of their second account, exhibited for probate on 23 March 1784. It records creditor payments, court allowances, commissions, and a remaining balance due to the accountants. The concluding oath and court approval are dated 13 March 1793.

Named payees and associates include Captain John Selby, John Ayres, Abraham Sturgis, and other creditors. The account does not name a child, heir, guardian, or distributee of the decedent.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary recorded estate account. It directly identifies Leah as Kendal Smock's wife by March 1793 and links both spouses to the continuing administration of John Selby Junior's estate. It also proves that a second account had been filed in March 1784.

The record does not expressly call Leah the widow of John Selby Junior, although her role as the original administrator and later co-accountant is consistent with that hypothesis. It does not name Major Selby and is not, by itself, evidence that the decedent was Major's father. The 1790 will and 1795 final account now supply the direct father-grandfather relationship evidence; the 1782 estate identity is assessed through correlation in the linked claim.
