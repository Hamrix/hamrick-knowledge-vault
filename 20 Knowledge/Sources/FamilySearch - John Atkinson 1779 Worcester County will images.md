# FamilySearch - John Atkinson 1779 Worcester County will images

## Citation

John Atkinson will, dated 21 March 1779 and proved 12 May 1779, Worcester County, Maryland, *Wills 1769-1783*, volume 4; FamilySearch Image Group 005080348, recorded-will image [`33SQ-GTBJ-S5CD`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBJ-S5CD?view=fullText&keywords=Atkinson&lang=en) and probate image [`33SQ-GTBJ-SPRR`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBJ-SPRR?view=fullText&keywords=Atkinson&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

The handwritten date line is difficult to read in isolation. The related 25 August 1780 appeal bond expressly recites the will date as 21 March 1779. The probate entry is clearly dated 12 May 1779; widow Mary Atkinson made her election concerning the estate on 18 June 1779.

## Record examined

John Atkinson identifies Angelo Atkinson as his father and Joshua Atkinson as his brother. He gives his wife Mary life or widowhood interests in two plantations, with remainders to Joshua's sons John and Angelo Atkinson.

The residue is divided among:

- Joshua Atkinson's sons Angelo and John;
- Joshua's daughter Rachel Atkinson;
- John Selby's sons John and William Atkinson Selby;
- Rachel Fleming, described as Isaac Bozman Schoolfield's daughter-in-law; and
- Major White's daughters Mary and Nancy White.

The probate page records Mary Atkinson's oath and the testimony of subscribing witnesses Major White, Samuel Ball, William Barkley Townsend, and James Parr.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a recorded primary probate instrument. Its express kinship language independently confirms the Angelo-John-Joshua Atkinson family group, confirms Joshua's three named children, and directly identifies John and William Atkinson Selby as sons of John Selby.

It does not identify John Selby's wife, name Major Selby, or establish that John Selby's son John was the John Selby Jr. whose estate administration began in 1782. It also does not identify either same-named John Selby as the father of the minor Major Selby recorded in 1785.
