# Library of Congress - Ben Selby Kentucky public chronology 1859-1886

## Notices examined

- 18 May 1859, *Louisville Weekly Courier*: "Col. Ben Selby of Oldham" was appointed secretary at a La Grange convention. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn84038556/1859-05-18/ed-1/?sp=1)
- 11 August 1860, *Louisville Weekly Courier*: Col. Ben Selby was scheduled to speak at Shelbyville. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn84038556/1860-08-11/ed-1/?sp=2)
- 24 August 1861, *Louisville Weekly Courier*: Col. Ben Selby was chosen president of a Port Royal Precinct, Henry County, meeting. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn84038556/1861-08-24/ed-1/?sp=3)
- 12 December 1866, *Kentucky Gazette*: Col. Ben Selby served on a committee at a Henry County Democratic meeting. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn84027460/1866-12-12/ed-1/?sp=3)
- 14 October 1874, *Kentucky Gazette*: Col. Ben Selby defended Marion Beckham in an examining trial. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn84027460/1874-10-14/ed-1/?sp=4)
- 7 May 1875, *Public Ledger*: Ben Selby of Henry represented the Seventh District on a convention resolutions committee. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn87056710/1875-05-07/ed-1/?sp=1)
- 17 July 1878, *Cincinnati Daily Star*: Col. Ben Selby appeared in a Kentucky campaign-speaking report. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn85025759/1878-07-17/ed-1/?sp=3)
- 14 February 1880, *Weekly Roundabout*: Col. Ben Selby of Eminence visited Frankfort. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn86069847/1880-02-14/ed-1/?sp=2)
- 5 June 1880, *Weekly Roundabout*: Col. Ben Selby of Henry was appointed a Greenback convention delegate and recommended as a state-at-large elector. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn86069847/1880-06-05/ed-1/?sp=1)
- 2 June 1883 and 11 October 1884, *Frankfort Roundabout*: notices identify Col. Ben Selby of Eminence. [1883](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn86069848/1883-06-02/ed-1/?sp=2) and [1884](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn86069848/1884-10-11/ed-1/?sp=2)
- 28 January 1886, *Anderson News*: a notice says Col. Ben Selby and family, of Clifton, had sold a sawmill. [Page image](https://www.loc.gov/resource/sn86069242/1886-01-28/ed-1/?sp=3)

## Reliability and limitations

These are contemporary newspaper page images. The Henry County, Eminence, legal, and political notices form a strong public-life chronology compatible with the target attorney documented in 1849, 1860, 1872, and 1880.

The notices do not name parents. "Colonel" may be a social, militia, political, or military title and is not proof of Confederate service. The 1859 Oldham, 1860 Shelbyville, 1878 Lexington, and 1886 Clifton notices require particular caution because the text does not independently establish that every appearance concerns one man. Later twentieth-century or turn-of-the-century notices naming a Ben Selby were excluded because they may concern Benjamin's son.

## Research value

The 1861 Henry County political meeting and the 1871 federal disability act point toward Reconstruction-era petition files rather than an assumed military-service file. The Eminence notices also support searching local newspapers, court dockets, deeds, and tax records under both Benjamin Selby and Col. Ben Selby.
