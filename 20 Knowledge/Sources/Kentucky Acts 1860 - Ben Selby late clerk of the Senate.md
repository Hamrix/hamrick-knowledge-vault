# Kentucky Acts 1860 - Ben Selby late clerk of the Senate

## Citation

Kentucky, *Acts Passed at the Session of the General Assembly for the Commonwealth of Kentucky*, vol. 1 (Frankfort: J. Bradford, printer to the Commonwealth, 1860), resolutions, p. 177.

## Description

Published session laws and resolutions of the Kentucky General Assembly. Google Books' table of contents identifies a resolution on page 177 concerning Ben Selby.

## Relevant extracts

The table of contents gives the resolution title:

**"Resolution authorizing the Auditor to settle with Ben Selby late clerk of the Senate"** — p. 177.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a contemporaneous published government record and is strong evidence that a Ben Selby had recently served as clerk of the Kentucky Senate by the 1860 session. The page image/text of the resolution itself was not successfully rendered in the browser during this research pass, so the current note relies on the title as presented in the digitized book's contents.

The record independently supports the public-career portion of the 1878 biography, which reports that the younger Ben Selby was elected Clerk of the State Senate in 1859 and resigned during that winter. The combination of date and office makes the identification with the younger Ben highly persuasive, although a full page image should still be preserved.

## Claims supported

- Ben Selby served as clerk of the Kentucky Senate shortly before the 1860 General Assembly resolution.
- The 1878 biography's reported 1859 Senate-clerk episode has independent contemporary government corroboration.

## Claims contradicted

None.
