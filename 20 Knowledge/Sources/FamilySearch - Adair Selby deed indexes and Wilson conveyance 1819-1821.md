# FamilySearch - Adair Selby deed indexes and Wilson conveyance 1819-1821

## Citation

Adair County, Kentucky, General Index to Real Estate Conveyances, grantees I-Z, Selby page; Deed Book E, pages 79-80 and 387-388; FamilySearch Image Group Numbers `8142219` and `8151276`.

## Description

The Selby grantee page was reviewed as a route around the missing Lingan Wilson Selby estate distribution. It lists several Benjamin Selby acquisitions and legal entries between 1812 and 1821. No reviewed entry names Lingan Selby as grantor or identifies Benjamin as Lingan's son, heir, or relative.

The most suggestive entry was an 1819 deed indexed from Ghalston Wilson, by attorney, to Benjamin Selby for 100 acres on the Cumberland River in Deed Book E, page 79. The original deed and its continuation show William Caldwell acting as Ghalston Wilson's attorney in fact in an ordinary conveyance to Benjamin. The instrument states no family relationship.

The same grantee page indexes Jenny Wilson's 1821 dower release at Deed Book E, page 387. The original release directly calls Jenny the wife of Ghalston Wilson and refers to the earlier Wilson conveyance. It does not identify her with Mary Selby, Lingan's widow, or state any relationship to Benjamin.

## Relevant extracts

- Grantee index: 1819, Selby, Benjamin; Ghalston Wilson by attorney and others; deed, Book E, page 79; 100 acres, Cumberland River.
- Deed Book E, page 79: William Caldwell acts under Ghalston Wilson's recorded power of attorney in the conveyance to Benjamin Selby.
- Deed Book E, page 80: Caldwell acknowledges the deed as attorney in fact for Ghalston Wilson; no kinship term appears.
- Grantee index: 1821, Selby, Benjamin; Jenny Wilson; release, Book E, page 387; dower release.
- Deed Book E, page 387: Jenny Wilson explicitly identifies herself as Ghalston Wilson's wife and relinquishes dower in the land covered by the foregoing deed.

## Bounded index result

The same Selby grantee page records Benjamin entries involving John T. Slater, John Beard Sr., Robert Thomas, Thomas I. Chilton, John Harvey, Daniel and Mary Trabue, Joseph and Nancy Ann Patterson, Cyrus and Flora Walker, Michael and Nancy Grant, Ghalston Wilson, and Jenny Wilson. None names Lingan Selby. The Selby grantor page includes Benjamin and Nancy in 1806 and 1809 and Benjamin in 1816, but no indexed 1821-1827 conveyance by Lingan's heirs was found under the Selby spelling.

## Related corrected searches

- Adair tax-book searches in Image Group Number `7834395` produced exact `Benjamin Selby` OCR hits in 1808 and 1810, but visual review places the name in the land-entry provenance column rather than identifying the taxpayer or an heir. Lingan hits occur in pre-death annual lists. No reviewed result identified a post-1821 child or heir.
- A Will Book B index OCR reading that appeared to associate Lingan with a later Benjamin settlement was checked against the original pages. The target record is for Benjamin Settle or Settles, deceased, not Benjamin Selby. The right-hand Settles page in the grantee index also contains an unrelated 1819 Benjamin Settles entry from Henry Smith.

## Reliability and limitations

The deed-book pages are original county recordings and are strong evidence for the named parties and stated legal acts. The index is a finding aid, not a relationship record. Its negative result does not exclude a deed indexed under another spelling, a conveyance by an attorney or purchaser rather than an heir, an unrecorded instrument, or an estate transfer recorded outside the reviewed years. The Wilson surname and Lingan's middle name are not evidence of kinship.
