# DNB 1895 - Edward III

## Citation

T. F. Tout, "Edward III (1312-1377)," in *Dictionary of National Biography*, 1885-1900, vol. 17 (1895), accessed 2026-07-27, https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Dictionary_of_National_Biography,_1885-1900/Edward_III.

## Description

Published biographical entry on Edward III by historian T. F. Tout, digitized by Wikisource.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Edward III as the eldest son of Edward II and Isabella of France.
- Dates his birth to 13 November 1312 at Windsor Castle.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a historical scholarly biography rather than a contemporary record. It independently corroborates the direct parent-child relationship, but the 1895 entry should not be treated as sole proof for disputed or more distant genealogy.
