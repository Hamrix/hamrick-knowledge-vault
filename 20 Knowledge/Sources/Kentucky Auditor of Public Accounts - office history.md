# Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts - office history

## Citation

Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts, "History," section "Past and Current Auditors," accessed 15 August 2026.

## Description

Official institutional history of the Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts. The page includes a chronological list of officeholders and their stated terms.

## Relevant extracts

The official list gives the early nineteenth-century sequence as:

- John Madison — 1816-1820
- Peter Clay — 1820
- **Ben Shelby — 1820-1834**
- **Thomas S. Page — 1834-1846** and 1851-1859
- H. Q. Bradley — 1846-1848
- John Baylor Temple — 1848-1850

The page also states that the office was established in 1792 and did not become elective until the 1850 constitution.

## Reliability and limitations

This is the Commonwealth office's own retrospective institutional chronology and is strong evidence for the official succession of auditors. It is not itself a contemporary appointment record. The spelling **Ben Shelby** must not automatically be normalized to **Benjamin Selby** without a contemporary record demonstrating that they are the same person.

The chronology materially conflicts with the 1878 *Biographical Encyclopaedia of Kentucky* profile of younger Ben Selby, which reports that his father, Hon. Benjamin Selby, became Auditor in 1834 and remained in office until his death in 1845. The official list instead assigns 1834-1846 to Thomas S. Page.

## Claims contradicted

- Any unqualified claim that Hon. Benjamin Selby served as Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts continuously from 1834 until 1845.
