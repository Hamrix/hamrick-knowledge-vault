# FamilySearch - elder Benjamin Selby 1811 marriage and 1830-1840 censuses

## Citations

- Adair County, Kentucky, marriage register, Benjamin Selby and Elizabeth McClain, 29 December 1811, original image ARK `3:1:9398-7M95-4C`; FamilySearch record variants `QKJM-B7NL` and `Q28D-ZWZG`.
- 1830 United States census, Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky, Benjn Selby household; FamilySearch record `XHGG-GX4`, original image ARK `3:1:33S7-9YYV-SK9`.
- 1840 United States census, Frankfort, Franklin County, Kentucky, Benjamin Selby household; FamilySearch record `XHT8-1C7`, original image ARK `3:1:33SQ-GYYT-9M6C`.

## Records examined

The 1811 register records the marriage of Benjamin Selby and Elizabeth McClain. The 1830 Columbia and 1840 Frankfort censuses record older Benjamin Selby household heads in the places associated with the public official described in the 1878 biography.

## Reliability and limitations

The marriage and census records are original contemporary records, but none names the younger attorney as a child. The 1840 census confirms that the Frankfort household head was an older man, not the younger Benjamin born in 1826. Elizabeth McClain is a candidate mother only; marriage to the elder Benjamin does not prove that she was the younger Benjamin's mother.
