## Reliability and limitations

The abstract is useful evidence that Lingan Wilson Selby was named as a son of William Magruder Selby in the reported 1773 probate record. Because this is a compiled web transcription rather than an inspected original probate image, preserve the source class and seek the archival record before treating every spelling and relationship detail as image-confirmed.

This record establishes Lingan Wilson Selby's parentage one generation upward. It does **not** identify Lingan's children and therefore does not support the proposition that Hon. Benjamin Selby was his son.
