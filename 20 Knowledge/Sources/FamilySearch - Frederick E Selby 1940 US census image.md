## Record examined

The reviewed original page lists a female Selby household head, age 50 and born in Louisiana, immediately followed by “Selby, Frederick E.,” age 28, with relationship to head recorded as “Son.” It records the household in New Orleans, Orleans Parish, Louisiana.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed image of an original federal census schedule. The handwriting and index rendering of the household head's name are not sufficiently clear on this review to substitute this page alone for a complete name-and-identity proof. It is retained as corroborating household evidence alongside the clearer 1930 page and the 1953 marriage license.

The page does not name Frederick's father or establish a colonial, medieval, or royal-family connection.
