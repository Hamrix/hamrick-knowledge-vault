# Maryland State Archives - Worcester marriage licenses C2027 1795-1851

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, *Marriage Records at the Maryland State Archives*, Worcester County, "Marriage Licenses, 1795-1851, MSA C2027," [online guide](https://guide.msa.maryland.gov/pages/viewer.aspx?page=marriage), accessed 8 August 2026; see also the [Worcester County Court series list](https://guide.msa.maryland.gov/pages/combined.aspx?jurisdiction=WORCESTER+COUNTY&type=C).

## Finding-aid result

The official guide identifies **Worcester County Court (Marriage Licenses), 1795-1851, MSA C2027** as a paper series available at the archives through the circulation desk. Its date span covers the derivative index's reported 8 December 1808 Major Selby-Hetty Cottingham entry.

[FamilySearch catalog 547494](https://www.familysearch.org/de/search/catalog/547494?availability=Family+History+Library) identifies image group 008960145 as compiled Daughters of the American Revolution Maryland material. That confirms the two online index entries are derivative and should not be mistaken for the original county license series.

## Reliability and limitations

This finding aid proves that a potentially relevant original-record series exists and gives its exact archival identifier. It does not prove that the requested license survives within the series or that the reported marriage occurred. Examination of the original paper record remains required.
