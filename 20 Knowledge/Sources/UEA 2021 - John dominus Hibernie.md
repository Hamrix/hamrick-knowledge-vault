# UEA 2021 - John dominus Hibernie

## Citation

Adam Fantosme, “A King in All But Name: John, *dominus Hibernie*, *frater regis*, and Unconsecrated Rulership in Two Kingdoms, 1185–99” (PhD thesis, University of East Anglia, 2021), https://ueaeprints.uea.ac.uk/id/eprint/90186/.

## Description

University research on John’s authority in England and Ireland before his accession as king.

## Relevant extracts

- Describes John as the youngest son of King Henry II.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a modern university thesis and independently corroborates the direct father-son relationship. Its research focus is John’s pre-accession rulership rather than a comprehensive genealogy.
