# FamilySearch Catalog - Hamilton County Indiana deed records 1825-1886

## Citation

FamilySearch Catalog, *Deed records, 1825-1886*, Hamilton County Recorder (Indiana), [catalog entry](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:111211), accessed 2026-08-04.

## Catalog information examined

The reviewed catalog describes forty microfilm reels of Hamilton County Recorder deed records for 1825-1886 and states that most volumes include surname indexes.

For the period most relevant to the later Hamilton County Major Selby research target, it identifies these index and deed-film targets:

- digital folder 8035350 / film 1321506: deed indexes A-G (1825-1840) and H-M (1840-1850);
- digital folder 8065587 / film 1321507: deed indexes N (1850-1854), O-S (1851-1854), and T-U (1854-1859);
- digital folder 8065588 / film 1321508: deed indexes T-W (1854-1859), V-Z (1859-1862), and volume 1 (1862-1865); and
- digital folder 8030385 / film 1321509: the continuation of volume 1 (1862-1865), then volumes 2-4 (1865-1870).

## Access and limitations

The catalog marks these images for access at the FamilySearch Library or a FamilySearch affiliate library. The listed index and deed images were not read in the reviewed session.

This finding aid does **not** establish that Major Selby, Harriet Selby, or any other Selby appears in a deed; it identifies the exact records that must be searched before a land transaction, residence, heir, or relationship can be asserted.

## Next research action

At a FamilySearch Library or affiliate library, search the applicable surname-index ranges first, then read and transcribe each referenced deed in full. Preserve the grantor, grantee, deed date, recording date, consideration, land description, witnesses, and every stated relationship before using any result to correlate the Hamilton County household with another Major Selby record.
