# Neal 2023 - Anglo-Scandinavian by-names

## Citation

Joshua Neal, “A Study on Anglo-Scandinavian *bý(r)*-names: Statistical Analysis and Case-Studies” (PhD thesis, University of Nottingham, 2023), 111, https://eprints.nottingham.ac.uk/74185/1/Joshua%20Neal%20-%2014312644%20-%20Thesis.pdf.

## Description

Doctoral study of Anglo-Scandinavian place-names ending in *bý(r)*. It identifies Selby, Yorkshire, among West Riding names first attested in the eleventh century.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Selby, Yorkshire, as one of three West Riding Yorkshire *bý(r)*-names first attested in the eleventh century.
- Places Selby in the scholarly context of Anglo-Scandinavian *bý(r)* place-names.

## Reliability and limitations

University research provides useful independent context for the Selby place-name and the *-by* element. This source does not itself establish the origin of a particular Selby family or show that every surname bearer derived from Selby, Yorkshire.
