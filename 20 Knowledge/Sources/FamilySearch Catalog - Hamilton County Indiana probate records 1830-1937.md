# FamilySearch Catalog - Hamilton County Indiana probate records 1830-1937

## Citation

Hamilton County (Indiana) Clerk of the Circuit Court, *Probate records, 1830-1937*; microfilm of records located at the Hamilton County Court House, Noblesville, Indiana; FamilySearch catalog [koha:48870](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:48870), accessed 2026-08-03.

## Reviewed content

The catalog identifies a two-reel probate series. The reel identified as DGS 7664201 (catalog film 1320374) includes:

- estate probate order book, volume 1, beginning February 1860;
- estate probate order book, volume 2, December 1878-November 1887;
- guardians probate order books, volumes 1-2; and
- general index to probate of estates, volume 1, 1830-1872.

The other reel, DGS 7664200 (catalog film 1320375), contains a general index to probate of estates, volume 1, 1829-1894; volume 2, 1891-1937; and a general index to guardianships, volume 1, 1834-1890. The catalog therefore identifies DGS 7664201 as the estate-order-book reel and DGS 7664200 as the broader general-index reel.

## Access result and limitation

The reviewed FamilySearch image viewer displayed the images for DGS 7664200 as restricted (lock icon) in the current signed-in session. Neither the relevant index nor the estate-order-book contents were read, so this finding aid supplies a retrieval target only. It does not establish that Major Selby had an estate in the series, identify an administrator or heir, or prove any relationship.

## Research use

Search the general index for **Selby, Major**, then read the corresponding entry in estate probate order book volume 1. Any relationship stated in an original order, inventory, or distribution must be transcribed and separately verified before it is used to connect Major to Benjamin, the Maryland Selbys, or a proposed royal line.

## Additional probate-order-book target

A separate FamilySearch catalog entry, [koha:48953](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/koha:48953), identifies the earlier Circuit Court probate order books. Its DGS 7664196 (film 1320407) contains:

- volume 3, 15 November 1859-14 January 1865; and
- volume 4, 1 May 1865-13 December 1866, including vacation entries through 19 December 1866.

These dates make volume 3 a particularly relevant retrieval target for any probate proceeding near Major Selby's reported 1861 death. In the reviewed signed-in session, the image viewer for DGS 7664196 did not display the images, and FamilySearch full-text search for the exact phrase **"Major Selby"** returned zero results. Neither result shows that the books lack an entry: the images have not been read and a zero full-text result is not proof of absence. No estate, heir, administrator, or relationship is established by this catalog information.
