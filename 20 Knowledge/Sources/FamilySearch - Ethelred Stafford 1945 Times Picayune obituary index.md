# FamilySearch - Ethelred Stafford 1945 Times Picayune obituary index

## Citation

*Times-Picayune*, obituary indexed for `Ethelred Reinrick Stafford`, 23 August 1945; database, "United States, GenealogyBank Historical Newspaper Obituaries, 1815-2013," FamilySearch, [indexed record](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q5M8-SQB6), accessed 21 August 2026.

## Record examined

The index names Fannie B. Coe as wife; Mrs. Margaret Z. Best and Mrs. Sarah E. Selby as daughters; Rev. John H. Stafford and Leonard L. Stafford as brothers; and Frederick E. Selby as a grandson.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a derivative newspaper-obituary index, not the reviewed newspaper image. Relationship labels are useful but may reflect automated extraction. The wife and daughters agree with independent vital, census, and marriage records. The brother-name pattern also agrees with John and Leon L. Stafford in the 1880 household. The heading `Ethelred Reinrick` is an OCR or indexing error and is not silently adopted as a separate name.

The index alone does not determine every unlabeled person's exact relationship and is not used to extend the line beyond the directly labeled family members.
