## Record examined

The reviewed schedule lists Benjn Selby, age 35, born in Kentucky; Sophia Selby, age 23, born in Kentucky; Tammy Selby, age 15; Catherine Selby, age 15; and Ben Selby, age 3, consecutively in one household. The 1860 schedule does not contain a relationship-to-head column.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed digitized image of an original federal census schedule. It directly supports the names, ages, birthplaces, and shared enumeration shown on the page.

It does not state relationships among household members. It therefore does not prove that this Benjn Selby is the Benjamin Selby in the 1854 license papers, that Sophia Selby is Sophia E. Stone, that Ben Selby was their child, or that anyone in this household was related to the Benjamin, Docia, and Payne Selby household of the 1880 census.
