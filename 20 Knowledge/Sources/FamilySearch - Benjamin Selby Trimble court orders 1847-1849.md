# FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby Trimble court orders 1847-1849

## Citation

Trimble County, Kentucky, county court order books: Benjamin Selby's admission to practice, 8 November 1847, ARK `3:1:3Q9M-C9PK-H9BZ-R`; appointment as county attorney, 13 March 1848, ARK `3:1:3Q9M-C9PK-H9B3-R`; compensation order, 9 October 1848, ARK `3:1:3Q9M-C9PK-H9BC-B`; and surety appearance, March 1849, ARK `3:1:3Q9M-C9PN-399J-H`; FamilySearch digital images.

## Record examined

The original court-order images were reviewed. They place Benjamin Selby in Trimble County's legal practice beginning in November 1847, appoint him county attorney in March 1848, and continue his professional activity through 1849.

## Reliability and limitations

These contemporary court minutes directly establish the named lawyer's occupation, role, place, and chronology. They contain no parentage statement and do not alone identify him with later Henry County records.
