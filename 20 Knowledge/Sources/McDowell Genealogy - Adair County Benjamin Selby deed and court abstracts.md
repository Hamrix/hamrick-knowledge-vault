# McDowell Genealogy - Adair County Benjamin Selby deed and court abstracts

## Citation

McDowell Genealogy, "Ephraim2Web," transcribed/abstracted Adair County deed and court records concerning the McDowell family, entries dated 14 October 1816 and 5 May 1823, accessed 15 August 2026.

## Description

A compiled genealogy page that quotes or abstracts identified Adair County deed and court-book entries. It is a derivative source pointing to specific county references and should be checked against the original record images.

## Relevant extracts

The page reports:

- **14 October 1816, Adair Deed Book D:200:** William Owens, Elijah Creel, **Benjamin Selby**, and John Montgomery, described as trustees of the Town of Columbia, conveyed lot no. 36 to Benjamin McDowell.
- **5 May 1823, Adair County Order Book D:361:** the court ordered **Benjamin Selby**, John Montgomery, and William Caldwell to act as commissioners to convey land after Mathew McDowell died without completing title.

The page also gives a later deed abstract reflecting the commissioners' conveyance.

## Reliability and limitations

The specific book/page-style references make these high-value research leads, but the website is a derivative family-history compilation rather than the county record itself. The original Adair County deed/order-book images should be inspected before exact legal wording is quoted in a final lineage proof.

The entries establish the presence of a civically active Benjamin Selby in Columbia/Adair County in the relevant period. They do not, standing alone, prove that he is the elder Hon. Benjamin Selby named in the 1878 biography, although the chronology and locality make that correlation worth testing.

## Claims supported

- A Benjamin Selby was named among Columbia town trustees in an 1816 deed abstract.
- A Benjamin Selby was named among court-appointed commissioners in an 1823 Adair County order-book abstract.
