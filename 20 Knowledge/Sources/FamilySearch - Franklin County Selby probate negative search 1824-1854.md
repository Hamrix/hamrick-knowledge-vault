# FamilySearch - Franklin County Selby probate negative search 1824-1854

## Search performed

FamilySearch catalog and original-image review covered the Franklin County will and estate-settlement collections spanning the elder Benjamin Selby's reported 1845 death:

- *Wills, 1795-1880, of Franklin County*, catalog `426325`, Image Group Number `4818779`.
- *Settlements of estates, 1828-1851, of Franklin County*, catalog `425201`, Image Group Number `4818781`.

Exact `Benjamin Selby` and surname `Selby` full-text searches returned no result in either image group. The original S index for Will Book 2, covering 1824-1854, was then manually reviewed and contains no Selby testator. The unindexed settlement volume was manually bracketed around the 1844-1846 court terms, including the October 1844 and May 1846 term headings, without finding a Selby settlement on the reviewed pages.

## Continued bond, guardianship, and order-book audit

The Franklin executor and administrator bond film, catalog `425002`, Image Group Number `4818780`, was searched for exact Selby and Shelby across 1844-1846. Neither spelling returned a result; an all-film Benjamin search produced unrelated Johnson entries. The catalog's executor bonds continue through 1877, but its administrator bonds end in 1840.

The guardian-bond film, catalog `425005`, Image Group Number `7553226`, was searched across 1844-1850 for Selby, Shelby, Benjamin, and `Sel*`. No Selby or Shelby entry was found; returned Benjamin and `Sel*` entries concerned unrelated people.

Franklin County Court Order Books K-L, 1834-1848, Image Group Number `7900559`, were searched across 1844-1848. Exact `Selby` and `Benjamin Shelby` returned no result. Ten `Shelby` hits were visually sampled and concern road or boundary geography such as a "Shelby line," not a person or estate. The available general order-book index ends in 1834, so this remains a transcript-dependent bounded search rather than a manual 1845 surname-index audit.

## Result

No Franklin County will, bond, guardianship, court order, or relationship-bearing estate settlement for the elder Benjamin Selby was found in this bounded search.

## Reliability and limitations

The Will Book 2 index review is a strong negative for a recorded Franklin County will under the Selby spelling. It does not exclude an intestate administration, a record under another spelling, an unrecorded estate packet, probate in another county, or a materially wrong death place or date. The settlement book has no general index, and only the date-bracketing pages and full-text results were reviewed rather than every page. The order-book and bond searches depend partly on automated transcription. Franklin executor-bond films extend beyond 1845, but the available administrator-bond run ends in 1840, so the filmed bond set is incomplete for a possible intestate 1845 estate.
