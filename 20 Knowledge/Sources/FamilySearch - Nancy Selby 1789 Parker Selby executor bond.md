# FamilySearch - Nancy Selby 1789 Parker Selby executor bond

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, executor bond of Nancy Selby for the estate of Parker Selby, 8 April 1789, *Probate Bonds, 1786-1793*; FamilySearch image group 005080351, image 464 of 536, image [`33S7-9T1P-C26`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9T1P-C26?view=fullText&keywords=Nancy+Selby%2CParker+Selby&searchForm=advanced&lang=en), accessed 13 August 2026.

## Record examined

The bond is dated 8 April 1789 and names **Nancy Selby** as executrix of the last will and testament of **Parker Selby**, late of Worcester County, deceased. It requires an inventory by 8 July and an administration account by 8 April 1790.

## Genealogical value

This original instrument confirms the opening administrative history of the Parker estate previously visible only in the estate indexes. It establishes Nancy's role under Parker's will but does not state that she was Parker's wife or widow, name his children, or identify his father.

The bond belongs to the April 1789 track and is distinct from [[FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1790 Worcester executor bond]], which names James Selby as executor, and [[FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1791 Worcester de bonis non bond]], which describes a former administration by John Selby, Esquire.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a contemporary recorded probate bond visually reviewed in the manuscript image. FamilySearch's automated transcript was used as a navigation aid; the names, role, estate, and date were checked against the image. The reviewed image remains outside the Git-backed vault.
