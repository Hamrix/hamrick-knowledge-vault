# Iowa State Historical Society - James F Selby 1918 death index

## Citation

State Historical Society of Iowa, "Death Certificate Index - Wayne County (1918-1920, 1935-1939)," entry for James F. Selby, [PDF index](https://history.iowa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-03/wayne-county.pdf), accessed 2026-08-03.

## Indexed facts

The official index lists James F. Selby with:

- birth date: 16 February 1827;
- birth place: Maryland;
- death date: 29 August 1918;
- county: Wayne, Iowa;
- certificate number: 93-1265; and
- box: D2553.

The index's mother's-maiden-name field is blank for this entry and it does not provide a father's name.

## Reliability and limitations

This is an official archival index, not the death-certificate image. It directly supports the indexed identity details and certificate retrieval reference but not any parent-child relationship or the accuracy of the underlying informant's knowledge.

It does not establish that this James F. Selby was the son of Major Selby and Harriet Mezick, connect him to a Hamilton County, Indiana household, or provide a bridge to Benjamin Selby, Worcester County, or any proposed royal line.
