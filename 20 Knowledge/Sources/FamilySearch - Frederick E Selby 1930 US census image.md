## Record examined

The reviewed original page lists Sarah E. Stafford as the household head and lists “Selby, Frederick E.” immediately below her. It records his relationship to head as “Son,” age 18, birthplace Louisiana, and residence in New Orleans, Orleans Parish, Louisiana.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed image of an original federal census schedule. It directly establishes how the enumerator recorded the household and relationship in 1930.

It does not name Frederick's father, establish a biological relationship beyond the census report, identify Frederick's full middle name, or connect this household to Major Selby, colonial Selby lines, medieval Staffords, or any proposed royal descent. The connection between this “Frederick E. Selby” and the FamilySearch profile Frederick Ethelred Selby is supported by matching name form, age, birthplace, and locality, but later identity records should be correlated before treating it as a completed modern identity proof.
