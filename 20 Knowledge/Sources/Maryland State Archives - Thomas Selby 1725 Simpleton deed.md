# Maryland State Archives - Thomas Selby 1725 Simpleton deed

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, Somerset County Court (Land Records), Liber GH, folios 312-313, Thomas Selby and Margaret Selby to William Selby, deed dated 12 October 1725, MSA CE102-19, accessed 2026-08-02, https://guide.msa.maryland.gov/pages/series.aspx?id=CE102.

## Description

Official digitized manuscript deed. Thomas Selby and his wife Margaret of Somerset County conveyed the remaining land and marsh within the 1,250-acre tract called Simpleton to William Selby.

## Reviewed content

- The deed's recital calls the 1,250-acre patent holder "Thomas Selby the eldest Grandfather" of the grantor Thomas Selby.
- It identifies the grantor as heir at law to his father and grandfather in the title history of the tract.
- In its operative language, it again describes the grantor as "Thomas Selby Grandson of Thomas Selby deceased."
- The grantors executed and acknowledged the deed, dated 12 October 1725.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed digitized image of the official manuscript land record. It directly records the grantor's stated descent from the Thomas Selby who held the Simpleton patent. It does not name the intermediate father in a way that distinguishes him from other Thomas Selbys, and it does not identify the 1725 grantor with the Thomas Selby named as a son in the 1701 will or with the Thomas Selby whose will was proved in 1758.
