# FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby 1840 Frankfort census image

## Citation

1840 United States census, Frankfort, Franklin County, Kentucky, page 328, household of Benjamin Selby; NARA M704; FamilySearch image `33SQ-GYYT-9M6C`.

## Record examined

The row reports a household headed by Benjamin Selby, including one free white male aged fifty to fifty-nine.

## Reliability and limitations

This excludes the Benjamin born on 31 January 1826 as the household head. It does not name any other household member and therefore cannot, by itself, prove whether the younger Benjamin was present. He would have been fourteen on the census date, so the earlier reliance on the fifteen-to-nineteen column was not a valid exclusion.
