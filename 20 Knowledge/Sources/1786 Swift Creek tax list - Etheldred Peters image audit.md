# 1786 Swift Creek tax list - Etheldred Peters image audit

## Source identity

- Jurisdiction: Craven County, North Carolina
- Tax district: Swift Creek
- Date: 1786
- Record group description: Treasurer's and Comptroller's Papers, County Settlements with the State, Settlements of Individuals
- Image filename: `Treasurer's and Comptroller's Papers_County Settlements with the State_Settlements of Individuals_1786_SR.204.11.50_3.jpg`
- Public access copy: high-resolution scan linked by East Carolina Roots and hosted in Google Drive
- Google Drive file ID: `1QIxPNKFR-W8K6iIUe5E5gIoF1Wqw7D8p`

East Carolina Roots states that it obtained high-resolution scans of the surviving original tax-list strips from the North Carolina Department of Natural and Cultural Resources. The image audited here is the full-resolution third page.

## Direct reading

The relevant entry begins `Etheldred Peters his Inventory` and then lists three polls. The operative phrase reads approximately:

> three polls my self son John Peters and one negro

The remaining lines list about 400 acres where Etheldred lived and about 120 acres associated with Little Contentnea/Contentney and Warehouse Landing. Exact spelling should be transcribed diplomatically from the image before publication.

## Grammatical and numerical structure

The entry announces **three polls** and then enumerates:

1. `my self` — Etheldred Peters;
2. `son John Peters` — John Peters, identified as Etheldred's son;
3. one enslaved person.

The manuscript does not read `son of John Peters`. There is no `of` after `son`, and reading John as Etheldred's father would fail to account for all three polls.

## Published-transcription conflict

A published East Carolina Roots transcription renders the entry as though Etheldred were a `son of John Peters`. Inspection of the supplied high-resolution image contradicts that punctuation and interpretation.

The original-image reading supports a son named John; it does not identify Etheldred's father.

## Evidentiary conclusions

- **Documented:** Etheldred Peters reported a son named John Peters as a taxable poll in 1786.
- **Documented:** Etheldred's household assessment included one enslaved person.
- **Supported:** Etheldred held or was assessed for approximately 400 acres at his residence and approximately 120 acres near Little Contentnea/Contentney or Warehouse Landing.
- **Unresolved:** Etheldred Peters's own parents.
- **Withdrawn as a reading of this source:** the assertion that this entry identifies Etheldred as a son of John Peters.

## Genealogical limit

This source does not name Betsey or Elizabeth Peters and does not establish that she was Etheldred's daughter. Etheldred's 1788 estate file remains the best surviving record target for a fuller child, widow, administrator, guardian, or distribution list.
