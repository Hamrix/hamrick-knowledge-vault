# Maryland State Archives - Worcester County sheriffs John Selby Jr

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, Maryland Manual On-Line, "Worcester County, Maryland - Sheriffs," entry for John Selby Jr., 1779-1782, [official list](https://msa.maryland.gov/msa/mdmanual/36loc/wo/jud/sheriffs/former/html/00list.html), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

The official historical list places **John Selby Jr.** as Worcester County sheriff for the 1779-1782 term. It follows William Selby Jr., listed for 1777-1779, and precedes a blank 1782-1785 entry.

## Reliability and limitations

This official officeholder list directly supports the name, office, county, and term. It does not identify John Selby Jr.'s wife, children, parents, residence, estate, or date of death.

The shared name, county, and terminal year make the officeholder useful context for the estate bond filed under John Selby Jr. on 20 October 1782. The list does not expressly identify the sheriff as that decedent and does not connect either man to the minor Major Selby named in 1785.
