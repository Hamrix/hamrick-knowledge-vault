# FamilySearch - John Selby of Captain John 1782 Worcester inventory

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, inventory of John Selby `(of John, Capt.)`, appraised 8 November 1782 and returned by Kendal Smock and Leah his wife on 18 October 1783, *Inventories 1783-1790*, volume 11, manuscript pages 28-29; FamilySearch image group 005080353, [image `33SQ-GT15-TGG`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GT15-TGG?view=fullText&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

The heading describes the decedent as **"John Selby (of John, Capt.) late of Worcester County deceased"** and dates the appraisal to 8 November 1782.

The return identifies:

- **James Selby** and **Parker Selby** as the decedent's "Nearest of Kin."
- **William Selby** as one of the appraisers.
- **Kendal Smock and Leah his wife** as the people who appeared on 18 October 1783; the oath identifies Leah as administratrix of John Selby's goods, chattels, and credits.

The parenthetical wording is a contemporary identifier. It strongly indicates that the decedent was John associated with, and probably the son of, Captain John Selby. The inventory does not itself use the full phrase "son of" and does not name Major Selby.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary recorded inventory. It directly establishes the decedent's identifying designation, the two men recorded as nearest kin, Leah's administrative role, and Leah's marriage to Kendal by 18 October 1783.

"Nearest of kin" is a probate category and does not, by itself, state whether James and Parker were brothers, uncles, cousins, or another degree of kin. The record also does not expressly call Leah the decedent's widow or identify any child. Its relationship value comes from correlation with the 1785 guardianship, the 1790 will, and the 1795 final account.
