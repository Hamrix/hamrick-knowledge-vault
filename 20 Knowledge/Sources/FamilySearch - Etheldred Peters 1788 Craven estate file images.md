## Record examined

- Image 492 is the estate-file folder cover, labelled “Peters, Etheldred 1788 Estate.”
- Images 493-494 are an inventory. Its closing statement identifies it as the “true and perfect Inventory of the Estate of Etheldred Peters Decd.”
- Image 495 identifies the inventory as Etheldred Peters's estate and records its return to the December 1788 court.
- Images 496-499 are the associated sale-account leaves. They include entries to Elizabeth Peters and John Peters, among many other purchasers.

## Reliability and limitations

These are reviewed digitized images of the original loose estate packet. They directly establish that an estate inventory and sale account for the deceased Etheldred Peters were returned to the Craven court in 1788.

The reviewed leaves do not call Elizabeth Peters or John Peters a widow, wife, child, heir, administrator, guardian, or other relative. Their appearances as purchasers therefore do not establish any relationship to Etheldred. No parent of Etheldred is named in the reviewed packet.
