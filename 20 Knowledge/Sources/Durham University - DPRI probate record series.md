# Durham University - DPRI probate record series

## Citation

Durham University Library Archives and Special Collections, *Durham Probate Records: pre 1858*, series DPRI/2, accessed 2026-07-29, https://reed.dur.ac.uk/xtf/view?docId=ark/32150_s1bz60cw24m.xml.

## Description

Official catalogue description of the pre-1858 Durham probate collection.

## Relevant extracts

- The collection is titled Durham Probate Records: pre 1858.
- Series DPRI/2 consists of will registers containing registered copies of wills proved in the diocese of Durham.
- The associated North East Inheritance guidance likewise identifies DPRI/2 as the diocesan will-register series.

## Reliability and limitations

This is Durham University's own archival catalogue. It establishes the archival function of DPRI/2. It does not by itself identify the exact contents of volume 23 or exclude the possibility that a will register contains incidental language relevant to landholding.

## Claims supported

- [[DPRI 2 is a Durham probate will-register series, not a manorial court-roll series]]

## Claims contradicted

- The legacy description of `DPRI/2/23` as the archival reference for a 1703 manorial court roll entitled Selby v Ogle.
