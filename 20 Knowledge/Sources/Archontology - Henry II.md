# Archontology - Henry II

## Citation

Archontology.org, “Biography of Henry II,” accessed 2026-07-27, https://www.archontology.org/nations/uk/england/king_england/henry2.php.

## Description

Modern reference biography of Henry II, with citations to W. L. Warren’s *Henry II* and Z. N. Brooke and C. N. L. Brooke’s article in the *English Historical Review*.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Henry II as the son of Geoffrey Plantagenet and Empress Matilda.
- Identifies Empress Matilda as a daughter of Henry I.
- Notes the byname “Henry FitzEmpress.”

## Reliability and limitations

This is a modern reference compilation that identifies its historical authorities. It is useful corroboration for the immediate parent-child relationships, but it is not a substitute for examining the cited specialist works or medieval sources where a more contested claim is at issue.
