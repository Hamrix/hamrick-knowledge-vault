# FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby Adair probate and guardianship negative searches

## Search performed

FamilySearch full-text searches across Adair County material dated 1830-1850 returned no relevant result for the exact phrases `estate of Benjamin Selby`, `Benjamin Selby deceased`, `heirs of Benjamin Selby`, `Ben Selby deceased`, `Benjamin Selby infant`, `Benjamin Selby orphan`, or `Benjamin Selby guardian`.

Broader surname-plus-legal-term searches produced false positives and collateral Selby proceedings. Reviewed Adair pages from 1841-1847 concerned other parties, including Sally and Green Selby heir matters, and did not identify the elder Benjamin's heirs or the younger Benjamin's parent.

## Reliability and limitations

This is an OCR- and index-dependent negative search, not proof that no estate, guardianship, or relationship record exists. Handwriting recognition, variant spellings, unindexed volumes, and a possibly incorrect death range limit the result. The search narrows the next work to manual probate indexes, estate packets, tax discontinuities, and original court order books.
