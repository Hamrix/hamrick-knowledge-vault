# Maryland State Archives - Thomas Selby 1701 will images

## Citation

Maryland State Archives, Worcester County Register of Wills (Wills), CM1124-1, Book MH 3, 1666-1742, digital image 37 of 210, CR 50311, accessed 2026-08-02, https://guide.msa.maryland.gov/pages/item.aspx?ID=CM1124-1.

## Description

Official digitized manuscript will book. Image 37 contains the dispositive clauses and execution of a will by Thomas Selby of Somerset County, Maryland, dated 28 October 1701. The record-book notation on the same image states that the will was recorded 24 October 1702.

## Reviewed content

- The will names "my son Thomas Selby" in a bequest.
- It later appoints "my son Tho Selby" sole executor.
- The testator signed and sealed the will on 28 October 1701; the manuscript notation records it on 24 October 1702.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed digitized image of an official manuscript will book. It directly establishes only the relationship expressly stated between the testator Thomas Selby and a son also named Thomas Selby. The will does not identify that son with the distinct Thomas Selby whose 1757 will was proved in 1758. It therefore cannot, by itself, support a generational bridge between the two men, Major Selby, Etheldred Peters, or any proposed royal descent.
