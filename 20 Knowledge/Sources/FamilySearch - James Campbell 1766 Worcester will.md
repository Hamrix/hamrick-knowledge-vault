# FamilySearch - James Campbell 1766 Worcester will

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, recorded will of James Campbell, dated 25 December 1766 and proved 16 January 1767, manuscript pages 145-146; FamilySearch image 397 of 426, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GTBJ-9ZDB?view=fullText&keywords=Charles+Parker&searchForm=advanced&lang=en, accessed 2026-08-13.

## Relevant extract

The will names Charles Parker in a plantation-sale clause involving William Smock, a debt due to William Truitt, and costs of suit. It separately names James Campbell's children William, John, and Elizabeth and appoints sons William and John as executors.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a contemporary recorded will and establishes only that a Charles Parker appeared in James Campbell's financial or legal network. The clause does not call Charles Parker a child, heir, executor, witness, or relative of James Campbell. Because punctuation and several words are difficult, no narrower legal role is assigned without a line-by-line archival image transcription.

This record does not identify Charles Parker with either the 1761 Worcester decedent, the living co-obligor of the 1761 bond, or Margaret Selby's nineteenth-century Kentucky brother.
