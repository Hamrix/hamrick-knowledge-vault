## Required original record

## Evidentiary purpose

The marriage bond can document the Stephen Stafford–Elizabeth/Betsey Peters couple and identify associates. It cannot by itself prove that Elizabeth was a daughter of Etheldred Peters or that Ethelred R. Stafford was their son. Those are separate atomic relationships requiring estate, census, probate, land, church, or civil evidence.
