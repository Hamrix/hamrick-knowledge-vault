# Hanks - Dictionary of American Family Names Selby

## Citation

Patrick Hanks, ed., “Selby,” *Dictionary of American Family Names*, 2nd ed. (Oxford University Press, 2003, 2006), reproduced on FamilySearch, https://www.familysearch.org/en/surname?surname=SELBY.

## Description

Scholarly dictionary entry for the surname *Selby*, reproduced by FamilySearch. The source is the credited published dictionary, not the user-contributed family-tree material also hosted by FamilySearch.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies an English (Nottinghamshire) and Scottish habitational origin from Selby, Yorkshire.
- Explains the place-name through a willow-related Old English first element and Old Norse *bȳ*, “farmstead, village.”
- Also records a separate possibility: an Americanized form of a similar-sounding Jewish surname.

## Reliability and limitations

The cited dictionary is an expert-authored reference work published by Oxford University Press. The hosted page should be used only for the dictionary entry, not for any user-contributed trees or profiles on the same platform.
