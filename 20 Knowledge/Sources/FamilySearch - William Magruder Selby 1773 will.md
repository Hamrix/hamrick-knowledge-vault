# FamilySearch - William Magruder Selby 1773 will

## Citation

Prince George's County, Maryland, wills, volume 1, will of William Magruder Selby, dated 28 February 1773 and proved or recorded January 1783, FamilySearch images 419-420 of 672, ARK `3:1:33SQ-GTYR-4NM`; "Maryland, Register of Wills Records, 1629-1999."

## Record examined

The original will images were visually reviewed. William Magruder Selby distributes his land called "Chivers Folly" between sons Josiah Wilson Selby and Lingan Wilson Selby and names additional family members. The handwriting and automated transcript garble part of Lingan's name; the reading is corroborated by the Prince George's County MDGenWeb will abstract.

## Reliability and limitations

The will is direct evidence that William Magruder Selby named Lingan Wilson Selby as his son. The abstract is used only to assist the difficult name reading. This relationship is an upstream evidence island: no reviewed record yet connects Lingan to Hon. Benjamin Selby or to the proven Frederick Payne Selby chain.
