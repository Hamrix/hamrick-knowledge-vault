# Selby Families of Colonial America pages 64-66 image audit

## Source examined

Three user-supplied page images from *Selby Families of Colonial America*, section **William Selby of Prince George's County, Md.**, pages 64-66.

This is a derivative compiled source, not itself the original will, deed, bond, inventory, or court record. Its value is increased by its detailed footnotes to Maryland patents, Prince George's County land records, wills, inventories, bonds, administration accounts, and court records.

## Claims stated in the source

### Immigration and identity

The source reports that William Selby:

- was transported to Maryland in 1659 by Lieutenant Thomas Truesman;
- appears in a land record dated 11 February 1667 involving Herring Creek, Anne Arundel County;
- later resided at the plantation *Leith* in Prince George's County;
- married a woman named Mary, whose maiden surname is not supplied;
- died testate shortly before 25 February 1698/99.

### Will chronology

The source states that William Selby made his will on **5 November 1698** and that it was proved on **25 February 1698/99**.

### Wife and children

The source identifies wife **Mary Selby** and gives the children, in the order stated in the will, as:

1. Mary Selby, wife of Robert Sollars;
2. William Selby, a minor in 1698;
3. Charles Selby, probably died young;
4. Susannah Selby;
5. Samuel Selby, a minor in 1698;
6. Amee Selby, wife of Robert Hucker;
7. Ann Selby, born posthumously in 1699.

### Land devised in the will

The source reports these dispositions:

- *Good Luck* and *Packs Meadow* to daughter Mary and Robert Sollars;
- *Leith* to sons William and Charles;
- portions of *Groom's Lot* to sons William and Charles;
- 150 acres of *Twogood* to Susannah and 350 acres to Samuel;
- *Twyford* to Amee Hucker;
- *The Farme* for wife Mary's benefit;
- residue of the estate to wife Mary, executrix.

The source does **not** identify *Selby's Choice* as land owned or devised by this William Selby.

### Estate and later family evidence

The source reports:

- an estate inventory taken 23 May 1699;
- Mary later married Joshua Cecil;
- deeds of 1709 identified Ann as William's posthumous child;
- William Selby and Samuel Selby signed as next of kin in connection with Joshua Cecil's estate in 1717;
- Mary Selby Cecil died by 5 September 1718.

## Evidence assessment

### Strongly supported within this derivative source

- William Selby and Mary were a married couple.
- William named a son William in his 1698 will.
- The son William was a minor in 1698.
- William and Samuel survived into the 1710s and were treated as next of kin in the Cecil estate context.
- Ann was a posthumous daughter.
- The principal tract names associated with this immigrant William are *Good Luck*, *Packs Meadow*, *Leith*, *Groom's Lot*, *Twogood*, *Twyford*, and *The Farme*.

### Not proved by these pages alone

- Mary's maiden surname.
- That the minor son William is the same man whose 1732 will named William Magruder Selby, although the identity is strongly indicated.
- Any ownership by this William of a tract called *Selby's Choice*.
- Any direct connection from this family to Hon. Benjamin Selby.
