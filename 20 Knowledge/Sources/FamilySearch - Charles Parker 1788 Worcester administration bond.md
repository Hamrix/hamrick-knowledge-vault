# FamilySearch - Charles Parker 1788 Worcester administration bond

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, Register of Wills, *Bonds, 1786-1793*, volume 16, administration bond and letters for Charles Parker, 16 February 1788; FamilySearch DGS 005080351, images 445-446 of 536, https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9T1P-CFC?view=fullText&keywords=Selby%20Parker&lang=en and https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9T1P-CJY?view=fullText&keywords=Selby+Parker&lang=en, accessed 2026-08-13.

## Record examined

The opening text dated 16 February 1788 names **Selby Parker, Schoolfield Parker, and John Selby (of Ezekiel)** as the bound parties. The condition identifies Selby Parker as administrator of the goods, chattels, and credits of Charles Parker, late of Worcester County, deceased, **with a copy of the deceased's will annexed**. The following letters grant that administration to Selby Parker on the same date.

## Signature conflict

The opening line clearly names John Selby with the parenthetical identifier "of Ezekiel." The third signature on the continuation image is difficult and appears overwritten or corrected; it may read James or John Selby. The opening wording is preserved as the confirmed textual fact, while the signature ambiguity remains unresolved.

## Reliability and limitations

This original recorded bond directly proves the administrative role and the named bond-party association. It does not state how Selby Parker, Schoolfield Parker, John Selby, Ezekiel Selby, or Charles Parker were related. The phrase "of Ezekiel" is retained as a contemporary identifier and is not expanded into a parent-child claim without corroboration.
