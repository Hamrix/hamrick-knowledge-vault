## Reliability and limitations

This is an official archival index/transcription, rather than a reviewed image of the original assessment page. It verifies an indexed assessment entry but does not give age, parentage, residence beyond the hundred, or a chain of title. An adult assessed in 1783 cannot automatically be identified with a person merely reported to have been born in 1783.
