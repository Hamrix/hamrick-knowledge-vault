## Record examined

The original license was opened, magnified, and visually reviewed. It names Ethelred H. Stafford, age 28 and a native of Jefferson Parish, Louisiana, as a son of John E. Stafford and Eveline M. Hamrick. It names Fannie B. Coe, age 23 and a native of Franklin Parish, Louisiana, as a daughter of Jefferson Coe and Sallie Flowers.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a digitized image of a contemporary official marriage license. Its parent fields directly state both spouses' parent pairs. The structured index agrees that Jefferson Coe was Fannie's father and Sallie Flowers was her mother.

FamilySearch's automated full-text transcript misreads several fields, including Ethelred's age and Sallie Flowers's surname as `Thomas`. Magnified visual review supports `Flowers`, which also matches the structured relationship index. The record does not establish either parent pair's own ancestry.
