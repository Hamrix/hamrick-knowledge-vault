## Record examined

The reviewed original schedule lists Ben Selby, age 53, as head; Docia Selby, age 42, as wife; Robert Selby, age 5, as son; and Payne Selby, age 18, as son. The household was enumerated in Eminence, Henry County, Kentucky.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a reviewed image of an original federal census schedule. It directly establishes the reported household relationship in 1880.

The page records “Payne Selby,” whereas the 1884 marriage license calls the later groom “Frederick Payne Selby.” The 1880 record is strong corroboration when read with that license, but the identity correlation should remain explicit. It does not identify Ben's or Docia's parents, or establish a colonial, medieval, or royal link.
