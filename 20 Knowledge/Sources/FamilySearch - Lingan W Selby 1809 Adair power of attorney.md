## Record examined

The original recorded instrument and the later general index were visually reviewed. Lingan W. Selby identifies himself as a resident of Adair County, Kentucky, appoints an attorney residing in Prince George's County, Maryland, and authorizes that attorney to convey all of Lingan's land in the Maryland county to another resident Selby. Lingan acknowledged the instrument in Adair County on 16 April 1809, and clerk William Caldwell recorded it. The general index independently locates the instrument as a power of attorney in deed book A, page 363.

The attorney's surname and the other Selby's given name are difficult to read in the manuscript and are not asserted here. FamilySearch's automated transcript is used only as a navigation aid.

## Contextual records reviewed

- An Adair estate-appraisal entry records Lingan Selby as one of the sworn appraisers of James Ewing's estate in 1803: FamilySearch image 30 of 700, ARK `3:1:33SQ-GP3X-6BR`.
- Original Adair tax-list pages place Lingan Selby in the county in 1809 and 1810: FamilySearch images 53 and 357 of 1,136, ARKs `3:1:3Q9M-CS3J-DQMX-W` and `3:1:3Q9M-CS3J-DQSZ-1`.

## Reliability and limitations

The power of attorney directly establishes Lingan's Adair residence and continuing land interest in Prince George's County in 1809. It does not name a parent, spouse, child, or heir. The combined name, middle initial, Maryland county, Kentucky residence, and chronology strongly identify this Adair man with William Magruder Selby's son Lingan Wilson Selby, but no single reviewed record expressly makes that cross-record identification.
