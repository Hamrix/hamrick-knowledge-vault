# Catalogue of the Harleian Manuscripts Volume 1 - Harley MS 301

## Citation

*A Catalogue of the Harleian Manuscripts in the British Museum*, volume 1 (London, 1808), entry for Harley MS 301.

## Description

Published archival catalogue entry describing the contents, date, and scribal provenance of Harley MS 301.

## Relevant finding

The catalogue describes Harley MS 301 as a paper folio collection in the hand of Sir Simonds D'Ewes or his amanuenses. It contains collected transcripts from Exchequer records, including pleas and fines of Richard I and pleas of King John's reign.

## Reliability and limitations

The catalogue establishes the nature and provenance of the manuscript: Harley MS 301 is an early modern transcript collection, not a contemporary medieval pedigree. The project reviewed the searchable catalogue text but did not locate or inspect an image of folio 171b. Consequently, the 1904 report that folio 171b calls Walter's wife `Sibilla de Kyme` remains unverified at manuscript level.

## Claims supported

- [[Walter de Ver was husband of Sibilla de Kyme]] - identifies the nature of the still-unreviewed source behind the `de Kyme` component.

## Claims contradicted

- The catalogue does not support describing Harley MS 301 itself as a contemporary medieval pedigree.
