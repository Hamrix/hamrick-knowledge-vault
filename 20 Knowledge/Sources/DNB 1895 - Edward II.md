# DNB 1895 - Edward II

## Citation

T. F. Tout, "Edward II of Carnarvon (1284-1327)," in *Dictionary of National Biography*, 1885-1900, vol. 17 (1895), accessed 2026-07-27, https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Dictionary_of_National_Biography,_1885-1900/Edward_II.

## Description

Published biographical entry on Edward II by historian T. F. Tout, digitized by Wikisource.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Edward II as the fourth son of Edward I and his first wife, Eleanor of Castile.
- Dates his birth to 1284 at Caernarfon Castle.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a historical scholarly biography rather than a contemporary record. It independently corroborates the direct parent-child relationship, but the 1895 entry should not be treated as sole proof for disputed or more distant genealogy.
