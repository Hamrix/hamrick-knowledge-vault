# Fowler 1893 - Coucher Book of Selby Volume II page 296

## Citation

J. T. Fowler, editor, *The Coucher Book of Selby*, volume II (Yorkshire Archaeological and Topographical Association, 1893), printed page 296, no. MCCXVIII, `Carta Nicholai de Chauncurt`.

## Description

Scholarly edition of the Selby Abbey cartulary. The full printed page and Latin charter text were visually reviewed from the public-domain Google Books scan.

## Relevant extracts

Nicholas de Chauncurt makes his grant `pro amore Dei et ... Gundredae de Ver, uxoris meae`, for the love of God and Gundreda de Ver, his wife. He later refers again to `praedictae Gundredae uxoris meae`.

The charter also records the assent and consent of `Willelmi de Ver, filii mei`, William de Ver, Nicholas's son. The literal statement is retained, but this audit does not decide from that phrase alone whether William was Nicholas's biological son or stepson.

## Reliability and limitations

The marital wording is explicit and first-person in a scholarly charter edition. The project reviewed the edition, not the medieval cartulary manuscript or a surviving original charter.

The charter does not mention Adam of Goxhill or Walter de Ver and therefore does not prove that Gundreda was Adam's widow or Walter's mother. Selby in this source is the abbey and place, not evidence of a Selby-surname lineage.
