# FamilySearch - Benjamin Selby and Lucy S Hunter 1844 Indiana marriage

## Citation

Clark County, Indiana, marriage register, page 57, Benjamin Selby and Lucy S. Hunter, married 27 February 1844 by Charles Bonner; "Indiana, Marriages, 1811-2019," FamilySearch record `XXPM-T2Z`, original image ARK `3:1:S3HT-68V3-BV2`.

## Record examined

The original register image and indexed record were reviewed. They identify Benjamin Selby and Lucy S. Hunter as the marrying parties and give the marriage date, county, page, and officiant.

## Reliability and limitations

The contemporary civil register directly proves the marriage. It names no parent and does not by itself prove that this Benjamin was the later Kentucky attorney. The unusual spouse-name and date agreement with the 1878 biography, followed by wife initials `L. S.` in the 1850 household, make the identity correlation strong.
