# Royal Family - Henry III

## Citation

The Royal Family, “Henry III (r. 1216–1272),” accessed 2026-07-27, https://www.royal.uk/henry-iii.

## Description

Official Royal Household historical profile of Henry III.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Henry III as King John’s son.
- States that he became king at age nine after John’s death in 1216.

## Reliability and limitations

This is an official historical profile and provides a clear statement of the immediate father-son relationship. It is not a full scholarly genealogy, so the relationship is corroborated with an independent institutional source.
