# FamilySearch - Worcester County will index 1742-1821

## Citation

Worcester County, Maryland, *Will Indexes, 1742-1821*, volume 1, digitized images, FamilySearch, [full-text viewer](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33S7-9T1D-YJG?view=fullText&lang=en&groupId=M9LR-V8C), images 1-87, accessed 2026-08-04; collection: [Maryland, Register of Wills Records, 1629-1999](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1803986).

## Record examined

The reviewed original index volume covers Worcester County will-related entries from 1742 through 1821. Its digitized images have a FamilySearch full-text transcription.

The visible transcription was searched page by page for the exact phrase **"Major Selby"** (and the reversed order **"Selby Major"**). No exact phrase was returned in images 6-87, the substantive portion of the 87-image volume. The early images are title and introductory material.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a digitized original index, but the name check used the viewer's OCR-derived transcription rather than a manually read entry for every possible variant. A zero result does **not** prove that no Major Selby probate or will-related record exists: the name may be absent from this index, be misread by OCR, appear under a different legal role, or occur in a record series outside the volume.

The index records do show many other Selby names in probate-administration contexts. Those name appearances do not establish kinship with Major Selby and must not be joined to the later Maryland/Kentucky/Indiana target without an original record that expressly identifies the relationship.

## Next research action

Use the index as a locator only. Read any candidate underlying will, administration, account, or guardianship page in image form and transcribe all stated kinship terms. The still-unreviewed 1797 chancery packet, MSA S512-6-4300, remains the higher-value target because it names Major Selby directly.
