# FamilySearch - Scarborough Parker estate account images

## Citation

Account of Thomas Parker, Mary his wife and executrix, and Samuel Taylor, executors of Scarborough Parker; inventory exhibited 9 January 1789; FamilySearch images `3Q9M-CSTB-QQGP-J` and `3Q9M-CSTB-QQRG-5`.

## Record examined

The continuation records an allowed payment to William Atkinson Selby on an account proved and receipted.

## Reliability and limitations

The original heading and parties identify Worcester County. FamilySearch full-text discovery incorrectly surfaced the item under a Bourbon County collection label. The record establishes a payment and associate network, not a family relationship.
