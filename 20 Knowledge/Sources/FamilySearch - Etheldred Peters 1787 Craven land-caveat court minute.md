# FamilySearch - Etheldred Peters 1787 Craven land-caveat court minute

## Citation

North Carolina State Archives, *Craven County Court Minutes, 1784-1787*, page 237, 31 August 1787, land caveat of Etheldred Peters against Anthony Vanpelt; digitized at FamilySearch, image 402 of 426, accessed 2026-08-03, [image viewer](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3Q9M-CSY8-V956-4?view=fullText&keywords=Etheldred+Peters&lang=en).

## Relevant extract and finding

The minute records the sheriff's return “upon the Caveat of Etheldred Peters agt Anthony Vanpelt.” It describes the jury as sworn on a land caveat “between Etheldred Peters plaintiff and Anthony Vanpelt defendant,” and records the jury's conclusion that Vanpelt's warrant and survey were outside Peters's land lines and that Peters had his complement of land according to his patent's courses and distances. The return is dated 31 August 1787; the court ordered a certificate to issue accordingly.
