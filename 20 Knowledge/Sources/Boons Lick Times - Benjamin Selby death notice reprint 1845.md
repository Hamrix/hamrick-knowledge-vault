# Boons Lick Times - Benjamin Selby death notice reprint 1845

## Citation

"The Frankfort (Ky.) Commonwealth announces," *Boon's Lick times* (Fayette, Missouri), 7 June 1845, 2, Library of Congress, LCCN `sn83016957`.

## Record examined

The issue's searchable page text was checked against the highlighted newspaper item. It states: "The Frankfort (Ky.) Commonwealth announces the death of Benjamin Selby, Auditor of State, on the 25th ult."

Because the issue is dated 7 June 1845, `the 25th ult.` means **25 May 1845**. The item is a contemporaneous reprint attributing the announcement to the Frankfort *Commonwealth*.

## Evidence value

The notice independently supplies an exact death date and identifies the deceased Benjamin Selby with the state-auditor office. [[Kentucky Senate journals - Benjamin Selby auditor appointment and death succession 1834-1846|Original Kentucky Senate journals]] independently prove that Benjamin Selby was appointed Auditor of Public Accounts in 1834 and that a successor was appointed in his place after his death.

This materially corroborates the public-office description in [[Armstrong 1878 - Ben Selby biographical sketch]] and [[Courier-Journal - Col Ben Selby 1883 interview]]. It does not name a wife, child, parent, heir, residence, or estate representative.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a near-contemporaneous newspaper notice, but it is a reprint rather than the original Frankfort item. Its title `Auditor of State` is compatible with the original journal's `Auditor of Public Accounts`, but the original *Commonwealth* issue should still be inspected for fuller wording or an obituary.

The notice proves neither that the younger Benjamin was the deceased auditor's son nor that Lingan Wilson Selby was the auditor's father. It must not be used to merge those relationships.
