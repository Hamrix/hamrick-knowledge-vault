# FamilySearch - Parker Selby 1747 Worcester executor bond

## Citation

Mary Selby renunciation and executor bond for the estate of Parker Selby, Worcester County, Maryland, 1747, *Probate Records 1667-1753*, FamilySearch image 310 of 394, image group 005080349, [`33SQ-GT1G-PYS`](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:33SQ-GT1G-PYS?view=fullText&lang=en), accessed 8 August 2026.

## Record examined

Mary Selby relinquished her right to administer the estate of her deceased husband Parker Selby in favor of the other executors named in his will. The following bond names John Selby and Parker Selby as executors, with Joshua Mitchell, William Walton Jr., and Peter Claywell as sureties.

## Reliability and limitations

This is a primary probate record that corroborates the widow and executor identities in Parker's 1746 will and places administration of the same estate in Worcester County. The bond does not itself call John and Parker sons of the deceased; that relationship comes directly from the will.
