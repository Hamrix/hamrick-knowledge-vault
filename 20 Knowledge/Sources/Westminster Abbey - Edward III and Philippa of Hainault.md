# Westminster Abbey - Edward III and Philippa of Hainault

## Citation

Westminster Abbey, "Edward III and Philippa of Hainault," accessed 2026-07-27, https://www.westminster-abbey.org/abbey-commemorations/royals/edward-iii-and-philippa-of-hainault.

## Description

Institutional historical profile for Edward III and Philippa of Hainault, maintained by Westminster Abbey.

## Relevant extracts

- Identifies Edward III as the son of Edward II and Isabel (Isabella) of France.
- Dates Edward III's birth to 13 November 1312 at Windsor Castle.

## Reliability and limitations

Westminster Abbey maintains the royal commemoration and historical profile. It provides direct support for the named parent-child relationship, but it does not prove a more distant pedigree or any modern descent.
