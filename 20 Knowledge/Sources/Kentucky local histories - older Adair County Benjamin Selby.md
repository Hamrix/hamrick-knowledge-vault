# Kentucky local histories - older Adair County Benjamin Selby

## Sources examined

- Kentucky Heritage Council, National Register of Historic Places nomination, *Columbia Commercial Historic District*, describing a Columbia law office used by Benjamin Selby around 1825: [nomination PDF](https://heritage.ky.gov/historic-places/national-register/Property%20Listings/Adair_ColumbiaCommercialDis.pdf).
- *Columbia Magazine*, Adair County sheriff history, identifying Benjamin Selby as a deputy sheriff in 1812: [article](https://www.columbiamagazine.com/index.php?sid=130129).

## Reliability and limitations

These are later historical summaries, not the underlying 1812 appointment record or an 1825 deed, tax entry, or directory. They are adequate to establish a strong same-name warning and to direct retrieval of the original Adair County records.

A man holding public office in 1812 and practicing law around 1825 cannot be the target Henry County attorney whose 1854 marriage license reports age 29. The sources do not identify either man's parents and do not by themselves explain whether the two men were related.
