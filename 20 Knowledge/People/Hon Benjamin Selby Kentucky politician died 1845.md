# Hon Benjamin Selby Kentucky politician died 1845

## Identity

**Hon. Benjamin Selby** is the elder Benjamin Selby identified in the 1878 *Biographical Encyclopaedia of Kentucky* profile as the father of lawyer **Ben Selby**, born 31 January 1826 at Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky.

The 1878 profile reportedly describes the elder Benjamin as Maryland-born, first settled in Bourbon County and later resident in Adair County, active for many years in Kentucky public affairs, and deceased in 1845. Those details remain source-reported until the original printed profile and independent contemporary records are inspected.

This person must **not** currently be equated without qualification with the **Ben Shelby** listed by the Kentucky Auditor of Public Accounts as auditor from 1820-1834.

## Relationships

- **Reported son:** [[Ben Selby born 1826]] — the 1878 biographical profile explicitly calls the younger Ben a son of Hon. Benjamin Selby. The vault's later records independently correlate the younger Ben with Docia/Sophia Stone and sons Frederick Payne Selby and Robert C. Selby.
- **Proposed parents:** Lingan Wilson Selby and Drucilla/Drusilla Fowler — **not established**. See [[Lingan Wilson Selby as father of Hon Benjamin Selby remains unproven]].
- **Possible wife/associate:** an 1811-1815 Adair land-record index has an entry for "Benjamin & Betsy" Selby. The deed image is required before Betsy's relationship can be stated.

## Verified facts

At the present evidence level, the safest factual statements are source-specific:

- A near-contemporary 1878 Kentucky biography reports a father-son relationship between Hon. Benjamin Selby and Ben Selby born in 1826.
- A Benjamin Selby appears repeatedly in an index to Adair County land records for 1811-1815, including one entry with Betsy.
- Adair County deed/court abstracts place a Benjamin Selby among Columbia town trustees in 1816 and among court-appointed commissioners in 1823. These are strong locality clues but require original-record correlation before they are treated as certain events for this exact man.
- The official Kentucky Auditor history lists **Ben Shelby** from 1820-1834 and **Thomas S. Page** from 1834-1846.

## Claims requiring evidence

- Whether this Hon. Benjamin Selby is the same individual as official auditor **Ben Shelby** (1820-1834).
- The 1878 biography's statement that Hon. Benjamin became auditor in 1834 and remained in office until his death in 1845. This conflicts with the official institutional chronology and must not be repeated as settled fact.
- Exact dates and districts of his reported nineteen years of Kentucky legislative service.
- Exact date and place of death in 1845.
- Identity of his wife or wives.
- His parents. The proposed Lingan Wilson Selby / Drucilla Fowler parentage is unresolved.
- His migration sequence from Maryland to Bourbon County and then Adair County.
