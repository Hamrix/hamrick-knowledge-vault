# Sophia Docia Elizabeth Stone Selby

## Identity

**Sophia Docia Elizabeth Stone Selby** is the preferred Aetheling Evidence Project identity for the woman who appears under the source-specific forms Sophia E. Stone, Sophia Stone, Sophia Selby, Docia/Docie Stone or Selby, and Dora Stone.

This preferred name is a correlated project identity. No single reviewed record currently displays the entire name.

## Relationships

- **Mother:** Sophia Bryan directly called Sophia Stone “my daughter” in the 4 January 1854 marriage-license consent.
- **Spouse or intended spouse:** Benjamin Selby; a license was issued on 5 January 1854, although the reviewed paper sequence does not yet include a minister's return.
- **Reported son:** Frederick Payne Selby; his 1884 marriage license names Benjamin Selby and Docia Stone as his parents, and the 1880 census records Payne as the son of Ben and Docia Selby.
- **Reported son:** Robert C. Selby; the 1880 census records Robert as a son of Ben and Docia, and his 1938 death certificate names Benjamin Selby and Dora Stone as his parents.

## Source-exact appearances

- **1854:** Sophia Stone / Sophia E. Stone in Shelby County marriage-license papers.
- **1860:** Sophia Selby in the Oldham County census household of Benjn Selby.
- **1874:** Docie Stone in the indexed birth entry for Roberts Selby; original image still needed.
- **1880:** Docia Selby, wife of Ben Selby, in Henry County, Kentucky.
- **1884:** Docia Stone in Frederick Payne Selby's Orleans Parish marriage-license parent fields.
- **1938:** Dora Stone in Robert C. Selby's Kentucky death-certificate parent fields.

## Project conclusion and limits

The project treats these forms as one correlated woman because of the repeated Benjamin/Ben Selby association, Kentucky geography, chronology, and recurring children Payne and Robert. Every citation should nevertheless preserve the name written in the underlying record.

The reviewed 1854 record gives only the middle initial **E.**. The project preference expands it to **Elizabeth**, but an explicit record spelling out Elizabeth has not yet been identified in the reviewed primary-source notes.
