# Ben Selby born 1826

## Identity

**Ben Selby**, reported born 31 January 1826 at Columbia, Adair County, Kentucky, is the Kentucky lawyer identified in the 1878 *Biographical Encyclopaedia of Kentucky* and correlated with the later Benjamin/Ben Selby who appears with Sophia/Docia Stone Selby and their children.

The exact birth detail presently comes from the transcription of the 1878 biography and should be checked against the original printed page and a primary vital, church, Bible, or contemporary family record if available.

## Relationships

- **Reported father:** [[Hon Benjamin Selby Kentucky politician died 1845]] — explicitly stated on printed page 580 of the 1878 biography as represented in the current transcription; relationship rated strong, pending visual inspection of the page image and independent relationship-level corroboration.
- **First reported wife:** Lucy S. Hunter — marriage reported by the 1878 biography as 1844; independent marriage evidence not yet summarized in this person note.
- **Second wife / correlated spouse:** [[Sophia Docia Elizabeth Stone Selby]] — the 1878 biography reports a later marriage to Docia Stone; an 1854 Shelby County marriage-license sequence exists for Benjamin Selby and Sophia E. Stone, and later records repeatedly identify Docia/Dora Stone with Benjamin's children.
- **Son:** Frederick Payne Selby — his 1884 Orleans Parish marriage license names Benjamin Selby and Docia Stone as his parents.
- **Son:** Robert C. Selby — his 1938 Kentucky death certificate reports Benjamin Selby and Dora Stone as his parents; the 1880 census also places Robert in Ben and Docia's household.

## Verified facts

- The reviewed 1880 census places Ben Selby and wife Docia in Eminence, Henry County, Kentucky, with sons including Payne and Robert.
- Frederick Payne Selby's 1884 marriage license identifies his parents as Benjamin Selby and Docia Stone.
- Robert C. Selby's 1938 death certificate identifies his parents as Benjamin Selby and Dora Stone.
- The 1878 biography's distinctive combination of Columbia birth, legal career, Docia Stone marriage, and Eminence residence strongly correlates its subject with the same family group.
- A contemporaneous 1860 volume of Kentucky General Assembly acts includes a resolution titled **"Resolution authorizing the Auditor to settle with Ben Selby late clerk of the Senate"** on page 177. This independently corroborates the 1878 biography's report that the younger Ben served as Clerk of the State Senate around 1859-1860.
