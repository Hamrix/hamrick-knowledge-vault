# Humphrey Stafford, first duke of Buckingham

## Identity

English soldier and magnate who became first duke of Buckingham.

## Relationships

- Father: Edmund Stafford, fifth earl of Stafford.
- Mother: Anne of Gloucester, daughter of Thomas of Woodstock.
- Verified relationship claim: [[Humphrey Stafford was a great-grandson of Edward III through Anne of Gloucester]].

## Verified facts

- Born 15 August 1402 and died 10 July 1460, according to [[Rawcliffe 2008 - Humphrey Stafford]].
- His maternal great-grandfather was Edward III through Thomas of Woodstock and Anne of Gloucester.
