# Margaret, queen of Scots

## Identity

Queen consort of Scotland through her marriage to Malcolm III; daughter of Edward the Exile.

## Relationships

- Father: Edward the Exile, son of Edmund Ironside.
- Husband: Malcolm III, king of Scots.
- Daughter: Matilda of Scotland, wife of Henry I of England.
- Verified relationship claim: [[Margaret of Scotland linked Edward the Exile’s line to Henry I of England’s family]].

## Verified facts

- Died 16 November 1093, according to [[People of Medieval Scotland - Margaret queen of Scots]].
- Her documented relationships created a bridge from Edward the Exile’s line to Henry I’s family through Matilda.
